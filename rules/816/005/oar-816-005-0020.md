## Full text

816-005-0020 Vacancies in Office If the chairperson or vice chairperson is unable to complete the Officer’s respective term, then the Board must elect another chairperson or vice chairperson.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 676.780 & ORS 676.783 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 676.780
