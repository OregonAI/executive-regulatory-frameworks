## Full text

816-025-0080 SLI Legal License

(1) A SLI legal license holder may provide SLI services in a legal setting.

(2) Only a person holding a SLI legal license under this rule may provide SLI services in a legal setting.

(3) A SLI legal license is current for one year and becomes inactive on the last day of the month one year from the date of issuance.

(4) A SLI legal license may be renewed.

(5) The issuance or the licensee’s holding of a SLI legal license does not supersede, replace, or negate the need for the licensee to comply with other laws requiring licensure or registration or competent SLI services.

(6) A legal setting does not include proceedings in federal courts.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 676.768 & ORS 676.783 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 676.750, ORS 676.753, ORS 676.756, ORS 676.768 & ORS 676.783
