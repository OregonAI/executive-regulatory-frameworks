## Full text

824-060-0020 Behavior Analysis Interventionist Professional Methods and Procedures A registered behavior analysis interventionist is subject to ORS 676.612 and OAR 331-020-0075. In addition, a registered behavior analysis interventionist must:

(1) O nly perform duties within their scope of practice in applied behavior analysis in which they are competent .

(2) Tell a supervisor if the interventionist needs additional training to perform the intervention.

(3) Follow written treatment and behavior intervention protocols.

(4) Gather and report data accurately.

(5) Protect client confidentiality.

(6) Report time worked with clients accurately.

(7) Not accept gifts worth more than $10 from clients.

(8) Not engage in romantic or sexual relationships with current clients. Not engage in romantic or sexual relationships with prior clients for two years after the professional relationship ends.

(9) Not engage in romantic or sexual relationships with current supervisors until the supervision ends and the termination is documented.

(10) Must disclose any relationships with the client or the client’s family to their supervisor.

(11) Must tell their supervisor if their client’s family is requesting the performance of tasks outside the scope of the client’s intervention plan.
