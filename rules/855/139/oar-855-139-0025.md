## Full text

855-139-0025 Registration: Change of Business Name A RDSP Affiliated Pharmacy must notify the board a minimum of 15 days prior to any change of business name of a Retail Drug Outlet RDSP. The change must be reported by filing a new application for which no fee is required.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 475.035 & ORS 689.205 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 689.155
