## Full text

855-139-0350 Dispensing: Containers Each pharmacy must dispense a drug in a new container that complies with the current provisions of the Poison Prevention Packaging Act in 16 CFR 1700 (v. 01/01/2025), 16 CFR 1701 (v. 01/01/2025), and 16 CFR 1702 (v. 01/01/2025).
