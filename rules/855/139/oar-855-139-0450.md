## Full text

855-139-0450 Drugs and Devices: Disposal Drugs and devices that are outdated, damaged, deteriorated, misbranded, or adulterated must be quarantined and physically separated from other drugs until they are destroyed or returned to their supplier.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 475.035, ORS 689.205, ORS 689.305 & ORS 689.315 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 689.155
