## Full text

855-139-0310 Prescription: Verification of Authenticity Alteration of a written prescription, other than by an Oregon licensed Pharmacist's or practitioner's authorization, in any manner constitutes an invalid order unless verified with the prescriber.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 689.205 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 689.151 & ORS 689.155
