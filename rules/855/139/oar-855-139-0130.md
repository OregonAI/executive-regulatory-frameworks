## Full text

855-139-0130 Drug: Loss A RDSP and its RDSP Affiliated Pharmacy must:

(1) Ensure that disasters, accidents and emergencies which may affect the strength, purity, or labeling of drugs or devices are reported to the board immediately.

(2) Ensure that confirmed significant drug loss or any loss related to suspected drug theft of a controlled substance is reported to the board within one business day.

(3) Ensure that a Report of Theft or Loss of Controlled Substances (DEA Form 106) or Report of Theft or Loss of Listed Chemicals (DEA Form 107) is sent to the Drug Enforcement Administration, a copy is sent to the board at the same time.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 475.035, ORS 689.205 & ORS 689.315 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 689.155
