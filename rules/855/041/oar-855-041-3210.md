## Full text

855-041-3210 Telework: Registration The Oregon registered Drug Outlet Pharmacy and the Pharmacist-in-charge of the Drug Outlet Pharmacy are responsible for all licensees assisting in the practice of pharmacy at Telework Sites.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 689.135, ORS 689.151 & ORS 689.205 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 689.155
