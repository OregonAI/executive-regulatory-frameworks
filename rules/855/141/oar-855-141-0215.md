## Full text

855-141-0215 Outlet: Pharmacist Utilization A PPK and its PPK Affiliated Pharmacy must ensure that a prescription drug or device is not released from the PPK until the Pharmacist or Intern has:

(1) Provided counseling when required under OAR 855-115-0145 or when requested by the patient or patient’s agent; and

(2) Documented the interaction.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 689.205 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 689.155 & ORS 689.527
