## Full text

855-115-0122 Responsibilities: Supervision

(1) When supervising a Certified Oregon Pharmacy Technician or Pharmacy Technician, each Pharmacist may supervise as many Certified Oregon Pharmacy Technicians or Pharmacy Technicians as they believe in their reasonable professional judgment is appropriate to promote and protect patient health, safety and welfare.

(2) When supervising an Intern, each Pharmacist may supervise: (a) No more than four Interns participating in direct patient care activities. (b) As many Interns as they believe in their reasonable professional judgment is appropriate to promote and protect patient health, safety and welfare for Interns participating in non-direct patient care activities such as informational health fairs that provide general information, but not patient-specific information.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 689.205 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 689.155
