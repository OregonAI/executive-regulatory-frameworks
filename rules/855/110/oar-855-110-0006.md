## Full text

855-110-0006 Pharmacist Examination Fees Paid to NABP

(1) Pharmacist examination fees to be paid to the National Association of Boards of Pharmacy (NABP) for NAPLEX exam: (a) NAPLEX Examination fee - $520 (b) NAPLEX Reexamination fee - $520 (c) All other associated fees see NABP website.

(2) Pharmacist jurisprudence examination fees to be paid to NABP for MPJE exam: (a) MPJE Examination fee - $170 (b) MPJE Reexamination fee - $170 (c) All other associated fees see NABP website.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 689.205 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 689.135
