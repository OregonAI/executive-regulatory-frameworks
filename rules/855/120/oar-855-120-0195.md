## Full text

855-120-0195 Out-of-State Internship Experience

(1) In order for an Intern to obtain credit for experience obtained outside of Oregon as part of an COP or SOP with an Internship Program based in Oregon, an Intern must be licensed as required by state laws and rules in the state in which they practice.

(2) In order for an out-of-state intern to engage in the practice of pharmacy in the State of Oregon, the intern must: (a) Be licensed as an Intern by the State of Oregon; and (b) Comply with ORS 475, ORS 689 and OAR 855.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 689.151, ORS 689.205 & ORS 689.255

History: BP 17-2023, adopt filed 08/16/2023, effective 03/01/2024
