## Full text

855-120-0155 Grounds for Discipline - Intern The following are grounds for discipline:

(1) Continuing to practice as an Intern when one of the following has occurred: (a) Dismissal from the Doctor of Pharmacy degree program enrolled in to obtain the Intern license; or (b) Failure to maintain an active Intern license; or

(2) Any other grounds found in ORS 689.405.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 689.205 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 689.405
