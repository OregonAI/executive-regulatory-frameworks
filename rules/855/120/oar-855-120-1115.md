## Full text

855-120-1115 Responsibilities: Duty to Report - Preceptor Within 10 working days, unless state or federal laws relating to confidentiality or the protection of health information prohibit disclosure, each:

(1) Preceptor at an Internship Program site must report if they have dismissed an Intern from an Internship Program site for reasons including but not limited to patient safety, unprofessional conduct or suspected violation of ORS 475, ORS 689 or OAR 855.

(2) Internship Program Supervisor must report the following on behalf of a COP or SOP if it: (a) Has removed a Preceptor or Internship Program site from the Internship Program for reasons including but not limited to patient safety, unprofessional conduct or suspected violation of ORS 475, ORS 689 or OAR 855; or (b) Has dismissed an Intern from a Doctor of Pharmacy degree program.

(3) For

(1) and

(2) the Preceptor and Internship Program Supervisor must report the date and reason for the removal.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 689.205 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 676.150, ORS 689.151, ORS 689.155 & ORS 689.455
