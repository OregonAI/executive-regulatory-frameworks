## Full text

855-120-1155 Grounds for Discipline - Preceptor The board may suspend, revoke, or restrict the license of a Preceptor or may impose a civil penalty upon the Preceptor upon the following grounds:

(1) Continuing to supervise an Intern in an Internship Program when one of the following has occurred: (a) School has removed the Preceptor or Internship Program site from the Internship Program for reasons including but not limited to patient safety, unprofessional conduct or suspected violation of ORS 475, ORS 689 or OAR 855. (b) Licensee is not permitted to supervise an Intern per Board order. (c) Registrant is not permitted to utilize Interns per Board order.

(2) Any other grounds found in ORS 689.405.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 689.205 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 689.405
