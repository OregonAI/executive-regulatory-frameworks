## Full text

855-104-0105 Filing Exceptions and Argument to the Board After a proposed order has been served on a party, the party has 30 days to file written exceptions with the board from receipt of the proposed order.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 689.205 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 689.151
