## Full text

855-143-0345 Dispensing: General Requirements The PPL Affiliated Pharmacy must:

(1) Ensure each prescription, prescription refill, and drug order is correctly dispensed by the PPL in accordance with the prescribing practitioner's authorization; and

(2) Ensure the PPL dispenses prescriptions accurately and to the correct party.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 689.205 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 689.155 & ORS 689.527
