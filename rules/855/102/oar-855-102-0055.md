## Full text

855-102-0055 Board Compliance Director and Officers The board’s Compliance Director and Compliance Officers:

(1) Must be Pharmacists licensed in the State of Oregon; and

(2) Are authorized to provide appropriate deadline extensions.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 689.205 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 689.195
