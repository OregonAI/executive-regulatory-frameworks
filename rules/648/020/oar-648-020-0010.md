## Full text

648-020-0010 Purpose of Public Records Requests Processing Rules

(1) To implement the provisions of ORS 192.324(7) that require each public body to publish its methods for the public to obtain information and in order to implement the provisions of ORS 192.318 to provide reasonable rules necessary for the protection of Oregon Hemp Commission records and to prevent interference with the regular discharge of the commission’s duties, the commission adopts the procedures set forth in this division. Procedures for review of any denial of public records requests, or fee waiver or reduction, shall be in accordance with the provisions of ORS 192.311 to 192.478.

(2) Capitalized terms in this division not specifically defined are as defined in ORS 192.311.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 192.318, ORS 192.324, ORS 571.406(3) & ORS 571.423(19) Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 192.311 to ORS 192.478
