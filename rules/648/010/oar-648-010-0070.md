## Full text

648-010-0070 Dispute Regarding Amount of Assessment A Producer or a Vertically Integrated Producer may file a challenge to an Assessment with the Commission, on a form provided by the State Department of Agriculture, in accordance with OAR Chapter 603, Division 43.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 571.406(3), ORS 571.423 & ORS 571.447 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 571.406(3), ORS 571.423 & ORS 571.447 - 571.501
