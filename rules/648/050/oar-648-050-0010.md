## Full text

648-050-0010 Purpose and Objectives OAR chapter 648, division 50, implements ORS 571.444, which authorizes Commission to develop intellectual property that relates to hemp or assists in the implementation, maintenance or development of Commission programs. ORS 571.444 requires the Commission to adopt rules to govern the ownership, management, disposal and use of intellectual property and other activities of the Commission relating to intellectual property.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 571.423

(19) & ORS 571.444 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 571.444
