## Full text

648-050-0060 Waiver or Modification The Commission may, in the exercise of its discretion, waive or modify any requirements of OAR chapter 648, division 50, unless such waiver or modification would violate applicable federal or state law.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 571.423(19) & ORS 571.444 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 571.444
