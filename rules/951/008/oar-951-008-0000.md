## Full text

951-008-0000 Purpose The purpose of these rules is to provide guidance for the administration of the portion of the Oregon Production Investment Fund that is to be used for the Creative Opportunity Program as authorized by HB4153 (2022).

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 284.300 to 284.385 Statutes/Other Implemented: HB 4153, 2022; Chapter 75 (2022 Laws)
