## Full text

340-096-0280 Department Review of Waste Tire Carrier Permit Applications

(1) Applications for waste tire carrier permits shall be processed in accordance with the Procedures for Issuance, Denial, Modification and Revocation of Permits as set forth in OAR 340, Division 93, except as otherwise provided in this division.

(2) Applications for waste tire carrier permits shall be complete only if they: (a) Are submitted on forms provided by the Department, accompanied by all required exhibits, and the forms are completed in full and are signed by the applicant(s); (b) Include the appropriate application fee pursuant to OAR 340 Division 97; and (c) Include acceptable financial assurance pursuant to OAR 340-096-0260.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 459 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 459.745, 459.750 & 459.755
