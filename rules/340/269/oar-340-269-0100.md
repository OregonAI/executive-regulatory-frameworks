## Full text

340-269-0100 Voluntary Participation Requirements The voluntary participation in this program by any person conclusively establishes consent to be subject to the jurisdiction of the State of Oregon, its courts, and the administrative authority of DEQ to implement this program. If a person does not consent to such jurisdiction, then that person may not participate in the program.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 468.020, ORS 468.035 & 468A.813 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 468.035 & 468A.813

History: DEQ 7-2023, adopt filed 07/20/2023, effective 08/01/2023
