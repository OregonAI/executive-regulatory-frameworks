# Acronyms (OAR 340-269-0060)

## Full text

340-269-0060 Acronyms The following acronyms apply to this division:

(1) “C.F.R.” means Code of Federal Regulations and, notwithstanding OAR 340-200-0035 and except as otherwise identified, refers to the edition in effect on the date the EQC approves these rules.

(2) “DEQ” means Oregon Department of Environmental Quality.

(3) “DOC” means diesel oxidation catalyst.

(4) “DPF” means diesel particulate filter.

(5) "ECL" means emission control label.

(6) “EPA” means the federal Environmental Protection Agency.

(7) "ERN" means equipment registration number.

(8) “GVWR” means gross vehicle weight rating.

(9) “MEL” means manufacturer’s engine label.

(10) “ODOT” means Oregon Department of Transportation.

(11) “ZEE” means zero-emission equipment.

(12) “ZEV” means zero-emission vehicle.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 468.020 & ORS 468A.813 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 468A.813

History: DEQ 7-2023, adopt filed 07/20/2023, effective 08/01/2023
