## Full text

340-269-0120 Demonstration Approval Emissions Profile. DEQ will review each application and determine whether the applicant has provided sufficient information to document the emissions profile of each nonroad diesel engine, in addition to the engine profile for each concrete mixer truck and dump truck proposed to be registered. If DEQ determines the information is not sufficient, DEQ will notify the applicant and identify the additional information needed for DEQ to approve the application for each nonroad and onroad diesel engine to receive an ECL.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 468.020, ORS 468.035 & 468A.813 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 468.035 & 468A.813
