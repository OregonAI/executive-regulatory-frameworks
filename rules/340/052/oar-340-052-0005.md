# Purpose (OAR 340-052-0005)

## At a glance

OAR 340-052-0005 — Purpose. Chapter 340, Division 052. Statutory authority and AON
history are in the full text below.

## Full text

340-052-0005 Purpose The purpose of these rules is to prescribe requirements and procedures to obtain approval of plans and specifications as required by ORS 468B.055 for the construction, installation or modification of disposal systems, treatment works and sewerage systems.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 454.626, 454.780 & 468.020 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 468B.055

History: DEQ 27-1994, f. & cert. ef. 11-15-94 DEQ 3-1981, f. & ef. 2-6-81
