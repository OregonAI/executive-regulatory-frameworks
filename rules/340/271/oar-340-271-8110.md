# Deferrals (OAR 340-271-8110)

## At a glance

## Full text

340-271-8110 Deferrals DEQ may extend reporting or demonstration of compliance deadlines as DEQ deems necessary or appropriate and will issue written notice of any extensions.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 468.020, 468A.025 & 468A.040 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 468.020, 468A.025, 468A.040, 468.035, 468A.010, 468A.015 & 468A.045
