# Severability (OAR 340-271-8120)

## Full text

340-271-8120 Severability Each requirement of this division is severable, and if any requirement of this division is held invalid, the remainder of the requirements of this division will continue in full force and effect.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 468.020, 468A.025 & 468A.040 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 468.020, 468A.025, 468A.040, 468.035, 468A.010, 468A.015 & 468A.045
