# Severability (OAR 340-261-0090)

## At a glance

## Full text

340-261-0090 Severability Each requirement of this division is severable, and if any requirement of this division is held invalid, the remainder or the requirements in this division will continue in full force and effect.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 468.020, ORS 468A.025 & 468A.360 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 468A.025, 468A.360, ORS 468A.010, 468A.015, 468A.050 & 468A.279
