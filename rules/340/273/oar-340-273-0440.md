## Full text

340-273-0440 Compliance Periods

(1) The first compliance period is three consecutive calendar years. Each subsequent compliance period is two consecutive calendar years.

(2) The first compliance period begins with calendar year 2025 and includes calendar years 2026 and 2027.

(3) A new compliance period begins with the calendar year following the last calendar year of the preceding compliance period.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 468.020, 468A.025 & 468A.040 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 468.020, 468A.025, 468A.040, 468.035, 468A.010, 468A.015 & 468A.045
