## Full text

340-244-0237 Gasoline Dispensing Facilities: GDF 1 The owner or operator of a GDF 1 as described in OAR 340-244-0234(4) must comply with the following requirements:

(1) Work Practices, No Top Off, and Submerged Fill under OAR 340-244-0245; and

(2) Recordkeeping under OAR 340-244-0250. [NOTE: This rule is included in the State of Oregon Clean Air Act Implementation Plan that EQC adopted under OAR 340-200-0040 only for affected sources in the Portland-Vancouver, Medford-Ashland, and Salem-Keizer Area Transportation Study air quality management areas and all of Clackamas, Multnomah, and Washington counties.]

Statutory/Other Authority: 468.020 & ORS 468A.025 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 468A.025

History: DEQ 4-2024, adopt filed 03/25/2024, effective 03/25/2024
