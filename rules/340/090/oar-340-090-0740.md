# Reporting for plastic goal (OAR 340-090-0740)

## Full text

340-090-0740 Reporting for plastic goal To enable DEQ to assess progress toward the statewide plastic recycling goal, a producer responsibility organization’s annual reporting of the weight of plastic packaging and food serviceware sold in Oregon by member producers, required under ORS 459A.887(2)(c), must use a product categorization that aligns with the uniform statewide collection list and PRO recycling acceptance list categorizations of products.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 459A.975 & 468.020 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 459A.860 - 459A.975

History: DEQ 18-2023, adopt filed 11/17/2023, effective 11/17/2023
