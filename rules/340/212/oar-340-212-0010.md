# Definitions (OAR 340-212-0010)

## At a glance

OAR 340-212-0010 — Definitions. Chapter 340, Division 212. Statutory authority and AON
history are in the full text below.

## Full text

340-212-0010 Definitions The definitions in OAR 340-200-0020, 340-204-0010 and this rule apply to this division. If the same term is defined in this rule and OAR 340-200-0020 or 340-204-0010, the definition in this rule applies to this division. NOTE: This rule is included in the State of Oregon Clean Air Act Implementation Plan that EQC adopted under OAR 340-200-0040.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 468.020 & 468A Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 468A.025

History: DEQ 7-2015, f. & cert. ef. 4-16-15 DEQ 14-1999, f. & cert. ef. 10-14-99
