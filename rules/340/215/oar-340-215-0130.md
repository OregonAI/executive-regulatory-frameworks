# Separate Violations (OAR 340-215-0130)

## At a glance

## Full text

340-215-0130 Separate Violations Each metric ton of greenhouse gas emissions not reported according to the requirements of this division by a covered entity, as defined in OAR 340-273-0020, that affects applicability determinations, compliance instrument distribution, or compliance obligations under the Oregon Climate Protection Program, OAR Chapter 340 Division 273, is a separate violation of this division.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 468A.050 & 468A.280 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 468 & 468A
