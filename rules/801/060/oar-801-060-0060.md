# Scholarship Amounts (OAR 801-060-0060)

## At a glance

history are in the full text below.

## Full text

801-060-0060 Scholarship Amounts Scholarships awarded to eligible students may be an amount up to the cost of attendance at the eligible institution but no more than $5,000 per student, per academic year for attendance at a university, and no more than $3,000 per student, per academic year for attendance at a community college.

Statutory/Other Authority: SB 796 (2025) Statutes/Other Implemented: Chapter 370,OL

History: BOA 2-2025, adopt filed 10/22/2025, effective 10/22/2025
