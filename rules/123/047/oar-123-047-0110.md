## Full text

123-047-0110 ARPA Capital Projects Fund Broadband Deployment Program—Purposes and Objectives The Coronavirus Capital Projects Fund (42 U.S.C. 804) will be used to fund a broadband deployment program that provides grants towards the costs of physical broadband infrastructure (“ARPA Capital Projects Fund Broadband Deployment Program”).
