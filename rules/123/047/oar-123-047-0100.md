## Full text

123-047-0100 Broadband Equity, Access, and Deployment (BEAD) Program—Purposes and Objectives The federal Broadband Equity, Access, and Deployment (BEAD) Program, established under 47 U.S.C. 1702, will provide funding to Oregon. The Department will use a portion of the funds to establish a state broadband deployment program that provides grants towards the costs of physical broadband infrastructure .
