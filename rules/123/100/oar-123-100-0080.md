## Full text

123-100-0080 Forgivable Loan Information

(1) The Department may provide forgivable loans for Planning Project up to a maximum of 1% of the amount in the Fund.

(2) Forgivable Loans for a Planning Project shall be forgiven upon Project Sponsor providing documentation that the terms and conditions of the financing contract have been met, which may include special conditions such as listing the Project property on Oregon Prospector.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 285B.034 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 285B.032 - 285B.046
