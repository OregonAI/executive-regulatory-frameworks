## Full text

123-053-0111 Appeals and Exceptions

(1) The Authority’s Award decisions are final and may not be appealed.

(2) In its sole discretion, the Authority or its designee may waive non-statutory requirements of the Program if it is demonstrated that such a waiver would serve to further the goals or objectives of the Program.

Statutory/Other Authority: Oregon Laws 2025, Chapter 497 Statutes/Other Implemented: Oregon Laws 2025, Chapter 497
