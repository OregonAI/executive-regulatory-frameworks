## Full text

123-052-2200 Waiver The Department may, in its discretion, waive any of the requirements of these administrative rules to the extent such requirements are not otherwise imposed by law.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 285A.420 - 285A.435 & ch. 742 OL 2013 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 285A.420.420 - 285A.435 & ch. 742 OL 2013
