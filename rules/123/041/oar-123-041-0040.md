# Ineligible Project Costs (OAR 123-041-0040)

## At a glance

## Full text

123-041-0040 Ineligible Project Costs The following costs are ineligible for reimbursement under the Program:

(1) Cost of purchase of general-purpose motor vehicles and other equipment not directly related to the project; and

(2) Indirect costs incurred by the grant recipient or its contractors.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 285A.075 Statutes/Other Implemented: OR Laws 2023, Chapter 606

History: OBDD 21-2024, adopt filed 10/08/2024, effective 10/08/2024
