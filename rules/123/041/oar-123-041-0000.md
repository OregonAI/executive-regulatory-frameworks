# Purpose and Objective (OAR 123-041-0000)

## At a glance

## Full text

123-041-0000 Purpose and Objective These rules establish procedures for the administration of the Aquifer Recharge Due Diligence Grant Program (“Program”), established under Oregon Laws 2023, chapter 606, Sections 28 and 29 (Enrolled House Bill 2010), under which the Infrastructure Finance Authority within Oregon Business Development Department will award grants to eligible applicants to perform due diligence activities related to Aquifer Recharge and aquifer storage and recovery.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 285A.075 Statutes/Other Implemented: OR Laws 2023, Chapter 606

History: OBDD 21-2024, adopt filed 10/08/2024, effective 10/08/2024
