## Full text

123-039-0110 Waivers and Appeals

(1) The Department’s funding decisions are final and may not be appealed.

(2) In its sole discretion, the Director or designee of the Department may waive non-statutory requirements of the Program if it is demonstrated that such a waiver would serve to further the goals or objectives of the Program.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 285A.075 Statutes/Other Implemented: OR Laws 2023, Chapter 606
