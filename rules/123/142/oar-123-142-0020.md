# Ineligible Project Costs (OAR 123-142-0020)

## At a glance

## Full text

123-142-0020 Ineligible Project Costs The following costs are ineligible for funding under the Program:

(1) Purchase of general-purpose motor vehicles and equipment;

(2) Operation or maintenance costs; and

(3) Costs paid for by other financing for the Project, whether from the Authority or from another State of Oregon agency or any third party.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 285B.413 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 285B.420 & ORS 285B.421
