## Full text

123-401-0500 Certification Fee

(1) The Oregon Business Development Department is authorized to establish a fee for filing a written application for certification under ORS 315.522(4) adequate to recover the costs incurred by the department in reviewing the applications.

(2) The fee for companies applying for certification is $3,000.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 285A.075 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 315.518 - 315.522 & Oregon Laws 2023, chapter 298, section 5
