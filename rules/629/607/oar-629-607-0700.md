## Full text

629-607-0700 Forest Conservation Tax Credit – Deed Restriction

(1) The Small Forestland Owner Assistance Office shall provide the small forestland owner with the appropriate deed restriction document for recording with the county where the eligible forest conservation area is located.

(2) After filing the deed restriction, the small forestland owner shall submit documentation of the recording to the Small Forestland Owner Assistance Office.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 527.710 & Section 2(11), Chapter 34, Oregon Laws 2022 Statutes/Other Implemented: Section 3, Chapter 34, Oregon Laws 2022
