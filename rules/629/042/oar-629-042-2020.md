## Full text

629-042-2020 Enrollment

(1) Prior to burning, the entity conducting the burning must enroll the Prescribed Fire or Cultural Burn to reserve liability coverage on the Oregon Prescribed Fire Liability website.

(2) Enrollment does not guarantee fund availability.

(3) Enrollment may be limited based on fund availability.

(4) The enrollment period begins on July 1 of each year and ends on June 30 of the following year. If a project is not completed during the enrollment period, the entity conducting the burning must reenroll the project as a new project on or after July 1 to be considered eligible for reimbursement.

(5) The entity conducting the burning must update the status of the burn as “completed” on the Prescribed Fire Liability website no later than 7 calendar days after completion.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 526.016 Statutes/Other Implemented: HB 4016 (2024)
