## Full text

629-042-1020 Certified Burn Manager certification requirements. Application procedures. Individuals applying for a Certified Burn Manager certificate shall provide to the State Forester's Representative:

(1) A properly completed field certification book showing that the applicant has satisfactorily completed all training required by OAR 629-042-1025 and all tests required by OAR 629-042-1030; and

(2) All fees required by OAR 629-042-1070.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 526.360 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 526.016 & 526.041
