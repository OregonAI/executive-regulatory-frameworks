## Full text

629-042-1000 Purpose The purpose of OAR 629-042-1005 to 629-042-1070 is to set forth the standards, requirements, and procedures by which the Certified Burn Manager program will be operated, pursuant to ORS 526.360(3).

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 526.360 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 526.016 & 526.041
