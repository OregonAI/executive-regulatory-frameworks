## Full text

459-014-0045 Payment of Chapter 238 Pre-Retirement Death Benefits

(1) To be eligible for the benefit under ORS 238.395(2), the member’s spouse must be the member’s sole beneficiary under ORS 238.390.

(2) A beneficiary who elects to receive the optional spouse death benefit under ORS 238.395(2) will receive actuarially determined monthly payments for life only when the total monthly amount of their benefit is at least the amount established in ORS 238.315.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 238.650 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 238.315, ORS 238.390 & ORS 238.395

History: PERS 2-2022, adopt filed 01/31/2022, effective 01/31/2022
