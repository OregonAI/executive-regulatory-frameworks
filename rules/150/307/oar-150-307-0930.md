## Full text

150-307-0930 Eligibility for Workforce Housing For purposes of determining eligibility for workforce housing exemptions under Chapter 527, Oregon Laws 2021, “annual taxable income” means adjusted gross income from the applicant’s federal income tax return for the taxpayer’s federal tax year immediately preceding the federal tax year in which the applicant files an application.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 305.100 Statutes/Other Implemented: Section 2, Chapter 527, Oregon Laws 2021
