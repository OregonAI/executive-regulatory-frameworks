## Full text

150-475-4080 Psilocybin Tax: Psilocybin Service Center Receipt Requirements A psilocybin service center operator must provide clients a written or printed receipt at the point-of-sale of all psilocybin products that includes:

(1) The psilocybin service center operator’s business name;

(2) The address of the psilocybin service center;

(3) The separately stated total amount of the price of psilocybin services.

(4) The separately stated total amount of the sales price of psilocybin products prior to tax;

(5) The total state tax amount;

(6) The total cost to the client at the point-of-sale; and

(7) An alphanumeric or numeric identification unique to each receipt issued.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 305.100 & 475A.702 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 475A.662
