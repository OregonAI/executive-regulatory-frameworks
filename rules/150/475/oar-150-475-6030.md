## Full text

150-475-6030 Civil Penalties for Unregistered Kratom Processors

(1) The Department of Revenue may impose civil penalties on any person who sells, distributes, or exposes for sale kratom products on a wholesale basis to a retailer without first registering as a Kratom Processor under OAR 150-475-6010.

(2) The following civil penalties will be imposed on a per incident basis for a violation listed in section

(1) of this rule. (a) The department will issue a civil penalty of $500 for a kratom processor’s first violation. (b) The department will issue a civil penalty of $1,000 for a kratom processor’s second and each subsequent violation.

(3) Each sales invoice or other document indicating a sale of kratom will be considered a separate incident for determining the penalty imposed under subsection

(2) of this rule.

(4) If the department assesses a penalty under this administrative rule, the penalty assessment may be appealed as a contested case under ORS chapter 183 within 30 days of the date on the notice of penalty assessment.

Statutory/Other Authority: Oregon Laws 2022, chapter 41, section 7 Statutes/Other Implemented: Oregon Laws 2022, chapter 41, section 3
