## Full text

150-311-0570 Charges on the Personal Property Warrant Interest shall not be charged on nor added to the fee for service of a warrant allowed under ORS 311.633.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 305.100 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 311.635
