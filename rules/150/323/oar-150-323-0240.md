## Full text

150-323-0240 Refund of Overpayments Refund of overpayments on a Distributor’s Cigarette Tax Account may be made when an audit determines a net overpayment has been made during the audit period, and the distributor files a claim for such refund within six months of the date the determination was made. No refund shall be allowed after three years from the date of any overpayment.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 305.100 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 323.325
