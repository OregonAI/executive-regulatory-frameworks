## Full text

150-323-0220 Refund of Value of Unused or Mutilated, but Identifiable, Stamps When accompanied by a properly executed claim for refund, on forms supplied by the Department, a refund equal to the denominated value, less the discount allowed, will be made on all returned, unused or mutilated, but identifiable, cigarette revenue stamps.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 305.100 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 323.320
