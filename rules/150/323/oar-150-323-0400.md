## Full text

150-323-0400 Definitions As used in ORS 323.540, “pertinent papers” includes invoices of purchase or sale, bills of lading, receipts, reports of tax exempt shipments, and Tobacco Tax returns.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 305.100 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 323.540
