## Full text

150-323-0110 Definition of “Appropriate Stamp” As required by ORS 323.160(3), an appropriate stamp must be affixed to each package of cigarettes prior to the distribution of the cigarette; for purposes of that requirement an “appropriate stamp” is considered to be more than 50 percent of a single required Oregon tax stamp.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 305.100 & 323.440 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 323.160
