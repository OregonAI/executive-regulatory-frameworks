# Quarterly Tax Discount (OAR 150-323-0320)

## At a glance

## Full text

150-323-0320 Quarterly Tax Discount Each tobacco distributor shall retain one and one-half percent of the tax due and payable each quarter as an allowance to offset the distributor’s cost of recordkeeping and reporting.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 305.100 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 323.505
