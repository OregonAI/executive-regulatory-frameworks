# Payment Type for Cigarette Stamps (OAR 150-323-0130)

## At a glance

## Full text

150-323-0130 Payment Type for Cigarette Stamps Every licensed distributor purchasing tax stamps from the department must pay for the stamps with cash, cashier’s check, money order, or an electronic form of payment such as credit card, debit card, or electronic funds transfer (ACH Debit).

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 305.100 & 323.440 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 323.170
