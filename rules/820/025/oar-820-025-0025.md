# Dual Stamping Documents (OAR 820-025-0025)

## At a glance

## Full text

820-025-0025 Dual Stamping Documents No more than one registrant will seal documents unless it is clearly explained and denoted on the document by all registrants which portion of the work each registrant prepared and for which each registrant is responsible.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 670.310 & 672.255 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 672.002 - 672.325
