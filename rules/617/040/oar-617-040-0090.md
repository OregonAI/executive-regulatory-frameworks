617-040-0090 COMMISSIONER PER DIEM AND REIMBURSEMENTS - Applicability These division 40 rules apply to any activities by members of the Oregon Dairy Products Commission occurring on or after March 23, 2022.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 576.265 & ORS 576.304 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 576.265 & ORS 292.495
