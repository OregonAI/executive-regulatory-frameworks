## Full text

736-056-0016 Oregon Main Street Network Services The department will provide services to organizations maintaining participation requirements in the Oregon Main Street Network based on tier level, such as:

(1) Workshops and training opportunities like the Oregon Main Street Conference, on demand webinars, virtual workshops;

(2) Online resources such as guides and sample documents;

(3) Virtual and in-person networking opportunities;

(4) Orientation for new executive directors and board members;

(5) Technical assistance;

(6) Board strategic planning assistance;

(7) Program evaluation and advice; and

(8) Other services identified by the department.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 390.124(1) & 390.262(9) Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 390.262 & 390.264
