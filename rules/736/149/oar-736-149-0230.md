## Full text

736-149-0230 Eligibility to Bid or Propose; Registration or License

(1) Construction Contracts. The department shall not consider a person's offer to do work as a contractor, as defined in ORS 701.005(2), unless the person has a current, valid certificate of registration issued by the Construction Contractors Board at the time the offer is made.

(2) Landscape Contracts. The department shall not consider a Person's offer to do work as a landscape contractor as defined in ORS 671.520(2), unless the person has a current, valid landscape contractors license issued pursuant to 671.560 by the State Landscape Contractors Board at the time the offer is made.

(3) Noncomplying Entities. The department shall deem an offer received from a person that fails to comply with this rule nonresponsive and shall reject the offer as stated in OAR 736-149-1035, unless contrary to federal law or subject to different timing requirements set by federal funding agencies.
