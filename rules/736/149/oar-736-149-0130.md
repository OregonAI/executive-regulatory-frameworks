## Full text

736-149-0130 Competitive Bidding Requirement-Formal Procurement For public improvement contracts and ordinary construction contracts with an estimated contract price that exceeds $500,000, the department shall solicit Bids by Invitation to Bid ("ITB"), except as otherwise allowed or required by these Division 149 rules.
