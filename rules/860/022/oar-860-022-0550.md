## Full text

860-022-0550 Moratorium on Residential Rate Increases Any increase in residential rates may not take effect from November 1 to March 31.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 756.040 & ORS 756.060 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 757.025, ORS 757.210 & ORS 757.240
