## Full text

860-029-0047 Integration Charges

(1) Each public utility may assess Commission-approved integration charges on wind and solar qualifying facilities that are located within the public utility's Balancing Authority Area.

(2) The public utility bears the burden to establish the proposed integration charge or charges reflecting the costs of integrating the type of resource that will be subject to the charges.

(3) To the extent they are to be imposed by the public utility, any integration charges must be included in the public utility's avoided cost schedules.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 183, ORS 756, ORS 757 & ORS 758 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 756.040 & ORS 758.505-758.555

History: PUC 8-2023, adopt filed 07/25/2023, effective 07/26/2023
