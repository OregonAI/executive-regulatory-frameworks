## Full text

860-091-0010 Definitions

(1) "Electric company" has the meaning in ORS 756.005.

(2) "Nameplate capacity" means the full-load electrical quantities assigned by the designer to a generator and its prime mover or other piece of electrical equipment, such as transformers and circuit breakers, under standardized conditions, expressed in amperes, kilovoltamperes, kilowatts, volts, or other appropriate units. Nameplate capacity is usually indicated on a nameplate attached to the individual machine or device.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 756.060, ORS 469A.200 & ORS 469A.210 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 469A.210
