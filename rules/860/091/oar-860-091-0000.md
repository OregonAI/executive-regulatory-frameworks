## Full text

860-091-0000 Applicability of Rules

(1) The provisions of this division apply to electric companies subject ORS 469A.210.

(2) Upon request or its own motion, the Commission may waive any of the division 091 rules for good cause shown. A request for waiver must be made in writing, unless otherwise allowed by the Commission.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 756.060, ORS 469A.200 & ORS 469A.210 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 469A.210
