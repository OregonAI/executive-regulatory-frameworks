## Full text

860-300-0070 Reporting Requirements for Public Safety Power Shutoffs (PSPS)

(1) The Public Utility is required to file annual reports on de-energization lessons learned, providing a narrative description of all PSPS events which occurred during the fire season. Reports must be filed no later than December 31st of each year.

(2) Non-confidential versions of the reports required under this section must also be made available on the Public Utility's website.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 183, ORS 756 & ORS 757 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 756.040 & ORS 757.035

History: PUC 4-2022, adopt filed 05/24/2022, effective 05/24/2022
