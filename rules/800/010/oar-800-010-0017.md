## Full text

800-010-0017 Incompetence and Negligence Under ORS 673.700(3):

(1) A licensee is incompetent when they have engaged in conduct where a lack of ability, fitness, or knowledge to perform their professional functions is evident.

(2) A licensee is negligent when they have engaged in conduct detrimental to the client.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 670.310(1) & 673.730(10) Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 673.700(3) & 673.700(7)
