## Full text

800-025-0070 Keeping of Tax Consultant Records

(1) See , ORS 673.690. If an Oregon Licensed Tax Consultant is employed by another Oregon Licensed Tax Consultant, the records must be kept by the employing Licensed Tax Consultant.

(2) If an Oregon Designated Tax Consultant ceases to be connected with the corporation, business , or partnership, the records must be retained by the corporation, business, or partnership.

(3) Return documents must be kept for a period of not less than four

(4) years after the date of engagement.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 670.310(1) & 670.730(10) Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 673.690, 673.705–673.740 & 673.990(2)
