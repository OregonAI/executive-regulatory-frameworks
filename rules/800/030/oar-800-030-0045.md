## Full text

800-030-0045 Board Meeting Minutes Minutes of all Board meetings shall be recorded and maintained in the Board Office. Copies may be purchased at cost.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 192.650, 670.300, 670.310(1) & 670.730(10) Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 670.300, 673.705-673.740 & 673.990(2)
