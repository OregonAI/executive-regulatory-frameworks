## Full text

690-100-0015 Eligibility

(1) To be eligible to apply for partial repayment of a project financed under the Water Development Loan Program, an applcant shall meet the definition of a Water Developer, as stated in ORS 541.700.

(2) Any sponsor of a Proposed Project as defined in these rules is eligible for partial repayment of a water development project affording a public benefit.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 541 & 542

History: WRD 9-1986, f. & ef. 7-22-86 WRD 5-1982, f. & ef. 6-1-82
