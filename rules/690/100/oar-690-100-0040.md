## Full text

690-100-0040 Compliance Agreement

(1) A compliance agreement, in preliminary form, shall be submitted to the Legislative Assembly as part of the Commission’s Recommendation. The agreement shall, among other things, set forth the length of time the project is to be operated and maintained for public benefit. After approval by the Legislative Assembly, the compliance agreement shall be written in final form and executed by the project sponsor.

(2) The Director shall be responsible for administering the terms of the compliance agreement.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 541 & 542

History: WRD 9-1986, f. & ef. 7-22-86 WRD 5-1982, f. & ef. 6-1-82
