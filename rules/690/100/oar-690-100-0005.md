## Full text

690-100-0005 Purpose

(1) ORS 541.830 and 542.075 authorize the Legislative Assembly to partially fund water development projects which afford public benefits, in proportion to those benefits. This legislation was enacted in 1981.

(2) These rules provide the procedure for determining eligibility, applying for payment, determining public benefits and for the Water Resources Commission’s recommendation to the Governor and the Legislative Assembly.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 541 & 542

History: WRD 9-1986, f. & ef. 7-22-86 WRD 5-1982, f. & ef. 6-1-82
