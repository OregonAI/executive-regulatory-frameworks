## Full text

690-601-0600 Public Notice The Department shall denote applications with community engagement plan components for a water project during public notice processes for ORS 541.561-541.581 or ORS 541.651-541.696.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 536.027, ORS 541.551, ORS 541.651 - ORS 541.696 & ORS 541.561-541.581 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 541.551, ORS 541.651 - ORS 541.696 & ORS 541.561-541.581
