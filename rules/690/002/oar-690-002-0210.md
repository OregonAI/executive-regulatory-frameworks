## Full text

690-002-0210 Form of Testimony An administrative law judge shall, to the greatest extent practicable, require testimony to be provided orally. If written testimony is submitted, it must be subject to oral cross-examination at hearing.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 536.076(10) Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 536.076(6)
