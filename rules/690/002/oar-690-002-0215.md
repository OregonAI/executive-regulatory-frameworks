## Full text

690-002-0215 Hearing Location The administrative law judge shall determine the venue for conducting the hearing, subject to the approval of the Water Resources Department, and shall give preference to conducting a hearing by a remote method.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 536.076(10) Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 536.076(7)
