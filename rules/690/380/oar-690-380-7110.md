## Full text

690-380-7110 Permit Amendment on Behalf of a Public Corporation For an application made by or on behalf of a public corporation, the Department may issue a permit approving the application without requiring the applicant to obtain prior written authorization or an easement permitting access to lands affected by the proposed permit amendment that are not owned by the public corporation. However, nothing in this subsection shall be construed to allow any person to trespass on the lands of another person.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 536.025 & ORS 536.027 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 537.211
