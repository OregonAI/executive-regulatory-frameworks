# Advances to an Unarmed Combat Sports Manager (OAR 230-110-0060)

## At a glance

OAR 230-110-0060 — Advances to an Unarmed Combat Sports Manager. Chapter 230, Division 110. Statutory authority and AON
history are in the full text below.

## Full text

230-110-0060 Advances to an Unarmed Combat Sports Manager Without prior written permission of the superintendent, no promoter shall, directly or indirectly, incur any indebtedness on behalf of an unarmed combat sports competitor, or entertainment wrestler or manager whereby such person is obligated to repay such indebtedness. No promoter at any time shall, directly or indirectly, make any loan or advance to any manager.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 463.113 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 463.113
