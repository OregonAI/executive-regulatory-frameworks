# Dealing with Unlicensed or Suspended Persons Prohibited (OAR 230-110-0020)

## Full text

230-110-0020 Dealing with Unlicensed or Suspended Persons Prohibited No licensee shall enter into any agreement under the jurisdiction of the Superintendent with any unlicensed person, nor shall any licensee have any such dealings related to unarmed combat sports with any person or club whose license is currently under suspension, or revoked, or whose application for a license has been denied.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 463.113 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 463.113
