## Full text

230-070-0026 Combat Jiu Jitsu Event Open Mat

(1) Combat Jiu Jitsu events may be held on an open mat if approved by the Administrator.

(2) Promoters seeking to hold events on an open mat shall submit their pre-bout plan that states their intention to use an open mat and details the dimension of the proposed competition area.

(3) Prior to approval the Administrator may inspect the proposed venue to ensure compliance with the rules and regulation of unarmed combat sports.
