## Full text

230-145-0005 Persons Allowed in the Competition Area

(1) No persons other than the competitors and the referee may be in the fenced or competition area during the progress of a round.

(2) A referee may call a halt to a bout to request medical personnel or the Administrator to enter the fenced or competition area at the discretion of the referee.

(3) The referee may at their discretion; stop a bout if an unauthorized person enters the fenced or competition area during a round.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 463.113 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 463.113
