# When a Competitor is Deemed Down (OAR 230-145-0016)

## At a glance

OAR 230-145-0016 — When a Competitor is Deemed Down. Chapter 230, Division 145. Statutory authority and AON
history are in the full text below.

## Full text

230-145-0016 When a Competitor is Deemed Down To be considered a grounded competitor either both hands (palms or fists) need to be down, or any body part(s), with the exception of a single knee, other than the soles of the feet are touching the fighting area floor. Any time both knees, an arm, hip, buttocks, back, chest, head, leg, etc. are in contact with the fighting area floor that competitor is considered grounded.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 463.113 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 463.113

History: SAC 3-2023, adopt filed 09/07/2023, effective 10/01/2023
