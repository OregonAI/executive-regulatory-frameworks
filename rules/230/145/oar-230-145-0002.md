# Competitors Must Report (OAR 230-145-0002)

## At a glance

OAR 230-145-0002 — Competitors Must Report. Chapter 230, Division 145. Statutory authority and AON
history are in the full text below.

## Full text

230-145-0002 Competitors Must Report Competitors must report to the staging area at least one- and one-half hours prior to the scheduled time of the first bout, or at the designated report time approved by an authorized representative of the superintendent. All competitors must be ready to enter the competition area immediately upon the finish of the preceding bout. After the rules meeting, no competitor is allowed to leave the building until after their final bout and they are cleared by the OSAC assigned Physician to leave.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 463.113 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 463.113
