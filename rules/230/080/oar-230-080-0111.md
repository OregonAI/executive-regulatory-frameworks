230-080-0111 Association of Boxing Commission’s Unified Rules of Boxing Notwithstanding any contrary provision in these administrative rules, The Oregon State Athletic Commission hereby formally adopts, and incorporates by reference, the Association of Boxing Commissions Unified Rules of Boxing.
