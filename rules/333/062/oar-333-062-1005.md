## Full text

333-062-1005 Licensing

(1) No person shall operate an AQUATIC VENUE, without: (a) The AQUATIC VENUE having received a final construction inspection and approval from the AUTHORITY; (b) Making application for a license to operate; (c) Paying the license fee; and (d) Securing a license from the AUTHORITY.

(2) Such license terminates and is renewable on December 31 of each year.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 448.011 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 448.030
