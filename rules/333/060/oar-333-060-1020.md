## Full text

333-060-1020 Maintenance and Modification

(1) All equipment of AQUATIC FACILITIES AND AQUATIC VENUES shall be operational and shall be kept in good repair. Such equipment shall be maintained in conformance with the original design or better.

(2) The structural components of all AQUATIC FACILITIES AND AQUATIC VENUES shall be maintained in good repair.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 448.011 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 448.011
