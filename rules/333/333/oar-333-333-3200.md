## Full text

333-333-3200 Facilitator Exam

(1) Every applicant for a facilitator license must take the exam required by ORS 475A.330 and receive a passing score prior to being issued a facilitator license.

(2) A passing score on the exam is 75 percent correct. After the applicant takes the exam, the Authority will provide the applicant with the exam results.

(3) An applicant who does not pass the exam may retake the exam.

(4) The Authority will offer the exam at no cost via online interface. Applicants may take the exam at any location of their choosing. An applicant may retake the exam as many times as they chose to do so.

(5) Applicants may request to take the exam in alternative format or in a language other than English. The Authority will make reasonable efforts to accommodate requests for accommodation . Requests for accommodation must be made in advance of taking the exam.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 475A.235 & ORS 475A.330 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 475A.330
