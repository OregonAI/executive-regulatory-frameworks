## Full text

333-333-6110 Advertising Media, Coupons, and Promotions

(1) A licensee may not utilize television, radio, billboards, print media or internet advertising unless the licensee has reliable evidence that no more than 30 percent of the audience for the program, publication or Internet website in or on which the advertising is to air or appear is reasonably expected to be under the age of 21.

(2) A licensee who advertises via webpage must make reasonable efforts to prevent individuals under 21 years of age from visiting the webpage.
