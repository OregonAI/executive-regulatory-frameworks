# Allowable Species (OAR 333-333-2015)

## At a glance

OAR 333-333-2015 — Allowable Species. Chapter 333, Division 333. Statutory authority and AON
history are in the full text below.

## Full text

333-333-2015 Allowable Species A manufacturer may only cultivate, manufacture or possess fruiting bodies of the fungi species Psilocybe cubensis and psilocybin products derived from Psilocybe Cubensis on the licensed premises .

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 475A.235 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 475A.235
