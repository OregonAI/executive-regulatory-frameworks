## Full text

333-333-6120 Removal of Objectionable and Non-Conforming Advertising

(1) A licensee must remove any sign, display, or advertisement if the Authority determines it violates these rules.

(2) The Authority will notify the licensee to identify any non-conforming advertising and provide a reasonable period for the licensee to remove any sign, display or advertisement that does not comply with these rules.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 475A.235 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 475A.235
