## Full text

333-333-2070 Psilocybin Extract Manufacturing Safety Procedures

(1) A manufacturer with an extraction endorsement must: (a) Work in an environment with proper ventilation, controlling all sources of ignition where flammable vapors may be present; (b) Use only potable water in processing; (c) Have an emergency eye-wash station, rinse kit and emergency shower in any room in which solvents other than water are used; and (d) Make appropriate personal protective equipment available to employees.

(2) A manufacturer with a psilocybin extraction endorsement must create and maintain a comprehensive training program that includes the hazards presented by all solvents used at the licensed premises as described in the material safety data sheet for each solvent.
