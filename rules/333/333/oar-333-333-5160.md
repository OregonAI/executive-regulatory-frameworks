# Sale of Psilocybin Services (OAR 333-333-5160)

## At a glance

## Full text

333-333-5160 Sale of Psilocybin Services A facilitator must list fees for psilocybin products separately from fees for preparation, administration and integration sessions and must specify whether those fees will be paid directly to the facilitator or to the service center where the client receives psilocybin services.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 475A.235 & ORS 475A.340 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 475A.340

History: PH 206-2022, adopt filed 12/27/2022, effective 12/27/2022
