## Full text

333-333-6210 Licensee Responsibility

(1) A licensee is responsible for: (a) The violation of any of these rules and any provision of ORS 475A.210 to 475A.722. (b) Any act or omission of a licensee representative in violation of these rules or of ORS 475A.210 to 475A.722.

(2) If a facilitator is a licensee representative of a service center, a service center shall be responsible for any violation of these rules or ORS 475A.210 to 475A.722 that occurs on the service center’s licensed premises that is related to the facilitator’s acts or omissions.

(3) If a facilitator is not a licensee representative of a service center, a service center shall be responsible for any violations of these rules or ORS 475A.210 to 475A.722 that is related to the facilitator’s acts or omissions if: (a) The violation occurs on the licensed premises; and (b) The service center fails to take reasonable steps to prevent the violation.

(4) Sections (1),

(2) and

(3) of this rule apply to every individual and legal entity identified as a licensee on a license issued by the Authority.

(5) Violation of any of these rules or any provision of ORS 475A.210 to 475A.722 shall be attributed to every individual and legal entity identified as a licensee on a license issued by the Authority for the purpose of considering the individual or legal entities record of compliance under 475A.250(2)(h).
