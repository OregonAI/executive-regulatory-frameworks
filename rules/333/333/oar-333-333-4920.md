## Full text

333-333-4920 Sale of Records Licensees may not sell, monetize, or otherwise profit from client records, 303 client data or 303 service center data.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 475A.235 & ORS 475A.372 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 475A.235 & ORS 475A.372
