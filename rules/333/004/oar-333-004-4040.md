## Full text

333-004-4040 Client Eligibility To be eligible to have abortion services covered by the Abortion Access Plan, an individual must:

(1) Be an eligible client; and

(2) Have received services from an authorized agency or authorized provider that submits a claim for reimbursement using the RH Program-approved Abortion Access Plan claims form.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 413.042 & ORS 435.230 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 413.032 & ORS 435.230
