## Full text

333-067-0115 Violations; Schedule of Penalties

(1) The Authority may assess civil penalties under these rules for any of the following violations: (a) Failure to comply with an order or an Authority request to identify a third party; (b) Failure to comply with a stop work order as described in OAR 333-067-0110(8); (c) Knowingly contracting with a person who was not certified under ORS 431A.355 to perform lead-based paint activities or renovation when certification was required under ORS 431A.358. (d) Failure to timely comply with an order, or any part of an order, issued by the Authority as described in OAR 333-067-0110(2).

(2) The Authority may impose a civil penalty under this rule up to $5,000 per day per violation.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 413.042, ORS 431A.355 & ORS 431A.365 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 431A.355, ORS 431A.363 & ORS 431A.365
