## Full text

333-503-0010 Filing Hospital Staffing Complaints

(1) Only a hospital staff person or an exclusive representative of a hospital staff person may submit a complaint.

(2) Complaints must be submitted through the Oregon Health Authority (Authority)’s website, on a form prescribed by the Authority. Individuals that need an accommodation for filing a complaint can contact the Authority .

(3) Once a complaint has been filed, the Authority will process complaints in the manner described in ORS 441. 791 and OAR 333-503-0020.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 413.042 & ORS 441.760 - 441.795 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 441.151 - 441.177
