## Full text

333-035-0175 Health Care Interpreter Services A hospice program shall comply with ORS 413.559 and OAR 950-050-0160 to provide health care interpreting services as defined in OAR 950-050-0010 to a patient who prefers to communicate in a language other than English.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 443.860 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 413.559, 413.561 & 443.860
