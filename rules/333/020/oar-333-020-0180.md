## Full text

333-020-0180 Facility Responsibility if Transfer Occurs Prior to Congenital Cytomegalovirus (cCMV) Screening If a newborn is admitted to a hospital as a result of transfer from another hospital or birthing center, the receiving hospital from which the child is discharged to home shall be responsible for ensuring that a congenital cytomegalovirus (cCMV) screening is performed.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 433.321 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 433.321
