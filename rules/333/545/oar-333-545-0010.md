## Full text

333-545-0010 Purpose and Applicability of Rules

(1) OAR 333-545 through 670 establish procedures for the certificate of need program.

(2) OAR 333-545 through 670 apply to the division and persons subject to provisions of the certificate of need law.

(3) The authority for OAR 333-545 through 670 is ORS 431.120(6) and 442.315.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 431.120(6) & 442.315 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 431.120(6) & 442.315
