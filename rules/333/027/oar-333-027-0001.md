## Full text

333-027-0001 Compliance with Federal Law

(1) Every home health agency subject to ORS 443.014 to 443.105 must comply with the Conditions of Participation governing home health agencies prescribed by the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS), under 42 CFR Part 484, adopted by reference.

(2) Although a naturopathic physician may prescribe services and supplies under a plan of care pursuant to ORS 443.065, those services and supplies ordered by the naturopathic physician are not eligible for reimbursement by CMS.

(3) In addition to the requirements of 42 CFR Part 484, home health agencies licensed in Oregon must also comply with the rules in OAR 333-027-0000 through 333-027-0190.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 443.085 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 443.014 - 443.090
