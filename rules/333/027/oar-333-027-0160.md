## Full text

333-027-0160 Quality Assessment and Performance Improvement An agency must measure, analyze, and track quality indicators, adverse patient events, infection control and other aspects of performance that includes care and services furnished to a patient. Written documentation of quality assessment and performance improvement activities shall be recorded at least quarterly.
