## Full text

333-053-0075 eWIC Cards

(1) At the time of certification, a first cardholder will be identified and issued a eWIC card for the family. (a) If a family requires more than one authorized shopper, a second eWIC card for use by the family’s second cardholder will be issued. (b) Each family will have no more than two activated eWIC cards at a time. (c) No more than one activated eWIC card can be assigned to a cardholder at a time.

(2) The Authority may issue replacement eWIC cards at its discretion.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 413.500 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 413.500

History: PH 10-2013, f. 11-6-13, cert. ef. 12-1-13
