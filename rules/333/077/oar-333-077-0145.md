## Full text

333-077-0145 Laboratory Services

(1) A birthing center shall provide or shall make available laboratory services using a licensed clinical laboratory in the facility or through a written contract. (a) A list of available tests and procedures shall be maintained by the birthing center. (b) A written report of laboratory findings shall be recorded in the client's medical record in accordance with OAR 333-077-0130.

(2) The licensed clinical laboratory shall meet the requirements under ORS chapter 438 and OAR chapter 333, division 24.
