# Surgical Services (OAR 333-077-0140)

## Full text

333-077-0140 Surgical Services Surgical services may be performed but are limited to procedures pertaining directly to pregnancy, labor, and delivery care, and that are normally accomplished during uncomplicated childbirth such as episiotomy and repair. Procedures performed must be consistent with the individual clinical provider's license and scope of practice. Tubal ligation or abortion shall not be performed.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 441.025 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 441.025
