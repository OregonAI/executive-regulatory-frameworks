# Purpose (OAR 413-070-0300)

## At a glance

## Full text

413-070-0300 Purpose The purpose of OAR 413-070-0300 to 413-070-0380 is to prescribe conditions that must be met for the Department to remove a refugee child from home. These rules also establish the Refugee Child Welfare Advisory Committee (RCWAC) and set the criteria for its operations and duties.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 418.005 & 418.945 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 418.005 & 418.925 - 418.945
