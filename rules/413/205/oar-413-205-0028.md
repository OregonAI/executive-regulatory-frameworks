## Full text

413-205-0028 Purpose and Applicability of Certification Standards

(1) The purpose of OAR 413-205-0028 to 413-205-0085 is to describe the criteria for approval as a Certified Respite Provider.

(2) These rules apply to any individual who: (a) Has applied to become a certified respite provider; (b) Is currently a certified respite provider; or (c) Has applied to renew certified respite certificate of approval.
