## Full text

413-205-0070 Requirements Regarding Mandatory Reporting

(1) Any Certified Respite Provider must report information to the Department upon reasonable cause to believe that any child with whom the individual comes in contact has suffered abuse or that any adult with whom the individual comes in contact has abused a child under ORS 419B.005 and 418.257.

(2) Any certified respite provider with a caregiving employee, who is not a child care facility, must provide the caregiving employee or volunteer annual training and written materials on the requirements of mandatory reporting.
