## Full text

413-203-0055 Purpose of Relative Certification Standards The purpose of OAR 413-203-0055 to 413-203-0145 to describe the criteria for approval of a relative applicant as a relative resource parent, or potential adoptive resource and for continued certification of a relative resource parent. These rules apply to any individual who:

(1) Has applied to become a relative resource parent, or a potential adoptive resource; or

(2) Holds a current Relative Temporary Certificate of Approval or Relative Certificate of Approval.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 418.005 & ORS 409.050 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 409.050
