## Full text

350-082-0030 Maps The Land Use Designation, Landscape Settings, and Recreation Intensity Classes maps adopted by the Gorge Commission as part of the Management Plan for the Columbia River Gorge National Scenic Area are hereby incorporated by reference into this land use ordinance. Full-size paper maps and electronic maps are available at the Gorge Commission office. Electronic maps are also available on the Gorge Commission’s website. Reduced-size copies of the maps are in 350-082-0030 Appendix. [ED. NOTE: To view attachments referenced in rule text, click here for PDF copy.]
