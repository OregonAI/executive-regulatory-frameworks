# Compensation for Official Duties or Nonperformance (OAR 350-014-0070)

## Full text

350-014-0070 Compensation for Official Duties or Nonperformance No public official may, directly or indirectly, ask for or give or receive or agree to receive any compensation, gift, reward, or gratuity from a source for performing or omitting or deferring the performance of any official duty, unless otherwise authorized by law except an official compensation package as determined by the Gorge Commission.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 196.150, RCW 43.97.015 & 16 U.S.C. 544c(b) Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 196.150, RCW 43.97.015 & 16 U.S.C. 544c(b)
