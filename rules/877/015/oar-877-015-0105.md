# Purpose (OAR 877-015-0105)

## Full text

877-015-0105 Purpose This division of rules describes the procedures and requirements to obtain and renew a certificate of registration as a baccalaureate social worker and a license as a master’s social worker and the requirements regarding surrender and reapplication for a certificate or license.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 675.510-675.600 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 675.510-675-600 & ORS 675.990
