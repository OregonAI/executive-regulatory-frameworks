# Citations to Board Decisions (OAR 661-010-0029)

## At a glance

OAR 661-010-0029 — Citations to Board Decisions. Chapter 661, Division 010. Statutory authority and AON
history are in the full text below.

## Full text

661-010-0029 Citations to Board Decisions Citations to Board decisions shall be in the following form:

(1) For a Final Opinion and Order and Orders included in LUBA Reporter volumes 1 through 81: John Doe v. XYZ County, 5 Or LUBA 654 (1981).

(2) For an Order not included in LUBA Reporter volumes 1 through 81: John Doe v. XYZ County, LUBA No 80-123 (Feb 15, 1981) (slip order at 4).

(3) For a Final Opinion and Order issued after December 31, 2020: John Doe v. XYZ County, LUBA No 2020-987 (Jan 1, 2021) (slip op at 7).

(4) For a Published Order issued after December 31, 2020: John Doe v. XYZ County, LUBA No 2021-835 (Mar 15, 2021) (order at 2).

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 197.820(4) Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 197.830(13)(a)

History: LUBA 2-2025, adopt filed 12/10/2025, effective 01/01/2026
