## Full text

603-058-0117 Feed Terms For the purposes of ORS 633.006 to 633.089, 633.992, and OAR 603-058-110 to 603-058-290 the feed terms used in reference to commercial feed ingredients shall be those found in chapter 6 of the AAFCO Official Publication.
