## Full text

603-011-1051 Exhibition Registration A facility owner must register an exhibition with the Department at least 20 days prior to the start of the exhibition on a form provided by the Department. If an exhibition will use different property or facilities that have different facility owners, a facility owner for each property or facility must register.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 596.020 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 596.020
