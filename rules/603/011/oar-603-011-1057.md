## Full text

603-011-1057 Exhibition Compliance With the exception of OAR 603-011-1051, and without limiting the obligations of others, including the exhibition organizer or exhibitors, to comply with the requirements in this rule, the designated contact shall ensure that all relevant requirements in OAR 603-011-1050 to 603-011-1057 are followed.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 596.020 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 596.020
