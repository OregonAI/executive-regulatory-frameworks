## Full text

603-051-0680 Official Standards The United States Standards for Spinach Plants adopted by the United States Department of Agriculture effective November 19, 1956, and in effect on January 1, 1963, are hereby adopted as the standards for the State of Oregon for spinach plants. Such federal standards are contained in Title 7, Chapter 1, Part 51 of the Code of Federal Regulations in Sections 51.2880 to 51.2890, a copy of which is filed herewith and by this reference made a part hereof.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 561.190 & 632.900 - 632.980 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 632.900 - 632.980

History: AD 755, f. 7-2-63
