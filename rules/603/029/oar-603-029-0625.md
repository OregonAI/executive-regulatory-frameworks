## Full text

603-029-0625 Official Seals for Transportation of Products The official mark for use in sealing railroad cars or other means of conveyance as prescribed in OAR 603-029-1400 to 603-029-1480 shall be the inscription and a serial number as hereinafter shown [2] and any seal approved by the Director for applying such mark shall be an official device for purposes of this Division 29. This seal shall be attached to the means of conveyance only by a Department employee. [Image 1, 603-029-0625]
