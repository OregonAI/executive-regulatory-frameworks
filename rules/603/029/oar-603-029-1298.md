## Full text

603-029-1298 Breaded Products The amount of batter and breading used as a coating for breaded product shall not exceed 30 percent of the weight of the finished breaded product.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 603.085, ORS 619.042 & ORS 619.046 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 619.042, ORS 619.046, ORS 619.026 & ORS 619.036
