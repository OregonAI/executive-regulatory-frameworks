## Full text

603-029-1056 Labeling Applications for Nutrient Content Claims If a state-inspected establishment wishes to file a labeling application for a new nutrient content claim, the establishment must follow the process set forth in 9 CFR § 317.369 for seeking USDA approval of a new nutrient content claim.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 603.085, ORS 619.042 & ORS 619.046 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 603.085, ORS 619.042 & ORS 619.046
