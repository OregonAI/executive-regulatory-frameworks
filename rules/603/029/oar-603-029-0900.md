## Full text

603-029-0900 Authorization Required to Make Devices Bearing Oregon Marks No brand manufacturer, printer or other person shall cast, print, lithograph, or otherwise make or cause to be made any device containing any Oregon mark or simulation thereof without prior written authority therefor from the Department as provided for in OAR 603-029-1000 to 603-029-1058.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 603.085, ORS 619.042 & ORS 619.046 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 619.042, ORS 619.046, ORS 561.230, ORS 619.026 & ORS 619.036
