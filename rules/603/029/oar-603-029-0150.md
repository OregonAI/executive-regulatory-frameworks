# Sanitation and Adequate Facilities (OAR 603-029-0150)

## At a glance

## Full text

603-029-0150 Sanitation and Adequate Facilities Inspection shall not be inaugurated if an establishment is not in a sanitary condition nor unless the establishment agrees to maintain a sanitary condition and provides adequate facilities for conducting such inspection.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 603.085, ORS 619.042 & ORS 619.046 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 603.085, ORS 619.042 & ORS 619.046
