# Meat and Meat Byproducts from Meat Animals Which Have Been Exposed to Radiation (OAR 603-029-0572)

## At a glance

## Full text

603-029-0572 Meat and Meat Byproducts from Meat Animals Which Have Been Exposed to Radiation Meat and meat byproducts from meat animals which have been administered radioactive material shall be condemned unless the use of the radiation was in conformity with a regulation or exemption in effect pursuant to section 409 of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 603.085, ORS 616.700, ORS 619.042 & ORS 619.046 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 619.042, ORS 619.046, ORS 596.392, ORS 596.388, ORS 619.026 & ORS 619.036
