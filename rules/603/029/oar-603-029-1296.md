# Potted Meat Product and Deviled Meat Product (OAR 603-029-1296)

## Full text

603-029-1296 Potted Meat Product and Deviled Meat Product “Potted Meat Product” and “Deviled Meat Product” shall not contain cereal, vegetable flour, nonfat dry milk, or similar substances. The amount of water added to potted meat product and deviled meat product shall be limited to that necessary to replace moisture lost during processing.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 603.085, ORS 619.042 & ORS 619.046 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 619.042, ORS 619.046, ORS 619.026 & ORS 619.036
