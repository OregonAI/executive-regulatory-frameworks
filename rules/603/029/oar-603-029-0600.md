# General (OAR 603-029-0600)

## Full text

603-029-0600 General The marks, devices, and certificates prescribed or referenced in this part shall be Oregon marks, devices, and certificates for purposes of this Division 29, and shall be used in accordance with the provisions of OAR 603-029-0600 to 603-029-0620 and the regulations cited therein.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 603.085, ORS 619.042 & ORS 619.046 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 619.042, ORS 619.046, ORS 619.026 & ORS 619.036
