## Full text

603-029-0160 Reports of Violations Department employees shall report, in a manner prescribed by the Department, all violations of ORS chapter 619 or these Division 29 rules regarding which they have information.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 603.015, ORS 619.021 & ORS 619.042 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 603.015, ORS 619.021 & ORS 619.042
