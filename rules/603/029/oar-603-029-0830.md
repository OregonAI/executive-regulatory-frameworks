## Full text

603-029-0830 Carcasses of Meat Animals Condemned on Ante-Mortem Inspection not to Pass Through Edible Meat Product Areas Carcasses of meat animals which have been condemned on ante-mortem inspection shall not be taken through rooms or compartments in which an edible meat product is prepared, handled, or stored.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 603.085, ORS 616.700, ORS 619.042 & ORS 619.046 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 619.042, ORS 619.046, ORS 619.026 & ORS 619.036
