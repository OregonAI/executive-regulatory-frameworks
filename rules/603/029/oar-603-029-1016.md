## Full text

603-029-1016 Relabeling Products; Requirements When it is claimed by a state-inspected establishment that any of its products which bore labels bearing Oregon marks has been transported to a location other than a state-inspected establishment, and it is desired to relabel the product because the labels have become mutilated or otherwise damaged, a request for relabeling the product shall be sent to the Department, accompanied with a statement of the reasons therefor. Labeling material intended for relabeling inspected and passed product shall not be transported from a state-inspected establishment until permission has been received from the Department. The relabeling of inspected and passed product with labels bearing any Oregon marks shall be done under the supervision of a Department Inspector. The state-inspected establishment shall reimburse the Department, in accordance with the regulations of the Department, for any cost involved in supervising the relabeling of such product.
