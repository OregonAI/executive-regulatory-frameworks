## Full text

603-052-3010 Applicability

(1) The responsibility for the eradication and control of uncontrolled cannabis rests on cannabis producers, individual landowners and operators and any other persons who may be responsible for the establishment or spread of uncontrolled cannabis.

(2) Nothing in these rules exempts any person from complying with any other applicable laws.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 561.190 & ORS 570.405 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 570.405-570.415 & ORS 570.995
