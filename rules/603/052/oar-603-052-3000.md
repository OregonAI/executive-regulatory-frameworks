## Full text

603-052-3000 Purpose Cannabis may be lawfully cultivated in Oregon in a carefully controlled manner consistent with the laws of this state. However, cannabis if not carefully cultivated or controlled may lead to cannabis plants becoming quickly established in areas of private or public lands that are not permissible production areas. Volunteer cannabis may become uncontrolled cannabis and may spread rapidly on state, county and federally owned lands, as well as on property in individual ownership and so may constitute a menace to the agricultural and horticultural industries of this state.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 561.190 & ORS 570.405 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 570.405-570.415 & ORS 570.995
