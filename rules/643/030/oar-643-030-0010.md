## Full text

643-030-0010 Number of Commissioners, Terms The Oregon Hop Commission will consist of nine commissioners appointed by the Director of the Oregon Department of Agriculture for a term of three years or, if for a term following a prior term for that person, ending three years from the date of expiration of any prior term.

Statutory/Other Authority: 2003 OL Ch. 604 & ORS 576 Statutes/Other Implemented: 2003 OL Ch. 604 & ORS 576

History: HOP 1-2004, f. & cert. ef. 1-16-04
