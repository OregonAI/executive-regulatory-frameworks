# Payment of Civil Penalty (OAR 837-045-0110)

## Full text

837-045-0110 Payment of Civil Penalty A civil penalty shall be paid to the Department of the State Fire Marshal within ten days after an order assessing a civil penalty becomes final by operation of law or on an appeal.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 479.295 & 479.990 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 479.250 - 479.300 & 479.990
