## Full text

837-045-0055 Hotels with Approved Sprinkler Fire Suppression Systems Hotels that have installed an approved sprinkler fire suppression system are exempt from the requirements of ORS 479.257(1).

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 479.257 & 479.295 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 479.250 - 479.300
