## Full text

837-045-0040 Purpose and Scope The purpose of these rules is to establish safety requirements for the installation and maintenance of smoke alarms and smoke detectors in existing buildings, and to establish standards and procedures for the enforcement of those requirements, for the protection of Oregon residents from fire.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 479.255 & 479.295 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 479.250 - 479.300 & 479.990
