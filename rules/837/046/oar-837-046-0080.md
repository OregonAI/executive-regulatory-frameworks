## Full text

837-046-0080 Inspections The OSFM or an authorized representative may inspect Oregon wholesale dealers, agents, and retailers for compliance with 2009 HB 2365. Inspections include any documents to determine compliance with 2009 HB 2365.

Statutory/Other Authority: 2009 HB 2365 Statutes/Other Implemented: 2009 HB 2365
