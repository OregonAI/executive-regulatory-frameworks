## Full text

837-007-0220 Subject Individual The Department may require a Subject Individual to complete a criminal records check due to the following reasons:

(1) Is applying for employment or is employed with the Department; or

(2) Provides services or seeks to provide services to the Department as a contractor, subcontractor, vendor or volunteer.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 181A.195 & 2024 Senate Bill 1510 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 181A.195(9)
