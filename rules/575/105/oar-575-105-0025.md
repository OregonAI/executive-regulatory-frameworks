## Full text

575-105-0025 Requirements for Concurrently Enrolled Students

(1) The Commission will prescribe eligibility requirements and grant calculations for students concurrently enrolled in a community college established under ORS Chapter 341 and a public university listed in ORS 352.002.

(2) For students who are concurrently enrolled in two or more public institutions, the Oregon Tribal Student grant award will be based on the cost of attendance determined by the student’s “home” institution.
