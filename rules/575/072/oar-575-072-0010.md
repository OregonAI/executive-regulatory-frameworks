## Full text

575-072-0010 Administration The Commission shall administer the Oregon Nursing Loan Program, and shall make such rules as are required for the administration of the program.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 348.115 & 348.117 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 348.105 - 348.117
