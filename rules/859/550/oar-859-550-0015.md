## Full text

859-550-0015 Timing of Hearings The Board shall hold hearings for all youth under its jurisdiction within the timeframes set forth in its statutes in ORS chapter 419C.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 161.387 & OL 2007 889 § 6 (SB 328) Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 419C.532, 419C.538 & 419C.540 - ORS 419C.544
