## Full text

859-570-0035 Out-of-State Conditional Release Order The Board may consider and approve a conditional release plan to have the youth reside out of state.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 161.387, OL 2007 & Ch. 889 § 6 (SB 328) Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 161.332, 161.336, 161.346, 161.390, 419C.520, 419C.529, 419C.532 & 419C.538
