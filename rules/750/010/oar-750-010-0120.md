## Full text

750-010-0120 Severability. These rules are severable. If any rule under OAR Chapter 750, or part thereof or the application of such rule to any person or circumstance is declared invalid, that invalidity will not affect the validity of any remaining portion of the rules of the State Board of Towing under OAR Chapter 750.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 822.265 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 822.265
