## Full text

750-010-0030 Fiscal. The State Board of Towing fiscal year will be a twelve-month period beginning July 1 of one year and ending June 30 of the following year.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 822.265 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 822.265
