## Full text

750-001-0030 Contested Case Hearings. The State Board of Towing adopts the Attorney General’s Model Rules of Procedure for contested cases under ORS 183.413 through 183.502. These rules will be controlling except as otherwise required by law or rule.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 822.265 & ORS 183.413 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 822.265 & ORS 183.413

History: SBOT 1-2023, adopt filed 10/31/2023, effective 01/01/2024
