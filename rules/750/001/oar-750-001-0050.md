## Full text

750-001-0050 Civil Penalty Procedures. The State Board of Towing adopts the Attorney General's Model Rules of Procedure for civil penalties and related procedures and processes under ORS 183.745. These rules will be controlling except as otherwise required by law or rule.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 183.745 & ORS 822.995 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 183.745 & ORS 822.995
