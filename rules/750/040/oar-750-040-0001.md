## Full text

750-040-0001 Violations. The State Board of Towing may impose a civil penalty, sanctions, or take other disciplinary action under ORS 822.265: for violation of ORS 98.853, 98.854, 98.856, 98.858, 181A.350, 822.200, 822.215, 822.225, 822.230, 822.235 or 822.605 and other laws and rules assigned to the Board for administration.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 822.265, ORS 822.280, ORS 822.290 & ORS 822.995 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 822.265, ORS 822.280, ORS 822.290 & ORS 822.995
