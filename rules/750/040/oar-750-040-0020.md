## Full text

750-040-0020 Separate Violations. Each violation of a law assigned to the State Board of Towing for administration, an administrative rule adopted by the Board, or an Order issued by the Board is a separate violation subject to separate Board disciplinary actions and sanctions, including, but not limited to, civil penalties assessed by the Board.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 822.265 & ORS 822.995 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 822.265 & ORS 822.995
