## Full text

847-017-0040 Facility Administration and Equipment The licensee must ensure that specific and current arrangements are in place for obtaining laboratory, radiological, pathological and other ancillary services as may be required to support the surgical and/or anesthetic procedures undertaken.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 677.265 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 677.085, 677.097 & 677.265
