## Full text

847-017-0037 Reporting Requirement

(1) Licensees performing office-based surgery must report the following complications and adverse incidents to the Board within ten business days of the event if the complication occurred within 30 days of the procedure: (a) Surgical related death; (b) Emergency transfer of the surgical patient to the hospital; (c) Anesthetic or surgical event requiring cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR); and (d) Unscheduled hospitalization related to the office-based surgery.

(2) Licensees performing or intending to perform office-based surgery must report any restriction, limitation, loss or denial of privileges in a hospital or accredited outpatient facility within ten business days of the restriction, limitation, loss or denial of privileges.

(3) The Board will review reports made under this rule to determine whether an investigation is necessary.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 677.265 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 677.415
