## Full text

847-071-0007 Five-Needle Protocol Registration Required

(1) Except as provided in sections

(2) and

(3) of this rule, no person may provide five-needle protocol (5NP) treatment without first obtaining a registration from the Oregon Medical Board.

(2) An acupuncturist licensed under ORS 677.757 to 677.770 may provide 5NP treatment without additional 5NP registration.

(3) A physician licensed to practice medicine as a medical doctor or doctor of osteopathic medicine pursuant to ORS Chapter 677 may provide 5NP treatment without additional 5NP registration.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 677.265 & OL 2025, chapter 296, section 2 (HB 2143) Statutes/Other Implemented: OL 2025, chapter 296, section 2 (HB 2143)
