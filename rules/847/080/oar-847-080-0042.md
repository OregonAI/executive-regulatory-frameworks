## Full text

847-080-0042 Practice of Podiatry Podiatric physicians and surgeons practice podiatry as defined in ORS 677.010 and within their individual education, training, and experience. Podiatric physicians and surgeons are held to the standard and duty of care as described in ORS chapter 677.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 677.265 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 677.805
