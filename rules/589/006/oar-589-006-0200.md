## Full text

589-006-0200 Approval of Collegiate Courses

(1) The Commission delegates to the Office the authority to approve upper and lower division collegiate courses.

(2) A community college that is institutionally accredited, shall follow the Office's collegiate course approval procedure to request new courses.

(3) A community college that is not institutionally accredited shall apply for approval through their contracting college.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 350.075 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 350.075
