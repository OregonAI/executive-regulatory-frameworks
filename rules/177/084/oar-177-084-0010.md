# Game Description (OAR 177-084-0010)

## At a glance

history are in the full text below.

## Full text

177-084-0010 Game Description Cash Pop is a one of 15 Lottery game with set prize amounts. For each drawing, a player may choose to play from one to 15 numbers. At the time of purchase, a prize amount is randomly assigned to the player’s ticket and appears underneath the player’s number or numbers that are selected. The Lottery then randomly selects one winning number from one to 15 in a drawing. The Lottery awards prizes based on the extent to which the game play appearing on a player’s ticket matches the winning number selected in the drawing for which the player’s ticket was purchased.
