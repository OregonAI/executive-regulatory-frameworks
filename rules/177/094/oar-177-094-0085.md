## Full text

177-094-0085 Retailer Selling Bonus

(1) General: For the purposes of OAR 177-040-0025(2)(a), a retailer who sells any winning and validated Win for LifeSM top prize of $1,000 per week for life shall receive a bonus of $13,000 which is based on one percent (1%) of an estimated prize value of $52,000 per year paid over a period of 25 years ($52,000 x 25 = $1,300,000 x .01 = $13,000).

(2) Multiple Top Prize Winning Tickets: Notwithstanding section

(1) of this rule, if the Win for LifeSM top prize of $1,000 per week for life is reduced in accordance with OAR 177-094-0080(7)(g), the selling retailer shall receive a bonus based on one percent of the actual prize amount paid over a period of 25 years. For example, if the prize value paid is $31,200 per year, the bonus shall be $31,200 x 25 = $780,000 x .01 = $7,800.

Statutory/Other Authority: OR Const. Art. XV, Sec. 4(4) & ORS 461 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 461
