## Full text

438-013-0005 Expedited Claims Service Established Pursuant to the mandate of 1987 Oregon Laws, Chapter 884, Section 18, there is established within the Hearings Division the Expedited Claims Service. The purpose of the Expedited Claims Service is to provide for prompt, informal dispute resolution and to insure fair and just treatment of workers in all proceedings.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 656.291 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 656.291
