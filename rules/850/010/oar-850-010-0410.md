# Public Health Laws (OAR 850-010-0410)

## At a glance

## Full text

850-010-0410 Public Health Laws Naturopathic physicians shall be subject to all state, county, and municipal laws and rules relating to public health concerning the diagnosis and reporting of contagious and infectious diseases, as may be required, to the proper health authorities in the respective counties.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 685 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 685.110
