# Purpose (OAR 734-054-0005)

## At a glance

OAR 734-054-0005 — Purpose. Chapter 734, Division 054. Statutory authority and AON
history are in the full text below.

## Full text

734-054-0005 Purpose The purpose of Oregon Administrative Rules 734-054-0005 through 734-054-0035 is to identify the criteria by which the Oregon Department of Transportation will select STIP projects that may accommodate a broadband facility of a third party; the process by which the department will select a broadband facility that may be added to a STIP project; and other requirements that may apply to the broadband facility as part of its installation in the state highway.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 184.619, 184.920(2) & 184.925 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 184.911-184.925 & 23 CFR 645.301-645.309

History: DOD 4-2025, adopt filed 12/11/2025, effective 01/01/2026
