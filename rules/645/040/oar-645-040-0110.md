## Full text

645-040-0110 Qualified Members

(1) In accordance with the procedures set forth in OAR 645-040 - 0130 and OAR 645-040-0140, the Oregon Dungeness Crab Commission shall provide to a qualified member of the Oregon Dungeness Crab Commission: (a) Per diem compensation equal to the per diem paid to members of the Legislative Assembly under ORS 171.072, for each day or portion thereof during which the qualified member is actually engaged in the performance of official duties; and (b) Reimbursement of actual and necessary travel or other expenses actually incurred in the performance of a member’s official duties within the limits provided in Oregon Accounting Manual Policy 40.10.00.

(2) A qualified member of the Oregon Dungeness Crab Commission may decline to accept compensation or reimbursement of expenses related to the qualified member’s service on the Oregon Dungeness Crab Commission.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 576.265 & ORS 576.304 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 292.495 & ORS 576.265
