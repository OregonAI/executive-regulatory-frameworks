## Full text

645-040-0140 Procedures for Expense Reimbursement

(1) A member must submit a written, itemized claim for reimbursement of actual and necessary travel or other expenses to the Oregon Dungeness Crab Commission no later than the 15th day of the calendar month following the month in which the member incurred the expense. For example, a claim for compensation for May 1 and May 10 must be submitted by June 15. The claim for reimbursement must include the following information for each expense: (a) Date on which the member incurred the expense; (b) Nature of the expense; and (c) Amount of the expense.

(2) An expense that exceeds $1000.00 must be authorized by the Oregon Dungeness Crab Commission before the member incurs the expense.
