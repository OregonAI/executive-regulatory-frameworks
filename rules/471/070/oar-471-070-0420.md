## Full text

471-070-0420 Wages: Pensions An individual receiving a pension from a former employer shall not be considered an employee of that employer solely because of the pension. The amount of the pension is not wages

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 657B.340 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 657B.010

History: ED 4-2022, adopt filed 01/31/2022, effective 01/31/2022
