## Full text

471-070-0455 Wages: Cafeteria Plans Employee benefits paid through a cafeteria plan, as defined in the Internal Revenue Code Section 125, are not wages if listed as excluded in ORS 657.115, even if paid through a payroll deduction.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 657B.340 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 657B.010
