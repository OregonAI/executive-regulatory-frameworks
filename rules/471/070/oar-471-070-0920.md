## Full text

471-070-0920 Confidentiality: Responsibility of Paid Leave Oregon Staff

(1) Paid Leave Oregon staff must safeguard the confidentiality of customer information collected or obtained and disclose information about the customer only as authorized by law and as necessary to administer ORS chapter 657B.

(2) Paid Leave Oregon staff and all other entities or individuals with access to Paid Leave Oregon information are authorized to access confidential customer information only on a “need to know” basis.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 657B.340 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 657B.400 & Chapter 292, Oregon Laws 2023
