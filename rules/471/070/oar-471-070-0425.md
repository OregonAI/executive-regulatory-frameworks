## Full text

471-070-0425 Wages: Jury Pay Compensation, reimbursement, fees, lodging, meals or other remuneration paid or provided to an individual for services performed as a juror are not wages.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 657B.340 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 657B.010
