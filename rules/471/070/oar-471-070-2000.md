## Full text

471-070-2000 Self-employed: Definition “Taxable income from self-employment” means Oregon net income from self-employment or earned as an independent contractor as reported on the Oregon personal income tax return.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 657B.340 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 657B.130 & 657B.150
