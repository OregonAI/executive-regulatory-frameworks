# Grant Application Review Criteria (OAR 213-100-0050)

## At a glance

history are in the full text below.

## Full text

213-100-0050 Grant Application Review Criteria The advisory committee and the Commission shall review and evaluate each grant application based on the following criteria:

(1) The grant application’s responsiveness to the requirements set forth in OAR 213-100-0040(3).

(2) Other criteria that the Commission chooses to include in the grant solicitation.

Statutory/Other Authority: 2024 OL Ch.70 §85 Statutes/Other Implemented: 2024 OL Ch.70 §85
