## Full text

213-100-0080 Evaluating Efficacy The Commission will evaluate the progress and efficacy of programs and services funded by the Jail-based Medications for Opioid Use Disorder Grant Program through fiscal expenditure and program data collection. The Commission may develop other methods for evaluating program efficacy.

Statutory/Other Authority: 2024 OL Ch.70 §85 Statutes/Other Implemented: 2024 OL Ch.70 §85
