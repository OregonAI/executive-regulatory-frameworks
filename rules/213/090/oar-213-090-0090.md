# Evaluating Efficacy (OAR 213-090-0090)

## At a glance

## Full text

213-090-0090 Evaluating Efficacy The Commission will evaluate the progress and efficacy of programs and services funded by the Organized Retail Theft Grant Program through fiscal expenditure and program data collection. The Commission may develop other methods for evaluating program efficacy.
