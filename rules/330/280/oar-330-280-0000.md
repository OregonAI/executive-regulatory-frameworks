## Full text

330-280-0000 Purpose The purpose of these rules is to implement a heat pump rebate program established by Oregon Laws 2022, Chapter 86, Sections 19-23 (Senate Bill 1536). The rules provide procedures for: submitting reservation, rebate and grant requests, project and contractor eligibility, agency review of reservation, rebate, and grant requests, and agency audits and inspections to ensure compliance.

Statutory/Other Authority: Oregon Laws 2022, Chapter 86 (Senate Bill 1536) & ORS 469.040 Statutes/Other Implemented: Oregon Laws 2022, Chapter 86 (Senate Bill 1536)
