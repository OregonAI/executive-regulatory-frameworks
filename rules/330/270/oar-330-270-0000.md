# Purpose (OAR 330-270-0000)

## At a glance

OAR 330-270-0000 — Purpose. Chapter 330, Division 270. Statutory authority and AON
history are in the full text below.

## Full text

330-270-0000 Purpose The purpose of these rules is to implement a grant program established by Oregon Laws 2022, Chapter 86, Sections 14-18 to award grants to eligible entities to provide financial assistance for the purchase and installation of heat pumps and related upgrades to individuals. The rules provide procedures for: administration of the program, application to the program, use of program funds, awarding funds, funding agreements, record keeping and reporting, and program compliance.
