# Compliance (OAR 330-250-0150)

## At a glance

## Full text

330-250-0150 Compliance The department reserves the right to conduct a physical inspection of all projects and to audit all grantee or partner documentation relating to a project for which a grantee and the department have entered into a performance agreement.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 469.040 & Oregon Laws 2021, chapter 508 (House Bill 2021) Statutes/Other Implemented: Oregon Laws 2021, chapter 508 (House Bill 2021)
