## Full text

950-033-0040 Grant Approval Process

(1) Awards may be funded in-whole or in-part.

(2) Any applicants not funded will be notified in writing by the Oregon Health Authority, Equity and Inclusion Division .

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 413.446 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 413.446
