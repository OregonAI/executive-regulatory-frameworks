## Full text

731-035-0180 Federal Grant Match Application Project Administration If the federal agency that administers the federal grant applied for by the Recipient announces a Notice of Grant Award and the Approved Project is selected upon notification the Department will administer all projects in accordance with OAR 731-035-0080 .

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 184.619, 367.082 & Ch. 816 OL 2005 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 367.080 – 367.086, Ch. 750 OL 2017 & Ch. 816 OL 2005
