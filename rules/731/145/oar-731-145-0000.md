# Purpose (OAR 731-145-0000)

## At a glance

OAR 731-145-0000 — Purpose. Chapter 731, Division 145. Statutory authority and AON
history are in the full text below.

## Full text

731-145-0000 Purpose The purpose of these rules, OAR 731, Division 145, is to establish the process by which a city or county may apply for inclusion on the list of jurisdictional transfers of state highway facilities from the ownership of ODOT to the ownership of a city or county, to be recommended for funding and submitted to the Joint Committee on Transportation.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS184.619 & Ch 323 OL 2023 Statutes/Other Implemented: Ch 323 OL 2023

History: DOT 4-2024, adopt filed 08/06/2024, effective 08/06/2024
