# Authorized Entity Standards (OAR 411-303-0020)

## Full text

411-303-0020 Authorized Entity Standards An authorized entity must have formal approval from ODDS to deliver an ODDS-approved behavior intervention curriculum to instructors, providers, and designated persons.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 409.050, 418.529, 427.104 & 430.662 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 409.010, 418.529, 427.007, 427.104, 430.610 & 430.662
