## Full text

411-016-0020 Qualified Services Case Manager Services Case Managers (SCMs) must meet the following criteria:

(1) Be employed by an Area Agency on Aging, the Department or a subcontractor of a Type B AAA transfer that was subcontracting to provide OPI case management prior to June 1, 2024.

(2) Have completed the Department required training for case managers; and

(3) The AAA or the Department has determined that the SCM has the skills, knowledge and ability for the SCM position and responsibilities.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 410.070 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 410.060, 410.070 & 414.065
