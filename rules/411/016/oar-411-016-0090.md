## Full text

411-016-0090 In-Home Agency Services and Provider Enrollment

(1) For the purpose of this rule, (a) Case manager as used in OAR 411-033-0020 is synonymous with the Services Case Manager in OAR 411-014-0010; and (b) Individual as used in OAR 411-033-0020 is modified to apply to people aged 60 or older or an adult aged 18 or older with physical disability.

(2) In-Home Care Agency Services authorized as part of an OPI-M service plan must comply with OAR 411-033-0020 and with provider enrollment requirements and payment in OAR 411-033-0030.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 410.070 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 410.060, 410.070 & 414.065
