# Purpose (OAR 411-029-0000)

## At a glance

## Full text

411-029-0000 Purpose The purpose of this division is to establish requirements for the application, approval, distribution, and oversight of the Community-Based Care (CBC) Distressed Provider Relief Fund (Fund). This Fund provides a financial assistance grant to CBCs who are experiencing financial hardships and contemplating closure.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 410.070 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 410.070
