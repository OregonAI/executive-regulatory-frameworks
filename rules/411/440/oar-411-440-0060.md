## Full text

411-440-0060 Training Requirements for the Children's Extraordinary Needs (CEN) Program

(1) A parent provider must complete Department-approved training prior to delivering services to their child under these rules. The training must include: (a) An overview of federal and state administrative rules regulating home and community-based services; and (b) The impact of providing Department-funded services on parent-child relationships with respect to discipline, supervision, physical intervention, and self-determination of a client child.

(2) A client child must receive age-appropriate, accessible training, information, or materials related to self-advocacy with respect to choosing their own direct support professionals prior to being enrolled in the CEN program.
