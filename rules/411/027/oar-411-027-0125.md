## Full text

411-027-0125 Distressed Provider Relief Fund The Department may authorize a payment to a Medicaid Assisted Living Facility, Residential Care Facility or Memory Care (Endorsed Units Only) who meet the criteria described in OAR chapter 411, division 029. The Department will prioritize payments based on available funding and maintains sole authority to determine which providers will receive funding.
