## Full text

345-015-0400 Governing Provisions

(1) All contested case proceedings before the Council shall be conducted in accordance with OAR 137-003-0501 through 137-003-0700 (as of December 31, 2023), referred to herein as the Office of Administrative Hearing rules.

(2) The rules in this Division addressing contested cases (OAR 345-015-0400 through 345-015-0475) are intended to supplement the Office of Administrative Hearing rules by providing additional procedures governing requests for and the conduct of Energy Facility Siting Council contested cases.

(3) In any conflict between the Office of Administrative Hearing rules and Council rules, the Council shall apply its own rules.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 469.470, ORS 469.370(5) & ORS 183.417(2) Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 183.415, ORS 469.370, ORS 469.405 & ORS 469.440
