## Full text

345-015-0115 Appointment of Special Advisory Groups After receiving the notice of intent, the Department shall designate as a special advisory group the governing body of any local government within whose jurisdiction the facility is proposed to be located.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 469.470 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 469.480
