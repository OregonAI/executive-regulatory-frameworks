# Burden of Presenting Evidence (OAR 345-015-0440)

## Full text

345-015-0440 Burden of Presenting Evidence In a contested case regarding an application for a site certificate or amendment to a site certificate, each party or limited party bears the burden of presenting evidence in support of facts that party or limited party alleges and/or positions they take on any issue they submitted a petition for party or limited party status and on which the hearing officer determines they may participate.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 183.341, ORS 183.417, ORS 469.370(5), ORS 183.417(2) & ORS 469.470 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 183.450

History: EFSC 2-2024, adopt filed 10/01/2024, effective 10/01/2024
