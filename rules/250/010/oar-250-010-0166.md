## Full text

250-010-0166 Engine Cut-off Switches A person must use an engine cut-off switch link while operating a motorboat at a planing speed. This rule does not apply if:

(1) The boat is not equipped with an engine cut-off switch, or

(2) The main helm of the boat is installed within an enclosed cabin.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 830.110 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 830.110
