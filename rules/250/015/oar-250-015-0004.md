## Full text

250-015-0004 Optional Charter Licensing for Uninspected Passenger Vessels A boat carrying fewer than seven passengers for hire for angling, sightseeing, or other recreational purposes may be issued a charter boat license if the boat owner completes the charter boat application process and satisfies requirements for charter boats operating on sole state waters laid out in 250-015-0035(5).
