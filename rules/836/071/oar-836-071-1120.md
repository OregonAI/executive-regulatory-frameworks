## Full text

836-071-1120 Resident Adjuster Qualification The director may issue a public adjuster license to a resident applicant under this section if the director finds that the applicant:

(1) Submitted a complete and accurate application in accordance with OAR 836-071-1115; and

(2) Satisfies the requirements set forth under ORS 744.521 and ORS 744.525.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 731.244 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 744.521
