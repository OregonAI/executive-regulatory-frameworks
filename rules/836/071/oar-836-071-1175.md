# Escrow or Trust Accounts (OAR 836-071-1175)

## Full text

836-071-1175 Escrow or Trust Accounts A public adjuster who receives, accepts or holds any funds on behalf of an insured, towards the settlement of a claim for loss or damage, shall deposit the funds in a non-interest bearing escrow or trust account in a financial institution that is insured by an agency of the federal government in the public adjuster’s home state or where the loss occurred.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 731.244 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 744.521

History: ID 5-2025, adopt filed 07/25/2025, effective 08/01/2025
