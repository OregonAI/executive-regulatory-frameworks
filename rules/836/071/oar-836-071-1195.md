## Full text

836-071-1195 Unlicensed Actors A person or entity commits a fraudulent insurance act if they:

(1) Represents or advertises themself to be a public adjuster who has not met the requirements of licensure under this rule.

(2) Conducts business for which a license is required under this rule without a license.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 731.244 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 744.521
