## Full text

836-012-0230 Applicability This rule applies to reinsurance treaties that cede liabilities pertaining to Covered Policies issued by any life insurance company domiciled in this state. This rule and OAR 836-012-0046 shall both apply to such reinsurance treaties; provided, that in the event of a direct conflict between the provisions of this rule and OAR 836-012-0046, the provisions of this rule shall apply, but only to the extent of the conflict.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 731.244, ORS 731.508 - 731.511 & 731.514 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 731.508 - 731.511 & 731.514
