## Full text

180-001-0003 Effective Date; Applicability These rules are effective November 1, 2025 and shall apply to all requests for facilitated dispute resolution services active and pending before the Advocate or the Deputy, if the Advocate has delegated dispute resolution authority to the Deputy, under the provisions of ORS 192.461.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 192.464 & ORS 192.468 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 183.335, ORS 192.464 & ORS 192.468
