## Full text

255-033-0060 Notices Following Hearing Following a Board decision under this section, the Board shall send written notice of the Board's order to the AIC, district attorney of the committing jurisdiction, and Board registered victims.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 144.397 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 144.397
