## Full text

255-062-0018 Communication to Department of Corrections Upon Deferral The Board may, after issuing an order deferring an adult in custody’s release under this rule, denying re-release onto parole after a future disposition hearing under OAR 255-075-0096, or upon deferral of an adult in custody’s right to petition the Board for a hearing under OAR 255-032 and OAR 255-033, request the Department of Corrections provide correctional rehabilitative services, consistent with the Department’s administrative rules and policies.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 144.050, 144.085, 144.120, 144.125, 144.280, 144.397 & 144.780 Statutes/Other Implemented: OAR 255-075-0096
