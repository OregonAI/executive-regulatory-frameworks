## Full text

156-001-0005 Model Rules of Procedure The Legislative Administration Committee, in accordance with ORS 183.341, adopts, by reference, Division 1 of the Attor­ney General's Model Rules of Procedure under the Administrative Procedures Act. This adoption applies only to rules required by ORS 292.956.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 183 & 292 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 183.341

History: LAC 1-1988, f. & cert. ef. 8-18-88
