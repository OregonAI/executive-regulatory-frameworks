## Full text

419-480-0050 Secure Transportation Services: Staffing Ratios The secure transportation services provider must provide adequate supervision and protection to maintain the physical health, mental health and well-being for any child in care it is serving .

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 409.050, ORS 418.005, OR Laws 2021 Ch. 672, ORS 418.255 & ORS 418.240 Statutes/Other Implemented: OR Laws 2021 Ch. 672 & ORS 418.205 - 327
