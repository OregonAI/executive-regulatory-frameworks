## Full text

265-005-0015 Disciplinary Action for Misconduct Not Identified By These Rules Upon a finding of misconduct not specifically identified within these rules, a disciplining body may take disciplinary action pursuant to the employing law enforcement agency’s policies and practices so long as the disciplinary action is consistent with these rules.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 243.812 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 243.812
