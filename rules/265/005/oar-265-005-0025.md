## Full text

265-005-0025 Incorporation of Rules All disciplining bodies shall adopt policies incorporating these rules. The failure of a disciplining body to adopt policies incorporating these rules does not impair the application of these rules.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 243.812 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 243.812
