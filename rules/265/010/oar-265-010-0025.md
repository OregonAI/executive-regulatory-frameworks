## Full text

265-010-0025 Moral Character

(1) For the purposes of this rule, lack of good moral character means conduct constituting: (a) A felony under state or federal law (b) Domestic violence (c) Stalking (d) A drug-related offense, except for offenses involving use or possession of marijuana (e) A bias or hate crime under state or federal law (f) A sex crime (g) Untruthfulness (h) Misuse of authority for financial gain (i) Misuse of authority by soliciting or engaging in sexual acts or favors of a sexual nature

(2) If a law enforcement officer is convicted of a crime based on conduct identified in subsection

(1) of this rule, proof of the conviction is conclusive evidence that the conduct occurred.

(3) A disciplining body shall impose upon a law enforcement officer disciplinary action of termination upon a finding that the officer engaged in misconduct demonstrating a lack of good moral character.
