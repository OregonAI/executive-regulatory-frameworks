## Full text

605-020-0130 Procedures for Payment of Per Diem Compensation A qualified member must submit a written claim for compensation to the Oregon Beef Council no later than the 15th day of the calendar month following the month for which the qualified member seeks compensation. For example, a claim for compensation for May 1 and May 10 must be submitted by June 15. The qualified member must specify the amount of time the member spent performing official duties, as well as the nature of the official duties performed, for any day or portion thereof for which the qualified member claims compensation.
