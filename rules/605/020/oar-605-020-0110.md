## Full text

605-020-0110 Qualified Members

(1) In accordance with the procedures set forth in OAR 605-0020-0130 and OAR 605-020-0140, the Oregon Beef Council shall provide to a qualified member of the Oregon Beef Council: (a) Per diem compensation equal to the per diem paid to members of the Legislative Assembly under ORS 171.072, for each day or portion thereof during which the qualified member is actually engaged in the performance of official duties; and (b) Reimbursement of actual and necessary travel or other expenses actually incurred in the performance of a qualified member’s official duties within the limits provided in Oregon Accounting Manual Policy 40.10.00.

(2) A qualified member of the Oregon Beef Council may decline to accept compensation or reimbursement of expenses related to the qualified member’s service on the Oregon Beef Council.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 577.260 & 577.290 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 577.260 & 292.495
