## Full text

141-126-0100 Purpose and Applicability

(1) These rules: (a) Govern the granting and renewal of leases for communication site facilities on state-owned land. (b) Apply to the management of state-owned Constitutional Common School Fund Lands (school lands) and Statutory Common School Fund Lands (statutory lands) for communication site facilities. (c) Establish a process for authorizing such uses through the granting of leases. (d) Do not apply to the granting of proprietary authorizations for uses specifically governed by other department administrative rules.

(2) The director may determine other uses and developments similar to those specified in OAR 141-126-0120(8) that are also subject to a communication site facility lease and these rules.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 273.045, ORS 273.051(2)(b) & ORS 273.245 Statutes/Other Implemented: OR Const. Art. VIII, Sec. 2 & 5
