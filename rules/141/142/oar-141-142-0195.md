## Full text

141-142-0195 Whale Cove Marine Conservation Area

(1) All state-owned submerged and submersible land East of a line drawn across the mouth of Whale Cove, and defined by the following points, is within the Whale Cove Marine Conservation Area: (a) Beginning from a point at Latitude 44° 47.237' (44° 47’ 14” N), Longitude -124° 04.298' (124° 4’ 18” W) (Point A); (b) Then north to a point at Latitude 44° 47.367' (44° 47’ 22” N), Longitude -124° 04.32' (124° 4’ 19” W) (Point B).

(2) All coordinate information is provided in WGS84 coordinate system (geographic latitude/longitude) and has not been verified by a licensed surveyor.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 196.555 & ORS 273.045 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 196.555
