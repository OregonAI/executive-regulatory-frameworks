# Brookings Marine Research Area I (OAR 141-142-0110)

## At a glance

OAR 141-142-0110 — Brookings Marine Research Area I. Chapter 141, Division 142. Statutory authority and AON
history are in the full text below.

## Full text

141-142-0110 Brookings Marine Research Area I All state-owned submerged and submersible land in the intertidal area between a point 1/2 mile north of Harris Beach State Park and the mouth of the Chetco River, except the area within the Harris Beach Marine Garden, is within the Brookings Marine Research Area .

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 196.555 & ORS 273.045 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 196.555

History: DSL 1-2025, adopt filed 02/12/2025, effective 02/12/2025
