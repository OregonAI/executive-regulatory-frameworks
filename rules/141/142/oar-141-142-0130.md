# Cape Blanco Marine Research Area (OAR 141-142-0130)

## At a glance

OAR 141-142-0130 — Cape Blanco Marine Research Area. Chapter 141, Division 142. Statutory authority and AON
history are in the full text below.

## Full text

141-142-0130 Cape Blanco Marine Research Area All state-owned submerged and submersible land in the intertidal area along the north and south sides of Cape Blanco extending seaward to encompass all rock along the headland up to the sand beaches of the north and south sides of the cape is within the Cape Blanco Marine Research Area.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 196.555 & ORS 273.045 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 196.555

History: DSL 1-2025, adopt filed 02/12/2025, effective 02/12/2025
