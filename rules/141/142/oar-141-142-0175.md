# Harris Beach Marine Garden (OAR 141-142-0175)

## At a glance

OAR 141-142-0175 — Harris Beach Marine Garden. Chapter 141, Division 142. Statutory authority and AON
history are in the full text below.

## Full text

141-142-0175 Harris Beach Marine Garden All state-owned submerged and submersible land between a line perpendicular to shore from the Harris Beach State Park beach access parking area and a line perpendicular to shore from the road entrance to Harris Beach State Park off Highway 101 is within the Harris Beach Marine Garden.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 196.555 & ORS 273.045 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 196.555

History: DSL 1-2025, adopt filed 02/12/2025, effective 02/12/2025
