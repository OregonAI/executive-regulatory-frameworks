# Yachats Marine Garden (OAR 141-142-0200)

## At a glance

## Full text

141-142-0200 Yachats Marine Garden All state-owned submerged and submersible land in the intertidal area between the north and south boundaries of Yachats State Park is within the Yachats Marine Garden.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 196.555 & ORS 273.045 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 196.555

History: DSL 1-2025, adopt filed 02/12/2025, effective 02/12/2025
