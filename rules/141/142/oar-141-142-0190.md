## Full text

141-142-0190 Pirate Cove Marine Research Area

(1) All state-owned submerged and submersible land East of a line drawn across the mouth of Pirate Cove, and defined by the following points is within the Pirate Cove Marine Research Area: (a) Beginning from a point at Latitude 44° 49.083’ (44° 49’ 5” N), Longitude -124° 04.035’ (124° 4’ 2” W) (Point A); (b) Then to north a point at Latitude 44° 49.146’ (44° 49’ 9” N), Longitude - 124° 04.060’ (124° 4’ 4” W) (Point B).

(2) All coordinate information is provided in WGS84 coordinate system (geographic latitude/longitude) and has not been verified by a licensed surveyor.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 196.555 & ORS 273.045 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 196.555
