# Cape Lookout Marine Conservation Area (OAR 141-142-0145)

## At a glance

OAR 141-142-0145 — Cape Lookout Marine Conservation Area. Chapter 141, Division 142. Statutory authority and AON
history are in the full text below.

## Full text

141-142-0145 Cape Lookout Marine Conservation Area All state-owned submerged and submersible land beginning from the west-facing sand beach 880 yards north of Rover Creek and extending west along the Cape's south flank, around the tip, then east along the north flank for 1,425 yards is within the Cape Lookout Marine Conservation Area.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 196.555 & ORS 273.045 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 196.555

History: DSL 1-2025, adopt filed 02/12/2025, effective 02/12/2025
