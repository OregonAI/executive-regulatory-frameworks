# Chapman Point Marine Garden (OAR 141-142-0150)

## At a glance

OAR 141-142-0150 — Chapman Point Marine Garden. Chapter 141, Division 142. Statutory authority and AON
history are in the full text below.

## Full text

141-142-0150 Chapman Point Marine Garden All state-owned submerged and submersible land encompassing the shoreline around Chapman Pt, extending 194 yards north and 211 yards south of the tip of Chapman Pt (perpendicular to West 7th St. beach access), and extending out seaward to 405 yards from the tip of Chapman Pt is within the Chapman Point Marine Garden.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 196.555 & ORS 273.045 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 196.555

History: DSL 1-2025, adopt filed 02/12/2025, effective 02/12/2025
