# Yaquina Head Marine Garden (OAR 141-142-0205)

## At a glance

OAR 141-142-0205 — Yaquina Head Marine Garden. Chapter 141, Division 142. Statutory authority and AON
history are in the full text below.

## Full text

141-142-0205 Yaquina Head Marine Garden All state-owned submerged and submersible land in the intertidal area along the north and south sides of Yaquina Head, including rocky areas abutting the sandy beaches along the headland, is within the Yaquina Head Marine Garden.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 196.555 & ORS 273.045 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 196.555

History: DSL 1-2025, adopt filed 02/12/2025, effective 02/12/2025
