# Cape Arago Marine Research Area A (OAR 141-142-0115)

## At a glance

## Full text

141-142-0115 Cape Arago Marine Research Area A All state-owned submerged and submersible land between a line projected due west from Cape Arago lighthouse and the southern tip of Norton Gulch is within the Cape Arago Marine Research Area A.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 196.555 & ORS 273.045 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 196.555

History: DSL 1-2025, adopt filed 02/12/2025, effective 02/12/2025
