# Coquille Point Marine Garden (OAR 141-142-0155)

## At a glance

OAR 141-142-0155 — Coquille Point Marine Garden. Chapter 141, Division 142. Statutory authority and AON
history are in the full text below.

## Full text

141-142-0155 Coquille Point Marine Garden All state-owned submerged and submersible land in the intertidal area between a line perpendicular to the shore from the 8th St. beach access point and a line perpendicular to shore from the Coquille Point beach access staircase is within the Coquille Point Marine Garden.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 196.555 & ORS 273.045 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 196.555

History: DSL 1-2025, adopt filed 02/12/2025, effective 02/12/2025
