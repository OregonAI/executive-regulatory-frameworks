# Cape Perpetua Marine Garden (OAR 141-142-0215)

## At a glance

OAR 141-142-0215 — Cape Perpetua Marine Garden. Chapter 141, Division 142. Statutory authority and AON

## Full text

141-142-0215 Cape Perpetua Marine Garden All state-owned submerged and submersible land in the intertidal area between the north side of Devils Churn and the north boundary of Neptune State Park is within the Cape Perpetua Marine Garden.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 196.555 & ORS 273.045 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 196.555

History: DSL 1-2025, adopt filed 02/12/2025, effective 02/12/2025
