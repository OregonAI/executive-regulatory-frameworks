## Full text

141-142-0170 Gregory Point Marine Research Area

(1) All state-owned submerged and submersible land bounded by the following points is within the Gregory Point Marine Research Area: (a) Beginning from a point at Latitude 43° 20.312’ (43° 20’ 19” N), Longitude -124° 22.838’ (124° 22’ 50” W) (Point A); (b) Then northwest to a point at Latitude 43° 20.598’ (43° 20’ 36” N), Longitude -124° 22.895’ (124° 22’ 54” W) (Point B); (c) Then east to a point at Latitude 43° 20.650’ (43° 20’ 39“ N), Longitude -124° 22.637’ (124° 22’ 38” W) (Point C); (d) Then southeast along the MHWL to Latitude 43° 20.402’ (43° 20’ 24“ N), Longitude -124° 22.545’ (124° 22’ 33“ W) (Point D); (e) Then in a southwesterly direction back to the point of beginning (Point A).

(2) All coordinate information is provided in WGS84 coordinate system (geographic latitude/longitude) and has not been verified by a licensed surveyor.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 196.555 & ORS 273.045 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 196.555
