# General Purpose and Scope; SEBT (OAR 461-196-0000)

## Full text

461-196-0000 General Purpose and Scope; SEBT Summer Electronic Benefit Transfer (SEBT) program is established for the purpose of providing nutrition assistance during the summer months for each eligible child, to ensure continued access to food when school is not in session for the summer.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 411.060 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 411.060
