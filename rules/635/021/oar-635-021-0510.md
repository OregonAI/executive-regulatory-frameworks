# Southeast District (OAR 635-021-0510)

## At a glance

## Full text

635-021-0510 Southeast District Effective June 1, 2026, through September 30, 2026, in Thief Valley Reservoir, Wolf Creek Reservoir, and Pilcher Creek Reservoir located in Union County, the following regulations apply:

(1) No minimum or maximum size limits,

(2) No daily or possession limits for rainbow trout and bass,

(3) Harvest will be allowed by hand, dip net, or angling and,

(4) A person with a valid Two-Rod Validation may use any number of rods.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 496.138 & ORS 496.146 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 496.162
