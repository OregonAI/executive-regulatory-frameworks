## Full text

635-009-0097 Committee Meetings

(1) Committee meetings are public meetings and must be conducted in compliance with requirements of the Oregon Public Meetings Law, ORS 192.610 et seq.

(2) The Committee will meet no less than twice each calendar year to carry out the business of the Committee.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 496.138 & ORS 496.146 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 496.460 & ORS 192.610 – ORS 192.695
