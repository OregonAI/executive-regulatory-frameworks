## Full text

635-097-0000 Purpose The purpose of the Private Forest Accord Grant Program is to fund projects that mitigate the impacts of certain forest practices on species covered by the Private Forests Accord Habitat Conservation Plan (HCP), which is to be submitted by the Board of Forestry to meet requirements of Oregon Laws 2022, chapter 33, section 11. The rules governing the Private Forest Accord Grant Program are OAR 635-097-0000 through 635-097-0110, referred to herein as the “PFA Grant rules”.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 496.012, 496.138, 496.146 & Oregon Laws 2022, Chapter 33, Sections 30-32 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 496.012 & Oregon Laws 2022, Chapter 33, Sections 30-32
