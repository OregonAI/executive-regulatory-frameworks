# John Day District (OAR 635-019-0500)

## Full text

635-019-0500 John Day District Effective June 16 through August 31, in the John Day River from the mainline railroad bridge at the mouth upstream to a marker one-quarter mile below Tumwater Falls, the daily adult salmon and steelhead bag limit may not include more than one hatchery steelhead.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 496.138 & ORS 496.146 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 496.162
