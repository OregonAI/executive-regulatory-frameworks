# John Day District (OAR 635-019-0500)

## At a glance

## Full text

635-019-0500 John Day District Effective September 1, 2026 through December 31, 2026, angling for and retention of all steelhead is closed in the John Day River from the mainline railroad bridge at the mouth of the Columbia and all sections upstream, including both the North and Middle Forks of the John Day River, as well as all tributaries.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 496.138 & ORS 496.146 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 496.162
