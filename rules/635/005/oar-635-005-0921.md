## Full text

635-005-0921 Market Squid Fishery License Limitation If the Commission adopts a future license limitation measure, January 1, 2022 will be used as the control date for establishing eligibility related to past participation in the commercial market squid purse seine fishery.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 506.036, ORS 506.109, ORS 506.119 & ORS 506.129 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 506.109 & ORS 506.129
