## Full text

635-004-0515 Pacific Ocean Herring Season There is no closed season for the commercial taking of herring in the Pacific Ocean.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 506.036, 506.109, 506.119 & 506.129 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 506.109 & 506.129
