## Full text

715-045-0034 False, Deceptive, Inaccurate or Misleading Representations No private career school or its agents may intentionally or knowingly make false, deceptive, inaccurate or misleading representations of fact in any oral, written, visual or electronic presentation in connection with the offering or publicizing of a subject or course of instruction.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 345.325 & 326.051 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 345.325
