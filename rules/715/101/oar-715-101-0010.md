## Full text

715-101-0010 Use of Equity Lens The Oregon Conservation Corps Advisory Committee shall use the Equity Lens adopted by the Higher Education Coordinating Commission in awarding Oregon Conservation Corps Program grants to eligible organizations.

Statutory/Other Authority: 2021 Oregon Laws, Chapter 592 Statutes/Other Implemented: 2021 Oregon Laws, Chapter 592
