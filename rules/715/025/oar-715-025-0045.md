## Full text

715-025-0045 PROGRAM TERMINATION An institution may terminate a program subject to a CAP. It shall notify the Council at the time the termination decision is made, and shall ensure students enrolled in the affected program pursuant to the CAP at the time of termination are provided a completion pathway that satisfies the conditions of the CAP.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 350.395 & ORS 350.429 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 350.395, ORS 350.429, ORS 350.404 & ORS 350.412
