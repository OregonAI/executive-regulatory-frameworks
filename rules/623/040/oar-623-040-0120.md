## Full text

623-040-0120 Non-Qualified Members

(1) In accordance with the procedures set forth in OAR 623-040-0130, and only if there are funds available in the budget for such compensation, the Oregon Hazelnut Commission shall provide to a non-qualified member of the Oregon Hazelnut Commission who is not employed in full-time public service per diem compensation equal to the per diem paid to members of the Legislative Assembly under ORS 171.072, for each day or portion thereof during which the non-qualified member is actually engaged in the performance of official duties.

(2) In accordance with the procedures set forth in OAR 623-040-0140, the Oregon Hazelnut Commission shall provide to a non-qualified member of the Oregon Hazelnut Commission reimbursement of actual and necessary travel or other expenses actually incurred in the performance of a non-qualified member’s official duties within the limits provided in Oregon Accounting Manual Policy 40.10.00.

(3) A non-qualified member of the Oregon Hazelnut Commission may decline to accept compensation or reimbursement of expenses related to the non-qualified member’s service on the Oregon Hazelnut Commission.
