# Notification (OAR 291-209-0035)

## At a glance

## Full text

291-209-0035 Notification At the time of the first meeting with the adult on supervision, the supervising officer shall notify the adult on supervision of their eligibility for earned discharge, and then at least quarterly, shall track and document their progress towards achieving earned discharge and share that document with them.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 137.633, 179.040, 423.020, 423.030 & 423.075 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 137.633, 179.040, 423.020, 423.030 & 423.075
