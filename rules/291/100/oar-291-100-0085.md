# Credit for Time Served as Part of a Probationary Sentence (ORS 137.372(2)) (OAR 291-100-0085)

## Full text

291-100-0085 Credit for Time Served as Part of a Probationary Sentence (ORS 137.372(2)) An AIC who has been ordered confined as part of a probationary sentence for a crime committed on or after July 18, 1995, shall receive credit for time served in jail after arrest and before commencement of the probationary term unless the sentencing judge orders otherwise.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 137, 144, 161.620, 179.040, 421, 423.020, 423.030 & 423.075 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 137, 144, 161.610, 179.040, 421, 423.020, 423.030 & 423.075
