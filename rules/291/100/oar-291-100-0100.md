## Full text

291-100-0100 Prison Term and Sentence Reduction Credits

(1) Statutory Good Time and Extra Good Time Credits: Statutory Good Time and Extra Good Time Credits may be applied in accordance with ORS 421.120(2), 421.122, and the department’s rules on Prison Term Modification (OAR 291-097).

(2) Earned Time Credits: Earned Time Credits may be applied in accordance with ORS 421.121 and the department’s rules on Prison Term Modification (OAR 291-097).

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 137, 144, 161.620, 179.040, 421, 423.020, 423.030 & 423.075 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 137, 144, 161.610, 179.040, 421, 423.020, 423.030 & 423.075
