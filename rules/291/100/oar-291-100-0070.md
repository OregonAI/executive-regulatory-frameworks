## Full text

291-100-0070 First and Last Day Credit

(1) An AIC will receive a full day of credit for the date of commitment to the department.

(2) An AIC will receive a full day of credit for the last day of the sentence in a department facility.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 137, 144, 161.620, 179.040, 421, 423.020, 423.030 & 423.075 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 137, 144, 161.610, 179.040, 421, 423.020, 423.030 & 423.075
