## Full text

291-160-0130 Late Offers, Late Withdrawals and Late Modifications Any Offer received after Closing is late. An Offeror's request for withdrawal or modification of an Offer received after Closing is late. DOC shall not consider late Offers, withdrawals or modifications except as permitted in OAR 291-160-0140.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 179.040, 421.438, 423.020, 423.030 & 423.075 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 179.040, 421.438, 423.020, 423.030 & 423.075
