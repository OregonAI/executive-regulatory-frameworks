## Full text

291-160-0380 Termination of Price Agreements DOC may terminate a Price Agreement as follows:

(1) As permitted by the Price Agreement;

(2) If DOC fails to receive funding, appropriations, limitations, allotments or other expenditure authority, including the continuation of program operating authority, sufficient, as determined in the discretion of DOC, to sustain purchases at the levels contemplated at the time of contracting;

(3) Due to any change in law, program termination, or facility closure that makes purchases under the Price Agreement no longer authorized or appropriate for DOC’s use; or

(4) As otherwise permitted by applicable law.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 179.040, 421.438, 423.020, 423.030 & 423.075 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 179.040, 421.438, 423.020, 423.030 & 423.075
