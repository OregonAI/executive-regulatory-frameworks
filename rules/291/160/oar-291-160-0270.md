## Full text

291-160-0270 Availability of Award Decisions

(1) Contract Documents. To the extent required by the Solicitation Document, DOC shall deliver to the successful Offeror a Contract, Signed purchase order, Price Agreement, or other Contract document(s) as applicable.

(2) Availability of Award Decisions. A person may obtain tabulations of Awarded Bids or evaluation summaries through the electronic procurement system approved by the State Chief Procurement Officer.

(3) Availability of Procurement Files. After notice of intent to Award, DOC shall make procurement files available in accordance with applicable law.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 179.040, 421.438, 423.020, 423.030 & 423.075 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 179.040, 421.438, 423.020, 423.030 & 423.075
