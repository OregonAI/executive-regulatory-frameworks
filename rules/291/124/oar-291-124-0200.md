## Full text

291-124-0200 Doula Services Health Services shall operate a doula program for pregnant and postpartum adults in custody at Coffee Creek Correctional Facility. Doula services are available to an adult in custody who is pregnant, or who has been pregnant or has given birth in the preceding 12 months. Doula services include those set forth in ORS 421.173(4) including, but not limited to, one-on-one patient visits and assistance, preparation of the birth plan, on-site support at the birth, education, and group work.
