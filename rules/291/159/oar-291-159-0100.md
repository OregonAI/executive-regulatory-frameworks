## Full text

291-159-0100 Fragmentation A Procurement may not be artificially divided or fragmented for the purpose of restricting competition or circumventing a legal requirement.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 179.040, 279A.050(6)(h) - (6)(i), 279A.065 & 279A.070 Statutes/Other Implemented: 279A.065 & 279A.070
