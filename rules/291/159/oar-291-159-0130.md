## Full text

291-159-0130 Protests

(1) Protests of Solicitation Documents. (a) For Procurements under OAR 291-159-0010(1)(a), see OAR Chapter 137 Division 046. (b) For Procurements under OAR 291-159-0010(2)(b) and (2)(c), see OAR 125-247-0240, 125-248-0240, or 125-249-0450, as applicable.

(2) Protests of Notices of Intent to Award and Contract Awards. (a) For Procurements under OAR 291-159-0010(1)(a), see OAR 137-046-0470. (b) For Procurements under OAR 291-159-0010(2)(b) and (2)(c), see OAR 125-247-0700 to 125-247-0740.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 179.040, 279A.050(6)(h) - (6)(i) & 279A.120 Statutes/Other Implemented: 279A.120
