## Full text

291-180-0107 Definitions Adult on Supervision: Any person under the supervision of the Department of Corrections or a county community corrections agency who is on probation, parole, post-prison supervision, leave, or any other form of supervision.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 144.600, 179.040, 423.020, 423.030 & 423.075 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 144.600, 179.040, 423.020, 423.030 & 423.075
