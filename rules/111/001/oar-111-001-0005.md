## Full text

111-001-0005 Uniform and Model Rules of Procedure The Attorney General's Uniform and Model Rules of Procedure under the Administrative Procedure Act, dated 2024, are adopted as rules of procedure of the Oregon Educators Benefit Board and are made a part of OAR chapter 111. [ED. NOTE: The full text of the Attorney General's Model Rules of Procedures is available from the office of the Attorney General or the Oregon Educators Benefit Board.]

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 243.860 - 243.886 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 183.310-183.750
