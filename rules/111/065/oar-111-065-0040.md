## Full text

111-065-0040 Continuation of Coverage

(1) Early Retirees and dependents have COBRA rights consistent with 111-050-0001.

(2) Loss of coverage due to failure to make a premium payment is not a Qualified Status Change.

Statutory/Other Authority: 243.860 - 243.886 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 243.864(1)(a)
