## Full text

111-065-0020 Early Retiree Overpayments

(1) OEBB will include overpayment amounts on the monthly invoice. The invoice will include the total payment received, the date it was received, the amount of premium payment due, and any remaining balance of additional premiums paid.

(2) OEBB will automatically apply any overpayments to the next month’s premium due. The Early Retiree may complete a Request for Reimbursement form if a refund of an overpayment is desired. The Early Retiree may be responsible for processing fees associated with refunds less than $100. Reimbursements will be refunded via check.

(3) Remaining balances on coverage that has ended will be refunded in full within 30 days of the coverage end date or the date OEBB is notified that coverage should end, whichever occurs later.
