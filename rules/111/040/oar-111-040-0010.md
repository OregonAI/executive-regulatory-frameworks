## Full text

111-040-0010 Newly-hired and Newly-eligible Employees

(1) Newly-hired and newly-eligible employees must enroll in OEBB-sponsored benefit plans through the OEBB benefit management system or paper equivalent within 31 calendar days of the date of hire or date of gaining eligibility, unless determined otherwise in a separate OEBB administrative rule or in a collective bargaining agreement or documented Entity policy in effect on June 30, 2008.

(2) An employee enrolling in OEBB-sponsored benefit plans and terminating employment before the effective date of benefit coverage is not eligible to receive benefits.

Statutory/Other Authority: 2007 OL Ch. 7 Statutes/Other Implemented: 2007 OL Ch. 7 & Sec. 3
