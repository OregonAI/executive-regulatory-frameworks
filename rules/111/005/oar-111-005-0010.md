# Policy (OAR 111-005-0010)

## At a glance

## Full text

111-005-0010 Policy The policy of the Oregon Educators Benefit Board (OEBB) is to select Contractors and Consultants in an expeditious, fair, and efficient manner that is consistent with the goal of delivering high-quality benefits and other services at a cost that is affordable to the Eligible Employees, Dependents, Eligible Domestic Partners, and Eligible Early Retirees, and meets the requirements of ORS 243.866. The Board may enter into more than one Contract for each type of Benefit Plan or other service sought.

Statutory/Other Authority: Ch. 7 OL 2007 Statutes/Other Implemented: Ch. 7 OL 2007
