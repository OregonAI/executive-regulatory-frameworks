## Full text

952-001-0005 Recruitment Partner Organizations for Board of Directors Pursuant to ORS 757.547(1)(c) through (e), prospective members of the Oregon Utility Notification Center Board of Directors are solicited from the following organizations:

(1) Telecommunications Cooperatives - Oregon Broadband Association

(2) Railroads – ODOT Rail Safety, Association of American Railroads, Oregon Short Line Railroad Association, Union Pacific, BNSF Railway

(3) Natural Gas Utilities regulated by the Public Utility Commission - Oregon Public Utility Commission, Northwest Natural Gas, Avista Utilities, Cascade Natural Gas

(4) Electric Cooperatives - Oregon Rural Electric Cooperative Association

(5) Cable System Operators - Oregon Cable Telecommunications Association

(6) Counties - Association of Oregon Counties, Oregon Association of County Engineers and Surveyors

(7) Oregon Public Utility Commission - Oregon Public Utility Commission, Governor’s office

(8) Oregon Department of Transportation - Oregon Department of Transportation, Governor’s office

(9) Electric Utilities regulated by the Public Utility Commission - Oregon Public Utility Commission, Portland General Electric, Pacific Power

(10) Contractors - Associated General Contractors

(11) Special Districts - Special Districts Association of Oregon

(12) People’s Utility Districts - Oregon People's Utility District Association

(13) Telecommunications utilities serving more than 50,000 access lines and regulated by Public Utility Commission - Oregon Broadband Association

(14) Municipal Electric Utilities - Oregon Municipal Electric Utilities Association

(15) Telecommunications utilities serving fewer than 50,000 access lines and regulated by Public Utility Commission - Oregon Telecommunications Association

(16) Interstate Natural Gas Transmission/Pipeline - Oregon Public Utility Commission

(17) Cities with a population of 25,000 or more - League of Oregon Cities

(18) Oregon Utilities Coordinating Council – Oregon Utilities Coordinating Council

(19) Cities with a population under 25,000 - League of Oregon Cities

(20) Excavators - Northwest Utility Contractors Association

(21) National Utility Locating Association (NULCA) - NULCA

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 757.552(2)(c) Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 757.547(1)(c) - (e)
