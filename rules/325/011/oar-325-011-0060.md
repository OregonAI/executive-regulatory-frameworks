## Full text

325-011-0060 List of Participants The Oregon Patient Safety Commission (OPSC) shall maintain a list on its website of eligible entities that, in the 12-month period preceding the creation of the list, have:

(1) Submitted one or more patient safety data reports related to an adverse event or close call to the Oregon Patient Safety Reporting Portal; or

(2) Reported patient safety data as part of one or more patient safety activities convened or facilitated by OPSC.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 442.820 & 442.831 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 442.819-442.851

History: PSC 2-2025, adopt filed 05/16/2025, effective 05/16/2025
