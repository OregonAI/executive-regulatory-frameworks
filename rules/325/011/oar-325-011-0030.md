## Full text

325-011-0030 Waivers The Oregon Patient Safety Commission (OPSC) may waive any provision of these rules if the waiver will not adversely affect OPSC’s mission described in ORS 442.820.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 442.820 & 442.831 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 442.819-442.851
