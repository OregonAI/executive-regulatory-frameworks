## Full text

741-110-0010 Purpose and Scope of Rules OAR 741-110-0020 through 741-110-0090 and 741-115-0010 through 741-115-0080 provide uniform standards for the location and type of protective devices to be installed and maintained at grade crossings.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 184.616, 184.619, 823.011, 824.202 & 824.220 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 824.220
