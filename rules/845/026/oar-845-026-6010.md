## Full text

845-026-6010 Fees The Commission shall charge the following non-refundable fees:

(1) Hemp item registration: $400.

(2) Hemp item registration renewal: $400.

(3) Change to previously approved hemp item registration: $25.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 571.295 & 571.299 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 571.295
