## Full text

137-106-0040 Calculation of Time for Purposes of These Rules In computing any period of time prescribed or allowed by these rules, the period shall be calculated as provided in Oregon Rule of Civil Procedure 10A.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 180.477 & 180.483 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 180.477 & 180.483
