## Full text

125-125-0360 Coordination with the Capital Projects Advisory Board

(1) Projects submitted to the Capital Projects Advisory Board as part of an Agency Facility Plan under ORS 276.227(3)(b) that are within the area described by ORS 276.054 shall be reviewed by the Commission by July 31 of each even-even numbered year, as stipulated in OAR 125-125-0250(1). In accordance with ORS 276.059(2), projects that have been reviewed by the Capitol Planning Commission under this section need not be reviewed by the Capital Projects Advisory Board.

(2) Joint review of specific projects may be requested at the discretion of both committee chairs.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 276.055 & 276.227 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 276.056, 276.058, 276.059 & 276.227

History: DAS 4-2023, adopt filed 09/26/2023, effective 10/01/2023
