## Full text

125-090-0003 Eligibility

(1) Requirements for assignments in Parking Facilities: DAS may limit Parking eligibility to any State Employee based on the State Employee’s Work Location. DAS may limit eligibility at any Parking Facility controlled by DAS to State Employees whose Work Location is at that building.

(2) State Employees who receive assigned parking at designated Tenant-only DAS owned or managed Parking Facility must be a Tenant of that building.

(3) Any State Employee who has received a parking assignment in a facility for Tenants only and has moved to a different Work Location is no longer eligible for a parking assignment in that Parking Facility.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 98.805 - 98.818, 184.340, 190.240, 276.591 - 276.601, 283.100 & 283.110 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 98.805, 190.240, 283.110 & 276.591
