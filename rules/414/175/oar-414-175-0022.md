## Full text

414-175-0022 Age Requirements

(1) If the year of an individual's birth is known but the month is unknown, the month of birth is presumed to be July. If the date of birth is unknown, the date of birth is presumed to be the first of the month.

(2) To be determined eligible for ERDC benefits at the time of application or recertification, a child must be: (a) Under 13 years of age; or (b) Under 18 years of age, and: (A) Physically or mentally incapable of self-care; (B) Under court supervision; (C) Receiving foster care; (D) Eligible for the high need rate for child care in OAR 414-175-0076; or (E) Subject to circumstances that significantly compromise the child's safety or the caretaker's ability to work or participate in an assigned activity if child care is not available.
