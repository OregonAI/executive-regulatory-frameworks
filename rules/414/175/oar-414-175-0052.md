## Full text

414-175-0052 Concurrent and Duplicate Program Benefits An individual receiving ERDC benefits may not receive benefits for the same period as a member of two or more different Need Groups unless that individual is a child residing in two different households.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 329A.500 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 329A.500
