## Full text

414-310-0220 Arrival and Departure

(1) A school-age center may only release a child to a parent or another person named and identified by the parent(s). A person picking up the child must show identification if not known to staff.

(2) A school-age center must also be in compliance with OAR 414-310-0260 (Notifications), OAR 414-310-0210 (Permissions), and OAR 414-310-0170 (Policies).

(3) If a child has parental permission to arrive or depart on their own (also see Parental Permissions OAR 414-310-0210), a staff member must sign the child in or out, as appropriate.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 329A.280 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 329A.280
