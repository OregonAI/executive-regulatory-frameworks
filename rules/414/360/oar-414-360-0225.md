## Full text

414-360-0225 Immunizations

(1) A provider must comply with Oregon Health Authority’s administrative rules (see OAR 333-050-0040) relating to the immunization of children. If a child is enrolled in a public or private elementary school, immunizations are not required to be documented by the child care facility.

(2) A provider may provide care for children who are in foster care or experiencing homelessness while parents/guardians are taking necessary actions to comply with immunization requirements of the certified family child care.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 329A.260 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 329A.280
