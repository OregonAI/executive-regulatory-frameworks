## Full text

414-999-0050 Verifying Out-of-State Professional Development The Oregon Registry shall maintain a streamlined, equitable, and transparent process that allows the Early Learning Workforce to use out-of-state or international training, degrees, certifications, coursework, and credentials to document their professional development in the Early Childhood Education Workforce Registry Database.
