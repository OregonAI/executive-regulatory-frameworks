## Full text

414-055-0015 Reimbursement for Hiring a Substitute

(1) As used in OAR 414-055-0010(4), “other expenses” includes expenses incurred by a member of the Commission or Council in employing a substitute to carry out duties, including personal duties, normally performed by the member, which the member is unable to carry out because of the performance of official duties and which, by the nature of such duties, cannot be delayed without risk to health or safety.

(2) The amount that a member may be reimbursed for expenses incurred in employing a substitute must not exceed $25 per day, pursuant to ORS 292.495(3).

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 292.495 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 292.495 & ORS 326.425
