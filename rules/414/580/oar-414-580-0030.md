## Full text

414-580-0030 Reporting

(1) Upon request by the Department, any Early Learning and Care Program receiving IECMHC or technical assistance from Every Child Belongs must make available to the Department any information and data that the Department determines is necessary to monitor and evaluate the Every Child Belongs program.

(2) Regional Service Providers must cooperate and participate in the Department’s program monitoring, program evaluation, and reporting requirements.
