## Full text

813-395-0035 Notice of Final Sale The seller of the awarded property must submit a Notice of Final Sale on a form provided by OHCS within 30 business days of financial closing. OHCS will not provide a certification of the seller’s claim to credit before receiving this form.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 315.281 - ORS 315.291 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 315.281 - ORS 315.291
