## Full text

813-395-0015 Publicly Supported Housing Seller's Tax Credit Manual

(1) Additional policies and instructions are outlined in the PuSH Seller’s Tax Credit Manual dated August 2024, incorporated into and adopted as part of this division of administrative rules by reference.

(2) The manual may be accessed online at OHCS’ website.
