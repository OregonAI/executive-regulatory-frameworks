## Full text

813-395-0045 Compliance

(1) OHCS retains the ability to ensure that properties remain in compliance by undertaking activities including, but not limited to, auditing the files associated with the property (e.g., rents charged and income certifications) and physical conditions of the property, at any time.

(2) If the transaction is awarded other funding from OHCS in addition to the PuSH Seller’s Tax Credits, the compliance procedures and requirements associated with the other funding source(s) will apply.
