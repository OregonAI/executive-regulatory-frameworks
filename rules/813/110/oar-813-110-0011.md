## Full text

813-110-0011 Oregon Affordable Housing Tax Credit Manual

(1) Additional policies and instructions are outlined in the OAHTC Manual dated April 2026, incorporated into and adopted as part of this division of administrative rules by reference.

(2) The manual may be accessed online at OHCS’s website.
