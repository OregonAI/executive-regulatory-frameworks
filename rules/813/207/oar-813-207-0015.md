## Full text

813-207-0015 Manuals The State Home Oil Weatherization Program Manual (SHOW Program Manual) with the requirements and standards therein, dated August 1, 2026, is incorporated into and adopted as part of this division of administrative rules, by reference. The SHOW Program Manual may be accessed online at the OHCS website.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 456.594-456.599 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 456.594-456.599 & ORS 469.421
