## Full text

813-261-0020 Program Guidance The ORE-DAP program guidance, dated October 1, 2025 (the “Program Guidance”), is incorporated into and adopted as part of this division of administrative rules, by reference. The ORE-DAP Program Guidance may be accessed online at the OHCS website.
