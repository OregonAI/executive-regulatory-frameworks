# General Purpose (OAR 813-052-0000)

## Full text

813-052-0000 General Purpose The rules of OAR 813, division 52 establish and implement the Homeownership Development Incubator Program (HDIP), the purpose of which is to provide grants and low-interest loans to create new homeownership opportunities for Oregonians.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 456.555 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 456.561
