## Full text

813-022-0035 Reservation of Funds

(1) An Approved Lender may reserve funds (also known as “locking a loan”).

(2) Funds are reserved on a first-come-first-served loan by loan basis.

(3) Details regarding reservation of funds is available in the OHCS Flex Lending Program 's NextStep Product Guideline Manual.
