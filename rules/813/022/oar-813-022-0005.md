## Full text

813-022-0005 Program Definitions The following definitions and those in ORS Chapters 456 and 458 apply to OAR Chapter 822, unless the context indicates otherwise, or the term is otherwise defined in a subsequent division:

(1) “Approved Lender” is a mortgage lender that is authorized by Oregon law to originate mortgage loans in the state and has executed an agreement to provide mortgage loans to the Department. ( 2) "Department's Servicer" means the loan servicer that has an agreement with the Department to provide mortgage loan servicing.
