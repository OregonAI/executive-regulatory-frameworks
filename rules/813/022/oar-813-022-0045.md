813-022-0045 Manual The Flex Lending Program's NextStep Product Guideline Manual with the requirements and standards therein, is incorporated into and adopted as part of this division of administrative rules, by reference. The program manual may be accessed online at the OHCS website. The manual is dated April 15, 2025. [ED. NOTE: To view attachments referenced in rule text, click here for PDF copy.]
