## Full text

813-270-0045 Applicant Eligibility

(1) ORI services shall be made available to households who meet eligibility requirements as outlined in the ORI Manual.

(2) Income limitations and U.S. citizenship of household members may not be considered as eligibility criterion to qualify for assistance under the ORI program.

(3) Specific requirements and documentation to provide proof of eligibility for ORI services are detailed in the ORI Manual.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 456.555 & 456.559 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 458.390, 458.392 & 456.561
