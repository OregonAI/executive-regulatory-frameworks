## Full text

813-203-0007 Manuals The LIHWA Intake Operations Manual (program manual) with the requirements and standards therein, dated (as revised) May 30, 2024, is incorporated into and adopted as part of this division of administrative rules, by reference. The program manual may be accessed online at the OHCS website. [ED. NOTE: To view attachments referenced in rule text, click here for PDF copy.]
