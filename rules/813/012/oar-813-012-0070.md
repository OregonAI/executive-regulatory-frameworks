## Full text

813-012-0070 Insurance and Guarantees The borrower shall be responsible for obtaining any required HUD/FHA mortgage insurance or other payment guarantees satisfactory to the Department.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 90.800 - 90.840, 90.630, 183.335, 456.515 - 456.723 & 458.210 - 458.650 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 456.625
