## Full text

813-275-0010 Purpose and Objectives The purpose of OAR chapter 813, division 275, of the administrative rules is to implement the Statewide Shelter Program (SSP). OHCS is designated as the state agency responsible for administering the SSP. The SSP establishes a statewide shelter program for the purpose of reducing unsheltered homelessness and increasing housing stability through enhanced coordination and stability of funding.
