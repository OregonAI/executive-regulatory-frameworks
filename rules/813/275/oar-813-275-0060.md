## Full text

813-275-0060 Funding Agreement Prior to providing services using SSP funding, Regional Coordinators must:

(1) Enter into an agreement with OHCS; and

(2) Submit a Regional Assessment and Plan to OHCS.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 456.555 & HB 3644 (2025) Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 456.625 & HB 3644 (2025)
