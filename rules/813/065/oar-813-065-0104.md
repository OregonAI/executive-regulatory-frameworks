# Required Notification Conveyance/Transfer of Facility Deed (OAR 813-065-0104)

## At a glance

OAR 813-065-0104 — Required Notification Conveyance/Transfer of Facility Deed. Chapter 813, Division 065. Statutory authority and AON
history are in the full text below.

## Full text

813-065-0104 Required Notification Conveyance/Transfer of Facility Deed Facility owner(s) or landlord's agent(s) must deliver both written notification of any sale or other transfer or exchange of deed of a facility, per ORS 90.849, to the Department, and written notification of any sale or other transfer or exchange of deed of a facility, per ORS 90.849, to tenants and tenant committees including the required information under ORS 90.849 within 14 calendar days after conveyance, transfer, or the exchange of deed has been officially recorded.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 90.850 & ORS 90.645 - 90.671 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 90.645 - 90.671
