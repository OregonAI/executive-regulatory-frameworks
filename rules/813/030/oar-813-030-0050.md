## Full text

813-030-0050 Elderly and Disabled Sinking Fund The department may utilize the elderly and disabled sinking fund as outlined in ORS 456.543. Surplus funds may be loaned as either program loans or program contributions as determined appropriate and necessary by the Department to expand or preserve housing opportunities and supply for elderly households and persons with a disability. Resources will be disbursed in a manner consistent with other housing resource opportunities administered by the Department.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 456.543 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 456.543
