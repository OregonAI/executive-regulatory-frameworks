# ELIGIBLE GRANT USES (OAR 813-375-0021)

## At a glance

OAR 813-375-0021 — ELIGIBLE GRANT USES. Chapter 813, Division 375. Statutory authority and AON
history are in the full text below.

## Full text

813-375-0021 ELIGIBLE GRANT USES New or expanded capacity uses shall demonstrate that funding will boost current or new production of modular home or component by, but not necessarily limited to, the following activities:

(1) Increasing manufacturing space;

(2) Purchasing equipment;

(3) Standardizing design;

(4) Developing infrastructure;

(5) Enhancing transportation; and

(6) Making advancements in materials and technology.
