# FUND AWARD (OAR 813-375-0026)

## At a glance

history are in the full text below.

## Full text

813-375-0026 FUND AWARD The Department, in its sole discretion, shall determine Grant(s) awarded from the Fund.

(1) Upon approval of an award from the Fund, the Department will enter into a binding Grant Agreement with the successful Applicant(s).

(2) The Grant Agreement shall be in a form provided by the Department.

(3) OHCS will make Grantee information available to Oregon’s Office of Emergency Management and affordable housing development partners.
