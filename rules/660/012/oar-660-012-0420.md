## Full text

660-012-0420 Exemption for Communities without Parking Mandates

(1) Cities and counties that adopt land use regulations that do not include parking mandates are exempt from OAR 660-012-0425 through OAR 660-012-0450.

(2) Cities and counties that retain land use regulations with parking mandates shall conform with OAR 660-012-0425 through OAR 660-012-0450.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 197.040 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 197.012 & ORS 197.712
