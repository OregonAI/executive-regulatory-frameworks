## Full text

660-008-0001 Applicability of Division in Specified Circumstances The rules in this division adopted on December 5, 2024, are effective on January 1, 2025 and the rules in this division adopted on December 6, 2025, are effective January 1, 2026, except that a local government may adopt a Housing Capacity Analysis or Housing Production Strategy that conforms to the applicable rules of this division in place prior to January 1, 2025 or January 1, 2026, if the local government submits the adopted plan amendment as provided in OAR 660-018-0040 or OAR 660-025-0175 or Housing Production Strategy report to the department on or before January 1, 2027.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 197.040 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 197A.018, ORS 197A.270 & ORS 197A.280

History: LCDD 6-2026, temporary adopt filed 07/06/2026, effective 07/07/2026 through 01/01/2027
