# Purpose (OAR 416-106-0010)

## Full text

416-106-0010 Purpose The Oregon Youth Authority (OYA) values treating victims of crime with dignity and respect, and keeping them informed as prescribed by law. These rules delineate when OYA may disclose certain information about adjudicated youth to victims of crime.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 420A.025 & ORS 420A.115 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 420A.115
