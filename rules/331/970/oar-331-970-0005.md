## Full text

331-970-0005 WORK EXPERIENCE FOR PERSONNEL In addition to the requirements outlined in Section 4 of House Bill 2665 (2023), every authorization holder must ensure that any personnel placed with a client or hospital entity has the appropriate work experience, training, and background needed to provide the services required by the client or hospital entity.

Statutory/Other Authority: Senate Bill 1549 (2022), ORS 676.560-676.625 & House Bill 2665 (2023) Statutes/Other Implemented: Senate Bill 1549 (2022), ORS 676.560-676.625 & House Bill 2665 (2023)
