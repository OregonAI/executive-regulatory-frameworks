## Full text

331-965-0010 AUTHORIZATION ISSUANCE

(1) Authorizations become not current on the last day of the month, one year from the date of issuance, and are inactive.

(2) If a temporary staffing agency’s authorization is in inactive status, the temporary staffing agency cannot operate in Oregon.

(3) An authorization that has been inactive for more than three years is expired and cannot be renewed. If a temporary staffing agency’s authorization is expired, the temporary staffing agency must apply for an initial authorization and meet the current requirements.

(4) The authorization must be kept on file and made available upon request.
