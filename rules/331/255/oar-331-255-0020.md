## Full text

331-255-0020 Temporary License - Licensed Genetic Counselor Temporary License

(1) A licensed genetic counselor temporary license authorizes a holder to practice genetic counseling.

(2) A licensed genetic counselor temporary license is valid for one year and may be renewed once, resulting in the temporary license being valid for a two-year maximum duration, in any of the following circumstances: (a) The temporary license holder submits to the HLO satisfactory proof that the holder has not yet passed the certification examination and that there is no available certification examination before the temporary license becomes inactive. (b) The temporary license holder submits to the HLO satisfactory proof of extenuating circumstances that impact the holder’s ability to take the certification examination, including, but not limited to, a medical condition, automobile accident, catastrophe, natural disaster, or epidemic
