# OFFICE APPROVALS (OAR 331-375-0005)

## At a glance

## Full text

331-375-0005 OFFICE APPROVALS The Health Licensing Office approves the following:

(1) Programs accepted by Art Therapy Credentials Board, Inc. (ATCB), before and as of November 1, 2025.

(2) The requirements to be credentialed as a registered art therapist by the ATCB, before and as of November 1, 2025.

(3) The requirements as a board certified art therapist by the ATCB, before and as of November 1, 2025.

(4) The requirements as a provisional registered art therapist by the ATCB, before and as of November 1, 2025.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 681.740, 681.743, 681.746, 681.749, 681.752, 681.755, 681.758, 676.992 & House Bill 3761 (2025) Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 681.740, 681.743, 681.746, 681.749, 681.752, 681.755, 681.758, 676.992 & House Bill 3761 (2025)
