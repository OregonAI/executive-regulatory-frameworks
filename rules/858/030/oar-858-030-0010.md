## Full text

858-030-0010 Fee Refunds Fees are nonrefundable, except that overpayment of fees or fees submitted as part of application before required will be refunded.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 675.110 & 675.115 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 675.110 & 675.115
