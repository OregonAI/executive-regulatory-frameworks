## Full text

664-020-0140 Procedures for Expense Reimbursement

(1) A member must submit a written, itemized claim for reimbursement of actual and necessary travel or other expenses to the Oregon Clover Seed Commission no later than the last day of the fiscal year in which the member incurred the expense. For example, a claim for compensation any time from July 1 through June 30 must be submitted by June 30 of that fiscal year. The claim for reimbursement must include the following information for each expense: (a) Date on which the member incurred the expense; (b) Nature of the expense; and (c) Amount of the expense.

(2) An expense that exceeds $200.00 must be authorized by the Oregon Clover Seed Commission before the member incurs the expense.
