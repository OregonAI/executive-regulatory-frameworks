## Full text

669-030-0130 Procedures for Payment of Per Diem Compensation A member must submit a written claim for compensation to the Oregon Sweet Cherry Commission no later than the 15th day of the calendar month following the month for which the member seeks compensation. For example, a claim for compensation for May 1 and May 10 must be submitted by June 15. The member must specify the amount of time the member spent performing official duties, as well as the nature of the official duties performed, for any day or portion thereof for which the member claims compensation.
