## Full text

669-030-0140 Procedures for Expense Reimbursement

(1) A member must submit a written, itemized claim for reimbursement of actual and necessary travel or other expenses to the Oregon Sweet Cherry Commission no later than the 15th day of the calendar month following the month in which the member incurred the expense. For example, a claim for compensation for May 1 and May 10 must be submitted by June 15. The claim for reimbursement must include the following information for each expense: (a) Date on which the member incurred the expense; (b) Nature of the expense; and (c) Amount of the expense.

(2) An expense that exceeds $1000.00 [WR1] must[KA2] be authorized by the Oregon Sweet Cherry Commission before the member incurs the expense.
