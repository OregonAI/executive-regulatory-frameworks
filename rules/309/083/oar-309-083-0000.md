## Full text

309-083-0000 Purpose and Scope This division establishes the Behavioral Health Care Bonus and Housing Stipend Incentive Program within the Oregon Health Authority (Authority). The program offers grants to organizations that employ credentialed behavioral health care workers with the aim of diversifying the behavioral health workforce by improving recruitment efforts of culturally responsive, culturally specific, and linguistically specific behavioral health care workers.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 675.655 & 413.042 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 675.655
