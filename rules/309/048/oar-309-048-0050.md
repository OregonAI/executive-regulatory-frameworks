## Full text

309-048-0050 Statement of Purpose and Statutory Authority

(1) Purpose. These rules prescribe Division standards and procedures regarding the screening, evaluation and provision of specialized services to persons with mental illness who apply for or reside in Medicaid certified nursing facilities. They implement Public Law 100-203 of the Omnibus Budget Reconciliation Act of 1987 that added Section 1919 to the Social Security Act.

(2) Statutory authority and procedure. These rules are authorized by ORS 413.042 and to carry out the provisions of ORS 410.535, 414.065 & 426.490 to 426.500 and Public Law 100-203.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 413.042 & 426.500 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 410.535, 414.065 & 426.490 to 426.500
