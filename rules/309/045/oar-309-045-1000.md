## Full text

309-045-1000 Purpose and Scope The purpose of these rules is to establish minimum standards and procedures for an Agency with Choice to perform administrative employer functions and support individuals in self-directed care and services in their home for individuals with behavioral health conditions. These rules ensure Agencies with Choice provide self-directed services to maximize independence, empowerment, dignity, and human potential through the provision of flexible, efficient, and person-centered services enabling the individual to move into or continue to live in their own home.
