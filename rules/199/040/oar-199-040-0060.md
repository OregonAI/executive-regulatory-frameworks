## Full text

199-040-0060 Prohibition on Making Decisions in Executive Session Unless otherwise authorized by statute, a governing body shall not make a decision, including a vote, in executive session. A governing body may reach an informal consensus in executive session, but any decision may only be made in open session.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 244.290 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 192.610, ORS 192.685 & ORS 192.660
