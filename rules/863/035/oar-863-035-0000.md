## Full text

863-035-0000 Application and Purpose

(1) This division sets forth the requirements and process for licensing timeshare sales agents, as that term is defined in ORS 696.010.

(2) The purpose of this division is to specify the requirements for obtaining a timeshare sales agent license.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 696.022 Statutes/Other Implemented: HB 2373, 2025 Regular Session
