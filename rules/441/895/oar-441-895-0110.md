# Displaying Student Loan Servicer Licensing Information (OAR 441-895-0110)

## At a glance

## Full text

441-895-0110 Displaying Student Loan Servicer Licensing Information Licensees can meet the requirements of Oregon Laws 2021, chapter 651, section (3)(6) by posting a form provided by the Department of Consumer and Business Services completed with current and valid NMLS licensing information. The licensee must also prominently display their NMLS number on their website.

Statutory/Other Authority: Or Laws 2021, ch 651, secs 1-10 Statutes/Other Implemented: Or Laws 2021, ch 651, secs 2-10
