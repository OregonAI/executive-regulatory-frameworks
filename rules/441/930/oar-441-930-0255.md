## Full text

441-930-0255 Reporting by Limited Operations Certified Provider A limited operations certified provider must report the following activities to the director within 15 days of the occurrence:

(1) Transfer of any contract to another certified provider; or

(2) Sale of any new prearrangement or preconstruction contract.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 97.926 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 97.933

History: FCS 14-2010, f. 12-30-10, cert. ef. 1-1-11
