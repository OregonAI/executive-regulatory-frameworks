## Full text

441-735-0080 Qualifications of Person in Charge of Licensed Office A licensee must not place any person in charge of a licensed office unless the person has a thorough understanding of ORS 725A.010 – 725A.990 and these rules.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 725A.092 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 725A.024
