## Full text

441-735-0050 Notes and Agreements

(1) All forms of notes and agreements pertaining to loans and security for loans used by a licensee must be so worded that they comply with all provisions of ORS 725A.010 through 725A.990 and these rules.

(2) Any forms or agreements required or authorized by federal statute or regulation, and in compliance with those statutes or regulations are considered in compliance with and authorized by ORS 725A.010 through 725A.990.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 725A.092 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 725A.092
