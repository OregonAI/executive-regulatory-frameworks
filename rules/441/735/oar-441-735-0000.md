## Full text

441-735-0000 Statutory Authority; Purpose OAR 441-735-0000 to 441-735-0320 are adopted pursuant to the rulemaking authority granted the director by ORS 725A.092. These rules are considered necessary to assure the proper conduct of the business regulated and to protect the public.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 725A.092 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 725A.092
