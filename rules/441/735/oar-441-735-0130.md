# Index of Obligors to Be Maintained (OAR 441-735-0130)

## Full text

441-735-0130 Index of Obligors to Be Maintained A licensee must maintain an alphabetical index, or other system approved by the director, of every person obligated, directly or contingently, on a loan that is serviced at that office in each of its licensed offices.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 725A.092 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 725A.052
