## Full text

441-735-0060 Loans Not to Be Payable on Demand; Exception

(1) Except as provided by section

(2) of this rule, a loan must not be made payable on demand.

(2) A loan may provide that, if there is a default under the note or collateral security agreement, the loan may become due and payable immediately or on demand.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 725A.092 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 725A.060
