## Full text

695-030-0095 Waiver and Periodic Review of Rules The Director may waive the requirements of Division 30 for individual grant applications unless required by statute, when doing so will result in more efficient or effective implementation of the Board’s technical assistance grant program. Any waiver must be in writing and included in the grant file to which the waiver applies. The administrative rules for technical assistance grants shall be periodically reviewed by the Board and revised as necessary and appropriate.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 541.906 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 541.890-541.969
