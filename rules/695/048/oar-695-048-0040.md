695-048-0040 Eligible Applicants

(1) Eligible applicants include W ater S uppliers with service populations at or below 25,000 users . Applicants described in OAR 695-005-0040

(2) are not eligible for Drinking Water Source Protection Grants unless they otherw ise qualify as W ater S uppliers . ( 2 ) Eligible W ater S uppliers may form P artnerships with eligible H olders .
