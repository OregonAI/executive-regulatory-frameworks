## Full text

695-046-0190 Matching Contributions

(1) The following costs and activities will qualify as Match: (a) All costs listed under OAR 695-046-0185, including in-kind contributions of those costs. (b) Funding commitments made by others as a result of grant applicant efforts. (c) Any donated portion of the interest in water.

(2) OWEB funds shall not qualify as matching contributions.

(3) The Director retains the discretion to determine whether matching costs are reasonable and will be recognized as qualifying matching costs.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 541.906 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 541.932(9) & ORS 541.958(1)
