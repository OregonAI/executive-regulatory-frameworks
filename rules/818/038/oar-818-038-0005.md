## Full text

818-038-0005 Dental Therapy Education Program The Board defines “Dental Therapy Education Program” as:

(1) A program accredited by the Commission on Dental Accreditation of the American Dental Association, or its successor organization, and approved by the Board by rule;

(2) A dental pilot project as defined in ORS 679.600 and includes at least 500 hours of combined didactic and hands-on clinical dental therapy practice.

(3) Beginning January 1, 2025, no new applicants may qualify for licensure under section 2, unless they completed training within a fully approved OHA dental therapy pilot project prior to January 1, 2025.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 679 & ORS 679.600 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 679.621, ORS 679.600 & ORS 679.603
