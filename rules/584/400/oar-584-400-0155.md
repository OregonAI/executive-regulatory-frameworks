## Full text

584-400-0155 Registered Apprenticeships The Oregon Teaching Apprenticeship Standard is approved by the Oregon State Apprenticeship and Training Council and is in alignment with the TSPC Program Review and Standards Handbook. Those seeking to establish a Registered Teaching Apprenticeship program should contact the Administrator of the Joint Apprenticeship Training Committee (JATC) for the K-12 Teacher Occupation listed here: https://www.oregon.gov/boli/apprenticeship/Pages/apprenticeship-opportunities.aspx.
