# Effective Date and Supersedure (OAR 584-435-0010)

## At a glance

OAR 584-435-0010 — Effective Date and Supersedure. Chapter 584, Division 435. Statutory authority and AON

## Full text

584-435-0010 Effective Date and Supersedure Effective August 1, 2022, programs for personnel service will be issued under the rules set forth in this division, which supersedes preceding divisions OAR 584-018 & 584-400 in case of conflict.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 342.138, ORS 342.147 & ORS 342.165 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 342.138 & ORS 342.147
