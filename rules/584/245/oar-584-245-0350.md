584-245-0350 Reinstatement of Expired School Social Worker License

(1) Purpose: The Commission has adopted reinstatement provisions to allow qualified applicants to reinstate eligible, expired Oregon School Social Worker licenses.

(2) School Social Worker and Professional School Social Worker Licenses: School Social Worker and Professional School Social Worker Licenses are eligible to be reinstated if the applicant meets the requirements of section (4).

(3) District-Sponsored School Social Worker Licenses: District-sponsored school social worker licenses and registrations may not be reinstated. A holder of an expired district-sponsored school social worker license or registration may be issued a new district-sponsored license or registration based on a new application. (a) District-sponsored school social worker licenses include, but are not limited to, emergency and restricted licenses. (b) Applicants must meet the requirements in effect for the district-sponsored license at the time of the new application. (c) Applicants for new district-sponsored licenses are not required to pay reinstatement fees. (d) Applicants for new district-sponsored licenses are not required to complete reinstatement PDUs.

(4) Applying for Reinstatement: To be eligible to apply for the reinstatement of an eligible Oregon school social worker license, an applicant must: (a) Meet the renewal or advancement requirements that would have been required if the Oregon license had not lapsed; (b) Meet the requirements for reinstatement of Oregon suspended or revoked licenses as provided in Chapter 584, Division 50, if applicable; and (c) Meet the professional development requirements for reinstatements as provided in section

(5) of this rule, if required. (d) Submit a complete and correct application in the form and manner required by the Commission, including payment of the required fees as provided in OAR 584-200-0050; (e) If an applicant holds an emergency school social worker license, they can reinstate the regular license after one year of service after application and positive review of the school district.

(5) To qualify as continuing PDUs for Oregon reinstatement requirements, the PDUs must have been completed within three

(3) years of the date of application for the reinstatement of the school social worker license and within five

(5) years of the date of application for reinstatement of the professional school social worker license.

(6) All PDUs for reinstatement must be verified as provided in 584-255-0030 Verification of Professional Development.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 342.165 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 342.125, ORS 342.136, ORS 342.138 & ORS 342.165
