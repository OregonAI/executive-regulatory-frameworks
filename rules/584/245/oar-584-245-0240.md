584-245-0240 Reinstatement of Expired School Psychologist Licenses

(1) Purpose: The Commission has adopted reinstatement provisions to allow qualified applicants to reinstate eligible, expired Oregon school psychologist licenses.

(2) Applying for Reinstatement: To be eligible to apply for the reinstatement of an eligible Oregon school psychologist license, an applicant must: (a) Meet the renewal or advancement requirements that would have been required if the Oregon license had not lapsed; (b) Meet the requirements for reinstatement of Oregon suspended or revoked licenses as provided in Chapter 584, Division 50, if applicable; and (c) Meet the professional development requirements for reinstatements as provided in section (4), if required. (d) Submit a complete and correct application in the form and manner required by the Commission, including payment of the required fees as provided in OAR 584-200-0050;

(3) To qualify as continuing PDUs for Oregon reinstatement requirements, the PDUs must have been completed within three

(3) years of the date of application for the reinstatement of school psychologist license and within five

(5) years of the date of application for reinstatement of the professional school psychologist license.

(4) All PDUs for reinstatement must be verified as provided in 584-255-0030 Verification of Professional Development.
