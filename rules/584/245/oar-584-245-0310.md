584-245-0310 School Social Worker License

(1) Purpose of the License: The School Social Worker License qualifies its holder to provide counseling support services in Oregon schools. The School Social Worker License is issued to new school social workers who have: sufficient personal qualifications, successfully completed a master’s program, and completed a qualifying school social worker program, or who have entered the state as a licensed Social Worker. The School Social Worker license signifies that the licensee is a school social worker who has not acquired the advanced competencies and experience to qualify for a Professional School Social Worker License.

(2) Term of Licensure: The School Social Worker is valid for three years and is renewable as provided in section

(5) of this rule. The first license will be issued for three years plus time to the applicant's birthday.

(3) Assignments: The School Social Worker License is valid for: (a) School social work at all age or grade levels; and (b) Substitute counseling at any level.

(4) Applying for License: To be eligible to apply for a School Social Worker License, an applicant must: (a) Possess the personal qualifications for licensure, including attainment of at least eighteen years of age; possessing good moral character; and the mental and physical health necessary for employment as an educator (b) Hold a master’s degree or higher from an Approved Institution in the United States, or the foreign equivalent of such degree approved by the Commission; (c) Complete a School Social Worker License Preparation Program. (A) Completers of Oregon Programs: Provide evidence of admission to and completion of a Commission-approved School Social Worker License preparation program; or (B) Completers of Out-of-State Programs: Provide evidence of: (i) Completion of an approved out-of-state school social worker program; (ii) An active and valid non-provisional school social worker license from a NASDTEC jurisdiction; and (iii) Meet the requirements for knowledge of U.S. and Oregon civil rights laws and professional ethics. (C) Verification of Equivalency and Program Completion provided by an Oregon-approved program. (d) Complete a background clearance that includes: (A) Furnishing fingerprints, as required by ORS 342.223; (B) Providing satisfactory responses to character questions in the form and manner prescribed by the Commission; and (e) Submit a complete and correct application in the form and manner required by the Commission, including payment of all required fees as provided in 584-200-0050.

(5) Renewal: To be eligible to apply for renewal of the School Social Worker License, the applicant must: (a) Complete professional development requirements in accordance with OAR 584-255-0010; and (b) Report on completion of any continuing education regarding suicide risk assessment, treatment and management; (c) Submit a complete and correct renewal application in the form and manner required by the Commission, including payment of all required fees as provided in 584-200-0050.

(6) Persons holding a School Social Worker License may not: (a) Substitute as a School Counselor for a period greater than three consecutive months without obtaining the School Counselor License; (b) Substitute as a School Psychologist; or (c) Accept any full or part-time position as a School Counselor or as a School Psychologist; or (d) Go by the title of School Counselor or School Psychologist.

(7) Violations of section

(6) above may result in referral to the Commission for violation of professional practices.
