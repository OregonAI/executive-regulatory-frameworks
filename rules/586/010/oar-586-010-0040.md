## Full text

586-010-0040 Official Records The official records of the Fair Dismissal Appeals Board shall be maintained in the office of the executive secretary. The executive secretary shall be the custodian of records.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 183

History: FDA 1-1985, f. 3-25-85, ef. 3-27-85 FDA 4, f. 3-11-74, ef. 4-11-74 FDA 2(Temp), f. & ef. 1-4-74
