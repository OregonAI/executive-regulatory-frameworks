## Full text

581-017-0905 Funding

(1) Applicants must submit a detailed budget that explains how Grant funds will be used.

(2) Administrative costs, which include indirect costs, will be allowed as a percentage of the Grant funds disbursed as follows: (a) Up to five percent for school districts, education service districts, early learning hubs, or post-secondary institutions of education; (b) Up to fifteen percent for community-based organizations, or providers of early learning services.
