## Full text

581-051-0355 Use of Polystyrene: Prohibited Use Beginning in the 2021-2022 school year, all school districts that provide breakfast or lunch are prohibited from providing prepared foods and beverages in products made from polystyrene on school campuses except as provided in OAR 581-051-0360.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 336.445 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 336.445
