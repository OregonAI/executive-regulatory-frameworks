## Full text

581-030-2000 Teacher Qualifications

(1) For teacher licensing, employment experience at an approved recovery school shall be considered equivalent to experience in public schools.

(2) Any person employed as an administrator or teacher in an approved recovery school shall be licensed by the Teacher Standards and Practices Commission.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 336.680 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 336.680
