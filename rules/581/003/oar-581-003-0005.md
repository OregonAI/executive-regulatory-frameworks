## Full text

581-003-0005 Definitions for Education Accountability Act: Third Grade Reading Proficiency “Third-grade reading proficiency rate” means the percentage of students in the third grade who are determined to be proficient or above based on Oregon’s statewide summative assessments in English Language Arts (ELA).
