# Reconsideration (OAR 581-003-0060)

## Full text

581-003-0060 Reconsideration After a minimum of two years in either Directed Coaching or the Intensive Coaching Program, the department will take into consideration interim test data as well as the school district’s performance growth target data to determine if the school district will no longer be required to participate in Directed Coaching or the Intensive Coaching Program.
