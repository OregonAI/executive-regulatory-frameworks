## Full text

581-003-0075 Intensive Coaching Program & Student Success Teams: Eligibility

(1) The Department shall prioritize school district participation in the intensive coaching program in accordance with OAR 581-003-0035 to 581-003-0060.

(2) The Intensive Coaching Program shall begin no later than July 1 after the determination is made pursuant to OAR 581-003-0050.
