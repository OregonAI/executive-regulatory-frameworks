## Full text

581-003-0015 Local Metrics

(1) “Local metric rate” means the school district’s performance on a measurable student performance outcome.

(2) School districts shall select one of the following Local Metrics for inclusion in their Performance Growth Targets: (a) 5th Grade Science Achievement; (b) Academic Growth; (c) Career and Workforce Readiness; (d) Multilingual Proficiency; or (e) Postsecondary Readiness.

Statutory/Other Authority: SB 141 (2025) Statutes/Other Implemented: SB 141 (2025)
