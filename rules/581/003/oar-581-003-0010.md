## Full text

581-003-0010 Definitions for Education Accountability Act: Eighth Grade Math Proficiency “Eighth-grade math proficiency rate” means the percentage of students in the eighth grade who are determined to be proficient or above based on Oregon’s statewide summative assessments in Mathematics.

Statutory/Other Authority: SB 141 (2025) Statutes/Other Implemented: SB 141 (2025)
