## Full text

581-003-3035 Interim Tests

(1) School districts and public charter schools shall administer interim tests in accordance with ORS 329.485.

(2) In administering interim assessments under this rule, school districts and public charter schools shall select interim tests from among the most recently approved list of interim tests adopted by State Board of Education. The list of interim tests referenced is available from the Oregon Department of Education upon request.
