# Operational Plans for the 2022-23 School Year (OAR 581-022-0107)

## Full text

581-022-0107 Operational Plans for the 2022-23 School Year School districts and public charter schools must periodically submit to the Department a plan for operation during the 2022-23 school year. The plan must be submitted on a form and on a timeline to be determined by the Department.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 326.051 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 326.051
