## Full text

735-050-0005 Collision Reporting to DMV A person required to submit a collision report under ORS 811.720 must complete and submit either of the following reports to DMV: 1) An Oregon Traffic Collision and Insurance Report (DMV form 735-32); or 2) A DMV2U eCollision Report located on DMV’s website (https://dmv2u.oregon.gov/eServices/_/).

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 184.619, 802.010 & 811.725 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 811.720 & 811.725

History: DMV 32-2024, adopt filed 12/04/2024, effective 01/01/2025
