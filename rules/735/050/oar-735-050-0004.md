## Full text

735-050-0004 Definitions For purposes of implementing various provisions of the Oregon Vehicle Code, ORS chapters 801 to 826, referring to an event involving a vehicle, the terms “accident,” “collision,” and “crash” have the same meaning, unless context suggests otherwise.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 184.619 & 802.010 Statutes/Other Implemented: 802.220, 802.240, 811.700, 811.705, 811.710, 811.720, 811.725, 811.730, 811.735, 811.740, 811.745, 811.748 & 811.750
