# Per-mile Road Usage Charge Program Removal and Fees (OAR 735-030-0125)

## Full text

735-030-0125 Per-mile Road Usage Charge Program Removal and Fees The owner of a subject vehicle, as defined in ORS 319.883, that has been removed from the per-mile road usage charge program must pay the additional fee listed in ORS 803.422 for the subject vehicle unless:

(1) All owners of the subject vehicle listed in DMV records are deceased;

(2) All registered owners have moved and titled or registered the vehicle in another jurisdiction;

(3) The vehicle is totaled as defined in ORS 801.527; or

(4) The vehicle is stolen and not recovered at the time of removal from the RUC program.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 184.619, 802.010 & 319.890 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 319.890
