735-080-0100 Submission of Disabled Person Parking Permit Applications and Renewals

(1) An applicant must submit all required documents for Disabled Person Parking Permits and placards, as specified in 735-080-0020, as follows: (a) Individual, Wheelchair User, Oregon Wounded Warrior, Temporary Disability permit applications: (A) In person to a DMV office; (B) By mail to: DMV Driver Transaction Unit – Parking Permits Clerk, 1905 Lana Ave NE, Salem, OR 97314; or (C) By Fax to DMV Driver Transaction Unit: 503-945-5181. (b) Foreign Visitor permit applications – Present documentation, as described in ORS 811.611, in person to a DMV office. (c) Program, Family permit applications – by fax: (503) 945-7981; email: ORDMVDriverTrans@odot.oregon.gov; or mail to DMV Driver Transactions Unit – Parking Permits Clerk, 1905 Lana Ave NE, Salem, OR 97314. (d) Parking Identification Card permit applications – by mail to DMV Driver Transactions Unit – Parking Permits Clerk, 1905 Lana Avenue NE, Salem, Oregon 97314.

(2) An applicant for renewal of a parking permit, including Wounded Warrior, Wheelchair User, Parking Identification Card and Disabled Person Parking Permit, and Program, must submit the appropriate completed renewal application with all required documentation as follows: (a) The Parking Permit Renewal Reminder letter sent to each permit holder, except Family and Program permit holders, with the included Renewal Application and Certificate of Disability that must be completed by the holder’s licensed physician, a licensed nurse practitioner or a licensed physician assistant and signed and dated no more than six months prior to the receipt of the application by DMV. (b) A Disabled Person Parking Permit Application/Renewal: Individuals (DMV form 735-265), as described in OAR 735-080-0020(1), that includes the Certificate of Disability included on the application completed by the holder’s licensed physician, licensed nurse practitioner or licensed physician assistant signed and dated no more than six months prior to the receipt of the application by DMV. (c) A Disabled Person Parking Family Placard Application (DMV form 735-265 FPP) that includes the Certificate of Disability, included on the Application, completed by the licensed physician treating the disabled individual, and signed and dated no more than six months prior to the receipt of the application by DMV. (d) An Application for Program Disabled Person Parking Permit Placard (DMV form 735-265PP). (e) A Parking Identification Card and Disabled Person Parking Permit Placard Application (DMV form 735-265PIP) that meets requirements described in OAR 735-080-0060 including the Certificate of Disability, included on the application, completed by the holder’s licensed physician, licensed nurse practitioner or licensed physician assistant.
