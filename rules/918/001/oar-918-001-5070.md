## Full text

918-001-5070 Additional Organizational Partners The division may partner with additional governmental or non-governmental organizations to provide additional assistance to potential applicants. This assistance may include:

(1) Outreach and education;

(2) Application preparation and program intake; and

(3) Preparation of verification documents.

Statutory/Other Authority: Oregon Laws 2021, Chapter 669, Section 263 (House Bill 5006), Oregon Laws 2023, Chapter 605, Section 270 (House Bill 5506) & Oregon Laws 2024, Chapter 26, Section 3 (House Bill 4016) Statutes/Other Implemented: Oregon Laws 2021, Chapter 669, Section 263 (House Bill 5006), Oregon Laws 2023, Chapter 605, Section 270 (House Bill 5506) & Oregon Laws 2024, Chapter 26, Section 3 (House Bill 4016)
