## Full text

918-283-0100 Procedures for Submitting Training Proposals The employer shall submit to the board a detailed description of the electrical training program in a recognized branch of the electrical trade the employer will conduct.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 479.730 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 479.730
