## Full text

918-480-0015 Oregon Housing Needs Analysis Visitability Definition For the purpose of the Oregon House Needs Analysis, unless context requires otherwise, “Visitability” means a dwelling unit in a structure constructed under the Oregon Residential Specialty Code, that meets the provisions for Type C units in ICC A117.1 (2017).
