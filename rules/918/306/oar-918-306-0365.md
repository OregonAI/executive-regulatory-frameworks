## Full text

918-306-0365 Suspension or Termination of Field Evaluation Body Approval Term

(1) Any field evaluation body failing to comply with the requirements of this section or submitting false information may have its approval term suspended or terminated for one or more electrical product categories.

(2) The division may suspend, terminate, or refuse to renew the approval term of any field evaluation body found to be in non-compliance with the requirements of the State of Oregon.
