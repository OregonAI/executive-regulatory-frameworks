# Scope (OAR 543-080-0005)

## At a glance

## Full text

543-080-0005 Scope OAR chapter 543, division 80, applies to public libraries as defined in ORS 357.400(3) that were established under one of the methods described in ORS 357.216-286 or 357.400-621.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 357 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 357
