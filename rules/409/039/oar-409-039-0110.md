## Full text

409-039-0110 Regular Review of Waiver Conditions and Processes The authority will conduct periodic, annual review of conditions for requesting waivers and the effectiveness of the administration of the program.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 676.716 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 676.695 - 676.725
