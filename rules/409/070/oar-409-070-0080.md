## Full text

409-070-0080 Compliance with Conditions

(1) Following approval of a material change transaction, the Authority will verify compliance with any conditions that the Authority included in its approval of the transaction and issue such additional orders, following notice and opportunity for hearing, as may be necessary to enforce compliance with the terms and conditions of the approval of the transaction; provided however, that the Authority may not impose new conditions that are unrelated to, or not reasonably required to enforce compliance with, those conditions, if any, that were included in the Authority's approval of the transaction. Such verification of compliance must occur, at minimum, at the intervals required by ORS 415.501(19).

(2) The Authority may require that the parties provide such information, reports, analyses and documentation as the Authority may require in order to verify compliance with conditions.

(3) The entity or entities must provide all requested information in accordance with OAR 409-070-0070 and may not refuse to provide requested information. Failure to comply with or timely respond to a clarifying question or information request from the Authority may result in the imposition of civil penalties pursuant to OAR 409-070-0067.
