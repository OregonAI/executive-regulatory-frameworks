# Scope (OAR 409-058-0100)

## At a glance

## Full text

409-058-0100 Scope These rules (OAR 409-058-0100 to 409-058-0110) require the Health Evidence Review Commission to adopt bylaws to govern, among other things, the management of potential conflicts of interest, actual conflicts of interest, and significant personal interests among Commissioners, subcommittee members, and appointed ad hoc experts. These rules apply to the Health Evidence Review Commission and its subcommittees as of October 16, 2021.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 413.042 & 414.689 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 414.688 & 414.689

History: OHP 4-2021, adopt filed 11/03/2021, effective 11/03/2021
