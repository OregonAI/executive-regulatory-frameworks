## Full text

409-065-0042 Confidential Information

(1) An entity that submits information to the Authority may claim portions of the information a trade secret and exempt from disclosure under ORS 192.311 to 192.478. (a) The entity must submit two versions of such materials to the Authority: (A) One must be marked as “CONFIDENTIAL” and must contain the full unredacted version of the document. (B) The second must be marked as “PUBLIC” and must contain a redacted version of the document (from which the confidential portions have been removed or obscured) and must be posted on the Program website. (b) An entity claiming confidentiality must include a redaction log that provides a reasonably detailed statement of the grounds on which confidentiality is claimed and the reasoning for such redaction. This redaction log must be posted on the Program website.
