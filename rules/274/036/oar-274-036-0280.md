## Full text

274-036-0280 Misrepresentation of Emergency Financial Assistance The Department may require immediate reimbursement of funds, either in total or in part, if it is determined that applicant intentionally submitted invalid, incomplete, or fraudulent information when applying for funds from this Program.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 406.005(4) & ORS 406.030 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 408.115, ORS 408.125 & HB 4132 (2026)
