# Evidence of Need (OAR 274-036-0255)

## At a glance

history are in the full text below.

## Full text

274-036-0255 Evidence of Need When an application is made for financial assistance, the applicant shall provide the Department with the documentation required under Subsection 0220, 0230, and 0240, together with statement(s) and other documentation satisfactory to the Department indicating how the desired financial assistance will contribute to the applicant’s ability to complete their educational or training goals. The Department may require additional documentation or information from the applicant as it deems necessary or appropriate.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 406.005(4) & ORS 406.030 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 408.115, ORS 408.125 & HB 4132 (2026)
