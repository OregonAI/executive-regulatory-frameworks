## Full text

410-134-0000 Purpose

(1) The purpose of this OAR Chapter 410, division 134 is to set forth the rules that are specific to the Healthier Oregon program.

(2) The Healthier Oregon program is a medical assistance benefit package equal to OHP Plus defined and described in OAR 410-120-1210.

(3) The Healthier Oregon program is funded with state and federal dollars as described in OAR 410-134-0004.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 413.042 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 413.042, 414.025, 414.065, 414.231 & 414.706
