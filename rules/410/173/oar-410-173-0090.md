## Full text

410-173-0090 Home Delivered Meals

(1) 1915(i) home delivered meals shall be provided to eligible Individuals who are home bound, live in their own home, and are unable to complete meal preparation, to assist an Individual to remain in their own home and are intended to provide one

(1) meal per day;

(2) Home delivered meal services shall be provided in the following settings, as identified, and approved within the PCSP: (a) Community; or (b) Individuals own or family home.

(3) Home delivered meals are not available to Individuals residing in a setting in which residential providers are responsible to provide meals.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 410.070, ORS 411.060 & 411.070 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 410.070
