## Full text

410-151-0006 Transportation and scheduling assistance In accordance with the responsibilities in OAR 410-136-3000 Medical Transportation Services, the Authority or the MCE must offer and provide, when requested by the EPSDT Beneficiary or their parent or guardian:

(1) Transportation assistance to covered services;

(2) Scheduling assistance for appointments for covered services.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 413.042 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 414.025, 414.065 & 414.150

History: DMAP 97-2023, adopt filed 12/27/2023, effective 01/01/2024
