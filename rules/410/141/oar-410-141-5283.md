## Full text

410-141-5283 CCO ACQUISITIONS AND MERGERS: Compliance with Conditions

(1) Following approval of a Form A, the Authority may verify compliance with any conditions that the Authority included in its approval of the transaction and issue such additional orders, following notice and opportunity for hearing, as may be necessary to enforce compliance with the terms and conditions of the approval of the transaction; provided however, that the Authority may not impose new conditions that are unrelated to, or not reasonably required to enforce compliance with, those conditions, if any, that were included in the Authority's approval of the transaction.

(2) No person shall file or cause to be filed with the Authority or the Department any notice, article, certificate, report, statement, application or any other information required or permitted to be so filed and known to such person to be false or misleading in any material respect.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 413.042, 414.572, 414.591 & 414.605 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 414.570-414.686 & 415.001-415.430
