# No Premiums or Copayments. (OAR 410-115-0035)

## At a glance

## Full text

410-115-0035 No Premiums or Copayments. OHP Bridge members are not and shall not be required to pay any premiums, copayments and they do not and shall not have to pay any provider to receive covered services.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 413.042, 414.065, 414.241, 42 CFR 600.505, 600.510, 600.515, 600.520 & 600.525 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 413.042 & 414.241
