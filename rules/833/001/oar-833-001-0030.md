## Full text

833-001-0030 Board Member Compensation

(1) Board members shall receive compensation that is equal to the per diem paid to members of the Legislative Assembly under ORS 171.072(9) for each day or portion thereof where the member is engaged in the performance of official duties, which includes Board and committee meetings and activities that the Board or Executive Director has pre-approved or requested.

(2) Compensation provided under Section

(1) shall be in addition to the allowable reimbursement for travel or other expenses.

(3) Board members may decline to accept compensation or reimbursement of expenses.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 675.715 - 675.835 & ORS 292.495 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 292.495, ORS 171.072 & ORS 675.775(4)
