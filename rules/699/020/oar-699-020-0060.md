## Full text

699-020-0060 Reporting

(1) An Agency receiving State Agency Program Funds must submit a report to the Council biennially.

(2) The report shall be in a format provided by OWEB.

(3) Agency reports shall be due 60 days after the final day of the biennium.

Statutory/Other Authority: 541.869 Statutes/Other Implemented: 541.857 - 541.882
