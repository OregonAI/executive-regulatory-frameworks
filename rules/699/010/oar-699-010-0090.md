## Full text

699-010-0090 Reporting

(1) A Tribe receiving Tribal Nation Natural Resource Program Funds must submit a report to Council biennially.

(2) The report format will be developed in consultation with Tribes and provided to Tribes by OWEB.

(3) The report is due 90 days after the final day of each biennium.
