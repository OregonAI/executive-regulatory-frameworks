# Sources of Funds (OAR 699-010-0070)

## Full text

699-010-0070 Sources of Funds Moneys in the Tribal Nation Natural Resource Program Fund consist of moneys transferred to the fund from the Oregon Environmental Restoration Fund and moneys deposited or transferred into the fund from any other public or private source.

Statutory/Other Authority: 541.876 Statutes/Other Implemented: 541.857 - 541.882
