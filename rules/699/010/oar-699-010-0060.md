## Full text

699-010-0060 Deference to Tribal Nations Determinations of Eligible Use of Funds Tribes are sovereign nations, possessing experience and expertise in stewarding natural resources. As such, their determinations that Tribal Nation Natural Resource Program Funds are committed to eligible uses shall be given a high degree of deference by the Council.
