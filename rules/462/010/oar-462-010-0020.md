## Full text

462-010-0020 Commissioner Vacancies

(1) Following appointment by the Governor and approval by the Senate, a new Commissioner shall take the oath of office at the next regularly scheduled Commission meeting and shall serve, at the pleasure of the Governor, for a four year term, and until their successor is appointed and qualified.

(2) An unanticipated Commissioner vacancy shall be filled by appointment of the Governor for the unexpired term.

(3) Any Commissioner may be removed by the Governor for cause after a public hearing. Notice of hearing shall fix the time and place for the hearing and shall specify the charges. Copy of the notice shall be served on the commissioner by mailing it to the commissioner at the last-known address of the commissioner at least 10 days before the date fixed for the hearing.
