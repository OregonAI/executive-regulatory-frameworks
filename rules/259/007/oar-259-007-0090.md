## Full text

259-007-0090 Fragmentation A Procurement may not be artificially divided or fragmented for the purpose of restricting competition or circumventing a legal requirement.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 181A.410, ORS 279A.050, ORS 279A.065 & ORS 279A.070 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 279A.065 & ORS 279A.070
