## Full text

259-007-0040 Preference or Limited Competition Permitted For any Contract estimated to cost $50,000 or less, DPSST may allow a preference for, or limit competition to, contracting entities owned or controlled by disadvantaged persons described in ORS 279A.100(1).

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 181A.410, ORS 279A.050, ORS 279A.065, ORS 279A.070 & ORS 279A.100 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 279A.065, ORS 279A.070 & ORS 279A.100
