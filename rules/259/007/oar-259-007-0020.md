## Full text

259-007-0020 Definitions

(1) The Public Contracting Code and Attorney General’s Model Rules contain definitions applicable to capitalized terms in OAR Chapter 259 Division 7.

(2) “DPSST” or “Department” means the Department of Public Safety Standards and Training.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 181A.410, ORS 279A.050, ORS 279A.065 & ORS 279A.070 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 279A.065 & ORS 279A.070
