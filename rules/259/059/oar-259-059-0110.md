# Required Policies (OAR 259-059-0110)

## Full text

259-059-0110 Required Policies Private security entities must demonstrate the existence of use of force and citizen arrest policies unless the private security entity exclusively monitors alarm systems. For the purposes of this rule, demonstration of the existence of these policies means the policies must be provided to the Department upon request.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 181A.870 & ORS 181A.900 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 181A.900
