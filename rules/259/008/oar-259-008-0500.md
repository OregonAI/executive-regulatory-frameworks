## Full text

259-008-0500 Law Enforcement Agency Accreditation The Department designates the following bodies as accrediting bodies for the purpose of accrediting law enforcement agencies in this state:

(1) The Northwest Accreditation Alliance; and

(2) The Commission on Accreditation for Law Enforcement Agencies, Inc. (CALEA®).

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 181A.410 & ORS 181A.657 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 181A.410 & ORS 181A.657
