## Full text

436-050-0500 Assessment of Civil Penalties The director may assess a civil penalty under ORS 656.745(2) against an employer or insurer that violates ORS chapter 656, OAR 436-050, or an order of the director.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 656.726(4) Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 656.745(2)
