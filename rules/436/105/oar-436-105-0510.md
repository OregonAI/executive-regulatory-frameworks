## Full text

436-105-0510 Employer Eligibility To be eligible for the Employer-at-Injury Program, an employer must:

(1) Maintain Oregon workers’ compensation insurance coverage;

(2) Be the employer at injury; and

(3) Employ an eligible worker.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 656.622 & 656.726(4) Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 656.622
