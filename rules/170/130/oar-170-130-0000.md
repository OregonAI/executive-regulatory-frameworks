## Full text

170-130-0000 Purpose These rules prescribe the procedures to recover Escheat Property held by the Treasurer as provided for by ORS 98.319,112.055, 114.555, 116.193, 116.203, 708A.430, 722.262, 723.466, any form of non-probate administration of an Escheat Property, and property received by the Department of State Lands pursuant to ORS 179.540–179.550 prior to October 4, 1997.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 178.050 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 178.050
