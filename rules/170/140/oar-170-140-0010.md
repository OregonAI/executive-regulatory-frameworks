# Maximum Percentage of Costs (OAR 170-140-0010)

## At a glance

## Full text

170-140-0010 Maximum Percentage of Costs When the total value of Property of a verified Claim is $100.00 or more, the Treasurer may deduct a maximum of ten percent of the total value for the cost of locating the Owners under ORS 98.356(4).

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 98.422 & ORS 178.050 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 98.356

History: OST 2-2021, adopt filed 06/29/2021, effective 07/01/2021
