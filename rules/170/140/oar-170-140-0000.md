# Purpose (OAR 170-140-0000)

## At a glance

## Full text

170-140-0000 Purpose These rules establish:

(1) Fees required or allowed under ORS chapter 98; and

(2) Requirements and procedures for making a Claim to recover Property.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 98.422 & ORS 178.050 Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 98.422 & ORS 178.050
