# Who may institute proceedings (ORS 112.755)

## At a glance

ORS 112.755 — Who may institute proceedings. Chapter 112 (Intestate Succession and Wills), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

112.755 Who may institute proceedings. If the title to any property to which ORS 112.705 to 112.775 apply is held by the surviving spouse at the time of the decedent’s death, the personal representative or an heir or devisee of the decedent may institute an action to perfect title to the property. The personal representative has no fiduciary duty to discover or attempt to discover whether any property held by the surviving spouse is property to which ORS 112.705 to 112.775 apply, unless a written demand is made by an heir, devisee, or creditor of the decedent. [1973 c.205 §5]
