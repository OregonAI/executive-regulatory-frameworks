# Officer or employee of public body (ORS 146.088)

## At a glance

ORS 146.088 — Officer or employee of public body. Chapter 146 (Investigations of Deaths, Injuries and Missing Persons), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

146.088 Officer or employee of public body. A district medical examiner, medical-legal death investigator, assistant district medical examiner or designated pathologist is deemed to be an officer or employee of a public body for purposes of ORS 30.260 to 30.300 while acting as a district medical examiner, medical-legal death investigator, assistant district medical examiner or designated pathologist. [1995 c.744 §13; 2017 c.151 §12]
