# Accounting for and disposition of trial fee (ORS 52.440)

## At a glance

ORS 52.440 — Accounting for and disposition of trial fee. Chapter 52 (Civil Actions; Small Claims), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

52.440 Accounting for and disposition of trial fee. In a justice court, the trial fee is paid to the justice. The justice shall keep an account of such fees, and by whom paid, and distribute the amount among the jury in the particular case, in partial payment of their legal fees. (Trial and Judgment)
