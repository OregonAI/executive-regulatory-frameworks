# Agreement to supply additional water prior to approval of annexation petition (ORS 264.505)

## At a glance

ORS 264.505 — Agreement to supply additional water prior to approval of annexation petition. Chapter 264 (Domestic Water Supply Districts), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

264.505 Agreement to supply additional water prior to approval of annexation petition. Before approval of the board of commissioners is given to a petition for annexation, the board shall secure from the independent water supply source of the district, if any, an agreement to supply such additional water as may be needed.
