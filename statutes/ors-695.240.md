# Advertising secondhand watches (ORS 695.240)

## At a glance

ORS 695.240 — Advertising secondhand watches. Chapter 695 (Watch Dealers), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

695.240 Advertising secondhand watches. Any person advertising in any manner secondhand watches for sale shall state clearly in such advertising that the watches so advertised are secondhand watches.
