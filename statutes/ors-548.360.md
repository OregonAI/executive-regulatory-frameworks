# Moneys and securities; custody (ORS 548.360)

## At a glance

ORS 548.360 — Moneys and securities; custody. Chapter 548 (Provisions Applicable Both to Drainage Districts and to Irrigation), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

548.360 Moneys and securities; custody. All moneys and securities received under ORS 548.350 may be deposited with the State Treasurer for safekeeping. [Amended by 1989 c.182 §36]
