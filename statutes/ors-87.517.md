# Recording notice of lien (ORS 87.517)

## At a glance

ORS 87.517 — Recording notice of lien. Chapter 87 (Statutory Liens), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

87.517 Recording notice of lien. The recording officer of the county shall record the notices filed under ORS 87.507 in the statutory lien record. [1995 c.749 §5; 1997 c.744 §7; 2009 c.628 §4] Note: See note under 87.501.
