## At a glance

## Full text

62.511 Incorporators; articles of incorporation. One or more individuals 18 years of age or older, a domestic or foreign corporation or cooperative, a partnership or an association may act as incorporators of a cooperative by delivering articles of incorporation to the Office of Secretary of State for filing. [1995 c.195 §24]
