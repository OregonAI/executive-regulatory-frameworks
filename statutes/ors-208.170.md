## At a glance

## Full text

208.170 Administration of oaths. The county treasurer and the deputy of the county treasurer are authorized to administer all oaths necessary in the discharge of the duties of their office. 208.180 [Repealed by 1981 c.48 §8] 208.190 [1963 c.321 §1; repealed by 1981 c.48 §8] 208.200 [1969 c.694 §1; repealed by 2001 c.215 §1] 208.205 [1977 c.188 §1; repealed by 1981 c.526 §9] 208.210 [1969 c.694 §2; repealed by 2001 c.215 §1] 208.220 [1969 c.694 §3; repealed by 2001 c.215 §1]
