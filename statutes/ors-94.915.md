## Full text

94.915 Inspection of records; rules; uniform standards.

(1) Records of the sale of timeshares in a timeshare plan shall be subject to inspection by the Real Estate Commissioner.

(2) The Real Estate Agency shall adopt rules necessary to carry out ORS 94.803 and 94.807 to 94.945.

(3) The agency may cooperate with agencies performing similar functions in other jurisdictions to develop uniform filing procedures, forms, disclosure standards and administrative practices. [1983 c.530 §§38,40]
