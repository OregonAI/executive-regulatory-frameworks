# Improvement bonds (ORS 552.613)

## At a glance

ORS 552.613 — Improvement bonds. Chapter 552 (Water Improvement Districts), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

552.613 Improvement bonds. If any portion of the cost of a district works is assessed against the property directly benefited, the district board may issue improvement bonds in the total amount of the valid applications it has received to pay assessments in installments as provided by ORS 223.205 and 223.210 to 223.295. [1969 c.606 §42; 1995 c.333 §19] 552.615 [Repealed by 1969 c.168 §1]
