## Full text

442.463 Annual utilization report; contents; approval; rules.

(1) Each licensed health facility shall file with the Oregon Health Authority an annual report containing such information related to the facility’s utilization as may be required by the authority, in such form as the authority prescribes by rule.

(2) The annual report shall contain such information as may be required by rule of the authority and must be approved by the authority. [1985 c.747 §§18,19; 1995 c.727 §32; 1997 c.683 §28; 1999 c.581 §8; 2015 c.318 §35]
