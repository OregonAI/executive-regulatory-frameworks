# Unordered goods presumed gifts (ORS 98.450)

## At a glance

ORS 98.450 — Unordered goods presumed gifts. Chapter 98 (Lost, Unclaimed or Abandoned Property; Vehicle Towing), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

98.450 Unordered goods presumed gifts.

(1) If a person mails or sends goods, newspapers or periodicals of a value of less than $20 to a person in this state without first receiving an order for such items, the items are conclusively presumed to be a gift and no obligation shall accrue against the recipient.

(2) If a person deliberately and intentionally mails or sends goods, newspapers, or periodicals of a value in excess of $20 to a person in this state without first receiving an order for such items, the items are presumed to be a gift. [1969 c.354 §1]
