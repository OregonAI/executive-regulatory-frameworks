# Termination of support order (ORS 25.335)

## At a glance

ORS 25.335 — Termination of support order. Chapter 25 (Child Support Services), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

25.335 Termination of support order. When child support services are being provided under ORS 25.080, the enforcing agency shall notify the employer when there is no longer in effect a support order requiring health care coverage for which the enforcing agency is responsible. However, termination of the health care coverage is governed by the health benefit plan’s provisions for termination and by applicable federal law. [2003 c.637 §9; 2025 c.99 §27]
