# Preemption (ORS 653.661)

## At a glance

ORS 653.661 — Preemption. Chapter 653 (Chapter 653), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

653.661 Preemption. The State of Oregon preempts all charter and statutory authority of local governments as defined in ORS 174.116 to set any sick leave requirements. [2015 c.537 §15]
