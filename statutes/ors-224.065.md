# Writ of review (ORS 224.065)

## At a glance

ORS 224.065 — Writ of review. Chapter 224 (City Sewers and Sanitation), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

224.065 Writ of review. Notwithstanding any of the provisions of ORS 224.010 to 224.170, owners of any property against which an assessment for a local improvement under this chapter has been imposed may seek a review thereof under the provisions of ORS 34.010 to 34.100. [1967 c.280 §2 (enacted in lieu of 224.060 and 224.070)] 224.070 [Amended by 1959 c.220 §2; repealed by 1967 c.280 §1 (224.065 enacted in lieu of 224.070)]
