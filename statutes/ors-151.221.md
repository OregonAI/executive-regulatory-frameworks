# Status of officers and employees of commission (ORS 151.221)

## At a glance

ORS 151.221 — Status of officers and employees of commission. Chapter 151 (Public Defenders; Counsel for Financially Eligible Persons), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

151.221 Status of officers and employees of commission. Officers and employees of the Oregon Public Defense Commission are state officers or employees in the exempt service and are not subject to ORS chapter 240. [2003 c.449 §17; 2023 c.281 §5] Note: See note under 151.211.
