# Report to Legislative Assembly (ORS 751.101)

## At a glance

ORS 751.101 — Report to Legislative Assembly. Chapter 751 (Universal Health Care System), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

751.101 Report to Legislative Assembly.

(1) The Universal Health Plan Governance Board shall provide a status report no later than December 1 of each year, beginning in 2024, to the interim committees of the Legislative Assembly related to health, on the progress in the development of the comprehensive plan and any needed legislative changes.

(2) The report need not be in compliance with ORS 192.245.
