# Withdrawal from petition (ORS 261.118)

## At a glance

ORS 261.118 — Withdrawal from petition. Chapter 261 (People’s Utility Districts), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

261.118 Withdrawal from petition. After a petition has been filed with the county clerk, no person may withdraw that person’s name from it. [Formerly 261.040] 261.120 [Amended by 1975 c.647 §17; repealed by 1979 c.558 §30]
