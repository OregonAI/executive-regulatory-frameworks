# Rules (ORS 441.341)

## At a glance

ORS 441.341 — Rules. Chapter 441 (Health Care Facilities), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

441.341 Rules. The Department of Human Services shall adopt rules necessary to implement ORS 441.331 to 441.341, including but not limited to the criteria for the appointment of a temporary manager of a facility, the qualifications of a temporary manager and the powers and duties of a temporary manager.
