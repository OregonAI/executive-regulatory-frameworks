# UCC 9-342. Bank’s right to refuse to enter into or disclose existence of control agreement (ORS 79A.3420)

## At a glance

ORS 79A.3420 — UCC 9-342. Bank’s right to refuse to enter into or disclose existence of control agreement. Chapter 79A (Secured Transactions), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

79A.3420 UCC 9-342. Bank’s right to refuse to enter into or disclose existence of control agreement. This chapter does not require a bank to enter into an agreement of the kind described in ORS 79A.1040 (1)(b), even if its customer so requests or directs. A bank that has entered into such an agreement is not required to confirm the existence of the agreement to another person unless requested to do so by its customer. [Formerly 79.0342]
