# General obligation bond election (ORS 838.070)

## At a glance

ORS 838.070 — General obligation bond election. Chapter 838 (Airport Districts), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

838.070 General obligation bond election. Elections for the purpose of voting on the question of borrowing funds by issuance and sale of general obligation bonds shall be called by the district board. [Formerly 494.130]
