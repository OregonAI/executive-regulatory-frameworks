# Delivery of invoice to vendee; contents; duplicate invoice (ORS 695.230)

## At a glance

ORS 695.230 — Delivery of invoice to vendee; contents; duplicate invoice. Chapter 695 (Watch Dealers), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

695.230 Delivery of invoice to vendee; contents; duplicate invoice. Any person who sells a secondhand watch shall deliver to the vendee a written invoice setting forth the name and address of the vendor, the name and address of the vendee, the date of the sale, the name of the watch or its maker, and the serial numbers, if any, or other distinguishing numbers or identification marks on its case and movement. If the serial numbers, or other distinguishing numbers or identification marks have been erased, defaced, removed, altered or covered, this shall be set forth in the invoice. A duplicate of the invoice shall be kept on file by the vendor of the secondhand watch for at least one year from the date of the sale thereof.
