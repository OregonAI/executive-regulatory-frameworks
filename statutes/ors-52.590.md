# Judgment may not determine or affect title to real property (ORS 52.590)

## At a glance

ORS 52.590 — Judgment may not determine or affect title to real property. Chapter 52 (Civil Actions; Small Claims), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

52.590 Judgment may not determine or affect title to real property. Although the title to real property may be controverted or questioned in an action in a justice court, the judgment in the action shall in no way affect or determine the title as between the parties, or otherwise. (Enforcement and Setoff of Judgments; Executions)
