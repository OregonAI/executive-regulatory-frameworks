# Remedies for breach of collateral contracts not impaired (ORS 72.7010)

## At a glance

ORS 72.7010 — Remedies for breach of collateral contracts not impaired. Chapter 72 (Sales), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

72.7010 Remedies for breach of collateral contracts not impaired. Remedies for breach of any obligation or promise collateral or ancillary to a contract for sale are not impaired by the provisions of this chapter. [1961 c.726 §72.7010]
