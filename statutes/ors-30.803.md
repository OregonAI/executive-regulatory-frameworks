# Liability of licensed emergency medical services provider acting as volunteer (ORS 30.803)

## At a glance

ORS 30.803 — Liability of licensed emergency medical services provider acting as volunteer. Chapter 30 (Actions and Suits in Particular Cases), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

30.803 Liability of licensed emergency medical services provider acting as volunteer. A person may not maintain a cause of action for injury, death or loss against a licensed emergency medical services provider who acts as a volunteer without expectation of compensation, based on a claim of negligence unless the person shows that the injury, death or loss resulted from willful and wanton misconduct or intentional act or omission of the emergency medical services provider. [1987 c.915 §11; 2011 c.703 §19]
