# Compliance with ORS 92.010 to 92.192 required (ORS 92.012)

## At a glance

ORS 92.012 — Compliance with ORS 92.010 to 92.192 required. Chapter 92 (Subdivisions and Partitions), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

92.012 Compliance with ORS 92.010 to 92.192 required. No land may be subdivided or partitioned except in accordance with ORS 92.010 to 92.192. [1973 c.696 §2; 1975 c.643 §24]
