# District may construct and operate service facilities (ORS 451.420)

## At a glance

ORS 451.420 — District may construct and operate service facilities. Chapter 451 (County Service Facilities), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

451.420 District may construct and operate service facilities. When authorized as provided in ORS 451.410 to 451.610 a district may construct, maintain and operate any or all of the service facilities specified in ORS 451.010.
