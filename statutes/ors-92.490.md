# Civil penalty (ORS 92.490)

## At a glance

ORS 92.490 — Civil penalty. Chapter 92 (Subdivisions and Partitions), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

92.490 Civil penalty.

(1) In addition to any other penalties provided by law, the Real Estate Commissioner may impose a civil penalty for violation of the provisions of ORS 92.305 to 92.495. No civil penalty shall exceed $1,000 per violation.

(2) Civil penalties under this section shall be imposed as provided in ORS 183.745. [1975 c.643 §23; 1979 c.242 §8; 1983 c.696 §7a; 1989 c.706 §6; 1991 c.734 §4]
