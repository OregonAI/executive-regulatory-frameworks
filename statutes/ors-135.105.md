# Use of statement before grand jury or on trial (ORS 135.105)

## At a glance

ORS 135.105 — Use of statement before grand jury or on trial. Chapter 135 (Arraignment and Pretrial Provisions), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

135.105 Use of statement before grand jury or on trial. The statement of the defendant is competent testimony to be laid before the grand jury and may be given in evidence at the trial. [Formerly 133.700] 135.110 [Amended by 1973 c.836 §132; renumbered 135.030]
