# County authority over trails (ORS 368.021)

## At a glance

ORS 368.021 — County authority over trails. Chapter 368 (County Roads), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

368.021 County authority over trails.

(1) A county governing body has the same jurisdiction over trails as it has over local access roads.

(2) This section applies to trails that: (a) Are easements over land or by watercourse that are not part of a road right of way; (b) Provide certain forms of ingress to or egress from land or water or permit travel between places; (c) Do not provide vehicle access of the type provided by a road; and (d) Are not under the jurisdiction of a state or federal agency. [1981 c.153 §5]
