# Basis of appointment and promotion generally (ORS 241.205)

## At a glance

ORS 241.205 — Basis of appointment and promotion generally. Chapter 241 (Civil Service for County Employees), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

241.205 Basis of appointment and promotion generally. Except as otherwise expressly provided in ORS 241.016 to 241.990, the appointment and promotion of all persons to or in all positions subject to the provisions of ORS 241.016 to 241.990 shall be made solely upon merit, efficiency and fitness, which shall be ascertained by open competitive examination and impartial investigation.
