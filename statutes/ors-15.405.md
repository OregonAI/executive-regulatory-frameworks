# Applicability (ORS 15.405)

## At a glance

ORS 15.405 — Applicability. Chapter 15 (Choice of Laws), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

15.405 Applicability. ORS 15.400 to 15.460 govern the choice of law applicable to noncontractual claims when a choice between or among the laws of more than one state is at issue. ORS 15.400 to 15.460 do not supersede the provisions of other Oregon statutes that expressly designate the law governing a particular noncontractual claim. [Formerly 31.855] (Preliminary Issues)
