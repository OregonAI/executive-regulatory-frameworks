# Exclusion of services and merchandise offered under transportation protection agreement (ORS 97.012)

## At a glance

ORS 97.012 — Exclusion of services and merchandise offered under transportation protection agreement. Chapter 97 (Rights and Duties Relating to Cemeteries, Human Bodies and Anatomical Gifts), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

97.012 Exclusion of services and merchandise offered under transportation protection agreement. Services and merchandise offered under a transportation protection agreement shall not be considered funeral merchandise, funeral services, prearrangement sales or a prearrangement sales contract. [2025 c.242 §1] Note: 97.012 was enacted into law by the Legislative Assembly but was not added to or made a part of ORS chapter 97 or any series therein by legislative action. See Preface to Oregon Revised Statutes for further explanation.
