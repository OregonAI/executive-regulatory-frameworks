# County court may sell property of station upon discontinuance; disposition of proceeds (ORS 567.240)

## At a glance

## Full text

567.240 County court may sell property of station upon discontinuance; disposition of proceeds. In the event the station is discontinued, the property acquired under ORS 567.230 and 567.235, including land and improvements thereon as well as the improvements placed upon leased land, may be sold by the county court in such manner and upon such terms and conditions as the county court at a regular term thereof may determine. The proceeds arising from such sale shall be returned to the general fund of the county. 567.255 [Amended by 1969 c.594 §61; repealed by 2015 c.767 §4] 567.260 [Repealed by 2015 c.767 §4] 567.265 [Repealed by 2015 c.767 §4] 567.270 [Repealed by 2015 c.767 §4] 567.275 [Repealed by 2015 c.767 §4] 567.280 [Repealed by 1955 c.770 §1] 567.285 [Repealed by 1955 c.770 §1] 567.305 [Repealed by 1955 c.770 §1] 567.310 [Repealed by 1955 c.770 §1] 567.315 [Repealed by 1955 c.770 §1] 567.320 [Repealed by 1955 c.770 §1]
