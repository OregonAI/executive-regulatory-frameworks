# Distance learning course outlines (ORS 354.435)

## At a glance

ORS 354.435 — Distance learning course outlines. Chapter 354 (Chapter 354), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

354.435 Distance learning course outlines. Distance learning course outlines shall be submitted by the course providers to the Department of Education for review and evaluation. [1991 c.710 §5]
