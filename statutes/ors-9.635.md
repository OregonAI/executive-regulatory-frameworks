# Sources of client security fund (ORS 9.635)

## At a glance

ORS 9.635 — Sources of client security fund. Chapter 9 (Oregon State Bar; Attorneys; Law Libraries), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

9.635 Sources of client security fund. A client security fund may include:

(1) Transfers by the board of governors from other funds of the state bar;

(2) Voluntary contributions and payments by licensees under ORS 9.645;

(3) Claims recovered under ORS 9.665; and

(4) Income from investments of the fund. [1967 c.546 §4; 2025 c.32 §40] 9.640 [Repealed by 1953 c.609 §2]
