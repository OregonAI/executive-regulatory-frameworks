# Existing remedies not affected (ORS 823.107)

## At a glance

ORS 823.107 — Existing remedies not affected. Chapter 823 (Chapter 823), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

823.107 Existing remedies not affected. Nothing in ORS 823.101 to 823.107 shall deprive any holder of a receipt or bill of lading of any remedy or right of action which the holder has under existing law. [Formerly 771.050]
