# Permit for gate construction on public road (ORS 368.056)

## At a glance

ORS 368.056 — Permit for gate construction on public road. Chapter 368 (County Roads), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

368.056 Permit for gate construction on public road. A county governing body may issue a permit to a person to allow construction of a gate or stock guard on a public road under the jurisdiction of the county governing body. The county governing body may impose any conditions or specifications on the permit it determines advisable to preserve the purposes of the public road. Conditions on a permit under this section may include a requirement that the person issued the permit shall bear all costs of construction and maintenance of the gate or stock guard. [1981 c.153 §33] 368.060 [Repealed by 1981 c.153 §79]
