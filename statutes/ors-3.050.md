# Circuit judges to be licensees of bar (ORS 3.050)

## At a glance

ORS 3.050 — Circuit judges to be licensees of bar. Chapter 3 (Circuit Courts Generally), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

3.050 Circuit judges to be licensees of bar. No person is eligible to the office of judge of the circuit court unless the person is a licensee of the Oregon State Bar. [Amended by 1961 c.724 §3; 2025 c.32 §65]
