## At a glance

ORS 558.235 — Treatment of forestlands as benefited property. Chapter 558 (Weather Modification), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

558.235 Treatment of forestlands as benefited property. Forestlands within a forest protection district as defined in ORS chapter 477, shall not be considered benefited property and shall not be included in a weather modification district unless the owner of the forestlands petitions the county court having jurisdiction of the formation proceedings to have the lands of the owner included.
