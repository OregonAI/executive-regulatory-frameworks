# Conflicting claims; interpleader (ORS 77.6030)

## At a glance

ORS 77.6030 — Conflicting claims; interpleader. Chapter 77 (Warehouse Receipts, Bills of Lading and Other Documents of Title), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

77.6030 Conflicting claims; interpleader. If more than one person claims title to or possession of the goods, the bailee is excused from delivery until the bailee has a reasonable time to ascertain the validity of the adverse claims or to commence an action for interpleader. The bailee may assert an interpleader either in defending an action for nondelivery of the goods or by original action.
