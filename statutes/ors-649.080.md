# Unauthorized display of name or insignia of motor vehicle association (ORS 649.080)

## At a glance

ORS 649.080 — Unauthorized display of name or insignia of motor vehicle association. Chapter 649 (Insignia and Names of Organizations), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

649.080 Unauthorized display of name or insignia of motor vehicle association. A person may not display the name or insignia of a motor vehicle club or similar organization on a motor vehicle if the motor vehicle club or organization has registered the name or insignia under ORS 649.020, unless the constitution, bylaws, rules or regulations of the motor vehicle club or organization permit the person to display the name or insignia. [Amended by 2003 c.45 §2; 2015 c.277 §8] 649.090 [1991 c.132 §28; 1999 c.652 §19; 2003 c.45 §3; repealed by 2015 c.277 §15]
