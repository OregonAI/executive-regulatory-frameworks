# Prohibition against applying to enjoin director from continuing possession (ORS 711.450)

## At a glance

ORS 711.450 — Prohibition against applying to enjoin director from continuing possession. Chapter 711 (Merger; Conversion; Share Exchange; Acquisition; Liquidation; Insolvency), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

711.450 Prohibition against applying to enjoin director from continuing possession. An institution may not apply to the supervising court for an order requiring the Director of the Department of Consumer and Business Services to show cause why the director should not be enjoined from continuing possession pursuant to ORS 711.419. [Amended by 1973 c.797 §258; 1975 c.544 §37; 1985 c.786 §43] 711.455 [Repealed by 1973 c.797 §428] 711.460 [Repealed by 1973 c.797 §428]
