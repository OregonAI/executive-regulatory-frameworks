# County governing body; legislative authority; quorum (ORS 203.111)

## At a glance

ORS 203.111 — County governing body; legislative authority; quorum. Chapter 203 (County Governing Bodies; County Home Rule), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

203.111 County governing body; legislative authority; quorum. Unless otherwise provided by county charter, a county court shall be the governing body and shall exercise general legislative authority over all matters of county concern and shall consist of the county judge and two county commissioners and a majority of those persons shall constitute a quorum. [1981 c.140 §3 (enacted in lieu of 203.110)] 203.113 [1981 c.529 §2; 1983 c.827 §46; repealed by 1987 c.793 §2]
