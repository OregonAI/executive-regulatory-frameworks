# Short titles (ORS 71.1010)

## At a glance

ORS 71.1010 — Short titles. Chapter 71 (General Provisions for Uniform Commercial Code), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

71.1010 Short titles.

(1) This chapter and ORS chapters 72, 72A, 73, 74, 74A, 75, 77, 78 and 79A and ORS 80.1010 to 80.1070 may be cited as the Uniform Commercial Code.

(2) This chapter may be cited as Uniform Commercial Code–General Provisions. [1961 c.726 §71.1010; 1989 c.676 §79; 1991 c.83 §2; 1991 c.442 §§39,40; 2001 c.445 §129; 2009 c.181 §1; 2025 c.33 §1]
