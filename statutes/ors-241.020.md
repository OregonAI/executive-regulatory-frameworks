# Counties subject to ORS 241.016 to (ORS 241.020)

## At a glance

ORS 241.020 — Counties subject to ORS 241.016 to. Chapter 241 (Civil Service for County Employees), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

241.020 Counties subject to ORS 241.016 to 241.990. Except as provided in ORS 241.002 the provisions of ORS 241.016 to 241.990 apply to all counties of this state having a population of 500,000 persons or more. [Formerly 241.005; 1991 c.656 §1]
