# Limit on eligible individuals in retirement plan (ORS 523.710)

## At a glance

ORS 523.710 — Limit on eligible individuals in retirement plan. Chapter 523 (Geothermal Heating Districts), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

523.710 Limit on eligible individuals in retirement plan. Nothing in this chapter authorizes the district to budget, provide for payments or collect contributions to fund retirement benefits for an individual who is not in the employment of the district at the time of the creation of a membership status under a retirement plan. [1975 c.782 §46]
