# Definition for ORS 146.710 to (ORS 146.710)

## At a glance

ORS 146.710 — Definition for ORS 146.710 to. Chapter 146 (Investigations of Deaths, Injuries and Missing Persons), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

146.710 Definition for ORS 146.710 to 146.780. As used in ORS 146.710 to 146.780, “injury” means:

(1) A physical injury caused by a knife, gun, pistol or other dangerous or deadly weapon; or

(2) A serious physical injury. [1963 c.621 §1; 1965 c.472 §1; 1967 c.545 §1; 1971 c.451 §9; 2007 c.294 §1] 146.720 [1963 c.621 §§3,4; 1965 c.221 §17; repealed by 1965 c.472 §9]
