# Severability (ORS 67.805)

## At a glance

ORS 67.805 — Severability. Chapter 67 (Partnerships; Limited Liability Partnerships), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

67.805 Severability. If any provision of this chapter or its application to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the invalidity does not affect other provisions or applications of this chapter that can be given effect without the invalid provision or application, and to this end the provisions of this chapter are severable. [1997 c.775 §82]
