# Appeal to circuit court from assessment of damages (ORS 549.170)

## At a glance

ORS 549.170 — Appeal to circuit court from assessment of damages. Chapter 549 (Drainage and Flood Control Generally), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

549.170 Appeal to circuit court from assessment of damages. Any person aggrieved by the assessment of damages may appeal within 20 days to the circuit court.
