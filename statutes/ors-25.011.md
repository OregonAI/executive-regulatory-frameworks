# “Address” defined (ORS 25.011)

## At a glance

ORS 25.011 — “Address” defined. Chapter 25 (Child Support Services), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

25.011 “Address” defined. As used in ORS chapters 25, 106, 107, 108, 109 and 110, when a person is required to provide an address, “address” means a residence, mailing or contact address in the same state as the person’s home. [1993 c.448 §1; 1995 c.608 §25; 2019 c.13 §6; 2025 c.99 §52] Note: 25.011 was enacted into law by the Legislative Assembly but was not added to or made a part of ORS chapter 25 or any series therein by legislative action. See Preface to Oregon Revised Statutes for further explanation.
