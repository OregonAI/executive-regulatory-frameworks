# Effect of ORS 18.860 to 18.993 on court’s ability to direct seizure (ORS 18.993)

## At a glance

ORS 18.993 — Effect of ORS 18.860 to 18.993 on court’s ability to direct seizure. Chapter 18 (Judgments), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

18.993 Effect of ORS 18.860 to 18.993 on court’s ability to direct seizure. Nothing in ORS 18.860 to 18.993 affects the ability of a court to direct seizure of property under ORS 18.268 (2). [2005 c.542 §54]
