# Notice (ORS 36.605)

## At a glance

ORS 36.605 — Notice. Chapter 36 (Mediation and Arbitration), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

36.605 Notice.

(1) Except as otherwise provided in ORS 36.600 to 36.740, a person gives notice to another person by taking action that is reasonably necessary to inform the other person in ordinary course, whether or not the other person acquires knowledge of the notice.

(2) A person has notice if the person has knowledge of the notice or has received notice.

(3) A person receives notice when it comes to the person’s attention or the notice is delivered at the person’s place of residence or place of business, or at another location held out by the person as a place of delivery of such communications. [2003 c.598 §2] Note: See note under 36.600.
