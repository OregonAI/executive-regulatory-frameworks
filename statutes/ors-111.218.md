# Proof of service; proof of publication (ORS 111.218)

## At a glance

ORS 111.218 — Proof of service; proof of publication. Chapter 111 (General Provisions), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

111.218 Proof of service; proof of publication.

(1) Proof of service must be made for all documents for which statutes require giving notice. If notice of a document is not required, no proof of service is required.

(2) Proof of service of a document may be on the document filed in the form of a certificate of service that specifies the date of mailing or other service or may be by a separate filing.

(3) Proof of publication required to be filed in probate court must be made in the form required by ORCP 7 F. [2007 c.284 §7; 2021 c.282 §11] 111.220 [Repealed by 1957 c.411 §7]
