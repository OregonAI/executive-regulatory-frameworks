# Rule 602. Lack of personal knowledge (ORS 40.315)

## At a glance

ORS 40.315 — Rule 602. Lack of personal knowledge. Chapter 40 (Evidence Code), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

40.315 Rule 602. Lack of personal knowledge. Subject to the provisions of ORS 40.415, a witness may not testify to a matter unless evidence is introduced sufficient to support a finding that the witness has personal knowledge of the matter. Evidence to prove personal knowledge may, but need not, consist of the testimony of the witness. [1981 c.892 §44]
