# Election on formation (ORS 552.133)

## At a glance

ORS 552.133 — Election on formation. Chapter 552 (Water Improvement Districts), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

552.133 Election on formation. If an election is called on the question of formation, it shall be held on a date specified in ORS 255.345. An order creating a district shall include the maximum rate of any ad valorem tax levy for the district. [1969 c.606 §7; 1971 c.727 §165; 1983 c.350 §299] 552.135 [Repealed by 1969 c.168 §1]
