# Forfeiture for refusing copy of order or process (ORS 34.730)

## At a glance

ORS 34.730 — Forfeiture for refusing copy of order or process. Chapter 34 (Writs), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

34.730 Forfeiture for refusing copy of order or process. Any officer or other person refusing to deliver a copy of any order, warrant, process or other authority by which the officer or person detains any person, to anyone who demands a copy, and tenders the fees therefor, shall forfeit $200 to the person so detained. AMENDMENT OF PETITION OR ACTION TO SEEK PROPER REMEDY
