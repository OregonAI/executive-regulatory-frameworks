# Special assessment for street lighting, street maintenance and street cleaning; approval by electors (ORS 223.851)

## At a glance

ORS 223.851 — Special assessment for street lighting, street maintenance and street cleaning; approval by electors. Chapter 223 (Local Improvements and Works Generally), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

223.851 Special assessment for street lighting, street maintenance and street cleaning; approval by electors. When authorized at any properly called election, the governing body of a city may assess, levy and collect annual assessments upon any real property within its boundaries for street lighting, street maintenance and street cleaning services which benefit the property.
