# Penalties (ORS 608.990)

## At a glance

ORS 608.990 — Penalties. Chapter 608 (Fences to Prevent Damage by or to Animals), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

608.990 Penalties.

(1) Violation of ORS 608.330 is a Class C misdemeanor.

(2) Violation of ORS 608.380 is a Class A violation.

(3) Violation of ORS 608.510 is a Class D violation. [Amended by 1957 c.476 §3; 1999 c.1051 §207; 2011 c.597 §247] _______________
