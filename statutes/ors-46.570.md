# Fees (ORS 46.570)

## At a glance

ORS 46.570 — Fees. Chapter 46 (Small Claims Department of Circuit Court), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

46.570 Fees. The small claims department of a circuit court shall collect the following filing fees from the plaintiff when a claim is filed in the court, and from the defendant when the defendant demands a hearing:

(1) $57, when the amount claimed is $2,500 or less; and

(2) $102, when the amount is more than $2,500. [Formerly 46.221; 2003 c.737 §§44,45a,45c; 2005 c.702 §§49,50,51; 2007 c.129 §20; 2007 c.860 §7; 2011 c.595 §46; 2013 c.685 §§36,36a; 2014 c.76 §10; 2017 c.663 §8; 2019 c.605 §9]
