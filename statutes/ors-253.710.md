# Alteration of application prohibited; exceptions (ORS 253.710)

## At a glance

ORS 253.710 — Alteration of application prohibited; exceptions. Chapter 253 (Absent Electors), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

253.710 Alteration of application prohibited; exceptions. A person may not alter any information supplied on an application for a ballot for an absent elector except:

(1) An elections officer in the performance of official duties.

(2) The applicant. [1985 c.808 §33; 2013 c.520 §17] 253.990 [Subsection

(2) enacted as 1955 c.332 §18; repealed by 1979 c.190 §431] 253.995 [1985 c.808 §34; repealed by 1999 c.318 §55] _______________
