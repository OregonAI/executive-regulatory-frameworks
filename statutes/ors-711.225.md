# Report and transfer of unclaimed deposits (ORS 711.225)

## At a glance

ORS 711.225 — Report and transfer of unclaimed deposits. Chapter 711 (Merger; Conversion; Share Exchange; Acquisition; Liquidation; Insolvency), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

711.225 Report and transfer of unclaimed deposits. Six months after the mailing of the written notice described in ORS 711.220 (3), the Oregon stock bank shall deliver a report and all deposits that remain unclaimed to the State Treasurer as unclaimed property under ORS 98.352 and deliver a copy of the report filed with the State Treasurer to the Director of the Department of Consumer and Business Services. [Amended by 1957 c.670 §33; 1959 c.138 §1; 1973 c.797 §241; 1993 c.694 §36; 1997 c.631 §240; 2019 c.678 §71; 2021 c.424 §21]
