# Election to have ORS 94.645, 94.655 and 94.675 apply (ORS 94.677)

## At a glance

ORS 94.677 — Election to have ORS 94.645, 94.655 and 94.675 apply. Chapter 94 (Real Property Development), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

94.677 Election to have ORS 94.645, 94.655 and 94.675 apply. Unless contrary to the covenants, conditions or restrictions of a recorded declaration or other similar instrument, or the bylaws of the association adopted in accordance with documents governing the association, the homeowners association board of directors of a subdivision described in ORS 94.673

(1) may elect to be governed by ORS 94.645, 94.655 and 94.675, without further action by the association. [1983 c.206 §7]
