# Hearing (ORS 111.241)

## At a glance

ORS 111.241 — Hearing. Chapter 111 (General Provisions), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

111.241 Hearing.

(1) The court may require that a hearing be held on any petition or motion in a probate proceeding.

(2) A hearing must be held on a petition or a motion in a probate proceeding if an objection to the petition or motion is filed and the objection is not withdrawn before the hearing. [2021 c.282 §3]
