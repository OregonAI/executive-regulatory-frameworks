# Relation to Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act (ORS 93.979)

## At a glance

ORS 93.979 — Relation to Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act. Chapter 93 (Conveyancing and Recording), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

93.979 Relation to Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act. ORS 93.948 to 93.979 modify, limit and supersede the federal Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act, 15 U.S.C. 7001, et seq., but do not modify, limit or supersede 15 U.S.C. 7001(c), or authorize electronic delivery of a notice described in 15 U.S.C. 7003(b). [2011 c.212 §18] Note: See note under 93.948.
