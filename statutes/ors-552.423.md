# Advertising for bids on construction contract (ORS 552.423)

## At a glance

ORS 552.423 — Advertising for bids on construction contract. Chapter 552 (Water Improvement Districts), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

552.423 Advertising for bids on construction contract. After the approval of the engineering plan as provided by ORS 552.418 and before beginning the construction of any works, the district board shall give notice, by publication and otherwise, as it may consider advisable, calling for bids for the construction of such works, or any portion thereof. [1969 c.606 §36; 1989 c.182 §48] 552.425 [Repealed by 1969 c.168 §1]
