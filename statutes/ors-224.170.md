# Certain laws and charter provisions applicable to property beyond city limits (ORS 224.170)

## At a glance

ORS 224.170 — Certain laws and charter provisions applicable to property beyond city limits. Chapter 224 (City Sewers and Sanitation), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

224.170 Certain laws and charter provisions applicable to property beyond city limits. The provisions of the city charter applicable to curative measures or reassessments shall be applicable to property without the city limits as well as to the property within the city. The owners of property beyond the city limits shall have like rights of objection, remonstrances, hearing and other remedies as the owners of property within the city or town. The right of review of any reassessment by the circuit court as provided by ORS 224.065 relative to an original assessment, and the provisions of ORS 224.065, 224.080 and 224.100 relative to review of an original assessment shall be applicable to review of a reassessment.
