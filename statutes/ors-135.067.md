# Effect of failure to provide true name of defendant on certain types of release PRELIMINARY HEARING (Generally) (ORS 135.067)

## At a glance

ORS 135.067 — Effect of failure to provide true name of defendant on certain types of release PRELIMINARY HEARING (Generally). Chapter 135 (Arraignment and Pretrial Provisions), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

135.067 Effect of failure to provide true name of defendant on certain types of release. If a defendant, on or after August 12, 2003, fails to provide the defendant’s true name under ORS 135.060 or 135.065 and is on personal recognizance, conditional release or security release having deposited less than the full security amount set by the magistrate, the magistrate who released the defendant, upon a motion filed by the district attorney and supported by probable cause, shall cause the defendant to be brought before the magistrate. The magistrate shall conduct a hearing to establish release according to ORS 135.245. [2003 c.645 §7] Note: 135.067 was enacted into law by the Legislative Assembly but was not added to or made a part of ORS chapter 135 or any series therein by legislative action. See Preface to Oregon Revised Statutes for further explanation. PRELIMINARY HEARING (Generally)
