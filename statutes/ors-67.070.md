# General powers of partnership (ORS 67.070)

## At a glance

ORS 67.070 — General powers of partnership. Chapter 67 (Partnerships; Limited Liability Partnerships), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

67.070 General powers of partnership. Unless restricted by applicable law, a partnership has the same powers as an individual to do all things necessary or convenient to carry on its business and affairs. [1997 c.775 §10] (Relations of Partners to Persons Dealing With Partnership)
