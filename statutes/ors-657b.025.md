# Coordination of leave (ORS 657B.025)

## At a glance

ORS 657B.025 — Coordination of leave. Chapter 657B (Family and Medical Leave Insurance), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

657B.025 Coordination of leave. Any family leave or medical leave taken under this chapter must be taken concurrently with any leave taken by an eligible employee under the federal Family and Medical Leave Act of 1993 (P.L. 103-3) for the same purposes. [2019 c.700 §5; 2024 c.20 §14]
