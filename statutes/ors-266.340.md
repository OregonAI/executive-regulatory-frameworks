# Oath of office of board members (ORS 266.340)

## At a glance

ORS 266.340 — Oath of office of board members. Chapter 266 (Park and Recreation Districts), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

266.340 Oath of office of board members. A district board member when elected shall take the oath of office within 10 days after receiving the certificate of election. [Amended by 1969 c.345 §6; 1969 c.668 §§14,45] 266.350 [Repealed by 1971 c.403 §18] 266.360 [Amended by 1957 c.57 §5; 1969 c.668 §15; repealed by 1969 c.668 §46 and by 1969 c.669 §21]
