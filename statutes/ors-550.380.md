# Election laws applicable (ORS 550.380)

## At a glance

ORS 550.380 — Election laws applicable. Chapter 550 (Urban Flood Safety and Water Quality District), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

550.380 Election laws applicable.

(1) ORS chapter 255 governs the nomination and election of the board of directors of the urban flood safety and water quality district and the conduct of all district elections.

(2) The electors may exercise the powers of the initiative and referendum regarding a district measure in accordance with ORS 255.135 to 255.205. [2019 c.621 §24]
