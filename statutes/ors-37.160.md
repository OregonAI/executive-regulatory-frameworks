# Mailing and special notice lists to be maintained by receiver (ORS 37.160)

## At a glance

## Full text

37.160 Mailing and special notice lists to be maintained by receiver.

(1) A receiver shall maintain a master mailing list of the names and addresses of all parties to the receivership, all known creditors of the owner and interested persons who have filed notices of appearance in the receivership. The receiver shall make a copy of the current master mailing list available to any person on the list upon the person’s request. (2)(a) A receiver shall maintain a special notice list of the names and addresses of all parties to the receivership and any other person who requests to be placed on the list. The receiver shall make a copy of the current special notice list available to any person on the list upon the person’s request. (b) Any person on the special notice list may notify the receiver of the person’s preferred means of receiving notices and other communications. If the receiver is so notified, the receiver shall add the information to the special notice list. [2017 c.358 §16]
