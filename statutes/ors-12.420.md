# Purpose (ORS 12.420)

## At a glance

ORS 12.420 — Purpose. Chapter 12 (Limitations of Actions and Suits), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

12.420 Purpose. ORS 12.410 to 12.480 shall be applied and construed to effectuate its general purpose to make uniform the law with respect to the subject of ORS 12.410 to 12.480 among states enacting it. [1987 c.536 §6] Note: See note under 12.410.
