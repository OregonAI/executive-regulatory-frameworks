# Fees; apportionment between parties (ORS 28.220)

## At a glance

ORS 28.220 — Fees; apportionment between parties. Chapter 28 (Declaratory Judgments; Certification of Questions of Law), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

28.220 Fees; apportionment between parties. Fees and costs shall be the same as in civil appeals docketed before the Supreme Court and shall be equally divided between the parties unless otherwise ordered by the certifying court in its order of certification. [1983 c.103 §5] Note: See note under 28.200.
