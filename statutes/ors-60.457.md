# Effect of amendment (ORS 60.457)

## At a glance

ORS 60.457 — Effect of amendment. Chapter 60 (Private Corporations), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

60.457 Effect of amendment. An amendment to articles of incorporation does not affect a cause of action existing against or in favor of the corporation, a proceeding to which the corporation is a party or the existing rights of persons other than shareholders of the corporation. An amendment changing a corporation’s name does not abate a proceeding brought by or against the corporation in its former name. [1987 c.52 §111] (Amendment of Bylaws)
