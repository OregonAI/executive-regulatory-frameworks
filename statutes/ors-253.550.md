# Applications made under federal statutes (ORS 253.550)

## At a glance

ORS 253.550 — Applications made under federal statutes. Chapter 253 (Absent Electors), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

253.550 Applications made under federal statutes. Whenever provision is made for absentee voting by a statute of the United States, including the Uniformed and Overseas Citizens Absentee Voting Act, 42 U.S.C. 1973ff (Public Law 99-410), an application for a ballot made under that law may be given the same effect as an application for a ballot made under ORS 253.500 to 253.640.
