# Oregon Military Museum established at Camp Withycombe (ORS 396.555)

## At a glance

ORS 396.555 — Oregon Military Museum established at Camp Withycombe. Chapter 396 (Militia Generally), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

396.555 Oregon Military Museum established at Camp Withycombe.

(1) The Oregon Military Museum is established at Camp Withycombe in Clackamas County. The Oregon Military Department shall establish an official repository in the museum for military weapons, documents and artifacts relating to the military history of the citizens of Oregon, whether service is in the Oregon National Guard or the Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Coast Guard or Space Force of the United States.

(2) The department may enter into agreements with the contributors of such artifacts as it considers necessary. [1975 c.235 §2; 1989 c.360 §7; 2001 c.656 §1; 2025 c.453 §16]
