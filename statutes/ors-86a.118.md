# Action against applicant or licensee for act or omission of associate; exception (ORS 86A.118)

## At a glance

ORS 86A.118 — Action against applicant or licensee for act or omission of associate; exception. Chapter 86A (Mortgage Lending), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

86A.118 Action against applicant or licensee for act or omission of associate; exception. The Director of the Department of Consumer and Business Services may enter an order against the applicant or licensee under ORS 86A.115 if any partner, officer or director of a mortgage banker or mortgage broker, any person occupying a similar status or performing similar functions, or any person directly or indirectly controlling the mortgage banker or mortgage broker has been guilty of any act or omission which would be cause for denying, suspending or revoking the license of an individual mortgage banker or mortgage broker. This section shall not apply to ORS 86A.115 (1). [Formerly 59.870]
