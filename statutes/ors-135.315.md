# Types of pleading (ORS 135.315)

## At a glance

ORS 135.315 — Types of pleading. Chapter 135 (Arraignment and Pretrial Provisions), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

135.315 Types of pleading. The only pleadings on the part of the defendant are the demurrer and plea. [Formerly 135.430] 135.320 [Amended by 1961 c.696 §2; 1967 c.475 §2; 1973 c.836 §134; renumbered 135.045]
