# Use of Oregon products (ORS 276.106)

## At a glance

ORS 276.106 — Use of Oregon products. Chapter 276 (Public Facilities), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

276.106 Use of Oregon products. To the degree practicable, the Oregon Department of Administrative Services shall use or ensure the use of Oregon products in constructing, remodeling, equipping, furnishing and decorating the state executive residence. [1967 c.615 §3; 1987 c.702 §2; 2003 c.796 §§13,14; 2005 c.217 §§20,21] 276.108 [1967 c.615 §4; 1977 c.598 §29; 1987 c.702 §3; repealed by 1993 c.742 §44]
