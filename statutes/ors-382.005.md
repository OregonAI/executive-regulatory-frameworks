382.005 “County court” defined. As used in this chapter, unless the context requires otherwise, “county court” includes board of county commissioners. 382.105 [Repealed by 2007 c.531 §19] 382.110 [Repealed by 2007 c.531 §19] 382.115 [Repealed by 2007 c.531 §19] 382.120 [Repealed by 2007 c.531 §19] 382.125 [Amended by 1963 c.602 §1; 1981 c.153 §75; repealed by 2007 c.531 §19]
