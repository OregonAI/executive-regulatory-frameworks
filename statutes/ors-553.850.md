# Dissolution upon majority vote (ORS 553.850)

## At a glance

ORS 553.850 — Dissolution upon majority vote. Chapter 553 (Water Control Districts), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

553.850 Dissolution upon majority vote. Any water control district may be dissolved whenever a majority vote of the electors of the district voting at an election for such purpose favors the dissolution.
