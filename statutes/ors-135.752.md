# When trial not commenced within time limit (ORS 135.752)

## At a glance

ORS 135.752 — When trial not commenced within time limit. Chapter 135 (Arraignment and Pretrial Provisions), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

135.752 When trial not commenced within time limit. If a trial is not commenced as required by ORS 135.746, the court shall order the charging instrument to be dismissed without prejudice unless the court finds on the record substantial and compelling reasons to allow the proceeding to continue. [2014 c.73 §3] Note: See note under 135.746. DISMISSAL OF ACTION
