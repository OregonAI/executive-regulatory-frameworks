# Prohibition of identification of jurors by name in open proceedings (ORS 10.097)

## At a glance

ORS 10.097 — Prohibition of identification of jurors by name in open proceedings. Chapter 10 (Juries), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

10.097 Prohibition of identification of jurors by name in open proceedings.

(1) A juror may not be identified by name in any court proceeding open to the public.

(2) Notwithstanding subsection

(1) of this section, a court shall ensure that the names of jurors are available to the parties to a proceeding unless the court determines that there is good cause to order otherwise. [2021 c.295 §2]
