# Rules (ORS 702.063)

## At a glance

ORS 702.063 — Rules. Chapter 702 (Student Athletes), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

702.063 Rules. The State Board of Education may adopt any rules necessary to carry out the provisions of ORS 702.005 to 702.065, 702.991 and 702.994. [1999 c.1079 §4a; 2005 c.525 §18]
