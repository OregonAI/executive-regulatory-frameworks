# Construction not to interfere with oyster production (ORS 780.060)

## At a glance

ORS 780.060 — Construction not to interfere with oyster production. Chapter 780 (Improvement and Use of Navigable Streams), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

780.060 Construction not to interfere with oyster production. Nothing in this chapter authorizes the construction of a wharf, dock, pier, moorage or similar structure at any place where its construction and operation will substantially impair or interfere with the cultivation and taking of oysters subject to the provisions of ORS 622.210 to 622.300 and 622.320. [1969 c.675 §20; 1973 c.328 §4] _______________
