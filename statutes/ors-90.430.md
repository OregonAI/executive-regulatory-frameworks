# Claims for possession, rent, damages after termination of rental agreement (ORS 90.430)

## At a glance

ORS 90.430 — Claims for possession, rent, damages after termination of rental agreement. Chapter 90 (Residential Landlord and Tenant), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

90.430 Claims for possession, rent, damages after termination of rental agreement. If the rental agreement is terminated, the landlord may have a claim for possession and for rent and a separate claim for actual damages for breach of the rental agreement. [Formerly 91.845]
