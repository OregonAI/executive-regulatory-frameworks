# Determination of amount of grain; warehouse certificate as evidence; owner liable for actual amount (ORS 587.160)

## At a glance

ORS 587.160 — Determination of amount of grain; warehouse certificate as evidence; owner liable for actual amount. Chapter 587 (Storage of Grain as Basis of Farm Credit), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

587.160 Determination of amount of grain; warehouse certificate as evidence; owner liable for actual amount. Whenever the amount of grain certified to on the warehouse certificate has been computed by cubic measurements or by number of bags, the amount shall be deemed to be prima facie the amount of the grain, but the actual amount shall be determined by the actual weight thereof. The owner, however, is responsible and liable to the holder of the certificate, for the delivery of the amount of grain indicated on the certificate by actual weight, or the value of any shortage thereof. 587.170 [Repealed by 1959 c.201 §11] 587.180 [Repealed by 1959 c.201 §11]
