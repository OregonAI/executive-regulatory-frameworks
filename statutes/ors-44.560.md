# Application to subpoenas for depositions (ORS 44.560)

## At a glance

ORS 44.560 — Application to subpoenas for depositions. Chapter 44 (Witnesses), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

44.560 Application to subpoenas for depositions. ORS 44.552, 44.554 and 44.558 apply to subpoenas issued for the taking of depositions of police officers and employees of the Department of State Police. [1991 c.550 §6; 2011 c.547 §26] Note: See note under 44.550.
