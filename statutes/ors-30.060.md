# Appeal from order of distribution or apportionment (ORS 30.060)

## At a glance

ORS 30.060 — Appeal from order of distribution or apportionment. Chapter 30 (Actions and Suits in Particular Cases), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

30.060 Appeal from order of distribution or apportionment. In the case of an order of distribution under ORS 30.030

(5) or an order of apportionment made under either ORS 30.040 or 30.050, any individual who in the probate court or trial court claims to be a beneficiary may appeal therefrom, or from any part thereof, to the Court of Appeals, within the time, in the manner and with like effect as though such order was a judgment of the circuit court. [Amended by 1973 c.718 §6]
