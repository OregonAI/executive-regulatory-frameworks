# Uniformity of application and construction (ORS 119.076)

## At a glance

ORS 119.076 — Uniformity of application and construction. Chapter 119 (Revised Uniform Fiduciary Access to Digital Assets Act), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

119.076 Uniformity of application and construction. In applying and construing this uniform act, consideration must be given to the need to promote uniformity of the law with respect to its subject matter among the enacting states. [2016 c.19 §17]
