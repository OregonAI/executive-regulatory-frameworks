# Judicial enforcement of preaward ruling by arbitrator (ORS 36.680)

## At a glance

ORS 36.680 — Judicial enforcement of preaward ruling by arbitrator. Chapter 36 (Mediation and Arbitration), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

36.680 Judicial enforcement of preaward ruling by arbitrator. If an arbitrator makes a preaward ruling in favor of a party to the arbitration proceeding, the party may request the arbitrator to incorporate the ruling into an award under ORS 36.685. A prevailing party may make a petition to the court for an expedited order to confirm the award under ORS 36.700, in which case the court shall summarily decide the petition. The court shall issue an order to confirm the award unless the court vacates, modifies, or corrects the award under ORS 36.705 or 36.710. [2003 c.598 §18] Note: See note under 36.600.
