# Mandatory indemnification (ORS 60.394)

## At a glance

ORS 60.394 — Mandatory indemnification. Chapter 60 (Private Corporations), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

60.394 Mandatory indemnification. Unless limited by its articles of incorporation, a corporation shall indemnify a director who was wholly successful, on the merits or otherwise, in the defense of any proceeding to which the director was a party because of being a director of the corporation against reasonable expenses incurred by the director in connection with the proceeding. [1987 c.52 §96]
