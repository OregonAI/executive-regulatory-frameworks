# Memorandum decisions (ORS 19.435)

## At a glance

ORS 19.435 — Memorandum decisions. Chapter 19 (Appeals), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

19.435 Memorandum decisions. The Supreme Court or the Court of Appeals may decide cases before it by means of memorandum decisions and shall prepare full opinions only in such cases as it deems proper. [Formerly 19.180] (Attorney Fees and Penalties)
