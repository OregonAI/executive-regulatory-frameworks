# Report to legislature on military cultural barriers ARMORIES, (ORS 396.375)

## At a glance

ORS 396.375 — Report to legislature on military cultural barriers ARMORIES,. Chapter 396 (Militia Generally), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

396.375 Report to legislature on military cultural barriers. On or before October 1 of each even-numbered year, the Oregon Military Department shall report to a legislative committee dealing with the military on the existing military cultural barriers that discourage service members from identifying their medical, emotional, psychological and other barriers to reintegration into civilian life. [2011 c.128 §1] Note: 396.375 was enacted into law by the Legislative Assembly but was not added to or made a part of ORS chapter 396 or any series therein by legislative action. See Preface to Oregon Revised Statutes for further explanation. ARMORIES, CAMPS AND OTHER PROPERTY
