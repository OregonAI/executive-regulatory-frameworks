# Jurisdiction of all appeals exclusive; exceptions (ORS 2.516)

## At a glance

ORS 2.516 — Jurisdiction of all appeals exclusive; exceptions. Chapter 2 (Supreme Court; Court of Appeals), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

2.516 Jurisdiction of all appeals exclusive; exceptions. Except where original jurisdiction is conferred on the Supreme Court by the Oregon Constitution or by statute and except as provided in ORS 19.405 and 138.255, the Court of Appeals shall have exclusive jurisdiction of all appeals. [1977 c.158 §2; 1981 c.550 §5]
