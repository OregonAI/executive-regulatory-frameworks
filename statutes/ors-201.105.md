# Land transferred to Douglas County (ORS 201.105)

## At a glance

ORS 201.105 — Land transferred to Douglas County. Chapter 201 (Boundaries of Counties), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

201.105 Land transferred to Douglas County. That portion of Coos County which constitutes the southeast quarter of the southeast quarter of section 36, township 28 south, range 9 west of the Willamette Meridian is transferred to and made a part of Douglas County. [1983 c.778 §1] Note: 201.105 was enacted into law by the Legislative Assembly but was not added to or made a part of ORS chapter 201 or any series therein by legislative action. See Preface to Oregon Revised Statutes for further explanation.
