# Public service charge pro rata apportionment (ORS 90.570)

## At a glance

ORS 90.570 — Public service charge pro rata apportionment. Chapter 90 (Residential Landlord and Tenant), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

90.570 Public service charge pro rata apportionment. A landlord, upon 60 days’ written notice to a tenant, may unilaterally amend a rental agreement to require a tenant to pay to the landlord, as part of the utility or service charge, a pro rata proportion of any new or increased public service charge billed to the landlord by a utility or service provider or a local government for a public service provided directly or indirectly to the tenant’s dwelling unit or to the facility common areas. [2019 c.625 §41]
