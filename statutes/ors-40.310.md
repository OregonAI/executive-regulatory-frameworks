# Rule 601. General rule of competency (ORS 40.310)

## At a glance

ORS 40.310 — Rule 601. General rule of competency. Chapter 40 (Evidence Code), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

40.310 Rule 601. General rule of competency. Except as provided in ORS 40.310 to 40.335, any person who, having organs of sense can perceive, and perceiving can make known the perception to others, may be a witness. [1981 c.892 §43]
