# Release of prisoner prohibited (ORS 135.770)

## At a glance

ORS 135.770 — Release of prisoner prohibited. Chapter 135 (Arraignment and Pretrial Provisions), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

135.770 Release of prisoner prohibited. No adult in the custody of a sheriff under ORS 135.767 shall be released pending a criminal proceeding under ORS 135.760 to 135.773 or any appeal therefrom. [Formerly 134.550; 2019 c.213 §19]
