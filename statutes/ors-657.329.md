# Applicability of ORS 657.321 to (ORS 657.329)

## At a glance

ORS 657.329 — Applicability of ORS 657.321 to. Chapter 657 (Unemployment Insurance), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

657.329 Applicability of ORS 657.321 to 657.329. ORS 657.321 to 657.329 shall apply to individuals meeting the requirements thereof for the week ending January 16, 1971, and any week thereafter. [1971 c.2 §6] 657.330 [1969 c.156 §2; 1971 c.82 §1; renumbered 657.337 in 1989] (Additional Benefits)
