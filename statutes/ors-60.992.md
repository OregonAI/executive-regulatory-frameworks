# Penalty for signing false document (ORS 60.992)

## At a glance

ORS 60.992 — Penalty for signing false document. Chapter 60 (Private Corporations), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

60.992 Penalty for signing false document.

(1) A person commits the crime of signing a false document for filing if the person: (a) Knows the document is false in any material respect; and (b) Signs the document with an intent that the document be delivered to the office of the Secretary of State for filing under this chapter.

(2) Signing a false document for filing is a Class A misdemeanor. [Formerly 60.990; 2013 c.158 §23]
