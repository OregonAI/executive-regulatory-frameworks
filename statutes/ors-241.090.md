# Offices and clerical help for commission (ORS 241.090)

## At a glance

ORS 241.090 — Offices and clerical help for commission. Chapter 241 (Civil Service for County Employees), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

241.090 Offices and clerical help for commission. The board of county commissioners shall provide the commission with suitable and convenient rooms and accommodations at the county courthouse, and cause the same to be furnished, heated, lighted and supplied with all office supplies and equipment necessary to carry on the business of the commission. The board shall also provide the commission with such clerical assistance as may be necessary.
