# Benefits exempt from execution (ORS 748.207)

## At a glance

ORS 748.207 — Benefits exempt from execution. Chapter 748 (Fraternal Benefit Societies), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

748.207 Benefits exempt from execution. No money or other benefit, charity, relief or aid to be paid, provided or rendered by any society, shall be liable to attachment, garnishment or other process, or to be seized, taken, appropriated or applied by any legal or equitable process or operation of law to pay any debt or liability of a member or beneficiary, or any other person who may have a right thereunder, either before or after payment by the society. [1987 c.490 §19] 748.210 [1967 c.359 §614; repealed by 1987 c.490 §58]
