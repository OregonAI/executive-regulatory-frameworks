# Taking salmon for commercial purposes in coastal streams prohibited (ORS 511.060)

## At a glance

ORS 511.060 — Taking salmon for commercial purposes in coastal streams prohibited. Chapter 511 (Local and Special Regulations), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

511.060 Taking salmon for commercial purposes in coastal streams prohibited. It is unlawful to take salmon for commercial purposes from all waters inland from the mouth of all streams or tributaries thereof that empty into the Pacific Ocean south of the mouth of the Columbia River, except as provided in ORS 511.070. [1965 c.570 §143]
