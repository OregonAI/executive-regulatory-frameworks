# Deadlines for actions required for exemption (Single-Unit Housing) (ORS 307.637)

## At a glance

ORS 307.637 — Deadlines for actions required for exemption (Single-Unit Housing). Chapter 307 (Property Subject to Taxation; Exemptions), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

307.637 Deadlines for actions required for exemption. An exemption for multiple-unit housing may not be granted under ORS 307.600 to 307.637 unless:

(1) In the case of multiple-unit housing described in ORS 307.603 (5)(a), the application for exemption is made to the city or county on or before January 1, 2032.

(2) In the case of multiple-unit housing described in ORS 307.603 (5)(b), the construction, addition or conversion is completed on or before January 1, 2032.
