# Receipt of rent without obligation to maintain premises prohibited (ORS 90.250)

## At a glance

ORS 90.250 — Receipt of rent without obligation to maintain premises prohibited. Chapter 90 (Residential Landlord and Tenant), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

90.250 Receipt of rent without obligation to maintain premises prohibited. A rental agreement, assignment, conveyance, trust deed or security instrument may not permit the receipt of rent free of the obligation to comply with ORS 90.320

(1) or 90.730. [Formerly 91.750; 1999 c.676 §10]
