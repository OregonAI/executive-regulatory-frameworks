# Evidence to prove a will (ORS 41.520)

## At a glance

ORS 41.520 — Evidence to prove a will. Chapter 41 (Evidence Generally), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

41.520 Evidence to prove a will. Evidence of a will shall be the written instrument itself, or secondary evidence of the contents of the will, in the cases prescribed by law. [Amended by 1969 c.591 §271]
