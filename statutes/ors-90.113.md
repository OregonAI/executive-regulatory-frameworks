# Additional exclusion from application of chapter (ORS 90.113)

## At a glance

ORS 90.113 — Additional exclusion from application of chapter. Chapter 90 (Residential Landlord and Tenant), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

90.113 Additional exclusion from application of chapter. Residence in a licensed program, facility or home described in ORS 430.306 to 430.375, 430.380, 430.381, 430.397 to 430.401, 430.405 to 430.565, 430.570, 430.590, 430.709, 443.400 to 443.455, 443.705 to 443.825 or 443.835 is not governed by this chapter. [2007 c.715 §2; 2009 c.595 §58]
