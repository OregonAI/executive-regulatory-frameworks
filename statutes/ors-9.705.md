# Definitions for ORS 9.705 to 9.757 (ORS 9.705)

## At a glance

ORS 9.705 — Definitions for ORS 9.705 to 9.757. Chapter 9 (Oregon State Bar; Attorneys; Law Libraries), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

9.705 Definitions for ORS 9.705 to 9.757. As used in ORS 9.705 to 9.757:

(1) “Affected licensee” means a licensee or former licensee of the Oregon State Bar whose law practice is placed within the jurisdiction of the court under ORS 9.720 or as to whom a petition has been filed to place such law practice within the jurisdiction of the court under ORS 9.715.

(2) “Law practice” means a practice conducted by an individual, a partnership or a professional corporation. [1979 c.252 §2; 1985 c.512 §4; 1989 c.1052 §15; 2015 c.6 §1; 2023 c.72 §20; 2025 c.32 §49]
