# Definitions for ORS 30.870 and 30.875 (ORS 30.870)

## At a glance

ORS 30.870 — Definitions for ORS 30.870 and 30.875. Chapter 30 (Actions and Suits in Particular Cases), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

30.870 Definitions for ORS 30.870 and 30.875. As used in this section and ORS 30.875:

(1) “Agricultural produce” means any plant including, but not limited to, trees, or animals, kept, grown or raised upon real property, and the products of those plants and animals.

(2) “Mercantile establishment” means any place where merchandise is displayed, held or offered for sale, either at retail or wholesale.

(3) “Merchandise” means all things movable and capable of manual delivery.

(4) “Owner” means any person who owns or operates a mercantile establishment or farm, or the agents or employees of that person. [1979 c.592 §1; 1981 c.716 §5]
