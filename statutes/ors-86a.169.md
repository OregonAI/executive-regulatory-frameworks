# Disclosure required before closing mortgage loan or mortgage banking loan (ORS 86A.169)

## At a glance

ORS 86A.169 — Disclosure required before closing mortgage loan or mortgage banking loan. Chapter 86A (Mortgage Lending), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

86A.169 Disclosure required before closing mortgage loan or mortgage banking loan. Prior to the closing of any mortgage loan or any mortgage banking loan, the mortgage banker or mortgage broker shall supply the borrower with a disclosure as required by the real estate lending provisions of 15 U.S.C. 1601 et seq. and Regulation Z, 12 C.F.R. Part 226. [Formerly 59.955]
