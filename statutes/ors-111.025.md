# Oregon Tax Court jurisdiction (ORS 111.025)

## At a glance

ORS 111.025 — Oregon Tax Court jurisdiction. Chapter 111 (General Provisions), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

111.025 Oregon Tax Court jurisdiction. For purposes of ORS chapters 111 to 116, the Oregon Tax Court is not a court having probate jurisdiction and is limited to the trial of appeals on estate tax matters.
