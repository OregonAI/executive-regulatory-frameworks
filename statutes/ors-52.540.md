# Payment of disbursements for change of venue; subpoenaed witnesses (ORS 52.540)

## At a glance

ORS 52.540 — Payment of disbursements for change of venue; subpoenaed witnesses. Chapter 52 (Civil Actions; Small Claims), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

52.540 Payment of disbursements for change of venue; subpoenaed witnesses.

(1) The disbursements of the change of venue shall be paid by the party applying therefor, and not taxed as a part of the costs in the case.

(2) It shall not be necessary to issue new subpoenas to witnesses, but the witnesses shall appear before the justice before whom the cause has been transferred without the issue of any other notice than the allowance of the motion for the change of venue.
