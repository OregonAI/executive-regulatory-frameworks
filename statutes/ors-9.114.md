# Mandatory training on duties relating to reporting child abuse and abuse of elderly persons; rules (ORS 9.114)

## At a glance

ORS 9.114 — Mandatory training on duties relating to reporting child abuse and abuse of elderly persons; rules. Chapter 9 (Oregon State Bar; Attorneys; Law Libraries), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

9.114 Mandatory training on duties relating to reporting child abuse and abuse of elderly persons; rules. The Oregon State Bar shall adopt rules to establish minimum training requirements for all active attorney licensees of the bar relating to the duties of attorneys under ORS 124.060 and 419B.010. Rules adopted under this section are subject to review and approval by the Supreme Court.
