# Voting requirements (ORS 65.244)

## At a glance

ORS 65.244 — Voting requirements. Chapter 65 (Nonprofit Corporations), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

65.244 Voting requirements.

(1) Unless this chapter, the articles of incorporation or the bylaws require a greater vote or voting by class, if a quorum is present, the affirmative vote of a majority of the votes represented and voting is the act of the members.

(2) An amendment to the articles of incorporation or bylaws to add to, change or delete the vote required for any action of the members must be approved by the members. [1989 c.1010 §63; 2019 c.174 §48]
