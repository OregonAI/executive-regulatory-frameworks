# Definitions for ORS 45.272 to 45.297 (ORS 45.272)

## At a glance

ORS 45.272 — Definitions for ORS 45.272 to 45.297. Chapter 45 (Testimony Generally), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

45.272 Definitions for ORS 45.272 to 45.297. As used in ORS 45.272 to 45.297:

(1) “Adjudicatory proceeding” means: (a) Any contested case hearing conducted under ORS chapter 183; or (b) Any hearing conducted by an agency in which the individual legal rights, duties or privileges of specific parties are determined if that determination is subject to judicial review by a circuit court or by the Court of Appeals.

(2) “Agency” has that meaning given in ORS 183.310.

(3) “Critical stage of the proceeding” has the meaning given that term in ORS 147.500.

(4) “Victim” has the meaning given that term in ORS 147.500. [1999 c.1041 §3; 2015 c.155 §1]
