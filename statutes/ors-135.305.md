# Types of answer (ORS 135.305)

## At a glance

ORS 135.305 — Types of answer. Chapter 135 (Arraignment and Pretrial Provisions), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

135.305 Types of answer. If the defendant does not require time, as provided in ORS 135.380, or if the defendant does, then on the next day or at such further day as the court may have allowed the defendant, the defendant may, in answer to the arraignment, move against the accusatory instrument or demur or plead thereto. [Formerly 135.420] 135.310 [Renumbered 135.040]
