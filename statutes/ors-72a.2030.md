# Seals inoperative (ORS 72A.2030)

## At a glance

ORS 72A.2030 — Seals inoperative. Chapter 72A (Leases), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

72A.2030 Seals inoperative. The affixing of a seal to a record evidencing a lease contract or an offer to enter into a lease contract does not render the record a sealed instrument and the law with respect to sealed instruments does not apply to the lease contract or offer. [1989 c.676 §12; 2025 c.33 §19]
