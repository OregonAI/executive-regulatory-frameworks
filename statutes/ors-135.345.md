# Legal effect of plea of no contest (ORS 135.345)

## At a glance

ORS 135.345 — Legal effect of plea of no contest. Chapter 135 (Arraignment and Pretrial Provisions), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

135.345 Legal effect of plea of no contest. A judgment following entry of a no contest plea is a conviction of the offense to which the plea is entered. [1973 c.836 §160] 135.350 [Amended by 1973 c.836 §137; renumbered 135.065]
