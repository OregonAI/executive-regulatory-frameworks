# Disposition of funds of dissolved district (ORS 371.535)

## At a glance

ORS 371.535 — Disposition of funds of dissolved district. Chapter 371 (Road Districts and Road Assessment Plans), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

371.535 Disposition of funds of dissolved district. If a district is dissolved, all funds then on hand or proceeds of assessments theretofore levied shall be held by the county court for the benefit of the roads within the area theretofore within the boundaries of the district.
