# Limited license (ORS 677.535)

## At a glance

ORS 677.535 — Limited license. Chapter 677 (Chapter 677), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

677.535 Limited license. The Oregon Medical Board may grant a limited license to a physician associate if the applicant meets the qualifications of the board, the application file is complete and no derogatory information has been submitted but board approval is pending. [1981 c.220 §8; 1983 c.486 §48a; 1989 c.830 §35; 1999 c.582 §7; 2010 c.43 §6; 2011 c.550 §6; 2024 c.73 §141]
