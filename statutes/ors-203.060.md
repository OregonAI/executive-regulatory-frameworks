# Judicial review and invalidation of ordinances (ORS 203.060)

## At a glance

ORS 203.060 — Judicial review and invalidation of ordinances. Chapter 203 (County Governing Bodies; County Home Rule), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

203.060 Judicial review and invalidation of ordinances. Ordinances adopted under ORS 203.030 to 203.075 shall be subject to judicial review and invalidation on account of unreasonableness, procedural error in adoption, or conflict with paramount state law or constitutional provision. [1973 c.282 §7]
