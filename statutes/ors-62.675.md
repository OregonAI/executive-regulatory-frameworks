# Effect of revocation of voluntary dissolution proceedings (ORS 62.675)

## At a glance

ORS 62.675 — Effect of revocation of voluntary dissolution proceedings. Chapter 62 (Cooperatives), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

62.675 Effect of revocation of voluntary dissolution proceedings. Upon the revocation of voluntary dissolution proceedings the cooperative may again carry on its business. [1957 c.716 §53; 1965 c.631 §23]
