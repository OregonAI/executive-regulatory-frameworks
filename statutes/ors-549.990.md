# Penalties (ORS 549.990)

## At a glance

ORS 549.990 — Penalties. Chapter 549 (Drainage and Flood Control Generally), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

549.990 Penalties.

(1) Subject to ORS 153.022, violation of ORS 549.180 is a Class D violation, and the violator shall be compelled to restore the drainage to the condition previously existing.

(2) Violation of ORS 549.400 is a Class C misdemeanor. [Amended by 1999 c.1051 §197; 2011 c.597 §230] _______________
