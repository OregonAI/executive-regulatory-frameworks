# Authority to form special road districts (ORS 371.305)

## At a glance

ORS 371.305 — Authority to form special road districts. Chapter 371 (Road Districts and Road Assessment Plans), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

371.305 Authority to form special road districts. Contiguous territory lying within any county and not incorporated within the limits of a city may be formed into, or included in, a special road district in the manner provided by ORS 371.305 to 371.360.
