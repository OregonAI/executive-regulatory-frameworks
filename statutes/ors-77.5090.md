# Adequate compliance with commercial contract WAREHOUSE RECEIPTS AND BILLS OF LADING: (ORS 77.5090)

## At a glance

ORS 77.5090 — Adequate compliance with commercial contract WAREHOUSE RECEIPTS AND BILLS OF LADING:. Chapter 77 (Warehouse Receipts, Bills of Lading and Other Documents of Title), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

77.5090 Adequate compliance with commercial contract. Whether a document of title is adequate to fulfill the obligations of a contract for sale, a contract for lease or the conditions of a letter of credit is determined by ORS chapter 72, 72A or 75. [1961 c.726 §77.5090; 2009 c.181 §85]
