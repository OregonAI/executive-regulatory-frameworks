# Maximum occupancy limit (ORS 90.112)

## At a glance

ORS 90.112 — Maximum occupancy limit. Chapter 90 (Residential Landlord and Tenant), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

90.112 Maximum occupancy limit. A maximum occupancy limit may not be established or enforced by any local government, as defined in ORS 197.015, for any residential dwelling unit, as defined in ORS 90.100, if the restriction is based on the familial or nonfamilial relationships among any occupants. [2021 c.24 §1] Note: 90.112 was enacted into law by the Legislative Assembly but was not added to or made a part of ORS chapter 90 or any series therein by legislative action. See Preface to Oregon Revised Statutes for further explanation.
