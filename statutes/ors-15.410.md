# Characterization (ORS 15.410)

## At a glance

ORS 15.410 — Characterization. Chapter 15 (Choice of Laws), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

15.410 Characterization.

(1) Oregon law determines the scope and meaning of terms used in ORS 15.400 to 15.460, including whether a claim is a noncontractual claim.

(2) The law of the state determined to be applicable under ORS 15.400 to 15.460 determines the scope and meaning of terms used in that law. [Formerly 31.860]
