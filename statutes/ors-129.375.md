# UPIA 413. Property not productive of income (ORS 129.375)

## At a glance

ORS 129.375 — UPIA 413. Property not productive of income. Chapter 129 (Uniform Principal and Income Act), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

129.375 UPIA 413. Property not productive of income.

(1) If a marital deduction is allowed for all or part of a trust whose assets consist substantially of property that does not provide the spouse with sufficient income from or use of the trust assets, and if the amounts that the trustee transfers from principal to income under ORS 129.215 and distributes to the spouse from principal pursuant to the terms of the trust are insufficient to provide the spouse with the beneficial enjoyment required to obtain the marital deduction, the spouse may require the trustee to make property productive of income, convert property within a reasonable time or exercise the power conferred by ORS 129.215 (1). The trustee may decide which action or combination of actions to take.

(2) In cases not governed by subsection

(1) of this section, proceeds from the sale or other disposition of an asset are principal without regard to the amount of income the asset produces during any accounting period. [2003 c.279 §22]
