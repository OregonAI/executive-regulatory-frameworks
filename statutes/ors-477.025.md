# Variability of wildland-urban interface fire protection problems (ORS 477.025)

## At a glance

ORS 477.025 — Variability of wildland-urban interface fire protection problems. Chapter 477 (Chapter 477), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

477.025 Variability of wildland-urban interface fire protection problems. The Legislative Assembly recognizes that the wildland-urban interface in Oregon varies by condition, situation, fire hazard and risk, that different wildland-urban interface fire protection problems exist across the state because of this variability, and that these different problems necessitate varied fire prevention and protection practices. [1997 c.429 §9; 2021 c.592 §32]
