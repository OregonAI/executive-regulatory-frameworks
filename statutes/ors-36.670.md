# Representation by a lawyer; representation of legal or commercial entities (ORS 36.670)

## At a glance

ORS 36.670 — Representation by a lawyer; representation of legal or commercial entities. Chapter 36 (Mediation and Arbitration), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

36.670 Representation by a lawyer; representation of legal or commercial entities. A party to an arbitration proceeding may be represented by a lawyer admitted to practice in this state or any other state. A corporation, business trust, partnership, limited liability company, association, joint venture or other legal or commercial entity may be represented by a lawyer admitted to practice in this state or any other state, by an officer of the entity, or by an employee or other agent authorized by the entity to represent the entity in the proceeding. [2003 c.598 §16] Note: See note under 36.600.
