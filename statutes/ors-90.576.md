# Legislative findings (ORS 90.576)

## At a glance

ORS 90.576 — Legislative findings. Chapter 90 (Residential Landlord and Tenant), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

90.576 Legislative findings. The Legislative Assembly finds and declares that:

(1) Water is an essential and scarce resource;

(2) Conservation of water is critical for the future of this state; and

(3) Billing for water according to usage encourages users to conserve water and allows users to exercise better control over their costs. [Formerly 90.541]
