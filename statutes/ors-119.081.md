# Effect on other laws (ORS 119.081)

## At a glance

ORS 119.081 — Effect on other laws. Chapter 119 (Revised Uniform Fiduciary Access to Digital Assets Act), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

119.081 Effect on other laws. ORS 119.006 to 119.081 modify, limit or supersede the Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act, 15 U.S.C. 7001 et seq., but do not modify, limit or supersede 15 U.S.C. 7001(c) or authorize electronic delivery of any of the notices described in 15 U.S.C. 7003(b). [2016 c.19 §18]
