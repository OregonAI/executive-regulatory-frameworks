# UCC 9-609. Secured party’s right to take possession after default (ORS 79A.6090)

## At a glance

## Full text

79A.6090 UCC 9-609. Secured party’s right to take possession after default.

(1) After default, a secured party: (a) May take possession of the collateral; and (b) Without removal, may render equipment unusable and dispose of collateral on a debtor’s premises under ORS 79A.6100.

(2) A secured party may proceed under subsection

(1) of this section: (a) Pursuant to judicial process; or (b) Without judicial process, if it proceeds without breach of the peace.

(3) If so agreed, and in any event after default, a secured party may require the debtor to assemble the collateral and make it available to the secured party at a place to be designated by the secured party which is reasonably convenient to both parties. [Formerly 79.0609]
