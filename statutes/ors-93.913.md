# Forfeiture allowed for default under certain collateral assignments of interest (ORS 93.913)

## At a glance

ORS 93.913 — Forfeiture allowed for default under certain collateral assignments of interest. Chapter 93 (Conveyancing and Recording), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

93.913 Forfeiture allowed for default under certain collateral assignments of interest. In the event of a default under a collateral assignment of the interest of a seller or purchaser in a land sale contract, including a collateral assignment of the proceeds thereof, the assignee may enforce a remedy of forfeiture, as set forth in ORS 93.905 to 93.945, unless the agreement between the parties otherwise prohibits such remedy. [1989 c.516 §3] Note: 93.913 and 93.918 were added to and made a part of ORS chapter 93 by legislative action but were not added to any series therein. See Preface to Oregon Revised Statutes for further explanation.
