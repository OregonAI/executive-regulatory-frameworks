# Excise taxes (ORS 268.507)

## At a glance

ORS 268.507 — Excise taxes. Chapter 268 (METROPOLITAN SERVICE DISTRICTS), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

268.507 Excise taxes. Subject to the provisions of a district charter, a district may by ordinance impose excise taxes on any person using the facilities, equipment, systems, functions, services or improvements owned, operated, franchised or provided by the district.
