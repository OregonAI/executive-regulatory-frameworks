# Time of commencing actions (ORS 12.010)

## At a glance

ORS 12.010 — Time of commencing actions. Chapter 12 (Limitations of Actions and Suits), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

12.010 Time of commencing actions. Actions shall only be commenced within the periods prescribed in this chapter, after the cause of action shall have accrued, except where a different limitation is prescribed by statute. [Amended by 1979 c.284 §43]
