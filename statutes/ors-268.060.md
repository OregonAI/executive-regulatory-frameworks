# Costs of elections (ORS 268.060)

## At a glance

ORS 268.060 — Costs of elections. Chapter 268 (METROPOLITAN SERVICE DISTRICTS), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

268.060 Costs of elections.

(1) The cost of elections to nominate or elect elected officials of the district shall be paid by the district.

(2) When a district election is held on a district measure, the election shall be conducted under ORS chapter 255. [1977 c.665 §6a (enacted in lieu of 268.200); 1997 c.833 §6]
