# Elector approval of taxation related ordinance (ORS 203.055)

## At a glance

ORS 203.055 — Elector approval of taxation related ordinance. Chapter 203 (County Governing Bodies; County Home Rule), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

203.055 Elector approval of taxation related ordinance. Any ordinance, adopted by a county governing body under ORS 203.035 and imposing, or providing an exemption from, taxation shall receive the approval of the electors of the county before taking effect. [1973 c.282 §6; 1975 c.736 §3; 2013 c.753 §§11,12]
