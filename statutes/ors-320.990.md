## At a glance

320.990 Penalties. Violation of any provision of ORS 320.005 to 320.150 is a Class B misdemeanor. [Amended by 1955 c.574 §7; 1971 c.743 §356; 1999 c.501 §10; 2005 c.94 §99; 2011 c.597 §182] _______________
