# Authority of person verifying financial eligibility (ORS 151.491)

## At a glance

ORS 151.491 — Authority of person verifying financial eligibility. Chapter 151 (Public Defenders; Counsel for Financially Eligible Persons), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

151.491 Authority of person verifying financial eligibility.

(1) State courts or authorized designees who conduct the verification of the financial statement submitted by a person seeking or having appointed counsel payable at state expense under ORS 151.216 and 151.219 may require the person to execute and deliver any written requests or authorizations as may be necessary under applicable law to provide the state court or authorized designee with access to records of public or private source, otherwise confidential, as may be needed to evaluate eligibility.

(2) In performing the verification duties under subsection

(1) of this section, the state courts are authorized to obtain information from any public record office of the state or of any subdivision or agency of the state upon request and without payment of any fees ordinarily required by law. [1989 c.1053 §16; 2001 c.962 §36]
