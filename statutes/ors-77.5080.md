# Warranties of collecting bank as to documents (ORS 77.5080)

## At a glance

ORS 77.5080 — Warranties of collecting bank as to documents. Chapter 77 (Warehouse Receipts, Bills of Lading and Other Documents of Title), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

77.5080 Warranties of collecting bank as to documents. A collecting bank or other intermediary known to be entrusted with documents of title on behalf of another or with collection of a draft or other claim against delivery of documents warrants by the delivery of the documents only its own good faith and authority even if the collecting bank or other intermediary has purchased or made advances against the claim or draft to be collected. [1961 c.726 §77.5080; 2009 c.181 §84] 77.5090 Adequate compliance with commercial contract. Whether a document of title is adequate to fulfill the obligations of a contract for sale, a contract for lease or the conditions of a letter of credit is determined by ORS chapter 72, 72A or 75. [1961 c.726 §77.5090; 2009 c.181 §85]
