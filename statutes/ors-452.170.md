# Deposit and disbursement of moneys collected CONTROL OF VECTORS (By Counties) (ORS 452.170)

## At a glance

ORS 452.170 — Deposit and disbursement of moneys collected CONTROL OF VECTORS (By Counties). Chapter 452 (Vector Control), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

452.170 Deposit and disbursement of moneys collected. All money received from taxes collected for the use of any control district under ORS 452.160 shall be paid to the county treasurer of the county in which the district is situated. The county treasurer shall keep the moneys in a separate fund to the credit of the district and disburse them upon the warrant of the district signed by the president of the board, and countersigned by the treasurer of the board. [Amended by 1959 c.600 §12; 1965 c.609 §6; 1981 c.640 §13] 452.180 [1963 c.255 §3; repealed by 1971 c.727 §203]
