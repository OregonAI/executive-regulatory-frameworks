# Rules (ORS 459A.266)

## At a glance

ORS 459A.266 — Rules. Chapter 459A (Reuse and Recycling), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

459A.266 Rules. The Environmental Quality Commission shall adopt any rules necessary for the effective administration of ORS 459A.200 to 459A.266. Upon request, the State Board of Pharmacy shall assist the commission in adopting rules under this section. [2019 c.659 §23] Note: See note under 459A.200. PRODUCER RESPONSIBILITY FOR ELECTRONIC DEVICES
