# Indorsement of indictment as “a true bill” (ORS 132.400)

## At a glance

ORS 132.400 — Indorsement of indictment as “a true bill”. Chapter 132 (Grand Jury, Indictments and Other Accusatory Instruments), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

132.400 Indorsement of indictment as “a true bill.” An indictment, when found, shall be indorsed “a true bill,” and such indorsement signed by the foreman of the jury.
