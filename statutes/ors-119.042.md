# Disclosure to trustee who is original user (ORS 119.042)

## At a glance

ORS 119.042 — Disclosure to trustee who is original user. Chapter 119 (Revised Uniform Fiduciary Access to Digital Assets Act), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

119.042 Disclosure to trustee who is original user. Unless otherwise ordered by the court or provided in a trust, a custodian shall disclose to a trustee that is an original user of an account any digital asset of the account held in trust, including a catalog of electronic communications of the trustee and the content of electronic communications. [2016 c.19 §11] 119.045 [1959 c.419 §8; 1961 c.456 §4; 1977 c.666 §24a; repealed by 1997 c.99 §54]
