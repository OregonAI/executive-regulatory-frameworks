# Required officers (ORS 60.371)

## At a glance

ORS 60.371 — Required officers. Chapter 60 (Private Corporations), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

60.371 Required officers.

(1) A corporation has the officers described in its bylaws or appointed by the board of directors in accordance with the bylaws which shall include a president and a secretary.

(2) A duly appointed officer may appoint one or more officers or assistant officers if such appointment is authorized by the bylaws or the board of directors.

(3) The secretary shall have the responsibility for preparing minutes of the directors’ and shareholders’ meetings and for authenticating records of the corporation.

(4) The same individual may simultaneously hold more than one office in a corporation. [1987 c.52 §89]
