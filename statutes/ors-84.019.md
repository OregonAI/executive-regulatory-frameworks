# Legal recognition of electronic records, electronic signatures and electronic contracts (ORS 84.019)

## At a glance

ORS 84.019 — Legal recognition of electronic records, electronic signatures and electronic contracts. Chapter 84 (Electronic Transactions), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

84.019 Legal recognition of electronic records, electronic signatures and electronic contracts.

(1) A record or signature may not be denied legal effect or enforceability solely because it is in electronic form.

(2) A contract may not be denied legal effect or enforceability solely because an electronic record was used in its formation.

(3) If a law requires a record to be in writing, an electronic record satisfies the law.

(4) If a law requires a signature, an electronic signature satisfies the law. [2001 c.535 §7]
