# Prohibition against claim of lien (ORS 87.037)

## At a glance

ORS 87.037 — Prohibition against claim of lien. Chapter 87 (Statutory Liens), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

87.037 Prohibition against claim of lien. An original contractor may not claim a lien arising from the improvement of real property if a written contract for the work is required by ORS 701.305 and the contractor does not have a written contract. [2007 c.648 §5] Note: 87.037 was added to and made a part of 87.001 to 87.060 by legislative action but was not added to any smaller series therein. See Preface to Oregon Revised Statutes for further explanation.
