# Penalties (ORS 242.990)

## At a glance

ORS 242.990 — Penalties. Chapter 242 (Civil Service for City or School District Employees and Firefighters), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

242.990 Penalties.

(1) Violation of any of the provisions of the Custodians’ Civil Service Law is a misdemeanor.

(2) Willful violation of any of the provisions of ORS 242.720, 242.738, 242.768, 242.772, 242.792, 242.822 and 242.824 is a misdemeanor. [Subsection

(2) enacted as 1959 c.252 §45] _______________
