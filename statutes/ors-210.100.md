# Creation of office of county accountant (ORS 210.100)

## At a glance

ORS 210.100 — Creation of office of county accountant. Chapter 210 (County Accountants), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

210.100 Creation of office of county accountant. The governing body of any county may by ordinance create an office of county accountant. [1981 c.216 §1; 1983 c.310 §2] 210.110 [Repealed by 1981 c.216 §14]
