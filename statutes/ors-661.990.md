# Penalties (ORS 661.990)

## At a glance

ORS 661.990 — Penalties. Chapter 661 (Organized Labor; Union Labels), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

661.990 Penalties.

(1) Violation of ORS 661.040 is a Class A misdemeanor.

(2) Violation of ORS 661.210 or 661.220 is a Class C misdemeanor. [Amended by 2011 c.597 §275; 2015 c.277 §13] _______________
