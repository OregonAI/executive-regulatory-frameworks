# Applicability of other laws requiring security or regulating loans and investments (ORS 86.640)

## At a glance

ORS 86.640 — Applicability of other laws requiring security or regulating loans and investments. Chapter 86 (Mortgages; Trust Deeds), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

86.640 Applicability of other laws requiring security or regulating loans and investments. No law of this state requiring security upon which loans or investments may be made, or prescribing the nature, amount or form of such security, or prescribing or limiting the period for which loans or investments may be made, shall apply to loans or investments made pursuant to ORS 86.610 and 86.620. TRUST DEEDS (General Provisions)
