# Pleading a judgment (Plea) (ORS 135.325)

## At a glance

ORS 135.325 — Pleading a judgment (Plea). Chapter 135 (Arraignment and Pretrial Provisions), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

135.325 Pleading a judgment. In pleading a judgment or other determination of or proceeding before a court or officer of special jurisdiction, it is not necessary for the defendant to state the facts conferring jurisdiction; but the judgment, determination, or proceeding may be stated to have been duly given or made. The facts conferring jurisdiction, however, must be established on the trial. [Formerly 135.450] 135.330 [Amended by 1961 c.698 §1; 1967 c.628 §1; 1971 c.677 §1; renumbered 135.055] (Plea)
