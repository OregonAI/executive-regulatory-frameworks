# Payment by buyer before inspection (ORS 72.5120)

## At a glance

ORS 72.5120 — Payment by buyer before inspection. Chapter 72 (Sales), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

72.5120 Payment by buyer before inspection.

(1) Where the contract requires payment before inspection nonconformity of the goods does not excuse the buyer from so making payment unless: (a) The nonconformity appears without inspection; or (b) Despite tender of the required documents the circumstances would justify injunction against honor under the provisions of ORS 75.1090.

(2) Payment pursuant to subsection

(1) of this section does not constitute an acceptance of goods or impair the buyer’s right to inspect or any of the remedies of the buyer. [1961 c.726 §72.5120; 1997 c.150 §4]
