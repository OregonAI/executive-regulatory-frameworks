# Enjoining violations (ORS 619.426)

## At a glance

ORS 619.426 — Enjoining violations. Chapter 619 (Chapter 619), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

619.426 Enjoining violations. Circuit courts hereby are authorized, upon petition of the Oregon Sheep Commission, to enjoin by temporary or permanent injunction any violations of the provisions of ORS 619.411 to 619.426. [1989 c.616 §5]
