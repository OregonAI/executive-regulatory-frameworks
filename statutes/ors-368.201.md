# Basis for legalization of road (ORS 368.201)

## At a glance

ORS 368.201 — Basis for legalization of road. Chapter 368 (County Roads), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

368.201 Basis for legalization of road. A county governing body may initiate proceedings to legalize a county road under ORS 368.201 to 368.221 if any of the following conditions exist:

(1) If, through omission or defect, doubt exists as to the legal establishment or evidence of establishment of a public road.

(2) If the location of the road cannot be accurately determined due to: (a) Numerous alterations of the road; (b) A defective survey of the road or adjacent property; or (c) Loss or destruction of the original survey of the road.

(3) If the road as traveled and used for 10 years or more does not conform to the location of a road described in the county records. [1981 c.153 §21] 368.205 [Amended by 1975 c.774 §1; 1977 c.338 §1; repealed by 1981 c.153 §79]
