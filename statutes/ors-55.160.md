# Time within which appeal must be taken (ORS 55.160)

## At a glance

ORS 55.160 — Time within which appeal must be taken. Chapter 55 (Appeals), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

55.160 Time within which appeal must be taken.

(1) Except as provided in subsection

(2) of this section, a notice of appeal must be filed and served within 30 days of the date of entry in the justice court docket of the judgment or order being appealed.

(2) If, in a case involving a misdemeanor or a violation, a motion for a new trial or motion in arrest of judgment is timely served and filed, a notice of appeal must be served and filed within 30 days from the earlier of the following dates: (a) The date of entry of the order disposing of the motion; or (b) The date on which the motion is deemed denied. [2025 c.268 §8]
