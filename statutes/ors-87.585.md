# Foreclosure (ORS 87.585)

## At a glance

ORS 87.585 — Foreclosure. Chapter 87 (Statutory Liens), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

87.585 Foreclosure. The liens described in ORS 87.555 may be foreclosed by a suit in the circuit court. In any suit brought pursuant to the provisions of ORS 87.581 or this section, upon entering a judgment for the plaintiff, the court shall allow as part of the costs and disbursements all moneys paid for the filing and recording of the notice of lien, and reasonable attorney fees at trial and on appeal.
