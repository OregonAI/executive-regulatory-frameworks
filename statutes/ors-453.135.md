# Notice required prior to institution of criminal proceedings (Miscellaneous) (ORS 453.135)

## At a glance

ORS 453.135 — Notice required prior to institution of criminal proceedings (Miscellaneous). Chapter 453 (Hazardous Substances; Radiation Sources), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

453.135 Notice required prior to institution of criminal proceedings. Before any violation of ORS 453.005 to 453.135 and 453.990

(2) is reported to any district attorney or police official for the institution of a criminal proceeding, the person against whom such proceeding is contemplated shall be given appropriate notice and an opportunity to present the person’s views before the Director of the Oregon Health Authority or the designated agent of the director, either orally or in writing, in person, or by attorney, with regard to such contemplated proceeding.
