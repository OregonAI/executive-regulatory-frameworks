# Fees (ORS 677.840)

## At a glance

ORS 677.840 — Fees. Chapter 677 (Regulation of Medicine, Podiatry and Acupuncture), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

677.840 Fees. Every podiatric physician and surgeon shall pay to the Oregon Medical Board nonrefundable fees as determined by the board pursuant to ORS 677.265. [Formerly 682.065; 1989 c.830 §44]
