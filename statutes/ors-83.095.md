# Service charge computed by actuarial method (ORS 83.095)

## At a glance

ORS 83.095 — Service charge computed by actuarial method. Chapter 83 (Retail Installment Contracts), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

83.095 Service charge computed by actuarial method.

(1) Notwithstanding any other provision of ORS 83.010 to 83.190 and 83.820 to 83.895, a retail seller in a retail installment contract may contract for and charge, receive and collect a service charge computed by the actuarial method.

(2) When a retail installment contract provides for a service charge computed by the actuarial method: (a) The retail installment contract may provide for any other charge, cost or fee allowed under ORS 83.010 to 83.190 and 83.820 to 83.895, in addition to the service charge. (b) The amount to be disclosed as the service charge and used as the service charge component of the other amounts disclosed pursuant to ORS 83.010 to 83.190 and 83.820 to 83.895 shall be the amount of the service charge to be paid assuming all payments are made exactly as agreed. (c) The refund credit provisions of ORS 83.130 shall not apply. [1983 c.432 §4]
