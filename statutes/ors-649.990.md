# Penalties (ORS 649.990)

## At a glance

ORS 649.990 — Penalties. Chapter 649 (Insignia and Names of Organizations), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

649.990 Penalties.

(1) A violation of ORS 649.030 is a Class D violation.

(2) A violation of ORS 649.080 is a Class C misdemeanor. [Amended by 1971 c.743 §403; 1983 c.740 §238; 1983 c.338 §962; 1985 c.16 §475; 1999 c.1051 §214; 2011 c.597 §261; 2015 c.277 §9] _______________
