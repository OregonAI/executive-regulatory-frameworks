# UCC 9-601. Rights after default; judicial enforcement; consignor or buyer of accounts, chattel paper, payment intangibles or promissory notes (ORS 79A.6010)

## At a glance

## Full text

79A.6010 UCC 9-601. Rights after default; judicial enforcement; consignor or buyer of accounts, chattel paper, payment intangibles or promissory notes.

(1) After default, a secured party has the rights provided in ORS 79A.6010 to 79A.6280 and, except as otherwise provided in ORS 79A.6020, those provided by agreement of the parties. A secured party: (a) May reduce a claim to judgment, foreclose, or otherwise enforce the claim, security interest or agricultural lien by any available judicial procedure; and (b) If the collateral is documents, may proceed either as to the documents or as to the goods they cover.

(2) A secured party in possession of collateral or control of collateral under ORS 77.1060, 79A.1040, 79A.1050, 79A.1053, 79A.1060, 79A.1070 or 79A.1073 has the rights and duties provided in ORS 79A.2070.

(3) The rights under subsections

(1) and

(2) of this section are cumulative and may be exercised simultaneously.

(4) Except as otherwise provided in subsection

(7) of this section and ORS 79A.6050, after default, a debtor and an obligor have the rights provided in ORS 79A.6010 to 79A.6280 and by agreement of the parties.

(5) If a secured party has reduced its claim to judgment, the lien of any levy that may be made upon the collateral by virtue of an execution based upon the judgment relates back to the earliest of: (a) The date of perfection of the security interest or agricultural lien in the collateral; (b) The date of filing a financing statement covering the collateral; or (c) Any date specified in a statute under which the agricultural lien was created.

(6) A sale pursuant to an execution is a foreclosure of the security interest or agricultural lien by judicial procedure within the meaning of this section. A secured party may purchase at the sale and thereafter hold the collateral free of any other requirements of this chapter.

(7) Except as otherwise provided in ORS 79A.6070 (3), ORS 79A.6010 to 79A.6280 impose no duties upon a secured party that is a consignor or is a buyer of accounts, chattel paper, payment intangibles or promissory notes. [Formerly 79.0601]
