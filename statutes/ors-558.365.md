# Limitation on membership (ORS 558.365)

## At a glance

ORS 558.365 — Limitation on membership. Chapter 558 (Weather Modification), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

558.365 Limitation on membership. Nothing in ORS 558.200 to 558.440 authorizes the district to budget, provide for payments or collect contributions to fund retirement benefits for an individual who is not in the employment of the district at the time of the creation of a membership status under a retirement plan. [1969 c.698 §59] (Board of Commissioners)
