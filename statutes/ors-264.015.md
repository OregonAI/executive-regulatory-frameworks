# Application of ORS chapter 255 (ORS 264.015)

## At a glance

ORS 264.015 — Application of ORS chapter 255. Chapter 264 (Domestic Water Supply Districts), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

264.015 Application of ORS chapter 255.

(1) ORS chapter 255 governs the following: (a) The nomination and election of commissioners. (b) The conduct of district elections.

(2) The electors of a district may exercise the powers of the initiative and referendum regarding a district measure, in accordance with ORS 255.135 to 255.205. [1983 c.350 §105] 264.020 [1955 c.676 §3; 1969 c.666 §49; repealed by 1971 c.647 §149]
