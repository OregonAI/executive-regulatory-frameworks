# Exclusive right to register (ORS 649.060)

## At a glance

ORS 649.060 — Exclusive right to register. Chapter 649 (Insignia and Names of Organizations), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

649.060 Exclusive right to register. Any organization organized and existing on June 8, 1933, which had adopted any name or insignia has exclusive rights to the registration of such name and insignia officially adopted and in use by it at that time. 649.070 [Repealed by 2015 c.277 §15]
