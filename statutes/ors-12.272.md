# Action based on pesticide application (ORS 12.272)

## At a glance

ORS 12.272 — Action based on pesticide application. Chapter 12 (Limitations of Actions and Suits), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

12.272 Action based on pesticide application.

(1) Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, no action against a landowner, person for whom the pesticide was applied or pesticide operator arising out of the use or application of any pesticide shall be commenced unless the person commencing the action, within the time specified by ORS 634.172, files the report required by ORS 634.172 and mails or personally delivers true copies of that report to: (a) The landowner or pesticide operator who is allegedly responsible for the loss; and (b) The person for whom the pesticide was applied if that person is not the person commencing the action.

(2) For the purposes of this section: (a) “Landowner” includes any person, firm, corporation, the state, any county within the state, or municipality, shown by records of the county to be the owner of land or having such land under contract for purchase. (b) “Pesticide operator” has the meaning given in ORS 634.006. [1991 c.351 §3; 1995 c.96 §1]
