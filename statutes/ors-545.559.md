# Bonds and coupons to be negotiable instruments (ORS 545.559)

## At a glance

ORS 545.559 — Bonds and coupons to be negotiable instruments. Chapter 545 (Irrigation Districts), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

545.559 Bonds and coupons to be negotiable instruments. Any bearer bonds and the coupons attached thereto issued under ORS 545.557 are fully negotiable instruments under the laws of the State of Oregon.
