# County civil service commission; members; compensation (ORS 241.065)

## At a glance

ORS 241.065 — County civil service commission; members; compensation. Chapter 241 (Civil Service for County Employees), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

241.065 County civil service commission; members; compensation. There is created in each county subject to ORS 241.016 to 241.990 a civil service commission composed of three members appointed by the board of county commissioners, who shall serve without compensation and shall devote due time and attention to their duties.
