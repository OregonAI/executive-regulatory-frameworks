# Supplemental principles of law (ORS 67.044)

## At a glance

ORS 67.044 — Supplemental principles of law. Chapter 67 (Partnerships; Limited Liability Partnerships), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

67.044 Supplemental principles of law.

(1) Unless displaced by particular provisions of this chapter, the principles of law and equity supplement this chapter.

(2) If an obligation to pay interest arises under this chapter and the rate is not specified, the rate is that specified in ORS 82.010. [Formerly 67.020]
