# Time limit for obtaining signatures on petition for incorporation of city (ORS 199.526)

## At a glance

ORS 199.526 — Time limit for obtaining signatures on petition for incorporation of city. Chapter 199 (Local Government Boundary Commissions; City-County Consolidation), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

199.526 Time limit for obtaining signatures on petition for incorporation of city. When incorporation of a city is proposed within the jurisdiction of a local government boundary commission, all signatures on the petition described in ORS 221.031 and 221.040 must be obtained within any six-month period to be valid and effective to authorize a major boundary change proceeding under this chapter. [1987 c.882 §8] Note: 199.526 was enacted into law by the Legislative Assembly but was not added to or made a part of ORS chapter 199 or any series therein by legislative action. See Preface to Oregon Revised Statutes for further explanation. 199.530 [1969 c.494 §22; renumbered 199.455]
