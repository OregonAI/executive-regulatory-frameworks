# Terms and compensation of commissioners (ORS 242.350)

## At a glance

ORS 242.350 — Terms and compensation of commissioners. Chapter 242 (Civil Service for City or School District Employees and Firefighters), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

242.350 Terms and compensation of commissioners.

(1) Initial appointments of the commissioners shall be as follows: One for two years, one for four years and one for six years. Thereafter each appointment shall be for a term of six years.

(2) The commissioners shall serve without compensation.
