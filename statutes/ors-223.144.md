# Economic improvement district; business license fee ordinance (ORS 223.144)

## At a glance

ORS 223.144 — Economic improvement district; business license fee ordinance. Chapter 223 (Local Improvements and Works Generally), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

223.144 Economic improvement district; business license fee ordinance.

(1) A council, on its own motion or after receiving a petition for the formation of an economic improvement district signed by 33 percent or more of persons conducting business within the proposed district, may enact an ordinance establishing a procedure to be followed by the city in imposing a business license fee to raise revenue for the cost of an economic improvement. The business license fee authorized under this subsection may be in the form of a surcharge on an existing business license fee imposed by the city on any business, trade, occupation or profession carried on or practiced in the economic improvement district.

(2) In any ordinance adopted under subsection

(1) of this section, a city shall not be authorized to: (a) Include within an economic improvement district any area of the city that is not zoned for commercial or industrial use. (b) Impose a business license fee to raise revenue for an economic improvement that does not primarily benefit persons conducting business within the economic improvement district. [1991 c.698 §2] 223.145 [Repealed by 1971 c.741 §38]
