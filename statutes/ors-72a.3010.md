# Enforceability of lease contract (ORS 72A.3010)

## At a glance

ORS 72A.3010 — Enforceability of lease contract. Chapter 72A (Leases), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

72A.3010 Enforceability of lease contract. Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, a lease contract is effective and enforceable according to its terms between the parties, against purchasers of the goods and against creditors of the parties. [1989 c.676 §31]
