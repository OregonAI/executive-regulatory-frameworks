# Rule 702. Testimony by experts (ORS 40.410)

## At a glance

ORS 40.410 — Rule 702. Testimony by experts. Chapter 40 (Evidence Code), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

40.410 Rule 702. Testimony by experts. If scientific, technical or other specialized knowledge will assist the trier of fact to understand the evidence or to determine a fact in issue, a witness qualified as an expert by knowledge, skill, experience, training or education may testify thereto in the form of an opinion or otherwise. [1981 c.892 §58]
