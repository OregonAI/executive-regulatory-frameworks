# Access to information in Federal Parent Locator Service; rules (ORS 25.265)

## At a glance

## Full text

25.265 Access to information in Federal Parent Locator Service; rules. The Department of Justice shall adopt rules establishing a procedure by which a person authorized under federal law may access information in the Federal Parent Locator Service. [1997 c.746 §22a; 2003 c.73 §25] Note: 25.265 was enacted into law by the Legislative Assembly but was not added to or made a part of ORS chapter 25 or any series therein by legislative action.
