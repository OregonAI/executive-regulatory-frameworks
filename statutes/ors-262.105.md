# Taxation of agency property in same manner as private power corporations (ORS 262.105)

## At a glance

ORS 262.105 — Taxation of agency property in same manner as private power corporations. Chapter 262 (JOINT OPERATING AGENCIES FOR ELECTRIC POWER PUBLIC ORGANIZATIONS FOR COMMUNITY SERVICE GENERAL PROVISIONS), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

262.105 Taxation of agency property in same manner as private power corporations. All property, real and personal, owned, used, operated or controlled by a joint operating agency for the transmission, production or furnishing of electric power or energy shall be assessed and taxed in the same manner and for the same purposes as similar property owned, used, operated or controlled by private corporations, other than electric cooperatives, for the purpose of furnishing electric power or energy to the public. The joint operating agency and its directors and officers shall be subject to the same requirements as are provided by law in respect to such assessment and taxation. All taxes so levied shall be payable by the joint operating agency out of its revenues as an expense of its operation. [1973 c.722 §11] 262.110 [Repealed by 1969 c.12 §1]
