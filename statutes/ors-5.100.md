# Order of docketing and disposal of business; records of proceedings (ORS 5.100)

## At a glance

ORS 5.100 — Order of docketing and disposal of business; records of proceedings. Chapter 5 (County Courts (Judicial Functions)), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

5.100 Order of docketing and disposal of business; records of proceedings.

(1) The business of the county court at each term shall be docketed and disposed of in the following order: (a) Judicial business. (b) County business.

(2) The proceedings and records of the court pertaining to the respective classifications of business specified in this section shall be kept in separate books. [Amended by 1969 c.591 §266]
