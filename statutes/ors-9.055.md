# Chief executive officer (ORS 9.055)

## At a glance

ORS 9.055 — Chief executive officer. Chapter 9 (Oregon State Bar; Attorneys; Law Libraries), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

9.055 Chief executive officer. The board of governors shall appoint a chief executive officer of the Oregon State Bar. The chief executive officer is the chief administrative employee of the bar. The chief executive officer may, but need not, be a licensee of the bar. The chief executive officer serves at the board’s discretion and shall perform such duties as the board prescribes. [2017 c.94 §2; 2025 c.32 §8]
