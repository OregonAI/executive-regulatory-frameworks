# Hearing on claim; petition to compel action (ORS 98.402)

## At a glance

ORS 98.402 — Hearing on claim; petition to compel action. Chapter 98 (Lost, Unclaimed or Abandoned Property; Vehicle Towing), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

98.402 Hearing on claim; petition to compel action.

(1) A person aggrieved by a decision of the State Treasurer under ORS 98.302 to 98.436 may request a contested case hearing in accordance with ORS 183.413 to 183.470.

(2) If the State Treasurer fails to act on a claim within 120 days after a person files the claim under ORS 98.392, the claimant may petition a court under ORS 183.484 to compel the State Treasurer to act.
