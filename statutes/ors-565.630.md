## At a glance

## Full text

565.630 Regulation of public events; penalty. Any county fair board and every society mentioned in ORS 565.610 may regulate its prices of admission, licenses and all matters pertaining to the conduct of its annual fairs, exhibitions or other public events or meetings. The penalty for violation of its rules and regulations is a Class D violation. [Amended by 1953 c.675 §12; 1969 c.239 §7; 1973 c.537 §10; 2005 c.777 §44; 2011 c.597 §90a; 2013 c.492 §18]
