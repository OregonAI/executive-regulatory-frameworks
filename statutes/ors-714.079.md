## At a glance

ORS 714.079 — Closure and relocation of branches (Oregon Branches of Out-of-State Banks and Extranational Institutions). Chapter 714 (Branch Banking; Automated Teller Machines), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

714.079 Closure and relocation of branches. A banking institution may close and relocate branches provided the banking institution provides the Director of the Department of Consumer and Business Services with a copy of any notice required under 12 U.S.C. 1831r-1(a), or comparable federal law, at the time and in the form required by such law or laws.
