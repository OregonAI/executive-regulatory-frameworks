# Liability of limited liability company for acts, omissions or conduct of member or manager (ORS 63.170)

## At a glance

ORS 63.170 — Liability of limited liability company for acts, omissions or conduct of member or manager. Chapter 63 (Limited Liability Companies), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

63.170 Liability of limited liability company for acts, omissions or conduct of member or manager. A limited liability company is liable for loss or injury caused to a person, or for a penalty incurred, as a result of a wrongful act or omission, or other actionable conduct, of a member or manager acting in the ordinary course of the business of the limited liability company or with authority of the limited liability company. [1999 c.86 §7]
