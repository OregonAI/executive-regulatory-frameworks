# Executive secretary; support services (ORS 97.784)

## At a glance

ORS 97.784 — Executive secretary; support services. Chapter 97 (Rights and Duties Relating to Cemeteries, Human Bodies and Anatomical Gifts), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

97.784 Executive secretary; support services. The State Parks and Recreation Department shall provide support services to the Oregon Commission on Historic Cemeteries. One staff person of the department shall be the executive secretary of the commission. [1999 c.731 §8; 2003 c.173 §7] Note: See note under 97.772. CEMETERY CARE
