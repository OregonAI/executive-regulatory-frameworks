# Waiver of appearance at trial as release condition prohibited (ORS 135.253)

## At a glance

ORS 135.253 — Waiver of appearance at trial as release condition prohibited. Chapter 135 (Arraignment and Pretrial Provisions), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

135.253 Waiver of appearance at trial as release condition prohibited.

(1) The court may not condition a defendant’s release on the defendant’s waiver of appearance in person at trial.

(2) A release agreement may not contain a provision prohibited by subsection

(1) of this section. [2018 c.37 §4]
