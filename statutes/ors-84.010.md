# Prospective application (ORS 84.010)

## At a glance

ORS 84.010 — Prospective application. Chapter 84 (Electronic Transactions), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

84.010 Prospective application. ORS 84.001 to 84.061 apply to any electronic record or electronic signature created, generated, sent, communicated, received or stored on or after June 22, 2001. [2001 c.535 §4]
