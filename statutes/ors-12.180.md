# Coexisting disabilities must all be removed (ORS 12.180)

## At a glance

ORS 12.180 — Coexisting disabilities must all be removed. Chapter 12 (Limitations of Actions and Suits), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

12.180 Coexisting disabilities must all be removed. When two or more disabilities coexist at the time the right of action accrues, the limitation shall not attach until all are removed.
