# Formation of district (ORS 452.020)

## At a glance

ORS 452.020 — Formation of district. Chapter 452 (Vector Control), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

452.020 Formation of district. One or more districts may be formed in any county. The entire county may be included within a district or any portion of the entire county may be included. The district may include all or part of any incorporated cities in the county.
