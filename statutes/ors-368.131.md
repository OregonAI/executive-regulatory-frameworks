# Right of way over United States public lands (ORS 368.131)

## At a glance

ORS 368.131 — Right of way over United States public lands. Chapter 368 (County Roads), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

368.131 Right of way over United States public lands. The county governing body may by resolution accept the grant of rights of way for the construction of public roads over public lands of the United States. This section does not invalidate the acceptance of such grant by general public use and enjoyment.
