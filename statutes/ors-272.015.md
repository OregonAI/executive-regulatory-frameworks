# Service of process (ORS 272.015)

## At a glance

ORS 272.015 — Service of process. Chapter 272 (FEDERAL LANDS PUBLIC LANDS), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

272.015 Service of process. All civil or criminal process issued under the authority of this state may be executed by the proper officers of this state upon any person amenable to the process within the limits of lands acquired by the United States under ORS 272.020, 272.030, 272.033 and 272.036 in like manner and to the same effect as if those sections had not been enacted. [1967 c.421 §181]
