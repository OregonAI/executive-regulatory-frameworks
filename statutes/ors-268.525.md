# Refunding bonds (ORS 268.525)

## At a glance

ORS 268.525 — Refunding bonds. Chapter 268 (METROPOLITAN SERVICE DISTRICTS), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

268.525 Refunding bonds. Refunding bonds of the same character and tenor as those replaced thereby may be issued pursuant to a resolution adopted by the district governing body without submitting to the electors the question of authorizing the issuance of the bonds. [1969 c.700 §19a]
