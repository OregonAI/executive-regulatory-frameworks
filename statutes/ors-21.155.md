# Domestic relations filing fee (ORS 21.155)

## At a glance

ORS 21.155 — Domestic relations filing fee. Chapter 21 (State Court Fees), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

21.155 Domestic relations filing fee. A circuit court shall collect a filing fee of $301 when a complaint or other document is filed for the purpose of commencing one of the following proceedings and when an answer or other first appearance is filed in the proceeding:

(1) Proceedings for dissolution of marriage, annulment of marriage or separation.

(2) Proceedings to adjudicate parentage under ORS 109.124 to 109.171.

(3) Proceedings under ORS 108.110, 109.100 and 109.103. [2011 c.595 §11; 2013 c.685 §§32,32a; 2014 c.76 §6; 2017 c.663 §4; 2019 c.605 §3; 2025 c.592 §110]
