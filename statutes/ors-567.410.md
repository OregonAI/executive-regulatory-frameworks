# Station management and control (ORS 567.410)

## At a glance

ORS 567.410 — Station management and control. Chapter 567 (Experiment Stations), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

567.410 Station management and control. The station shall be under the control and management of Oregon State University through its director of the Oregon agricultural experiment station, as a branch station of the Oregon agricultural experiment station.
