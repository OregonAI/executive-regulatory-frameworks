# Application of statutes and rules governing conflict of laws (ORS 12.440)

## At a glance

ORS 12.440 — Application of statutes and rules governing conflict of laws. Chapter 12 (Limitations of Actions and Suits), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

12.440 Application of statutes and rules governing conflict of laws. If the statute of limitations of another state applies to the assertion of a claim in this state, the other state’s relevant statutes and other rules of law governing tolling and accrual apply in computing the limitation period, but its statutes and other rules of law governing conflict of laws do not apply. [1987 c.536 §3] Note: See note under 12.410.
