# Rules (ORS 86A.242)

## At a glance

ORS 86A.242 — Rules. Chapter 86A (Mortgage Lending), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

86A.242 Rules. The Director of the Department of Consumer and Business Services may adopt, amend and repeal rules that are necessary to carry out the provisions of ORS 86A.200 to 86A.239. [2009 c.863 §13a]
