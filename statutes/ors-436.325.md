# Effect on surgical procedures (ORS 436.325)

## At a glance

ORS 436.325 — Effect on surgical procedures. Chapter 436 (Sterilization), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

436.325 Effect on surgical procedures. Nothing in this chapter limits surgical procedures which are medically indicated and which may result in sterilization. However, a hysterectomy shall not be performed solely for the purpose of sterilization or for the purpose of hygiene and sanitary care of a female’s menses. [1983 c.460 §14]
