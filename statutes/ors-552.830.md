# Date of election on dissolution of district (ORS 552.830)

## At a glance

ORS 552.830 — Date of election on dissolution of district. Chapter 552 (Water Improvement Districts), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

552.830 Date of election on dissolution of district. An election on dissolution may be held only on the same date as the regular district election.
