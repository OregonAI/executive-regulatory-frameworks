# Effect of license revocation, suspension or surrender (ORS 725A.040)

## At a glance

ORS 725A.040 — Effect of license revocation, suspension or surrender. Chapter 725A (Short-Term Loans and Student Loan Servicing), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

725A.040 Effect of license revocation, suspension or surrender. Revocation, suspension or surrender of a license issued under ORS 725A.024 does not impair or affect the rights or obligations specified in a lawful contract between the licensee and a borrower that existed before the revocation, suspension or surrender. [2010 c.23 §13] (Regulation)
