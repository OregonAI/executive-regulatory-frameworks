# Rule 514. Effect on existing privileges (ORS 40.295)

## At a glance

ORS 40.295 — Rule 514. Effect on existing privileges. Chapter 40 (Evidence Code), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

40.295 Rule 514. Effect on existing privileges. Unless expressly repealed by section 98, chapter 892, Oregon Laws 1981, all existing privileges either created under the Constitution or statutes of the State of Oregon or developed by the courts of Oregon are recognized and shall continue to exist until changed or repealed according to law. [1981 c.892 §42]
