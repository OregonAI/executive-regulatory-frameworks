# Rules (ORS 744.124)

## At a glance

ORS 744.124 — Rules. Chapter 744 (Insurance Producers; Life Settlement Providers, Brokers and Contracts; Adjusters; Consultants; Third Party Administrators; Reinsurance Intermediaries; Limited L), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

744.124 Rules. The Director of the Department of Consumer and Business Services may adopt rules to implement and administer the provisions of ORS 744.101 to 744.121. [Formerly 744.111] Note: 744.124 was enacted into law by the Legislative Assembly but was not added to or made a part of ORS chapter 744 or any series therein by legislative action. See Preface to Oregon Revised Statutes for further explanation.
