# Private family burial grounds (ORS 97.040)

## At a glance

ORS 97.040 — Private family burial grounds. Chapter 97 (Rights and Duties Relating to Cemeteries, Human Bodies and Anatomical Gifts), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

97.040 Private family burial grounds. Except for ORS 97.730, 97.010 to 97.040, 97.110 to 97.450, 97.510 to 97.730, 97.810 to 97.920 and 97.990 do not apply to private family burial grounds where lots are not offered for sale. 97.050 [1977 c.183 §1; 1983 c.526 §4; 1985 c.747 §49; 1987 c.660 §16; 1989 c.1034 §8; renumbered 127.605 in 1989] 97.055 [1977 c.183 §2; 1979 c.211 §1; 1983 c.526 §5; renumbered 127.610 in 1989] 97.060 [1977 c.183 §3; renumbered 127.615 in 1989] 97.065 [1977 c.183 §4; renumbered 127.620 in 1989] 97.070 [1977 c.183 §5; renumbered 127.625 in 1989] 97.075 [1977 c.183 §6; repealed by 1983 c.526 §7] 97.080 [1977 c.183 §7; renumbered 127.630 in 1989]
