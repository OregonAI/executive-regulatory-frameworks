# Powers of other judicial officers; where powers may be exercised (ORS 1.270)

## At a glance

ORS 1.270 — Powers of other judicial officers; where powers may be exercised. Chapter 1 (Courts and Judicial Officers Generally), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

1.270 Powers of other judicial officers; where powers may be exercised. Every other judicial officer may, within the county, city, district or precinct in which the judicial officer is chosen:

(1) Exercise the powers mentioned in ORS 1.260 (1).

(2) Exercise any other power and perform any other duty conferred or imposed upon the judicial officer by statute. 1.280 [1959 c.552 §4; repealed by 1981 s.s. c.1 §25]
