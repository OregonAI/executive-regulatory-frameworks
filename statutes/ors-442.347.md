# Rural hospital required to report certain actions (ORS 442.347)

## At a glance

ORS 442.347 — Rural hospital required to report certain actions. Chapter 442 (Health Planning), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

442.347 Rural hospital required to report certain actions. A rural hospital exempted from the certificate of need requirement by ORS 442.315

(8) shall report any action taken by the hospital that would have required a certificate of need if the exemption did not exist. [1993 c.722 §4] Note: 442.347 was enacted into law by the Legislative Assembly but was not added to or made a part of ORS chapter 442 or any series therein by legislative action.
