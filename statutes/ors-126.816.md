# Irrevocable gifts or exercise of power of appointment (ORS 126.816)

## At a glance

ORS 126.816 — Irrevocable gifts or exercise of power of appointment. Chapter 126 (Property Held for the Benefit of Minors; Uniform Transfers to Minors Act), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

126.816 Irrevocable gifts or exercise of power of appointment. A person may make a transfer by irrevocable gift to, or the irrevocable exercise of a power of appointment in favor of, a custodian for the benefit of a beneficiary pursuant to ORS 126.832. [1985 c.665 §5; 2005 c.349 §4] 126.817 [1983 c.457 §3; repealed by 1985 c.665 §27]
