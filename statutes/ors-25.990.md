# Penalties (ORS 25.990)

## At a glance

ORS 25.990 — Penalties. Chapter 25 (Child Support Services), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

25.990 Penalties.

(1) Violation of ORS 25.720

(3) is a Class A violation.

(2) Violation of ORS 25.260 is a Class C misdemeanor.

(3) Violation of ORS 25.785

(3) is a Class A misdemeanor. [1985 c.671 §52(4); 1989 c.812 §3(2); 1999 c.1051 §147; 2003 c.610 §4; 2011 c.597 §151] _______________
