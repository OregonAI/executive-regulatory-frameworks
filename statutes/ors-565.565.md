## At a glance

## Full text

565.565 Unauthorized entry to fairgrounds; penalty. A person who gains or attempts to gain unauthorized entry to the Oregon State Fair or to an event held at fairground properties or facilities without paying a required admission fee commits a Class D violation. In addition to any enforcement officers specifically identified in ORS 153.005, the state fair director and other employees of the State Fair Council may issue citations for violations of this section. [2013 c.492 §12] 565.570 [Repealed by 1983 c.327 §16]
