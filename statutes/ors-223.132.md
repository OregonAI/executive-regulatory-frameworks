# Formation of economic improvement districts as additional power of cities (ORS 223.132)

## At a glance

ORS 223.132 — Formation of economic improvement districts as additional power of cities. Chapter 223 (Local Improvements and Works Generally), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

223.132 Formation of economic improvement districts as additional power of cities. The authority granted to cities by ORS 223.112 to 223.132, is in addition to any other authority a city may have under state law, its charter or its ordinances to create or finance economic improvement districts.
