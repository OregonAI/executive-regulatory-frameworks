# Employees (ORS 838.050)

## At a glance

ORS 838.050 — Employees. Chapter 838 (Airport Districts), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

838.050 Employees. The district board may employ whatever administrative, clerical, technical and other assistance is necessary for the proper functioning of the district, on whatever terms the board considers in the best interests of the district. [Formerly 494.080]
