# Taxation of county lands sold under purchase agreement (ORS 275.240)

## At a glance

ORS 275.240 — Taxation of county lands sold under purchase agreement. Chapter 275 (COUNTY LANDS PUBLIC LANDS), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

275.240 Taxation of county lands sold under purchase agreement. Land sold under a purchase agreement in accordance with ORS 275.190

(1) is subject to taxation to the same extent as other privately owned real property. When a purchase agreement is canceled, as provided in ORS 275.220, the real property must be removed from taxation and all taxes then unpaid must be canceled. [Amended by 2005 c.243 §8]
