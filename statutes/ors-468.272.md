# Application of other laws relating to bonds (ORS 468.272)

## At a glance

ORS 468.272 — Application of other laws relating to bonds. Chapter 468 (Chapter 468), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

468.272 Application of other laws relating to bonds. Any restrictions, limitations, conditions or procedures provided by other statutes relating to the issuance and sale of bonds or other obligations do not apply to the issuance and sale of bonds authorized by ORS 468.263 to 468.272. [1974 c.34 §10; 2005 c.443 §26] Note: See note under 468.263.
