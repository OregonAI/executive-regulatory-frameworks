# Rules, generally (ORS 2.120)

## At a glance

ORS 2.120 — Rules, generally. Chapter 2 (Supreme Court; Court of Appeals), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

2.120 Rules, generally. The Supreme Court shall have power to make and enforce all rules necessary for the prompt and orderly dispatch of the business of the court, and the remanding of causes to the court below. 2.130 Rules governing original jurisdiction. The Supreme Court is empowered to prescribe and make rules governing the conduct in that court of all causes of original jurisdiction therein. 2.140 [Repealed by 1953 c.345 §3]
