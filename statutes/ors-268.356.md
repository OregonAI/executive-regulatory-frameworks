# Automatic annexation of land annexed by city within urban growth boundary (ORS 268.356)

## At a glance

ORS 268.356 — Automatic annexation of land annexed by city within urban growth boundary. Chapter 268 (METROPOLITAN SERVICE DISTRICTS), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

268.356 Automatic annexation of land annexed by city within urban growth boundary. Notwithstanding the requirements for boundary changes under ORS 268.354 and ORS chapter 198, when a city within the urban growth boundary of a metropolitan service district annexes a territory under ORS chapter 222 and that territory is within the urban growth boundary of the district, the territory is annexed to the district without further proceedings. [2025 c.322 §2]
