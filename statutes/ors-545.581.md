# Assessment installments (ORS 545.581)

## At a glance

ORS 545.581 — Assessment installments. Chapter 545 (Irrigation Districts), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

545.581 Assessment installments. When amortizing bonds are issued as authorized by ORS 545.585, the installments in which the assessments are to be paid shall be in fixed amounts including both principal and interest, and only the principal portions of the installments shall be charged against total benefits in determining benefit surplus, as defined in ORS 545.575.
