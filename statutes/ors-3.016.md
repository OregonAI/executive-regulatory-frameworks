# Special provisions for sixth judicial district (Morrow and Umatilla Counties) (ORS 3.016)

## At a glance

ORS 3.016 — Special provisions for sixth judicial district (Morrow and Umatilla Counties). Chapter 3 (Circuit Courts Generally), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

3.016 Special provisions for sixth judicial district (Morrow and Umatilla Counties). In the sixth judicial district, the judges of the district shall hold court in Pendleton, Hermiston, Milton-Freewater and Heppner as required by caseload.
