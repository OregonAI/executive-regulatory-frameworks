## At a glance

## Full text

93.260 Tax statement information required in conveyancing instrument.

(1) All instruments prepared for the purpose of conveying or contracting to convey fee title to any real estate shall contain on the face of such instruments a statement in substantially the following form: ______________________________________________________________________________ Until a change is requested, all tax statements shall be sent to the following address: ______________________________________________________________________________

(2) Failure to contain the statement required by this section does not invalidate the conveyance and if an instrument is recorded without the statement required by this section, the recording is valid.

(3) This section applies to all instruments executed after January 1, 1974. [1973 c.422 §2]
