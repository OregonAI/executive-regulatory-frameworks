# Erection and maintenance of bridges by cities (ORS 382.505)

## At a glance

ORS 382.505 — Erection and maintenance of bridges by cities. Chapter 382 (Intrastate Bridges), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

382.505 Erection and maintenance of bridges by cities. Each incorporated city in this state may erect and maintain bridges across navigable or other water within or without the boundaries of the city when found necessary or convenient and take such action and proceedings as are suitable for obtaining the approval of the proper authorities of the United States Government therefor.
