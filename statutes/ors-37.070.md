# Eligibility to serve as receiver (ORS 37.070)

## At a glance

ORS 37.070 — Eligibility to serve as receiver. Chapter 37 (Receivership), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

37.070 Eligibility to serve as receiver.

(1) Any person, whether or not a resident of this state, may serve as a receiver, except for: (a) An entity that is not authorized to conduct business in this state; (b) A person who has been convicted of a crime involving moral turpitude, or is controlled by a person who has been convicted of a crime involving moral turpitude; and (c) The sheriff of any county, except as expressly permitted by statute.

(2) If a court appoints an entity as a receiver, the court may require a specific individual to appear in the receivership on behalf of the entity. [2017 c.358 §7]
