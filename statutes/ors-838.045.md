# Regulations (ORS 838.045)

## At a glance

ORS 838.045 — Regulations. Chapter 838 (Airport Districts), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

838.045 Regulations. The district board in accordance with ORS 198.510 to 198.600 may by ordinance make, modify or abolish regulations as convenient or necessary to provide for policing or regulating the use of airports, and any facilities located at or in conjunction with airports, owned, operated, leased, maintained or controlled by the district. [Formerly 494.070]
