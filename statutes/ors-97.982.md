# Alteration of document of anatomical gift prohibited (ORS 97.982)

## At a glance

ORS 97.982 — Alteration of document of anatomical gift prohibited. Chapter 97 (Rights and Duties Relating to Cemeteries, Human Bodies and Anatomical Gifts), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

97.982 Alteration of document of anatomical gift prohibited.

(1) A person commits the crime of alteration of a document of gift if the person, in order to obtain a financial gain, intentionally falsifies, forges, conceals, defaces or obliterates a document of gift, an amendment or revocation of a document of gift or a refusal.

(2) Alteration of a document of gift is a Class C felony. [2007 c.681 §22] Note: See note under 97.951.
