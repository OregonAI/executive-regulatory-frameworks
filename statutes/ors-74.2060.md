# Transfer between banks (ORS 74.2060)

## At a glance

ORS 74.2060 — Transfer between banks. Chapter 74 (Bank Deposits and Collections), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

74.2060 Transfer between banks. Any agreed method that identifies the transferor bank is sufficient for the item’s further transfer to another bank. [1961 c.726 §74.2060; 1993 c.545 §89]
