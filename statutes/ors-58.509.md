# Transferee of shares; limit on time as director, officer or shareholder (ORS 58.509)

## At a glance

ORS 58.509 — Transferee of shares; limit on time as director, officer or shareholder. Chapter 58 (Professional Corporations), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

58.509 Transferee of shares; limit on time as director, officer or shareholder. If all of the outstanding shares of a professional corporation organized for the purpose of practicing medicine are held by an administrator, executor, personal representative, guardian, conservator or receiver of the estate of a former shareholder, or by a transferee who receives such shares by operation of law or by a judgment, such administrator, executor, personal representative, guardian, conservator, receiver or transferee may be a director, officer or shareholder of the professional corporation for a period of six months following receipt or transfer of such shares. [Formerly 58.377]
