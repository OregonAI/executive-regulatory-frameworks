# Waiver of construction standards (ORS 448.180)

## At a glance

ORS 448.180 — Waiver of construction standards. Chapter 448 (Chapter 448), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

448.180 Waiver of construction standards. The Oregon Health Authority may grant waivers on construction standards if the authority is satisfied there will be no unreasonable risk to health. [1983 c.271 §2; 2009 c.595 §851]
