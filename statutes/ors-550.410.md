# Additional cause of action for certain impairments of district works (ORS 550.410)

## At a glance

ORS 550.410 — Additional cause of action for certain impairments of district works. Chapter 550 (Urban Flood Safety and Water Quality District), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

550.410 Additional cause of action for certain impairments of district works.

(1) As used in this section, “actionable condition” means a condition on public or private property that obstructs, clogs, damages, injures, reduces the efficiency of, impairs the usefulness of or otherwise has a negative impact on the district’s works.

(2) In addition to the remedies provided under ORS 550.390 and 550.400, when an actionable condition exists, the district may bring a civil action in Multnomah County Circuit Court against the person or persons responsible for creating or maintaining the actionable condition.

(3) The civil action may include a request for one or more of the following remedies: (a) Injunctive relief; (b) Authority to enter onto the property to abate the actionable condition; and (c) Remediation costs and damages that arise from the existence or abatement of the actionable condition.
