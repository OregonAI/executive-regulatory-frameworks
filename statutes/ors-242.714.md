# Preparation of budget; appropriations (ORS 242.714)

## At a glance

ORS 242.714 — Preparation of budget; appropriations. Chapter 242 (Civil Service for City or School District Employees and Firefighters), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

242.714 Preparation of budget; appropriations.

(1) The commission shall prepare and submit a budget to the governing body of the political subdivision prior to each fiscal year for which an appropriation is needed to carry out the provisions of ORS 242.702 to 242.824.

(2) The governing body of each political subdivision shall appropriate sufficient funds to carry out the provisions of ORS 242.702 to 242.824. [1959 c.252 §43]
