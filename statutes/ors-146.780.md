# Confidentiality of records and reports (ORS 146.780)

## At a glance

ORS 146.780 — Confidentiality of records and reports. Chapter 146 (Investigations of Deaths, Injuries and Missing Persons), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

146.780 Confidentiality of records and reports. Notwithstanding the provisions of ORS 192.311 to 192.478 relating to confidentiality and accessibility for public inspection of public records, records and reports made under the provisions of ORS 146.750 are confidential and are not accessible for public inspection.
