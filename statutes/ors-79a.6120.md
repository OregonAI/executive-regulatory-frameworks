# UCC 9-612. Timeliness of notification before disposition of collateral (ORS 79A.6120)

## At a glance

ORS 79A.6120 — UCC 9-612. Timeliness of notification before disposition of collateral. Chapter 79A (Secured Transactions), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

79A.6120 UCC 9-612. Timeliness of notification before disposition of collateral.

(1) Except as otherwise provided in subsection

(2) of this section, a notification of disposition sent after default and 15 days or more before the earliest time of disposition, as set forth in the notification, is sent within a reasonable time before the disposition.

(2) In a transaction other than a consumer transaction, a notification of disposition sent after default and 10 days or more before the earliest time of disposition set forth in the notification is sent within a reasonable time before the disposition. [Formerly 79.0612]
