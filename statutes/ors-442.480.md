# Rural Health Care Revolving Account (ORS 442.480)

## At a glance

ORS 442.480 — Rural Health Care Revolving Account. Chapter 442 (Health Planning), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

442.480 Rural Health Care Revolving Account.

(1) There is established the Rural Health Care Revolving Account in the General Fund.

(2) All moneys appropriated for the purposes of ORS 442.470 to 442.507 and all moneys paid to the Office of Rural Health by reason of loans, fees, gifts or grants for the purposes of ORS 442.470 to 442.507 shall be credited to the Rural Health Care Revolving Account.

(3) All moneys contained in the Rural Health Care Revolving Account are continuously appropriated to the Oregon Department of Administrative Services for the Office of Rural Health and shall be used for the purposes of ORS 442.470 to 442.507. [1979 c.513 §3; 1987 c.660 §14; 1989 c.708 §1; 2005 c.755 §37]
