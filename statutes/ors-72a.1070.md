# Waiver or renunciation of claim or right after default (ORS 72A.1070)

## At a glance

## Full text

72A.1070 Waiver or renunciation of claim or right after default. Any claim or right arising out of an alleged default or breach of warranty may be discharged in whole or in part without consideration by a waiver or renunciation in a signed record delivered by the aggrieved party. [1989 c.676 §7; 2025 c.33 §16]
