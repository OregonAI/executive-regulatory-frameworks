# State to provide supplies and personal property for courts (ORS 1.187)

## At a glance

ORS 1.187 — State to provide supplies and personal property for courts. Chapter 1 (Courts and Judicial Officers Generally), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

1.187 State to provide supplies and personal property for courts. Except as provided in ORS 1.185

(1) and subject to applicable provisions of a plan established by the Chief Justice of the Supreme Court, the state shall provide the supplies, materials, equipment and other personal property necessary for the operation of the circuit courts. The cost of property provided by the state shall be paid by the state from funds available for the purpose. [Formerly 1.167]
