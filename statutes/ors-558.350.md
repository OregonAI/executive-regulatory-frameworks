# Employees’ retirement system (ORS 558.350)

## At a glance

ORS 558.350 — Employees’ retirement system. Chapter 558 (Weather Modification), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

558.350 Employees’ retirement system.

(1) A weather modification district organized under ORS 558.200 to 558.440 may establish an employees’ retirement system. The commissioners may enter into agreements necessary to establish the system and carry out the plan and may agree to modifications of such agreements from time to time.

(2) The retirement plan may provide for retirement benefits measured on the basis of services rendered or to be rendered by an employee, either before or after the date on which such employee first becomes a member of the retirement plan. The retirement plan may provide for a minimum of years of service and a minimum and maximum age of retirement for the employee. [1969 c.698 §56]
