# Criminal penalties (ORS 687.991)

## At a glance

ORS 687.991 — Criminal penalties. Chapter 687 (Massage Therapists; Direct Entry Midwives), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

687.991 Criminal penalties. (1)(a) Except as provided in paragraph (b) of this subsection, violation of ORS 687.021 or of any rule adopted under ORS 687.121 is a Class A misdemeanor. (b) Violation of ORS 687.021 or of any rule adopted under ORS 687.121 is a Class C felony if, at the time of the offense, the person has a prior conviction for violating ORS 687.021 or any rule adopted under ORS 687.121.

(2) Violation of ORS 687.410 is a Class A misdemeanor. [1955 c.492 §14; 1977 c.507 §15; 2003 c.547 §27; 2025 c.308 §8] _______________
