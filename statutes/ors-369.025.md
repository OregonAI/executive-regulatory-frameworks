## At a glance

## Full text

369.025 County market road within city. Notwithstanding ORS 369.020 or any action pursuant thereto, a road within a city that was a designated county market road prior to January 1, 1974, shall be on the county road system until the county surrenders jurisdiction over the road within a city and the city accepts the county order and offer of surrender in the manner prescribed in ORS 373.270.
