# Garbage used in feeding swine (ORS 600.095)

## At a glance

ORS 600.095 — Garbage used in feeding swine. Chapter 600 (Swine), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

600.095 Garbage used in feeding swine. A person may feed garbage to swine only if the garbage is:

(1) Accumulated from the domestic household of the person;

(2) Solely from a fruit or vegetable cannery, processing plant or sorting establishment;

(3) Solely from a bakery; or

(4) A combination of garbage types allowed under this section. [2001 c.21 §1] 600.100 [Amended by 1953 c.384 §7; 1971 c.78 §3; repealed by 2001 c.21 §7]
