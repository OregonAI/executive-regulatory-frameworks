# UCC 9-402. Secured party not obligated on contract of debtor or in tort (ORS 79A.4020)

## At a glance

## Full text

79A.4020 UCC 9-402. Secured party not obligated on contract of debtor or in tort. The existence of a security interest, agricultural lien, or authority given to a debtor to dispose of or use collateral, without more, does not subject a secured party to liability in contract or tort for the debtor’s acts or omissions. [Formerly 79.0402]
