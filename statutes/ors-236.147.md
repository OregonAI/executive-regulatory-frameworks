# Exception to ORS 236.145 (ORS 236.147)

## At a glance

ORS 236.147 — Exception to ORS 236.145. Chapter 236 (Eligibility; Resignations, Removals and Vacancies; Discipline; Transfers), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

236.147 Exception to ORS 236.145. ORS 236.145 does not apply to any appointee to the position of executive director of a board who serves in that capacity as an ex officio member of the board making the appointment. [1983 c.402 §6] 236.150 [Repealed by 1985 c.565 §35] 236.160 [Repealed by 1985 c.565 §36]
