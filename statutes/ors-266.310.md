## At a glance

ORS 266.310 — Officers of district; qualifications. Chapter 266 (Park and Recreation Districts), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

266.310 Officers of district; qualifications.

(1) The officers of the district shall be a board of three or five members, to be elected by the electors of the district, and a secretary, to be appointed by the board.

(2) Every elector of a district is qualified to be a member of the board or officer of the district. [Amended by 1957 c.57 §2; 1969 c.668 §11; 1983 c.83 §42; 1983 c.350 §113]
