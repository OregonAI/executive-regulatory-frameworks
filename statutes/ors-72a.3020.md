# Title to and possession of goods (ORS 72A.3020)

## At a glance

ORS 72A.3020 — Title to and possession of goods. Chapter 72A (Leases), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

72A.3020 Title to and possession of goods. Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, each provision of this chapter applies whether the lessor or a third party has title to the goods, and whether the lessor, the lessee or a third party has possession of the goods, notwithstanding any statute or rule of law that possession or the absence of possession is fraudulent. [1989 c.676 §32]
