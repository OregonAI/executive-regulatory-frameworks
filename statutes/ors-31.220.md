# Effect of publication of correction or retraction prior to demand (ORS 31.220)

## At a glance

ORS 31.220 — Effect of publication of correction or retraction prior to demand. Chapter 31 (Tort Actions), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

31.220 Effect of publication of correction or retraction prior to demand. A correction or retraction published prior to notice of demand therefor shall have the same effect as a correction or retraction after demand, if the requirements of ORS 31.215 (2),

(3) and

(4) are substantially complied with. [Formerly 30.170]
