# State as single judicial district (ORS 3.013)

## At a glance

ORS 3.013 — State as single judicial district. Chapter 3 (Circuit Courts Generally), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

3.013 State as single judicial district. For the purposes of 15 U.S.C. 1692i, the state shall be considered a single judicial district. [1997 c.340 §7]
