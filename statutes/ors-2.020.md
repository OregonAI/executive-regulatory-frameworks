# Qualifications of judges (ORS 2.020)

## At a glance

ORS 2.020 — Qualifications of judges. Chapter 2 (Supreme Court; Court of Appeals), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

2.020 Qualifications of judges.

(1) The judges of the Supreme Court shall be citizens of the United States, and shall have resided in this state at least three years next preceding their election or appointment.

(2) All persons elected judges of the Supreme Court must, at time of their election, have been admitted to practice in the Supreme Court of Oregon.
