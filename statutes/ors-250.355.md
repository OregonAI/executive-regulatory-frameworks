# Date of election (ORS 250.355)

## At a glance

ORS 250.355 — Date of election. Chapter 250 (Chapter 250), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

250.355 Date of election. If a referendum petition contains the required number of verified signatures, the election on the city measure shall be held on the next available election date in ORS 221.230 that is not sooner than the 90th day after the referendum petition was filed with the city elections officer. [1989 c.503 §35; 2007 c.155 §8]
