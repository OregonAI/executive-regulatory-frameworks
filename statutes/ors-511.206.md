# Taking food fish for commercial purposes in or near Rogue River prohibited (ORS 511.206)

## At a glance

ORS 511.206 — Taking food fish for commercial purposes in or near Rogue River prohibited. Chapter 511 (Local and Special Regulations), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

511.206 Taking food fish for commercial purposes in or near Rogue River prohibited. It is unlawful to take any food fish except shellfish for commercial purposes from the Rogue River and its tributaries and within a radius of one mile from the center of the mouth of the Rogue River.
