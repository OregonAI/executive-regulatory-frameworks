# Assessment of public benefit (ORS 60.770)

## At a glance

ORS 60.770 — Assessment of public benefit. Chapter 60 (Private Corporations), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

60.770 Assessment of public benefit. The benefit company shall assess the extent to which the benefit company provides a general public benefit and any specific public benefit identified in the benefit company’s articles of incorporation or articles of organization against a third-party standard. [2013 c.269 §11] Note: See note under 60.750. RECORDS AND REPORTS (Records)
