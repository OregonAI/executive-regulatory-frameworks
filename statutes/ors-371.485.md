# Authority of district over roads and highways (ORS 371.485)

## At a glance

ORS 371.485 — Authority of district over roads and highways. Chapter 371 (Road Districts and Road Assessment Plans), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

371.485 Authority of district over roads and highways. A road assessment district has authority over all roads and highways within the district, except state primary and secondary highways, and streets, alleys or public ways within a city within the district other than as provided by ORS 371.480. [Amended by 2003 c.802 §108] 371.490 [Repealed by 1969 c.344 §8]
