# Election laws applicable (ORS 523.625)

## At a glance

ORS 523.625 — Election laws applicable. Chapter 523 (Geothermal Heating Districts), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

523.625 Election laws applicable.

(1) ORS chapter 255 governs the following: (a) The nomination and election of commissioners. (b) The conduct of district elections.

(2) The electors of a district may exercise the powers of the initiative and referendum regarding a district measure, in accordance with ORS 255.135 to 255.205. [1983 c.350 §298]
