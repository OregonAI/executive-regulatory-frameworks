# Revocation by divorce or annulment (ORS 112.315)

## At a glance

ORS 112.315 — Revocation by divorce or annulment. Chapter 112 (Intestate Succession and Wills), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

112.315 Revocation by divorce or annulment. Unless a will evidences a different intent of the testator, the divorce or annulment of the marriage of the testator after the execution of the will revokes all provisions in the will in favor of the former spouse of the testator and any provision in the will naming the former spouse as personal representative, and the effect of the will is the same as though the former spouse did not survive the testator.
