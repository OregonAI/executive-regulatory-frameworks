# Cooperative agreements with federal National Guard Bureau; rules (ORS 396.337)

## At a glance

ORS 396.337 — Cooperative agreements with federal National Guard Bureau; rules. Chapter 396 (Militia Generally), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

396.337 Cooperative agreements with federal National Guard Bureau; rules.

(1) The Oregon Military Department may enter into cooperative agreements with the federal National Guard Bureau.

(2) The department may select any available payment method option for a cooperative agreement, including the advance payment method.

(3) If the department selects the advance payment method for a cooperative agreement, the department may not incur a funding obligation that is payable or reimbursable from federal moneys under the cooperative agreement unless moneys sufficient to meet the obligation are available in the State Treasury and: (a) Are available for expenditure by the department; and (b) Are not committed or encumbered for any other purpose.

(4) The department may adopt rules as necessary to maintain the eligibility of the state to receive advance payments under cooperative agreements with the federal National Guard Bureau. [2019 c.367 §1] Note: 396.337 and 396.338 were enacted into law by the Legislative Assembly but were not added to or made a part of ORS chapter 396 or any series therein by legislative action. See Preface to Oregon Revised Statutes for further explanation.
