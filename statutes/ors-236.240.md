# Removal of county treasurer from office (ORS 236.240)

## At a glance

ORS 236.240 — Removal of county treasurer from office. Chapter 236 (Eligibility; Resignations, Removals and Vacancies; Discipline; Transfers), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

236.240 Removal of county treasurer from office. Whenever suit has been commenced on the official bond of any delinquent treasurer, the delinquent treasurer may be removed by the county court of the county. 236.250 [Repealed by 1983 c.310 §21 and 1983 c.327 §16]
