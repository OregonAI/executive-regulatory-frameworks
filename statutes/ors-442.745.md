## Full text

442.745 Disclosure of confidential information not waiver of right to protect information. If parties to a cooperative program agreement provide the Director of the Oregon Health Authority with written or oral information that is confidential or otherwise protected from disclosure under Oregon law, the disclosures shall not be considered a waiver of any right to protect the information from disclosure in other proceedings. [1993 c.769 §10; 2009 c.595 §763]
