# Persons ineligible for office because of membership in certain organizations (ORS 236.030)

## At a glance

ORS 236.030 — Persons ineligible for office because of membership in certain organizations. Chapter 236 (Eligibility; Resignations, Removals and Vacancies; Discipline; Transfers), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

236.030 Persons ineligible for office because of membership in certain organizations.

(1) No person who is a member of, or affiliated with, any organization which teaches the doctrine of, or advocates, the overthrow of the Government of the United States by force or violence shall be a candidate for public office or eligible for appointment to a public office.

(2) The name of a person defined in subsection

(1) of this section shall not be placed upon any ballot in connection with any election.
