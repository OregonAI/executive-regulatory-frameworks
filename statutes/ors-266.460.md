# District attorney to aid board; special counsel (ORS 266.460)

## At a glance

ORS 266.460 — District attorney to aid board; special counsel. Chapter 266 (Park and Recreation Districts), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

266.460 District attorney to aid board; special counsel. The district board may call upon the district attorney for advice as to any district business. The district attorney shall give advice when called on therefor by the board. The board may at any time employ special counsel for any purpose. [Amended by 1969 c.668 §20; 1971 c.268 §14] 266.470 [Amended by 1969 c.668 §21; 1971 c.268 §15; 1973 c.220 §2; repealed by 2011 c.597 §118]
