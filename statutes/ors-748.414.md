# Funds exempt from certain taxes (ORS 748.414)

## At a glance

ORS 748.414 — Funds exempt from certain taxes. Chapter 748 (Fraternal Benefit Societies), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

748.414 Funds exempt from certain taxes. Every society organized or licensed under this chapter is hereby declared to be a charitable and benevolent institution, and all of its funds shall be exempt from all and every state, county, district, municipal and school tax, other than taxes on real estate and office equipment.
