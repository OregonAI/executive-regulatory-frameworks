# Requirements for unsurveyed and unmonumented parcels on plats (ORS 92.055)

## At a glance

ORS 92.055 — Requirements for unsurveyed and unmonumented parcels on plats. Chapter 92 (Subdivisions and Partitions), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

92.055 Requirements for unsurveyed and unmonumented parcels on plats.

(1) A parcel larger than 10 acres that is created outside an urban growth boundary is not required to be surveyed and monumented and shall comply with the following: (a) The approximate acreage of each unsurveyed parcel shall be shown; and (b) Any unsurveyed parcel shall have the words “unsurveyed” placed in bold letters adjacent to the parcel number.

(2) Unsurveyed parcels need not comply with ORS 92.050 (5),

(7) and (8). [1995 c.382 §2; 1999 c.1018 §2; 2005 c.399 §6] Note: 92.055 was added to and made a part of 92.010 to 92.192 by legislative action but was not added to any smaller series therein. See Preface to Oregon Revised Statutes for further explanation.
