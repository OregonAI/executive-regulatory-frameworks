# Necessity of stating presumptions of law and matters judicially noticed (ORS 135.713)

## At a glance

ORS 135.713 — Necessity of stating presumptions of law and matters judicially noticed. Chapter 135 (Arraignment and Pretrial Provisions), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

135.713 Necessity of stating presumptions of law and matters judicially noticed. Neither presumptions of law nor matters of which judicial notice is taken need be stated in an accusatory instrument. [Formerly 132.570]
