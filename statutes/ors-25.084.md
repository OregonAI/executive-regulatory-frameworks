# Assignment of rights or written application required for services; incorporation into request for support or support judgment (ORS 25.084)

## At a glance

## Full text

25.084 Assignment of rights or written application required for services; incorporation into request for support or support judgment.

(1) The administrator may provide child support services as described in ORS 25.080 only if support rights have been assigned to the state or if a person has provided a written application to the administrator that: (a) Is signed by the person; and (b) Indicates that the person is applying for child support services.

(2) An application to enroll in services with the Oregon Child Support Program may be incorporated into a motion or petition that requests child support or a support judgment that provides for payment to the Department of Justice under ORS 25.020. [2009 c.352 §2; 2011 c.318 §5; 2019 c.291 §3; 2025 c.99 §12] Note: 25.084 was added to and made a part of ORS chapter 25 by legislative action but was not added to any smaller series therein.
