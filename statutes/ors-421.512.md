# Rulemaking (ORS 421.512)

## At a glance

ORS 421.512 — Rulemaking. Chapter 421 (Department of Corrections Institutions; Compacts), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

421.512 Rulemaking.

(1) The Department of Corrections shall adopt rules to carry out the provisions of ORS 421.504, 421.506, 421.507, 421.508 and 421.509.

(2) The Oregon Criminal Justice Commission shall adopt or amend rules as necessary to integrate the programs into sentencing guidelines. [1993 c.681 §5; 2003 c.464 §6; 2021 c.501 §7] Note: See note under 421.500. FAMILY SENTENCING ALTERNATIVE PROGRAM
