# “County court” defined (ORS 202.010)

## At a glance

ORS 202.010 — “County court” defined. Chapter 202 (Establishment of New Counties; Change of Boundaries), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

202.010 “County court” defined. As used in this chapter, unless the context requires otherwise, the term “county court” includes board of county commissioners.
