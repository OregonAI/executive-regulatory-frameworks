# Right to immediate possession in condemnation proceeding (ORS 545.245)

## At a glance

ORS 545.245 — Right to immediate possession in condemnation proceeding. Chapter 545 (Irrigation Districts), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

545.245 Right to immediate possession in condemnation proceeding. At any time after the board of directors of an irrigation district or board of supervisors of a drainage district has commenced proceedings to acquire title to any land necessary for rights of way, or for construction, alteration, repair or reservoir purposes, the district may enter into possession of the land and begin such work as may be necessary to the development of the district.
