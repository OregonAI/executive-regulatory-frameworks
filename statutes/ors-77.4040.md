# No liability for good faith delivery pursuant to document of title WAREHOUSE RECEIPTS AND BILLS OF LADING: (ORS 77.4040)

## At a glance

ORS 77.4040 — No liability for good faith delivery pursuant to document of title WAREHOUSE RECEIPTS AND BILLS OF LADING:. Chapter 77 (Warehouse Receipts, Bills of Lading and Other Documents of Title), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

77.4040 No liability for good faith delivery pursuant to document of title. A bailee that in good faith has received goods and delivered or otherwise disposed of the goods according to the terms of a document of title or pursuant to this chapter is not liable for the goods even if:

(1) The person from which the bailee received the goods did not have authority to procure the document or to dispose of the goods; or

(2) The person to which the bailee delivered the goods did not have authority to receive the goods. [1961 c.726 §77.4040; 2009 c.181 §76]
