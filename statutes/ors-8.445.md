# Fees (ORS 8.445)

## At a glance

ORS 8.445 — Fees. Chapter 8 (Court Officers and District Attorneys), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

8.445 Fees.

(1) The State Court Administrator shall establish and collect reasonable fees for applications, examinations, certifications and renewals of certification authorized by ORS 8.415 to 8.455.

(2) Fees collected under this section are nonrefundable.

(3) The fees established under this section may not exceed the cost of administering and enforcing ORS 8.415 to 8.455. [Formerly 703.410; 2003 c.737 §99; 2013 c.155 §6; 2013 c.470 §5]
