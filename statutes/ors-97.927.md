# Applicability of ORS 97.923 to 97.949 (ORS 97.927)

## At a glance

ORS 97.927 — Applicability of ORS 97.923 to 97.949. Chapter 97 (Rights and Duties Relating to Cemeteries, Human Bodies and Anatomical Gifts), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

97.927 Applicability of ORS 97.923 to 97.949.

(1) ORS 97.923 to 97.949, 97.992, 97.994 and 692.180 do not apply to a nonprofit memorial society charging less than a $100 membership fee.

(2) Notwithstanding subsection

(1) of this section, ORS 97.937 applies to a nonprofit memorial society charging less than a $100 membership fee. [Formerly 128.407; 2003 c.362 §2; 2007 c.661 §14; 2012 c.7 §3] Note: See note under 97.923.
