# Taking striped bass prohibited (ORS 511.016)

## At a glance

ORS 511.016 — Taking striped bass prohibited. Chapter 511 (Local and Special Regulations), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

511.016 Taking striped bass prohibited. It is unlawful to take striped bass for commercial purposes from any of the waters of this state. [1973 c.500 §2; 1975 c.416 §1] 511.055 [1957 c.1 §§1,2,3 (an Act proposed by an initiative petition approved at the general election on November 6, 1956); 1959 c.125 §1; repealed by 1965 c.570 §152]
