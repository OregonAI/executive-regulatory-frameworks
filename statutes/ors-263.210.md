# Creation of sports and convention facilities commission by ballot measure (ORS 263.210)

## At a glance

ORS 263.210 — Creation of sports and convention facilities commission by ballot measure. Chapter 263 (Convention Facilities), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

263.210 Creation of sports and convention facilities commission by ballot measure.

(1) The governing body of a county may submit to a vote by the electors of that county a proposition for the creation of a sports and convention facilities commission.

(2) The ballot measure shall specify: (a) The name of the commission, which shall be “The Multi-Event and Convention Facilities Commission of _____ County, Oregon” or other similar distinctive name. (b) The number of commissioners, which shall be nine.

(3) Upon the approval by the voters of such a ballot measure in a county-wide election, the commission shall be deemed established as a municipal corporation of this state and as a body corporate and politic exercising public powers. [1985 c.654 §1; subsections
