# Release of lien or return of money (ORS 87.181)

## At a glance

ORS 87.181 — Release of lien or return of money. Chapter 87 (Statutory Liens), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

87.181 Release of lien or return of money. The county recording officer shall record a written release of the lien for the storage of the chattel or the county treasurer in whose office money is deposited under ORS 87.177

(1) to

(3) shall return the money to the person who made the deposit when:

(1) The person who filed the bond or deposited the money presents a certified copy of a court’s order for the release of the bond or all or some of the money to that person; or

(2) The person who filed the bond or deposited the money presents a written release of lien signed by the lien claimant. [2003 c.193 §7] Note: See note under 87.177.
