# Expenditure of general road fund on city streets and bridges (ORS 368.722)

## At a glance

## Full text

368.722 Expenditure of general road fund on city streets and bridges. Counties may expend funds received by the general road fund pursuant to ORS 294.060 on city streets and bridges under such terms and conditions as the county may determine pursuant to the provisions of ORS 373.260.
