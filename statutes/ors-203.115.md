# County power to change fees (ORS 203.115)

## At a glance

ORS 203.115 — County power to change fees. Chapter 203 (County Governing Bodies; County Home Rule), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

203.115 County power to change fees. A county governing body shall not change the amount of a fee it has set pursuant to statute within six months after setting that fee.
