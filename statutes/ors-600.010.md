# Definitions (ORS 600.010)

## At a glance

ORS 600.010 — Definitions. Chapter 600 (Swine), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

600.010 Definitions. As used in ORS 600.095 and 600.105:

(1) “Garbage” means waste accumulations of vegetable matter.

(2) “Offal” means animal tissue of any kind and from any source.
