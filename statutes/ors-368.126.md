# Order establishing new road along existing road to identify parts of existing road to be vacated (ORS 368.126)

## At a glance

ORS 368.126 — Order establishing new road along existing road to identify parts of existing road to be vacated. Chapter 368 (County Roads), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

368.126 Order establishing new road along existing road to identify parts of existing road to be vacated. When a county governing body establishes a new public road following the general alignment of an existing public road, the final order or resolution shall identify all parts of any existing road that are to be vacated. Vacation of those parts described is effective without any other proceedings. A road so vacated shall not be closed to public use until the road laid out to replace it is actually opened to travel. [Formerly 368.540] 368.130 [Repealed by 1981 c.153 §79]
