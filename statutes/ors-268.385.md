# District as regional planning coordinator (ORS 268.385)

## At a glance

ORS 268.385 — District as regional planning coordinator. Chapter 268 (METROPOLITAN SERVICE DISTRICTS), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

268.385 District as regional planning coordinator.

(1) For the purposes of ORS 195.025, the district formed under this chapter shall exercise within the district the review, advisory and coordinative functions assigned under ORS 195.025

(1) to each county and city that is within the district.

(2) ORS 195.025

(3) and

(4) shall not apply to a district formed under this chapter. [1977 c.665 §19]
