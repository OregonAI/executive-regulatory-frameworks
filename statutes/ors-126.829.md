# Written acknowledgment of delivery as receipt (ORS 126.829)

## At a glance

ORS 126.829 — Written acknowledgment of delivery as receipt. Chapter 126 (Property Held for the Benefit of Minors; Uniform Transfers to Minors Act), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

126.829 Written acknowledgment of delivery as receipt. A written acknowledgment of delivery by a custodian constitutes a sufficient receipt and discharge for custodial property transferred to the custodian pursuant to ORS 126.805 to 126.886. [1985 c.665 §9] 126.830 [1959 c.640 §6; 1973 c.823 §111; repealed by 1985 c.665 §27]
