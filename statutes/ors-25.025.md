# Annual notice to parties receiving services under ORS 25.020 (ORS 25.025)

## At a glance

ORS 25.025 — Annual notice to parties receiving services under ORS 25.020. Chapter 25 (Child Support Services), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

25.025 Annual notice to parties receiving services under ORS 25.020. Once each year, the Department of Justice shall notify the parties in child support cases receiving services under ORS 25.020 of all the following:

(1) When physical custody of a child changes from the obligee to the obligor, the obligation to pay child support for the child is not automatically terminated.

(2) When a physical change of custody of a child occurs, either party may request a modification of the support order to terminate support based on a substantial change of circumstances.

(3) At the request of either party, child support may be established for the parent with current physical custody of the child.

(4) If a change in the physical custody of a child is temporary, the obligee may satisfy support accruing for the child for periods that the child is in the physical custody of the obligor as provided in ORS 18.225, 18.228, 18.232 and 18.235. [1997 c.385 §4; 2003 c.73 §18; 2003 c.576 §575] Note: 25.025 was enacted into law by the Legislative Assembly but was not added to or made a part of ORS chapter 25 or any series therein by legislative action. See Preface to Oregon Revised Statutes for further explanation.
