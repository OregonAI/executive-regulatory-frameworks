# Application of ORS chapter 255 (ORS 555.535)

## At a glance

ORS 555.535 — Application of ORS chapter 255. Chapter 555 (Reclamation Projects; Sand Control), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

555.535 Application of ORS chapter 255.

(1) ORS chapter 255 governs: (a) The nomination and election of district board members of a sand control district. (b) The conduct of sand control district elections.

(2) The electors of the district may exercise the initiative and referendum powers for a district measure as provided in ORS 255.135 to 255.205. [2015 c.560 §6]
