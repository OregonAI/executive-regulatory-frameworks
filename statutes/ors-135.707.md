# Discharge as bar to prosecution (ORS 135.707)

## At a glance

ORS 135.707 — Discharge as bar to prosecution. Chapter 135 (Arraignment and Pretrial Provisions), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

135.707 Discharge as bar to prosecution. A judgment entered under ORS 135.705 is a bar to another prosecution for the same crime. [Formerly 134.030; 2009 c.484 §10]
