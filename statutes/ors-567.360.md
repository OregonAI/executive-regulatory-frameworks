## Full text

567.360 Crop rotation experiments and demonstrations in Umatilla County. The director of the Oregon experiment stations may conduct in Umatilla County crop rotation experimental and demonstration work for the purpose of establishing a profitable cropping system that will reduce the summer fallow acreage, decrease the present size of the farm unit necessary to make a living and stabilize land values. 567.365 [Repealed by 2015 c.554 §4 and 2015 c.767 §4] 567.370 [Repealed by 1955 c.770 §1] 567.375 [Repealed by 1955 c.770 §1]
