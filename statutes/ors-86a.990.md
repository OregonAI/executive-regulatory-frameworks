# Criminal penalties for violations of ORS 86A.095 to 86A.198 (ORS 86A.990)

## At a glance

ORS 86A.990 — Criminal penalties for violations of ORS 86A.095 to 86A.198. Chapter 86A (Mortgage Lending), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

86A.990 Criminal penalties for violations of ORS 86A.095 to 86A.198.

(1) A person that knowingly violates a provision of ORS 86A.095 to 86A.198 or a rule adopted by the Director of the Department of Consumer and Business Services under ORS 86A.095 to 86A.198, except ORS 86A.145 (2), commits a Class C felony.

(2) Violation of ORS 86A.145

(2) is a Class A misdemeanor. [Formerly 59.992]
