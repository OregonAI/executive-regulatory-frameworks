# Justices of the peace given jurisdiction (ORS 565.650)

## At a glance

ORS 565.650 — Justices of the peace given jurisdiction. Chapter 565 (Fairs and Exhibits), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

565.650 Justices of the peace given jurisdiction. Justices of the peace shall have jurisdiction of all offenses against the provisions of ORS 565.610 to 565.640. MISCELLANEOUS
