# Affirmative defense to ORS 475.969, 475.971, 475.975 (1) and 475.976 (1) (ORS 475.980)

## At a glance

ORS 475.980 — Affirmative defense to ORS 475.969, 475.971, 475.975 (1) and 475.976 (1). Chapter 475 (Chapter 475), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

475.980 Affirmative defense to ORS 475.969, 475.971, 475.975

(1) and 475.976 (1). It is an affirmative defense to a charge of violating ORS 475.969, 475.971, 475.975

(1) or 475.976

(1) that the person possessed the precursor substance for a lawful purpose. [2001 c.615 §17]
