# Military or overseas elector procedures to conform to procedures for absent elector ballots (ORS 253.515)

## At a glance

ORS 253.515 — Military or overseas elector procedures to conform to procedures for absent elector ballots. Chapter 253 (Absent Electors), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

253.515 Military or overseas elector procedures to conform to procedures for absent elector ballots. Except as otherwise provided in ORS 253.500 to 253.640, procedures relating to military or overseas electors’ ballots shall be as nearly as possible the same as procedures for other absent electors’ ballots. [1979 c.190 §218; 1985 c.720 §4; 2013 c.520 §6] 253.520 [1955 c.332 §3; 1969 c.261 §4; repealed by 1979 c.190 §431]
