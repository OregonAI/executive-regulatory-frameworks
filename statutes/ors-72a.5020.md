# Notice after default (ORS 72A.5020)

## At a glance

ORS 72A.5020 — Notice after default. Chapter 72A (Leases), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

72A.5020 Notice after default. Except as otherwise provided in this chapter or the lease agreement, the lessor or lessee in default under the lease contract is not entitled to notice of default or notice of enforcement from the other party to the lease agreement. [1989 c.676 §49]
