# Definitions for ORS 92.830 to 92.845 (ORS 92.830)

## At a glance

ORS 92.830 — Definitions for ORS 92.830 to 92.845. Chapter 92 (Subdivisions and Partitions), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

92.830 Definitions for ORS 92.830 to 92.845. As used in ORS 92.830 to 92.845, unless the context requires otherwise:

(1) “Declarant” means a person who makes a declaration pursuant to ORS 92.845.

(2) “Lot” has the meaning given that term in ORS 92.010.

(3) “Manufactured dwelling” has the meaning given that term in ORS 90.100.

(4) “Manufactured dwelling park” and “mobile home park” have the meanings given those terms in ORS 446.003.

(5) “Person” has the meaning given that term in ORS 92.305.

(6) “Tenant” means a person who owns and occupies as a residence a manufactured dwelling or mobile home on a rented space in a manufactured dwelling park or mobile home park. [2001 c.711 §1; 2003 c.474 §5; 2011 c.503 §12] Note: 92.830 to 92.845 were enacted into law by the Legislative Assembly but were not added to or made a part of ORS chapter 92 or any series therein by legislative action. See Preface to Oregon Revised Statutes for further explanation.
