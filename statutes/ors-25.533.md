# Relief from compliance with order (ORS 25.533)

## At a glance

ORS 25.533 — Relief from compliance with order. Chapter 25 (Child Support Services), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

25.533 Relief from compliance with order. The court may, upon such terms as may be just at any time within one year after notice thereof, relieve a parent from an administrative order taken against that parent because of mistake, inadvertence, surprise or excusable neglect. [Formerly 416.465]
