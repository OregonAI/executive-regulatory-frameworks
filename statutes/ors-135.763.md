# Trial within 90 days of notice unless continuance granted (ORS 135.763)

## At a glance

ORS 135.763 — Trial within 90 days of notice unless continuance granted. Chapter 135 (Arraignment and Pretrial Provisions), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

135.763 Trial within 90 days of notice unless continuance granted.

(1) The district attorney, after receiving a notice requesting trial under ORS 135.760, shall, within 90 days of receipt of the notice, bring the adult in custody to trial upon the pending charge.

(2) The court shall grant any reasonable continuance with the consent of the defendant. Notwithstanding the defendant’s lack of consent, the court may grant a continuance on motion of the district attorney or on its own motion, for good cause shown. The fact of imprisonment is not good cause for the purposes of this subsection. [Formerly 134.520; 1993 c.542 §1; 2019 c.213 §16]
