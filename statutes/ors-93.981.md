# Effect of divorce or annulment on transfer on death deed (ORS 93.981)

## At a glance

ORS 93.981 — Effect of divorce or annulment on transfer on death deed. Chapter 93 (Conveyancing and Recording), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

93.981 Effect of divorce or annulment on transfer on death deed. Unless a transfer on death deed provided for under ORS 93.948 to 93.979 evidences a different intent of the transferor, the divorce or annulment of the marriage of the transferor after the recording of the transfer on death deed revokes all provisions in the transfer on death deed in favor of the former spouse of the transferor and the effect of the transfer on death deed is the same as though the former spouse did not survive the transferor. [2011 c.212 §19] Note: See note under 93.948.
