# Military Family Appreciation Day (ORS 396.595)

## At a glance

ORS 396.595 — Military Family Appreciation Day. Chapter 396 (Militia Generally), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

396.595 Military Family Appreciation Day. May 8 of each year shall be known as Military Family Appreciation Day. [2009 c.84 §1] Note: 396.595 was enacted into law by the Legislative Assembly but was not added to or made a part of ORS chapter 396 or any series therein by legislative action. See Preface to Oregon Revised Statutes for further explanation. 396.600 [2011 c.727 §1; repealed by 2017 c.532 §19] _______________
