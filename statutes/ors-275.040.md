# Conveyance pursuant to ORS 275.030 (ORS 275.040)

## At a glance

ORS 275.040 — Conveyance pursuant to ORS 275.030. Chapter 275 (COUNTY LANDS PUBLIC LANDS), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

275.040 Conveyance pursuant to ORS 275.030. The conveyance transferring the real estate sold pursuant to ORS 275.030 to the purchaser thereof shall contain the date of the order authorizing such sale and the page and journal where the order is entered. The conveyance shall be signed by the county judge or the chairperson of the board of county commissioners and acknowledged in the manner provided by law for acknowledgment of other conveyances of real estate. A conveyance so made conveys all the interest of the county in the property described therein. [Amended by 1983 c.537 §2] 275.050 [Repealed by 1983 c.537 §7]
