# Costs, expenses and charges (ORS 117.095)

## At a glance

ORS 117.095 — Costs, expenses and charges. Chapter 117 (Estates of Absentees), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

117.095 Costs, expenses and charges. The costs, expenses and charges attending the granting of letters and their revocation shall be paid out of the estate of the absentee. If the petition for administration is not granted, the petitioner shall pay the costs, expenses and charges.
