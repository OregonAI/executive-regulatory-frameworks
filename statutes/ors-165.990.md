# Penalties (ORS 165.990)

## At a glance

ORS 165.990 — Penalties. Chapter 165 (Offenses Involving Fraud or Deception), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

165.990 Penalties. Violation of ORS 165.692 is a Class C felony. Criminal prosecution of violators of ORS 165.692 must be commenced within five years after the commission of the crime. [Formerly 757.992; subsection

(4) of 1995 Edition enacted as 1995 c.496 §4; 2011 c.597 §161] _______________
