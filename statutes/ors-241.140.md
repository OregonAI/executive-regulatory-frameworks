# Conclusiveness of orders, decisions, rules or regulations; necessity for unanimity (ORS 241.140)

## At a glance

ORS 241.140 — Conclusiveness of orders, decisions, rules or regulations; necessity for unanimity. Chapter 241 (Civil Service for County Employees), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

241.140 Conclusiveness of orders, decisions, rules or regulations; necessity for unanimity. No informality in any proceeding or hearing, or in the manner of taking testimony before the commission, or designated commissioner, shall invalidate any order, decision, rule or regulation made, approved or confirmed by the commission. However, no order, decision, rule or regulation made by any designated commissioner conducting any hearing or investigation alone shall be of any force or effect whatsoever unless and until concurred in by the other two members of the commission.
