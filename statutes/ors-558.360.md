# Employee contribution (ORS 558.360)

## At a glance

ORS 558.360 — Employee contribution. Chapter 558 (Weather Modification), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

558.360 Employee contribution. The district may collect, as a contribution from any employee, that percentage of the salary received by the employee which is necessary to fund on an actuarial reserve basis the cost of retirement benefits which the employee is required to provide pursuant to the provisions of a retirement plan. [1969 c.698 §58]
