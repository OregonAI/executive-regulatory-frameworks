# Waiver or renunciation of claim or right after breach (ORS 71.3060)

## At a glance

ORS 71.3060 — Waiver or renunciation of claim or right after breach. Chapter 71 (General Provisions for Uniform Commercial Code), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

71.3060 Waiver or renunciation of claim or right after breach. A claim or right arising out of an alleged breach may be discharged in whole or in part without consideration by agreement of the aggrieved party in a signed record. [2009 c.181 §19; 2025 c.33 §5] Note: See note under 71.3010.
