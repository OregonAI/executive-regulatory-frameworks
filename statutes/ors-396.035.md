# Construction against implied repeal (ORS 396.035)

## At a glance

ORS 396.035 — Construction against implied repeal. Chapter 396 (Militia Generally), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

396.035 Construction against implied repeal. This chapter and ORS chapters 398 and 399 being a general law intended as a unified coverage of its subject matter, no part of them shall be deemed to be impliedly repealed by subsequent legislation if such construction can reasonably be avoided. [1961 c.454 §2] 396.040 [Repealed by 1961 c.454 §213]
