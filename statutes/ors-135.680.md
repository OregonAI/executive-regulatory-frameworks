# Procedure if resubmission of case not allowed (ORS 135.680)

## At a glance

ORS 135.680 — Procedure if resubmission of case not allowed. Chapter 135 (Arraignment and Pretrial Provisions), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

135.680 Procedure if resubmission of case not allowed. If the court does not allow the case to be resubmitted or an amended complaint or information filed, the defendant, if in custody, shall be discharged. If the defendant has been released, the release agreement shall be discharged. If the defendant has deposited any security, the security shall be returned to the defendant as provided by law. [Amended by 1973 c.836 §187]
