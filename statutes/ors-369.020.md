# Designation of county market roads as ways of public easement (ORS 369.020)

## At a glance

## Full text

369.020 Designation of county market roads as ways of public easement. On or before January 1, 1974, county courts of the respective counties or city councils of the respective cities may take action to designate former “county market roads” as ways of public easement. If no designation is made, such roads shall become a part of the respective county or city road systems as of that date.
