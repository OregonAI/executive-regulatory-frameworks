# Bonds as obligation of a political subdivision (ORS 268.650)

## At a glance

ORS 268.650 — Bonds as obligation of a political subdivision. Chapter 268 (METROPOLITAN SERVICE DISTRICTS), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

268.650 Bonds as obligation of a political subdivision. Revenue bonds, including refunding revenue bonds and bond anticipation notes issued under ORS 268.600 to 268.660, shall be considered to be bonds or obligations of a political subdivision of the State of Oregon for the purposes of all laws of the state. [1977 c.95 §14; 1987 c.623 §5]
