# Allocation of interim distributions (ORS 63.195)

## At a glance

ORS 63.195 — Allocation of interim distributions. Chapter 63 (Limited Liability Companies), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

63.195 Allocation of interim distributions. Distributions of cash or other assets of a limited liability company before the dissolution and winding up of the limited liability company shall be allocated among the members, and among classes of members, in the manner provided in the articles of organization or any operating agreement. If neither the articles of organization nor any operating agreement provides for such allocations, such distributions shall be allocated among the members in proportion to their right to share in the profits of the limited liability company. [1993 c.173 §39]
