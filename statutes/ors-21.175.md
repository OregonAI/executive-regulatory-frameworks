# Guardianship filing fees (ORS 21.175)

## At a glance

ORS 21.175 — Guardianship filing fees. Chapter 21 (State Court Fees), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

21.175 Guardianship filing fees.

(1) A circuit court shall collect the filing fee established under ORS 21.145 for the filing of the initial documents in a guardianship proceeding and for filing an appearance in a guardianship proceeding.

(2) The fees established under this section apply to county courts exercising probate jurisdiction. [2011 c.595 §27]
