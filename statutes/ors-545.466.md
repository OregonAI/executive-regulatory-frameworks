# Effect of ORS 545.462 and 545.464 on existing laws (Exemptions) (ORS 545.466)

## At a glance

ORS 545.466 — Effect of ORS 545.462 and 545.464 on existing laws (Exemptions). Chapter 545 (Irrigation Districts), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

545.466 Effect of ORS 545.462 and 545.464 on existing laws. ORS 545.462 and 545.464 do not repeal or amend any existing law relating to the manner of collection of unpaid irrigation district assessments. [Amended by 1995 c.42 §105] (Exemptions)
