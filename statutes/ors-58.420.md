# Effective time and date of document (ORS 58.420)

## At a glance

ORS 58.420 — Effective time and date of document. Chapter 58 (Professional Corporations), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

58.420 Effective time and date of document.

(1) Except as provided in subsection

(2) of this section and ORS 58.430, a document accepted for filing is effective on the date it is filed by the Secretary of State and at the time, if any, specified in the document as its effective time.

(2) If a document specifies a delayed effective time and date, the document becomes effective at the time and date specified. If a document specifies a delayed effective date but no time, the document becomes effective on that date. A delayed effective date for a document may not be later than the 90th day after the date it is filed. [1987 c.94 §7]
