# Electronic materials not subject to public records law (ORS 56.100)

## At a glance

ORS 56.100 — Electronic materials not subject to public records law. Chapter 56 (Duties of Secretary of State), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

56.100 Electronic materials not subject to public records law. Except as the Secretary of State provides by rule, the following are not public records for the purposes of ORS 192.311 to 192.478:

(1) Electronic data processing programs of the office; and

(2) Electronic media used to record, process or store documents filed with the office under the business registry functions of the office. [1983 c.717 §2a; 1987 c.414 §61] 56.105 [1987 c.843 §13; repealed by 2007 c.186 §21]
