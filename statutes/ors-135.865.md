# Effect of failure to comply with discovery requirements (ORS 135.865)

## At a glance

ORS 135.865 — Effect of failure to comply with discovery requirements. Chapter 135 (Arraignment and Pretrial Provisions), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

135.865 Effect of failure to comply with discovery requirements. Upon being apprised of any breach of the duty imposed by the provisions of ORS 135.805 to 135.873 and 135.970, the court may order the violating party to permit inspection of the material, or grant a continuance, or refuse to permit the witness to testify, or refuse to receive in evidence the material not disclosed, or enter such other order as it considers appropriate. [1973 c.836 §219; 1999 c.304 §6] 135.870 [Amended by 1971 c.743 §321; repealed by 1973 c.836 §358]
