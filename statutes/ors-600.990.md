# Penalties (ORS 600.990)

## At a glance

ORS 600.990 — Penalties. Chapter 600 (Swine), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

600.990 Penalties. Subject to ORS 153.022, notwithstanding ORS 596.990, violation of ORS 600.095, a quarantine imposed under ORS 600.105 or a rule or regulation of the State Department of Agriculture relating to the feeding of swine is a Class A misdemeanor. [Amended by 1999 c.1051 §319; 2001 c.21 §5; 2011 c.597 §244] _______________
