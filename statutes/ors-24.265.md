# Scope of application of ORS 24.260 to 24.335 (ORS 24.265)

## At a glance

ORS 24.265 — Scope of application of ORS 24.260 to 24.335. Chapter 24 (Enforcement and Recognition of Foreign Judgments; Foreign-Money Claims), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

24.265 Scope of application of ORS 24.260 to 24.335.

(1) ORS 24.260 to 24.335 apply only to a foreign-money claim in an action or distribution proceeding.

(2) ORS 24.260 to 24.335 apply to foreign-money issues even if other law under the conflict of laws rules of this state applies to other issues in the action or distribution proceeding. [1991 c.202 §3]
