# Surrender of custody under ORS 135.775 (ORS 135.785)

## At a glance

## Full text

135.785 Surrender of custody under ORS 135.775. The official in charge of a Department of Corrections institution in this state shall give over the person of any adult in custody thereof whenever so required by the operation of the Agreement on Detainers. [Formerly 134.645; 1987 c.320 §21; 2019 c.213 §21]
