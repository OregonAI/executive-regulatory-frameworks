# Filing boundary change with county assessor and Department of Revenue (ORS 547.482)

## At a glance

ORS 547.482 — Filing boundary change with county assessor and Department of Revenue. Chapter 547 (Drainage Districts), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

547.482 Filing boundary change with county assessor and Department of Revenue. For purposes of ad valorem taxation, a boundary change must be filed in final approved form with the county assessor and the Department of Revenue as provided in ORS 308.225. [2001 c.138 §42] Note: 547.482 was added to and made a part of ORS chapter 547 by legislative action but was not added to any smaller series therein. See Preface to Oregon Revised Statutes for further explanation.
