# Place of confinement (ORS 398.060)

## At a glance

ORS 398.060 — Place of confinement. Chapter 398 (Military Justice), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

398.060 Place of confinement.

(1) Confinement other than in an authorized military confinement facility, whether before, during or after trial by a military court, shall be executed in county jails, penitentiaries or prisons designated by the Governor or by such persons as the Governor may authorize to act.

(2) If no designation is made under subsection

(1) of this section, the person shall be confined in a county jail.

(3) Confinement under this section shall be without requirement of payment of any fee or charge for confining the person.
