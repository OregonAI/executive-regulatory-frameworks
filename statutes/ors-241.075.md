# Term of office and removal of commissioners (ORS 241.075)

## At a glance

ORS 241.075 — Term of office and removal of commissioners. Chapter 241 (Civil Service for County Employees), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

241.075 Term of office and removal of commissioners.

(1) The term of office of each commissioner is six years with the term of one commissioner expiring every two years.

(2) Any commissioner may be removed from office by the board of county commissioners for incompetency, incompatibility or dereliction of duty, or other good cause.
