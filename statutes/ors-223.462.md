# Review of reassessment (ORS 223.462)

## At a glance

ORS 223.462 — Review of reassessment. Chapter 223 (Local Improvements and Works Generally), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

223.462 Review of reassessment. Notwithstanding any of the provisions of ORS 223.405 to 223.485, owners of any property against which a reassessment for local improvements has been imposed may seek a review thereof under the provisions of ORS 34.010 to 34.100.
