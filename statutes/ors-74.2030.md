# Effect of instructions (ORS 74.2030)

## At a glance

ORS 74.2030 — Effect of instructions. Chapter 74 (Bank Deposits and Collections), 2025 Edition.

## Full text

74.2030 Effect of instructions. Subject to ORS 73.0420 concerning conversion of instruments and ORS chapter 73 and this chapter concerning restrictive indorsements, only a collecting bank’s transferor can give instructions that affect the bank or constitute notice to it, and a collecting bank is not liable to prior parties for any action taken pursuant to the instructions or in accordance with any agreement with its transferor. [1961 c.726 §74.2030; 1993 c.545 §86]
