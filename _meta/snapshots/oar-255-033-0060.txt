 Board of Parole and Post-Prison Supervision 
 Chapter 255 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 33 RELEASE HEARINGS FOR ADULTS IN CUSTODY WHEN OFFENSE COMMITTED AS JUVENILE 
 255-033-0060 
 Notices Following Hearing 
 
 Following a Board decision under this section, the Board shall send written notice of the Board's order to the AIC, district attorney of the committing jurisdiction, and Board registered victims. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 144.397 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 144.397 
 
 History:
