 Chapter 813 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 20 BOND RESIDENTIAL LOAN PROGRAM & FLEX LENDING FIRSTHOME PRODUCT 
 813-020-0110 
 Servicing Manual 
 
 The Oregon Bond Residential Loan Program Servicing Manual, is incorporated into and adopted as part of this division of administrative rules, by reference. The Servicing Manual be accessed online at the OHCS website. The Servicing Manual is dated December 2025.
