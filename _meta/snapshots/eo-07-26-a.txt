    EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 07 - 26

    AMENDMENT TO EXECUTIVE ORDER 07 - 21

    Governor's Task Force on Federal Forest Payments and County Services.

    Executive Order No. 07 ~21 is amended to increase the membership of the Task
    Force to 19 members and appoint Anne Ballew, Springfield City Councilor, as a
    member of the Task Force.
