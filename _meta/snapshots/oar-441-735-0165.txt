 Department of Consumer and Business Services 
 Finance and Securities Regulation - Chapter 441 
 
 
 
 
 Division 735 PAYDAY AND TITLE LENDING 
 441-735-0165 
 Unfair or Deceptive Practices 
 
 A licensee must not disguise the terms or provisions of any loan as a device or subterfuge to evade the requirements and fees and interest authorized by ORS 725A.010 through 725A.990. Such conduct will be deemed a violation of 725A.010, 725A.062, and 725A.064, and dishonest, fraudulent, or illegal practices under 725A.082. 
 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 725A.092 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 725A.010, 725A.026 & 725A.062
