 Department of Environmental Quality 
 Chapter 340 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 273 OREGON CLIMATE PROTECTION PROGRAM 
 340-273-8120 
 Severability 
 
 Each requirement of this division is severable, and if any requirement of this division is held invalid, the remainder of the requirements of this division will continue in full force and effect. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 468.020, 468A.025 & 468A.040 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 468.020, 468A.025, 468A.040, 468.035, 468A.010, 468A.015 & 468A.045 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 DEQ 18-2024, adopt filed 11/22/2024, effective 11/22/2024
