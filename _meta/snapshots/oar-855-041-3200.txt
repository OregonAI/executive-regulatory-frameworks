 Board of Pharmacy 
 Chapter 855 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 41 OPERATION OF PHARMACIES 
 855-041-3200 
 Telework: Purpose and Scope 
 
 The purpose of OAR 855-041-3200 through OAR 855-041-3250 is to provide minimum requirements for pharmacy services conducted via telework. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 689.135, ORS 689.151 & ORS 689.205 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 689.155 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 BP 37-2021, adopt filed 12/16/2021, effective 12/16/2021
