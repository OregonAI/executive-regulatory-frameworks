 Columbia River Gorge Commission 
 Chapter 350 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 82 LAND USE ORDINANCE 
 350-082-0050 
 Dates are Calendar Days 
 
 All dates contained in this land use ordinance are “calendar days.” When a deadline for accomplishing an act in this land use ordinance falls on a weekend or legal holiday, the deadline shall be the next business day, and all following deadlines shall be calculated from that deadline. A legal holiday is any day in which the United States Postal Service does not deliver mail or when the Gorge Commission is closed due to weather, natural disaster, or decision of the Executive Director. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 196.150, RCW 43.97.015, 16 USC § 544e(c) & 16 USC § 544f(l) 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 196.150, RCW 43.97.015, 16 USC § 544e(c) & 16 USC § 544f(l)
