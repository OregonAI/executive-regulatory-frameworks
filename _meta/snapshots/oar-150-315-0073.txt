 Department of Revenue 
 Chapter 150 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 315 PERSONAL INCOME TAX CREDITS 
 150-315-0073 
 Filing and Corrections of Agricultural Employer Overtime Credit Application. 
 
 (1) Agricultural employer overtime tax credit applications must be submitted electronically with the department. 

 (2) Corrections. 

 (a) An applicant may correct information submitted to the department no later than January 31 following the calendar year for which the applicant seeks credit. 

 (b) A corrected application must be submitted electronically with the department. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 305.100 & 2022 Oregon Laws Chapter 115, Section 9 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  2022 Oregon Laws Chapter 115 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 REV 26-2023, adopt filed 12/26/2023, effective 01/01/2024
