 Oregon Housing and Community Services Department 
 Chapter 813 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 270 OREGON REHOUSING INITIATIVE (ORI) 
 813-270-0085 
 Remedies 
 
 (1) OHCS reserves the right to identify deficiencies in the performance of any grantee or their subrecipients discovered during compliance monitoring activities and take remedial action upon such grantees as provided in the grant agreement, including, but not limited to, terminating its funding agreement with a grantee or subrecipient and requiring repayment of ORI funding.  

 (2) Upon identifying a deficiency in performance or compliance, OHCS may exercise any and all remedies set forth in the grant agreement. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 456.555 & 456.559 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 458.390, 458.392 & 456.561
