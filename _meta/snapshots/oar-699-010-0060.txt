 Oregon Watershed Enhancement Board 
 Oregon Watershed Enhancement Board, Environmental Restoration Council - Chapter 699 
 
 
 
 
 Division 10 Tribal Nation Natural Resource Program Fund 
 699-010-0060 
 Deference to Tribal Nations Determinations of Eligible Use of Funds 
 
 Tribes are sovereign nations, possessing experience and expertise in stewarding natural resources. As such, their determinations that Tribal Nation Natural Resource Program Funds are committed to eligible uses shall be given a high degree of deference by the Council. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  541.879 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  541.857 - 541.882 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 ERC 2-2026, adopt filed 07/22/2026, effective 07/22/2026
