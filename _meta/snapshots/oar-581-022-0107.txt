 Oregon Department of Education 
 Chapter 581 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 22 STANDARDS FOR PUBLIC ELEMENTARY AND SECONDARY SCHOOLS 
 581-022-0107 
 Operational Plans for the 2022-23 School Year 
 
 School districts and public charter schools must periodically submit to the Department a plan for operation during the 2022-23 school year. The plan must be submitted on a form and on a timeline to be determined by the Department. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 326.051 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 326.051 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 ODE 34-2022, adopt filed 06/23/2022, effective 06/23/2022
