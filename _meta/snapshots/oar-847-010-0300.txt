 Oregon Medical Board 
 Chapter 847 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 10 GENERAL 
 847-010-0300 
 Access to Mental Health Assessment, Treatment, or Services 
 
 Licensees may not deny any individual access to mental health assessment, treatment, or services on the basis that the individual also has an intellectual or developmental disability. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 677.265 & OL 2024, chapter 96, section 5 (SB 1557) as amended by OL 2025, chapter 313, section 1 (SB 729) 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  OL 2024, chapter 96, section 5 (SB 1557) as amended by OL 2025, chapter 313, section 1 (SB 729) 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 OMB 12-2025, adopt filed 10/08/2025, effective 10/08/2025
