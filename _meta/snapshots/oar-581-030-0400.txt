 Oregon Department of Education 
 Chapter 581 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 30 STANDARDS FOR APPROVED RECOVERY SCHOOLS 
 581-030-0400 
 Approved Recovery School Performance Evaluation 
 
 The Oregon Department of Education may evaluate the performance of an approved recovery school according to prevailing Oregon Department of Education Guidance. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 336.680 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 336.680 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 ODE 52-2024, adopt filed 10/25/2024, effective 10/25/2024
