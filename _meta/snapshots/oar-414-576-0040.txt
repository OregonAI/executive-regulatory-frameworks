 Department of Early Learning and Care 
 Chapter 414 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 576 BIRTH THROUGH FIVE LITERACY FUND 
 414-576-0040 
 Evaluation 
 
 The Department will submit a progress report on the Birth Through Five Literacy Plan to the interim committees of the Legislative Assembly related to early childhood no later than December 31 of each year.  

 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 327.839 -327.841  
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 327.839 -327.841  
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 DELC 9-2025, adopt filed 12/17/2025, effective 01/01/2026
