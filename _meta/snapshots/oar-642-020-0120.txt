 Department of Agriculture 
 Oregon Mint Commission - Chapter 642 
 
 
 
 
 Division 20 RATES FOR COMMISSIONER PER DIEM AND REIMBURSEMENT 
 642-020-0120 
 Non-Qualified Members 
 
 (1) In accordance with the procedures set forth in OAR 642-020-0130, and only if there are funds available in the budget for such compensation, the Oregon Mint Commission may provide to a non-qualified member of the Oregon Mint Commission who is not employed in full-time public service per diem compensation equal to the per diem paid to members of the Legislative Assembly under ORS 171.072, for each day or portion thereof during which the non-qualified member is actually engaged in the performance of official duties. 

 (2) In accordance with the procedures set forth in OAR 642-020-0140, the Oregon Mint Commission shall provide to a non-qualified member of the Oregon Mint Commission reimbursement of actual and necessary travel or other expenses actually incurred in the performance of a non-qualified member’s official duties within the limits provided in Oregon Accounting Manual Policy 40.10.00. 

 (3) A non-qualified member of the Oregon Mint Commission may decline to accept compensation or reimbursement of expenses related to the non-qualified member’s service on the Oregon Mint Commission. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 576.265 & ORS 576.304 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 292.495 & ORS 576.265
