 Columbia River Gorge Commission 
 Chapter 350 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 12 PUBLIC RECORDS 
 350-012-0090 
 Enforcement 
 
 Enforcement of this division is governed by the judicial review provisions of the Columbia River Gorge National Scenic Area Act, 16 U.S.C. § 544m, and applicable court rules. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 196.150, RCW 43.97.015, 16 U.S.C. 544c(b) & 16 U.S.C. 544m 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 196.150, RCW 43.97.015, 16 U.S.C. 544c(b) & 16 U.S.C. 544m 
 
 History:
