 Department of Energy 
 Energy Facility Siting Council - Chapter 345 
 
 
 
 
 Division 15 PROCEDURES GOVERNING COUNCIL AND DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY PROCEEDINGS, INCLUDING SITE CERTIFICATE HEARINGS 
 345-015-0440 
 Burden of Presenting Evidence 
 
 In a contested case regarding an application for a site certificate or amendment to a site certificate, each party or limited party bears the burden of presenting evidence in support of facts that party or limited party alleges and/or positions they take on any issue they submitted a petition for party or limited party status and on which the hearing officer determines they may participate. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 183.341, ORS 183.417, ORS 469.370(5), ORS 183.417(2) & ORS 469.470 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 183.450
