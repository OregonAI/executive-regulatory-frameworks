 Oregon Housing and Community Services Department 
 Chapter 813 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 375 MODULAR HOUSING FUND 
 813-375-0021 
 ELIGIBLE GRANT USES 
 
 New or expanded capacity uses shall demonstrate that funding will boost current or new production of modular home or component by, but not necessarily limited to, the following activities: 

 (1) Increasing manufacturing space; 

 (2) Purchasing equipment; 

 (3) Standardizing design; 

 (4) Developing infrastructure; 

 (5) Enhancing transportation; and 

 (6) Making advancements in materials and technology. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  HB 2761 (2023) 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  HB 2761 (2023)
