 Department of Consumer and Business Services 
 Insurance Regulation - Chapter 836 
 
 
 
 
 Division 200 DEPARTMENT REGULATORY PROGRAMS 
 836-200-0715 
 Eligibility Criteria 
 
 To qualify for premium assistance, an applicant must: 

 (1) Be an eligible entity as defined in Oregon Laws 2025, chapter 600, section 1(1)(a). 

 (2) Demonstrate ownership or operational control of eligible property, such as affordable housing dwellings, shelter locations, Project Turnkey sites, and navigation centers. 

 (3) Provide documentation of paid property or liability insurance premiums for eligible property. 

 (4) Maintain active property and liability insurance coverage for the duration of the assistance period. 

 (5) Non-profit applicants must provide documentation sufficient to establish their non-profit status. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 731.244 & Or Laws 2025, ch 600 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 746.100, ORS 746.110 & ORS 746.240
