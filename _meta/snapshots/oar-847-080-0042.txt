 Oregon Medical Board 
 Chapter 847 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 80 PODIATRIC MEDICINE 
 847-080-0042 
 Practice of Podiatry 
 
 Podiatric physicians and surgeons practice podiatry as defined in ORS 677.010 and within their individual education, training, and experience. Podiatric physicians and surgeons are held to the standard and duty of care as described in ORS chapter 677. 
  

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 677.265 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 677.805 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 OMB 8-2024, adopt filed 01/08/2024, effective 01/08/2024
