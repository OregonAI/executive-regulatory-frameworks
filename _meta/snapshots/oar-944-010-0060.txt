 Oregon Health Authority 
 Drug Treatment and Recovery Services - Chapter 944 
 
 
 
 
 Division 10 Behavioral Health Resource Networks 
 944-010-0060 
 Harm Reduction Services, Information and Education 
 
 (1) Harm reduction services provided by an entity funded under ORS 430.389(2) must be low-barrier and must reduce the negative individual and public health outcomes of substance use and substance related harm.  

 (2) Harm reduction services provided by a funded entity can include but are not limited to: 

 (a) Access to naloxone; 

 (b) Sterile syringes; 

 (c) Safer use and wound care supplies; 

 (d) Substance use-related infectious disease screening; 

 (e) Sobering support; 

 (f) Contingency management; 

 (g) Drug checking supplies; and 

 (h) Information and education about harm reduction services. 

 (i)  Referral for Hepatitis, HIV, STI, COVID-19, and Tuberculosis (TB) testing, vaccine, or other healthcare services. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 430.389, 430.390 & 430.391 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 430.383 & 430.392
