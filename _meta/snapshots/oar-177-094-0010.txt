 Oregon State Lottery 
 Chapter 177 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 94 WIN FOR LIFE 
 177-094-0010 
 Game Description 
 
 (1) Win for Life is a Lottery game based on fifteen horizontal sets of numbers.  (2) Each of the fifteen sets of numbers that match the winning numbers as prescribed by OAR 177-094-0080(1) selected at each drawing on the date(s) indicated on the player’s ticket determine any prize amount won. 
 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  OR Const. Art. XV, Sec. 4(4) & ORS 461 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 461
