 Oregon State Treasury 
 Chapter 170 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 140 UNCLAIMED PROPERTY CLAIMS 
 170-140-0000 
 Purpose 
 
 These rules establish: 

 (1) Fees required or allowed under ORS chapter 98; and 

 (2) Requirements and procedures for making a Claim to recover Property. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 98.422 & ORS 178.050 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 98.422 & ORS 178.050 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 OST 2-2021, adopt filed 06/29/2021, effective 07/01/2021
