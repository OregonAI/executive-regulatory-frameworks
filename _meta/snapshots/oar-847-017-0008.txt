 Oregon Medical Board 
 Chapter 847 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 17 OFFICE-BASED SURGERY 
 847-017-0008 
 Standard of Practice 
 
 A licensee performing office-based surgery must have received appropriate training and education in the safe and effective performance of all surgical procedures performed in the office. Such training and education should include:  (1) Indications and contraindications for each procedure;  (2) Identification and selection of appropriate patients for each procedure;  (3) Identification of realistic and expected outcomes of each procedure;  (4) Selection, maintenance, and utilization of products and equipment;  (5) Appropriate technique for each procedure, including infection control and safety precautions;  (6) Pharmacological intervention specific to each procedure;  (7) Identification of complications and adverse reactions for each procedure;  (8) Standards in surgical medical care; and  (9) Emergency procedures to be used in the event of:  (a) Complications;  (b) Adverse reactions; or  (c) Equipment malfunction. 
 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 677.265 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 677.265
