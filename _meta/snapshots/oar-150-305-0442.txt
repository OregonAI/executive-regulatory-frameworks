 Department of Revenue 
 Chapter 150 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 305 GENERAL ADMINISTRATION OF REVENUE LAWS 
 150-305-0442 
 Reimbursement for Hiring a Substitute 
 
 (1) As used in OAR 150-305-0406(1), “other expenses” includes expenses incurred by a member of the Commission or Council in employing a substitute to carry out duties, including personal duties, normally performed by the member, which the member is unable to carry out because of the performance of official duties and which, by the nature of such duties, cannot be delayed without risk to health or safety.   

 (2) The amount that a member may be reimbursed for expenses incurred in employing a substitute must not exceed $25 per day, pursuant to ORS 292.495(3). 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 305.100, 292.495 & 305.695(4) 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 292.495 & 305.695(4)
