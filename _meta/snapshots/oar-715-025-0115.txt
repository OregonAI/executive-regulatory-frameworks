 Higher Education Coordinating Commission 
 Chapter 715 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 25 Common Course Numbering and Credit Transfer 
 715-025-0115 
 OFFERING OF NEW OR MODIFICATION OF EXISTING COURSES BY INSTITUTIONS 
 
 (1) An institution shall notify the Transfer Council when it is seeking: 

 (a) to offer a course that is similar in course description and course learning outcomes to a course for which there is an adopted Common Course Numbering Articulation Policy (CCNAP) or CCNAP under consideration by the Transfer Council, but that is not subject to a CCNAP; or 

 (b) to modify a course that would have the effect of causing the institution to be out of compliance with obligations under a CCNAP. 

 (2) The Transfer Council shall, within one year, determine: 

 (a) Whether the institution may offer such course as proposed; 

 (b) Whether the institution may not offer such course unless it adheres to the terms of an adopted CCNAP or a CCNAP under consideration; or 

 (c) Whether an existing CCNAP shall be modified to account for such course. 

 (3) An institution shall notify the Transfer Council when it is seeking to offer a course for which it intends to adhere to the terms of an adopted CCNAP and provide any documentation required by the Council. Upon notification and the receipt of required documentation, the Council shall include the institution in such CCNAP. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 350.395, ORS 350.429 & ORS 350.423 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 350.395 & ORS 350.429
