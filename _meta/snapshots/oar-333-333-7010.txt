 Oregon Health Authority 
 Public Health Division - Chapter 333 
 
 
 
 
 Division 333 PSILOCYBIN 
 333-333-7010 
 Psilocybin Testing: Purpose 
 
 (1) The purpose of OAR 333-333-7010 to OAR 333-333-7150 is to establish minimum compliance testing standards for psilocybin products. 

 (2) A person licensed under ORS 475A.290 or 475A.305may not transfer, accept or provide a psilocybin product unless it has been sampled and tested in accordance with OAR 333-333-7010 to OAR 333-333-7150. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 475A.235 & ORS 475A.590 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 475A.235 & ORS 475A.590
