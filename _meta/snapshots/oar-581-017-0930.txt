 Oregon Department of Education 
 Chapter 581 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 17 STRATEGIC INVESTMENTS 
 581-017-0930 
 Reporting 
 
 (1) Grant recipients must report on the grant in the manner and form required by the Department of Education. 

 (2) The Department may require grant recipients to submit a final report prior to receiving their final request for funds. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  HB 4084 (2024) 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  HB 4084 (2024)
