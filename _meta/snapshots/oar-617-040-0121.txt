 Department of Agriculture 
 Oregon Dairy Products Commission - Chapter 617 
 
 
 
 
 Division 40 PER DIEM COMPENSATION, REIMBURSEMENT OF TRAVEL AND OTHER EXPENSES AND REIMBURSEMENT FOR HIRING A SUBSTITUTE 
 617-040-0121 
 COMMISSIONER PER DIEM AND REIMBURSEMENTS - Non-Qualified Members 
 
 (1) In accordance with the procedures set forth in OAR 617-04-0130, and only if there are funds available in the budget for such compensation, the Oregon Dairy Products Commission shall provide to a non-qualified member of the Oregon Dairy Products Commission who is not employed in full-time public service per diem compensation equal to the per diem paid to members of the Legislative Assembly under ORS 171.072, for each day or portion thereof during which the non-qualified member is actually engaged in the performance of official duties. 

 (2) In accordance with the procedures set forth in OAR 617-040-0140, the Oregon Dairy Products Commission shall provide to a non-qualified member of the Oregon Dairy Products Commission reimbursement of actual and necessary travel or other expenses actually incurred in the performance of a non-qualified member’s official duties within the limits provided in Oregon Accounting Manual Policy 40.10.00. 

 (3) A non-qualified member of the Oregon Dairy Products Commission may decline to accept compensation or reimbursement of expenses related to the non-qualified member’s service on the Oregon Dairy Products Commission. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 576.265 & ORS 576.304 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 576.265 & ORS 292.495 
 
 History:
