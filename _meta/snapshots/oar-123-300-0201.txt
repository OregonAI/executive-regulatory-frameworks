 Oregon Business Development Department 
 Chapter 123 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 300 COMMUNITY LENDER LOAN LOSS ACCOUNT PROGRAM 
 123-300-0201 
 Authority to Implement the Program 
 
 The manager of the Department’s Business Finance programs, or their designee, may execute documents reasonably necessary or convenient to implement and administer the Program. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 285A.075 
 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 OBDD 9-2023, adopt filed 06/13/2023, effective 06/13/2023
