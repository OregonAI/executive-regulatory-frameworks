 Board of Pharmacy 
 Chapter 855 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 143 PHARMACY PRESCRIPTION LOCKER 
 855-143-0215 
 Outlet: Pharmacist Utilization 
 
 A PPL and its PPL Affiliated Pharmacy must ensure that a prescription drug or device is not released from the PPL until the Oregon licensed Pharmacist or Intern has: 

 (1) Provided counseling when required under OAR 855-115-0145 or when requested by the patient or patient’s agent; and 

 (2) Documented the interaction.  

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 689.205 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 689.155 & ORS 689.527
