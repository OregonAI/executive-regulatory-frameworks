 Oregon Housing and Community Services Department 
 Chapter 813 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 52 HOMEOWNERSHIP DEVELOPMENT INCUBATOR PROGRAM (HDIP) 
 813-052-0000 
 General Purpose 
 
 The rules of OAR 813, division 52 establish and implement the Homeownership Development Incubator Program (HDIP), the purpose of which is to provide grants and low-interest loans to create new homeownership opportunities for Oregonians. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 456.555 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 456.561
