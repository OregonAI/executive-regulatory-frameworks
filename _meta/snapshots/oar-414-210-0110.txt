 Department of Early Learning and Care 
 Chapter 414 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 210 REGISTERED FAMILY CHILD CARE HOMES (EFFECTIVE JULY 2025) 
 414-210-0110 
 Purpose 
 
 (1)    A registered family child care home is defined as a child care facility that is registered to provide child care for a number of children up to the maximum capacity in a residential setting. 
(2)    The purpose of OAR 414-210-0100 through 414-210-1620 is to protect the health, safety, and wellbeing of children when cared for outside their own homes by providing requirements for inspecting, registering, monitoring and otherwise regulating care in a registered family child care home.  
(3)    An individual may not operate a registered family child care home without a valid registration issued by CCLD, unless providing care not requiring a license as provided in OAR 414-075-0250.  
  

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 329A.260 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 329A.330 
 
 History:
