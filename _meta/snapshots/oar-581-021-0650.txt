 Oregon Department of Education 
 Chapter 581 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 21 SCHOOL GOVERNANCE AND STUDENT CONDUCT 
 581-021-0650 
 Definitions for OAR 581-021-0650 to 581-021-0665 
 
 Under OAR 581-021-0650 to 581-021-0665: 

 (1) “Discrimination” has the meaning given that term in ORS 659.850. 

 (2) “District” means a school district or a public charter school. 

 (3) “Public charter school” means an elementary or secondary charter school offering a comprehensive instructional program under a written agreement entered into between a sponsor and an applicant pursuant to ORS chapter 338. 

 (4) “Sponsor” means: 

 (a) The board of the school district that has developed a written charter to create a public charter school. 

 (b) The State Board of Education pursuant to ORS 338.075. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 332.505, ORS 338.115, ORS 659.855 & ORS 326.051 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 332.505, ORS 338.115, ORS 659.855 & ORS 326.051
