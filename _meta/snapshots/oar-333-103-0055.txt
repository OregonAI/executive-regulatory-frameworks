 Oregon Health Authority 
 Public Health Division - Chapter 333 
 
 
 
 
 Division 103 RADIATION PROTECTION SERVICES FEES 
 333-103-0055 
 X-ray and Tanning Machine Vendor License Fee 
 
 (1) Persons shall obtain a vendor license for radiation machine and tanning services pursuant to OAR 333-101-0020 and 333-119-0200 and shall pay an annual validation fee of $500 to obtain or maintain the license. 

 (2) Upon written request and approval by the Authority, fees for new licenses may be prorated on a quarterly basis for the current calendar year. 

 (3) The licensing fee shall be due and payable on or before January 1 of each year and the Authority shall issue the licensee a certificate of validation. 

 (a) The licensee shall retain the certificate of validation during the current calendar year; and 

 (b) The licensee shall make available to the registrant receiving services a copy of the current certificate of validation as evidence of being currently licensed prior to initiating services pursuant to OAR 333-101-0005. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 453.757 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 453.757
