 Department of Consumer and Business Services 
 Building Codes Division - Chapter 918 
 
 
 
 
 Division 480 ONE- AND TWO-FAMILY DWELLING SPECIALTY CODE 
 918-480-0015 
 Oregon Housing Needs Analysis Visitability Definition 
 
 For the purpose of the Oregon House Needs Analysis, unless context requires otherwise, “Visitability” means a dwelling unit in a structure constructed under the Oregon Residential Specialty Code, that meets the provisions for Type C units in ICC A117.1 (2017). 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  Oregon Laws 2023, Chapter 13, Section 5 (House Bill 2001) 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  Oregon Laws 2023, Chapter 13, Section 5 (House Bill 2001)
