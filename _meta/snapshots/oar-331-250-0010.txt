 Oregon Health Authority 
 Health Licensing Office - Chapter 331 
 
 
 
 
 Division 250 GENETIC COUNSELING GENERAL ADMINISTRATION 
 331-250-0010 
 Definitions 
 
 The following definitions apply to OAR 331-200-0010 to OAR 331-210-0010: 

 (1) “ABGC” means the American Board of Genetic Counseling.  

 (2) “ABMGG” means the American Board of Medical Genetics and Genomics.  

 (3) “ACGC” Means the Accreditation Council for Genetic Counseling. 

 (4) “Genetic counseling” has the meaning set forth in House Bill 2619 (2021). 

 (5) “Good standing” means no unresolved or outstanding disciplinary actions. 

 (6) “Licensed genetic counselor” means a licensed genetic counselor as defined in House Bill 2619 (2021).  

 (7) “Office” means the Health Licensing Office.  

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  2021 House Bill 2619 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  2021 House Bill 2619
