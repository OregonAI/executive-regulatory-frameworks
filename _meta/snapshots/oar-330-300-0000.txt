 Department of Energy 
 Chapter 330 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 300 BUILDING ENERGY PERFORMANCE STANDARDS 
 330-300-0000 
 Purpose and applicability 
 
 (1) The purpose of the rules in Division 330-300 is to implement the Building Energy Performance Standard adopted under House Bill 3409 (2023), Sections 8 through 17, and codified as Oregon Revised Statutes 469.275-469.291. 

 (2) The rules in Division 330-300 apply to the owners of covered commercial buildings as defined in Oregon Revised Statutes 469.275. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 469.275-.291 & ORS 469.040 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 469.275-.291 
 
 History:
