 Oregon Housing and Community Services Department 
 Chapter 813 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 135 LOCAL INNOVATION AND FAST TRACK (LIFT) 
 813-135-0025 
 Local Innovation and Fast Track Homeownership Program Manual 
 
 (1) The Local Innovation and Fast Track (LIFT) Manual for Homeownership Development dated July 31, 2026, describes the requirements and guidelines for homeownership projects. 

 (2) The manuals may be accessed online at OHCS’s website. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 456.515 – 456.725 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 458.480-458.490
