 Oregon Business Development Department 
 Chapter 123 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 100 INDUSTRIAL SITE LOAN FUND PROGRAM 
 123-100-0000 
 Purpose and Objectives 
 
 The purpose of these rules is to implement the Industrial Site Loan Fund Program (“Program”), established to provide patient capital loans or forgivable loans to eligible Project Sponsors to support Industrial Land development in Oregon.  These rules are adopted pursuant to ORS 285B.034 to carry out the provisions of ORS 285B.032 through 285B.046. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 285B.034 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 285B.032 - 285B.046 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 OBDD 13-2026, temporary adopt filed 06/22/2026, effective 06/22/2026 through 12/18/2026
