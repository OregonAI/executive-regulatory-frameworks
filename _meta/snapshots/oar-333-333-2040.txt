 Public Health Division - Chapter 333 
 
 
 
 
 Division 333 PSILOCYBIN 
 333-333-2040 
 Pesticides 
 
 Manufacturers are prohibited from applying pesticides to fungi or growing medium. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 475A.235 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 475A.235 & ORS 475A.290
