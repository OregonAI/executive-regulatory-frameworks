 Teacher Standards and Practices Commission 
 Chapter 584 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 435 STATE STANDARDS FOR PERSONNEL SERVICE PREPARATION PROGRAMS 
 584-435-0010 
 Effective Date and Supersedure 
 
 Effective August 1, 2022, programs for personnel service will be issued under the rules set forth in this division, which supersedes preceding divisions OAR 584-018 & 584-400 in case of conflict. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 342.138, ORS 342.147 & ORS 342.165 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 342.138 & ORS 342.147 
 
 History:
