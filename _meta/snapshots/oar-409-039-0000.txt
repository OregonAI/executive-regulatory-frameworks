 Oregon Health Authority 
 Health Policy and Analytics - Chapter 409 
 
 
 
 
 Division 39 TEMPORARY HEALTH CARE STAFF RATE SETTING PROGRAM 
 409-039-0000 
 Purpose 
 
 These rules (OAR 409-039-0000 to 409-039-0120) establish requirements related to the maximum rates that a temporary staffing agency may charge to or receive from a hospital or facility that engages the temporary staffing agency. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 676.716 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 676.716 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 OHP 5-2024, adopt filed 11/22/2024, effective 11/25/2024
