 Oregon Health Authority 
 Public Health Division - Chapter 333 
 
 
 
 
 Division 333 PSILOCYBIN 
 333-333-2015 
 Allowable Species 
 
 A manufacturer may only cultivate, manufacture or possess fruiting bodies of the fungi species Psilocybe cubensis and psilocybin products derived from Psilocybe Cubensis on the licensed premises . 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 475A.235 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 475A.235
