 Department of Agriculture 
 Chapter 603 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 48 INDUSTRIAL HEMP 
 603-048-2342 
 Compliance Testing Requirements for Industrial Hemp-Derived Vapor Items 
 
 A handler must ensure that every process lot of an industrial hemp-derived vapor item for use by a consumer is tested as described in OAR 333-007-0342 prior to sale, transfer, or offering for sale or transfer. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 561.190, ORS 571.263, ORS 571.281 & ORS 571.330 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 571.263, ORS 571.281 & ORS 571.330
