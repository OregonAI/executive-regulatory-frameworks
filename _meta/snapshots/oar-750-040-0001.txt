 State Board of Towing 
 Chapter 750 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 40 Violations. 
 750-040-0001 
 Violations. 
 
 The State Board of Towing may impose a civil penalty, sanctions, or take other disciplinary action under ORS 822.265: for violation of ORS 98.853, 98.854, 98.856, 98.858, 181A.350, 822.200, 822.215, 822.225, 822.230, 822.235 or 822.605 and other laws and rules assigned to the Board for administration. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 822.265, ORS 822.280, ORS 822.290 & ORS 822.995 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 822.265, ORS 822.280, ORS 822.290 & ORS 822.995 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 SBOT 1-2023, adopt filed 10/31/2023, effective 01/01/2024
