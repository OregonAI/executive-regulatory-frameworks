 Department of Corrections 
 Chapter 291 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 131 MAIL (AIC) 
 291-131-0032 
 Corrections Ombudsman Mail 
 
 Mail that is addressed to or from the Corrections Ombudsman shall be processed in accordance with the requirements of ORS 423.440. Specifically, a letter to the Corrections Ombudsman from an adult in custody shall be forwarded immediately, unopened, to the Corrections Ombudsman. A letter from the Corrections Ombudsman to the adult in custody shall be immediately delivered, unopened, to the adult in custody. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 179.040, 423.020, 423.030, 423.075 & 423.400 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 179.040, 423.020, 423.030, 423.075 & 423.400 
 
 History:
