 Land Conservation and Development Department 
 Chapter 660 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 24 URBAN GROWTH BOUNDARIES 
 660-024-0001 
 Applicability of Division in Specified Circumstances 
 
 The rules in this division adopted on December 6, 2025, are effective January 1, 2026, except that a local government may adopt a plan amendment concerning the amendment of an urban growth boundary that conforms to the applicable rules of this division in place prior to January 1, 2026, if the local government submits the adopted plan amendment as provided in OAR 660-018-0040 or OAR 660-025-0175 to the department on or before January 1, 2027. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 197.040 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 197A.018, ORS 197A.270 & ORS 197A.280 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 LCDD 6-2026, temporary adopt filed 07/06/2026, effective 07/07/2026 through 01/01/2027
