 Department of Agriculture 
 Oregon Strawberry Commission - Chapter 668 
 
 
 
 
 Division 40 REIMBURSEMENT OF EXPENSES 
 668-040-0110 
 COMMISSIONER PER DIEM AND REIMBURSEMENTS Qualified Members 
 
 (1) In accordance with the procedures set forth in OAR 669-030-0130 and OAR 669-030-0140, the Oregon Strawberry Commission shall provide to a qualified member of the Oregon Strawberry Commission: 

 (a) Per diem compensation equal to the per diem paid to members of the Legislative Assembly under ORS 171.072, for each day or portion thereof during which the qualified member is actually engaged in the performance of official duties; and 

 (b) Reimbursement of actual and necessary travel or other expenses actually incurred in the performance of a member’s official duties within the limits provided in Oregon Accounting Manual Policy 40.10.00. 

 (2) A qualified member of the Oregon Strawberry Commission may decline to accept compensation or reimbursement of expenses related to the qualified member’s service on the Oregon Strawberry Commission. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 576.265 & ORS 576.304 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 576.265 & ORS 292.495
