 Department of State Police 
 Oregon State Athletic Commission - Chapter 230 
 
 
 
 
 Division 145 CONDUCT OF BOUT- COMBAT JIU JITSU 
 230-145-0009 
 Intentional Fouls 
 
 (1) If the referee determines that a bout may not continue because of an injury caused by an intentional foul, the competitor who committed the intentional foul loses by disqualification. The competitor's purse, if applicable, may be withheld from payment. The disposition of the purse, civil penalty or license action to be imposed upon the competitor shall be determined by action of the superintendent under ORS 463.113(3) and ORS 463.185. 

 (2) If the referee determines that a bout may continue despite an injury caused by an intentional foul, the referee shall immediately inform an authorized representative of the superintendent. 

 (3) If an injury caused by an intentional foul results in the bout being stopped in an overtime round: 

 (a) The bout will be declared a Technical Decision. The injured competitor wins by technical decision, if said competitor was ahead in the overtime rounds by escape time or first submission. 

 (b) The bout must be declared a technical draw, if the injured competitor is behind or even in the overtime rounds by escape time or down a submission in the bottom of the inning. 

 (4) If a competitor injures themselves while attempting to foul their opponent, the referee shall not take any action in their favor and the injury must be treated the same as any injury produced by a fair blow. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 463.113 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 463.113
