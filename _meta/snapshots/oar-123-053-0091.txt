 Oregon Business Development Department 
 Chapter 123 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 53 HOUSING INFRASTRUCTURE FINANCE PROGRAM 
 123-053-0091 
 Remedies 
 
 The Authority may invoke remedies for an “event of default” as described in the contract with the Award recipient, including but not limited to the following: 

 (1) Withholding of amounts otherwise due to the Award recipient; 

 (2) Barring the Award recipient from applying for future awards; 

 (3) Demanding return of any Award disbursed and any interest earned on the disbursed Award; and 

 (4) Any other remedies available at law or in equity. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  Oregon Laws 2025, Chapter 497 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  Oregon Laws 2025, Chapter 497
