 Oregon Health Authority 
 Health Policy and Analytics - Chapter 409 
 
 
 
 
 Division 39 TEMPORARY HEALTH CARE STAFF RATE SETTING PROGRAM 
 409-039-0100 
 Data Requests 
 
 (1) In furtherance of the Authority’s annual evaluation of the maximum bill rates pursuant to OAR 409-039-0090, the Authority may request information from temporary staffing agencies to assess and evaluate the maximum bill rates established under OAR 409-039-0040. 
(2) A temporary staffing agency must respond to all requests from the Authority under this rule within 45 calendar days from the date such request is issued.  

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 676.716 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 676.695 - 676.725
