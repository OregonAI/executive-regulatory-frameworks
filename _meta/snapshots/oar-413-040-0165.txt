 Department of Human Services 
 Child Welfare Programs - Chapter 413 
 
 
 
 
 Division 40 CASE MANAGEMENT — SERVICE PLANS 
 413-040-0165 
 Documentation Requirements for Reports Submitted to the Court Regarding an Indian Child Who is a Ward of the Court 
 
 Any report that the Department submits to the court regarding an Indian child who is a ward of the court, including home studies, placement reports or other reports required   under ORS chapters 109, 418, 419A and 419B, must address tribal customary adoption, as defined in OAR 413-115-0000 (43), as a permanency plan option 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 418.005 & ORS 409.050 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  Oregon Laws 2021
