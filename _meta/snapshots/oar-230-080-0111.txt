 Oregon State Athletic Commission - Chapter 230 
 
 
 
 
 Division 80 CONDUCT OF BOUT - BOXING 
 230-080-0111 
 Association of Boxing Commission’s Unified Rules of Boxing 
 
 Notwithstanding any contrary provision in these administrative rules, The Oregon State Athletic Commission hereby formally adopts, and incorporates by reference, the Association of Boxing Commissions Unified Rules of Boxing. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 463.113 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 463.113 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 SAC 1-2023, adopt filed 06/07/2023, effective 07/01/2023
