 Oregon Health Authority 
 Health Systems Division: Behavioral Health Services - Chapter 309 
 
 
 
 
 Division 80 CLINICAL SUPERVISION EXPANSION PROGRAM 
 309-080-0500 
 Data Sharing 
 
 (1) The Authority may not share data about Program participants, other than for purposes of planning, Program evaluation or analysis, or as otherwise required by law. 

 (2) Data may only be shared with: 

 (a) Agencies, offices, or contractors of the Authority; 

 (b) The Oregon Employment Department; and 

 (c) Non-governmental entities, not noted above, only with written approval from the Director of the Authority. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 413.042 & 675.658
