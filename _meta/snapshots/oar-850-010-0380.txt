 Oregon Board of Naturopathic Medicine 
 Chapter 850 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 10 GENERAL 
 850-010-0380 
 Licensee Duty to Self-Report 
 
 Licensees must self-report within 10 business days: 

 (1) Conviction of a misdemeanor crime; 

 (2) Arrest or conviction of a felony crime; 

 (3) Discipline by a licensing or regulatory agency; where licensee holds a license 

 (4) Service of medical malpractice claim and civil lawsuit filing 

 (5) Judgement and settlement of medical malpractice claim, civil lawsuit 

 (6) Suspension or expulsion from an accredited graduate or medical school 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 685 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 685.220 & ORS 685.222 
 
 History:
