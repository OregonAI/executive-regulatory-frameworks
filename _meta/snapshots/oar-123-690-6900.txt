 Oregon Business Development Department 
 Chapter 123 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 690 LONG-TERM RURAL ENTERPRISE ZONE INCENTIVES 
 123-690-6900 
 Property at Time of Zone Termination 
 
 (1) OAR 123-650-9500 to 123-650-9700 address treatment of facility property in an enterprise zone that has terminated. 

 (2) If the location of a certified business firm’s (proposed) facility becomes part of a newly designated or amended rural zone, then the firm’s certification transfers directly to that zone, provided that the certification was still valid consistent with OAR 123-674-9500 or 123-674-9600 as of the effective date of the designation or boundary change. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 285A.075 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 285C.245 & 285C.409 
 
 History:
