 Department of Fish and Wildlife 
 Chapter 635 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 9 SALMON AND TROUT ENHANCEMENT PROGRAM AND RIPARIAN LANDS TAX INCENTIVE PROGRAM 
 635-009-0097 
 Committee Meetings 
 
 (1) Committee meetings are public meetings and must be conducted in compliance with requirements of the Oregon Public Meetings Law, ORS 192.610 et seq. 

 (2) The Committee will meet no less than twice each calendar year to carry out the business of the Committee. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 496.138 & ORS 496.146 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 496.460 & ORS 192.610 – ORS 192.695 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 DFW 97-2023, adopt filed 09/19/2023, effective 09/19/2023
