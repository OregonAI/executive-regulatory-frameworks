 Oregon Business Development Department 
 Chapter 123 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 39 AQUIFER RECHARGE TESTING FORGIVABLE LOAN PROGRAM 
 123-039-0020 
 Eligible Applicants 
 
 The following entities are eligible to apply for a Program forgivable loan: 

 (1) A Municipal Subdivision; 

 (2) A port organized under ORS chapter 777 or 778; 

 (3) A district as defined in ORS 198.010; 

 (4) An Oregon Indian tribe as defined in ORS 294.805; and 

 (5) A county in this state. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 285A.075 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  OR Laws 2023, Chapter 606 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 OBDD 22-2024, adopt filed 10/08/2024, effective 10/08/2024
