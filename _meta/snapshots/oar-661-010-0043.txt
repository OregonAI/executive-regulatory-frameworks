 Land Use Board of Appeals 
 Chapter 661 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 10 RULES OF PROCEDURE FOR APPEALS 
 661-010-0043 
 Conferences 
 
 On its own motion or at the request of any party, the Board may conduct one or more conferences. Conferences may be by video conference call. The Board shall provide reasonable notice advising all parties of the time, place and purpose of any conference. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 197.820(4) 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 197.830(13)(a) 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 LUBA 2-2025, adopt filed 12/10/2025, effective 01/01/2026
