 Higher Education Coordinating Commission 
 Chapter 715 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 101 Oregon Conservation Corps 
 715-101-0010 
 Use of Equity Lens 
 
 The Oregon Conservation Corps Advisory Committee shall use the Equity Lens adopted by the Higher Education Coordinating Commission in awarding Oregon Conservation Corps Program grants to eligible organizations. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  2021 Oregon Laws, Chapter 592 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  2021 Oregon Laws, Chapter 592 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 HECC 6-2021, adopt filed 11/16/2021, effective 11/16/2021
