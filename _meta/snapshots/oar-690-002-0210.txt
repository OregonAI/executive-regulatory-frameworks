 Water Resources Department 
 Chapter 690 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 2 PROTESTS AND CONTESTED CASES 
 690-002-0210 
 Form of Testimony 
 
 An administrative law judge shall, to the greatest extent practicable, require testimony to be provided orally. If written testimony is submitted, it must be subject to oral cross-examination at hearing. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 536.076(10) 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 536.076(6)
