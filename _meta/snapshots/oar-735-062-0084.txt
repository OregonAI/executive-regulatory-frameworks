 Department of Transportation 
 Driver and Motor Vehicle Services Division - Chapter 735 
 
 
 
 
 Division 62 DRIVER LICENSING 
 735-062-0084 
 Waiving Examination Requirements for Certain Farm Endorsement Applicants 
 
 DMV will waive the examination requirement for an applicant for a farm endorsement when the applicant does not meet the waiver criteria provided in ORS 807.072(4) or (5) because of a traffic accident on the applicant’s two-part driving record that occurred within two years of the application date if: 

 (1) The applicant was not convicted of a traffic offense related to the accident; and 

 (2) The accident is not marked in the Oregon driving record as a preventable accident, as defined in OAR 735-072-0020. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 184.619, 802.010, 807.072 & 49 CFR §383.3(d)(1) 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  807.072 & 807.170 
 
 History:
