 Department of Consumer and Business Services 
 Insurance Regulation - Chapter 836 
 
 
 
 
 Division 71 INSURANCE LICENSING 
 836-071-1195 
 Unlicensed Actors 
 
 A person or entity commits a fraudulent insurance act if they: 

 (1) Represents or advertises themself to be a public adjuster who has not met the requirements of licensure under this rule. 

 (2) Conducts business for which a license is required under this rule without a license. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 731.244 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 744.521
