 Oregon State Lottery 
 Chapter 177 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 94 WIN FOR LIFE 
 177-094-0050 
 Drawings 
 
 (1) Win for Life drawings shall normally take place every Monday, Wednesday, and Saturday at approximately 7:30 P.M. or at other times as determined by the Director.  (2) Each drawing shall randomly select four, two-digit numbers from a possible seventy-seven numbers from one to seventy-seven which shall be the winning numbers. The winning numbers selected at each drawing shall be generated through the use of a computer-driven random number generator. 
 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  OR Const. Art. XV, Sec. 4(4) & ORS 461 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 461
