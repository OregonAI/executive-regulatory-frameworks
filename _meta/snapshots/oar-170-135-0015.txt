 Oregon State Treasury 
 Chapter 170 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 135 ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES 
 170-135-0015 
 Duty to Maintain Decedent's Property 
 
 (1) Except as provided by ORS 708A.430 and 723.466, a Person may not dispose of or diminish any assets of the estate of a Decedent, unless approved by the Treasurer, when the Decedent:  

 (a) Died Intestate; and 

 (b) Did not have a Known Heir. 

 (2) Section (1) of this rule: 

 (a) Applies to a guardian or conservator for the Decedent; and 

 (b) Does not apply to:  

 (A) A Personal Representative appointed under ORS 113.085(3); or 

 (B) An affiant authorized under 114.520 to file an affidavit under 114.515. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 178.050 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 113.328 
 
 History:
