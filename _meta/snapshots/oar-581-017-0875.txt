 Chapter 581 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 17 STRATEGIC INVESTMENTS 
 581-017-0875 
 Native Hawaiian/Pacific Islander Student Success Plan Grant Program: Reporting 
 
 Grant recipients must report project or program outcomes in the manner and form required by the Department. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 329.849
