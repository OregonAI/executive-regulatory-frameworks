 Department of Agriculture 
 Oregon Clover Seed Commission - Chapter 664 
 
 
 
 
 Division 20 PER DIEM COMPENSATION, REIMBURSEMENT OF TRAVEL AND OTHER EXPENSES AND REIMBURSEMENT OF HIRING A SUBSTITUTE 
 664-020-0110 
 Qualified Members 
 
 (1) In accordance with the procedures set forth in OAR 664-020-0130 and OAR 664-020-0140, the Oregon Clover Seed Commission shall provide to a qualified member of the Oregon Clover Seed Commission: 

 (a) Per diem compensation equal to the per diem paid to members of the Legislative Assembly under ORS 171.072, for each day or portion thereof during which the qualified member is actually engaged in the performance of official duties; and 

 (b) Reimbursement of actual and necessary travel or other expenses actually incurred in the performance of a member’s official duties within the limits provided in Oregon Accounting Manual Policy 40.10.00. 

 (2) A qualified member of the Oregon Clover Seed Commission may decline to accept compensation or reimbursement of expenses related to the qualified member’s service on the Oregon Clover Seed Commission. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 576.265 & ORS 576.304 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 292.495 & ORS 576.265
