 Board of Medical Imaging 
 Chapter 337 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 30 COMPLIANCE AND DISCIPLINE 
 337-030-0001 
 Unlicensed Practice 
 
 A person cannot practice a medical imaging modality on a patient in Oregon unless the person is licensed in accordance with the provisions of ORS 688.405 to 688.605. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 688.555 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 688.555 
 
 History:
