 Land Conservation and Development Department 
 Chapter 660 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 8 INTERPRETATION OF GOAL 10 HOUSING 
 660-008-0240 
 Housing Coordination Strategy 
 
 Metro must, and other regional governments or bodies may, develop housing coordination strategies as provided in ORS 197A.365. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 197.040 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 197.274 & ORS 197A.015 - 197A.470 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 LCDD 15-2024, adopt filed 12/20/2024, effective 01/01/2025
