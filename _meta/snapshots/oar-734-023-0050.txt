 Department of Transportation 
 Delivery and Operations Division - Chapter 734 
 
 
 
 
 Division 23 ENVIRONMENTAL PRODUCT DECLARATION SUPPORT FUND 
 734-023-0050 
 Eligible Activities for Environmental Product Declaration Support Fund Grants 
 
 An eligible applicant, as described in OAR 734-023-0040, may apply for reimbursement for activities related to the publication of a third party verified environmental product declaration that complies with ISO 14025 for a covered material that is subject to ORS 184.879. Eligible activities include: 

 (1) Education costs for employees related to the preparation of an environmental product declaration; and 

 (2) Fees directly related to the preparation and publication of a relevant environmental product declaration. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 184.619 & 184.879 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 184.879
