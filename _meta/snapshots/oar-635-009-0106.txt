 Department of Fish and Wildlife 
 Chapter 635 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 9 SALMON AND TROUT ENHANCEMENT PROGRAM AND RIPARIAN LANDS TAX INCENTIVE PROGRAM 
 635-009-0106 
 STEP Volunteers 
 
 (1) Citizens and groups assisting with implementation of STEP projects are subject to the Department’s policies governing volunteers. 

 (2) The Department will provide training as outlined in OAR 635-007-0506 and 635-007-0548. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 496.138 & ORS 496.146 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 496.440 & ORS 496.445 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 DFW 97-2023, adopt filed 09/19/2023, effective 09/19/2023
