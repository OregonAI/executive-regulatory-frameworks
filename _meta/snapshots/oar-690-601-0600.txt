 Water Resources Department 
 Chapter 690 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 601 BEST PRACTICES IN COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT FOR FUNDED WATER PROJECTS 
 690-601-0600 
 Public Notice 
 
 The Department shall denote applications with community engagement plan components for a water project during public notice processes for ORS 541.561-541.581 or ORS 541.651-541.696.  

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 536.027, ORS 541.551, ORS 541.651 - ORS 541.696 & ORS 541.561-541.581 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 541.551, ORS 541.651 - ORS 541.696 & ORS 541.561-541.581 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 WRD 8-2025, adopt filed 06/13/2025, effective 07/01/2025
