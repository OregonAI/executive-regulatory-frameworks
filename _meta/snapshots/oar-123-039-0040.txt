 Oregon Business Development Department 
 Chapter 123 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 39 AQUIFER RECHARGE TESTING FORGIVABLE LOAN PROGRAM 
 123-039-0040 
 Ineligible Project Costs 
 
 The following costs are ineligible for reimbursement under the Program: 

 (1) Cost of purchase of general purpose motor vehicles and other equipment not directly related to the project; and 

 (2) Indirect costs incurred by the applicant or a contractor. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 285A.075 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  OR Laws 2023, Chapter 606 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 OBDD 22-2024, adopt filed 10/08/2024, effective 10/08/2024
