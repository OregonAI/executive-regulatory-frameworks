 Oregon Department of Education 
 Chapter 581 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 17 STRATEGIC INVESTMENTS 
 581-017-0865 
 Native Hawaiian/Pacific Islander Student Success Plan Grant Program: Criteria 
 
 The Department will award Grants to eligible entities based on the following criteria: 

 (1) Whether the proposed project successfully implements the strategies provided in the Native Hawaiian/Pacific Islander Student Success Plan; 

 (2) Whether the applicant authentically and consistently engages Community Voice in the proposed project; and 

 (3) Whether the applicant demonstrates equitable distribution of Grant funds and resources in Partnership. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 329.849
