 Oregon Health Authority 
 Public Health Division - Chapter 333 
 
 
 
 
 Division 20 EARLY HEARING DETECTION AND INTERVENTION AND CONGENITAL CYTOMEGALOVIRUS SCREENING 
 333-020-0180 
 Facility Responsibility if Transfer Occurs Prior to Congenital Cytomegalovirus (cCMV) Screening 
 
 If a newborn is admitted to a hospital as a result of transfer from another hospital or birthing center, the receiving hospital from which the child is discharged to home shall be responsible for ensuring that a congenital cytomegalovirus (cCMV) screening is performed. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 433.321 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 433.321 
 
 History:
