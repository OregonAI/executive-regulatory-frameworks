 Water Resources Department 
 Chapter 690 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 100 PAYMENT FOR PUBLIC BENEFITS IN WATER PROJECTS 
 690-100-0040 
 Compliance Agreement 
 
 (1) A compliance agreement, in preliminary form, shall be submitted to the Legislative Assembly as part of the Commission’s Recommendation. The agreement shall, among other things, set forth the length of time the project is to be operated and maintained for public benefit. After approval by the Legislative Assembly, the compliance agreement shall be written in final form and executed by the project sponsor.  (2) The Director shall be responsible for administering the terms of the compliance agreement. 
 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 541 & 542
