 Department of Transportation 
 Rail Division - Chapter 741 
 
 
 
 
 Division 110 PROTECTIVE DEVICES AT CROSSINGS 
 741-110-0020 
 Application of Rules to Existing Protective Devices 
 
 (1) Protective devices installed on or after the effective date of these rules shall comply with these rules.  (2) Except as required by OAR 741-110-0050(2), protective devices installed at grade crossings are deemed to be in compliance with the rules and regulations of the Department, if the installations were performed in accordance with the rules in effect at the time of their installation. 
 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 184.616, 184.619, 823.011, 824.202 & 824.220 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 824.202
