 Oregon Health Authority 
 Public Health Division - Chapter 333 
 
 
 
 
 Division 77 BIRTHING CENTERS 
 333-077-0140 
 Surgical Services 
 
 Surgical services may be performed but are limited to procedures pertaining directly to pregnancy, labor, and delivery care, and that are normally accomplished during uncomplicated childbirth such as episiotomy and repair. Procedures performed must be consistent with the individual clinical provider's license and scope of practice. Tubal ligation or abortion shall not be performed. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 441.025 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 441.025
