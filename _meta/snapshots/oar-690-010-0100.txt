 Water Resources Department 
 Chapter 690 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 10 APPROPRIATION AND USE OF GROUNDWATER 
 690-010-0100 
 Purpose 
 
 The purpose of these rules is to guide implementation of the critical ground water area statutes, ORS 537.730 to 537.742.   

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 536.027 & ORS 537.780 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 537.730 - ORS 537.742 & ORS 537.780 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 WRD 12-2023, adopt filed 10/10/2023, effective 10/10/2023
