 Oregon Department of Education 
 Chapter 581 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 30 STANDARDS FOR APPROVED RECOVERY SCHOOLS 
 581-030-3000 
 Transportation 
 
 (1) The approved recovery school is responsible for transporting students who reside within the school district where the approved recovery school is located and are enrolled in the approved recovery school. 

 (2) An approved recovery school may establish bus routes or other transportation services to transport students who reside outside of the school district boundary where the approved recovery school is located. 

 (3) Any transportation costs incurred by a school district under this section shall be considered approved transportation costs for the purpose of ORS 327.013 in the same manner as transportation costs incurred by the school district for transporting students who attend other public schools are considered approved transportation costs for purpose of ORS 327.013. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 336.680 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 336.680
