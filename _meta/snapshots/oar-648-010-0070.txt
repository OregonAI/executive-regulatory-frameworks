 Department of Agriculture 
 Oregon Hemp Commission - Chapter 648 
 
 
 
 
 Division 10 REPORTING AND PAYMENT OF ASSESSMENTS 
 648-010-0070 
 Dispute Regarding Amount of Assessment 
 
 A Producer or a Vertically Integrated Producer may file a challenge to an Assessment with the Commission, on a form provided by the State Department of Agriculture, in accordance with OAR Chapter 603, Division 43. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 571.406(3), ORS 571.423 & ORS 571.447 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 571.406(3), ORS 571.423 & ORS 571.447 - 571.501 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 HEMP 4-2022, adopt filed 08/15/2022, effective 08/15/2022
