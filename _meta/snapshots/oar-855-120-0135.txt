 Board of Pharmacy 
 Chapter 855 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 120 INTERNS AND PRECEPTORS 
 855-120-0135 
 Responsibilities: Permitted Practices - Intern 
 
 Interns must only practice pharmacy as authorized by the rules of the board and as permitted by the supervising Pharmacist or Healthcare Preceptor with the practice of pharmacy in their scope. When practicing pharmacy, an Intern must adhere to all the applicable rules in OAR 855-115 for Pharmacists. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 689.205 & 2022 HB 4034 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 689.155 & 2022 HB 4034
