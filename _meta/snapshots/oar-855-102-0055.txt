 Board of Pharmacy 
 Chapter 855 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 102 BOARD ADMINISTRATION 
 855-102-0055 
 Board Compliance Director and Officers 
 
 The board’s Compliance Director and Compliance Officers: 

 (1) Must be Pharmacists licensed in the State of Oregon; and 

 (2) Are authorized to provide appropriate deadline extensions. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 689.205 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 689.195 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 BP 14-2023, adopt filed 08/15/2023, effective 03/01/2024
