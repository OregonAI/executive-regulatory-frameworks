 Department of Consumer and Business Services 
 Finance and Securities Regulation - Chapter 441 
 
 
 
 
 Division 735 PAYDAY AND TITLE LENDING 
 441-735-0080 
 Qualifications of Person in Charge of Licensed Office 
 
 A licensee must not place any person in charge of a licensed office unless the person has a thorough understanding of ORS 725A.010 – 725A.990 and these rules. 
 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 725A.092 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 725A.024
