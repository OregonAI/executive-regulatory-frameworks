 State Board of Tax Practitioners 
 Chapter 800 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 10 CODE OF PROFESSIONAL CONDUCT 
 800-010-0042 
 Communications
 
 
 If the Board sends a request for information to the licensee's or registrant’s address of record, on file with the board, a licensee, or registrant must respond to the board in writing within fifteen (15) business days of the sending date of the board’s mailed or emailed request for information. The written response to the board may be sent to the board by email or by mail. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 673.310(1), 673.730(7) & 673.730(10) 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 673.730(7), 673.705 - 673.740 & 673.990(2)
