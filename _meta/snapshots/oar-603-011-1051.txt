 Department of Agriculture 
 Chapter 603 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 11 LIVESTOCK HEALTH AND SANITATION 
 603-011-1051 
 Exhibition Registration 
 
 A facility owner must register an exhibition with the Department at least 20 days prior to the start of the exhibition on a form provided by the Department. If an exhibition will use different property or facilities that have different facility owners, a facility owner for each property or facility must register. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 596.020 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 596.020
