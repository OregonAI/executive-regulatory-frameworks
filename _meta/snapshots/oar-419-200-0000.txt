 Department of Human Services 
 Office of Training, Investigations and Safety - Chapter 419 
 
 
 
 
 Division 200 HEARINGS – CHILD ABUSE 
 419-200-0000 
 Purpose 
 
 The purpose of these rules, OAR chapter 419, division 200,  is to describe the contested case hearings and OTIS’s ability to have lay representation on “Founded” or “Substantiated” child abuse determinations made by OTIS in chapter 419, divisions 210 through 240. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 409.050 & 418.005 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 409.050, 418.005, 443.400 - 443.455, 409.185, 409.225, 419B.005 - 419B.050 & 419B.035
