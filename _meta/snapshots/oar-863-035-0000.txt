 Real Estate Agency 
 Chapter 863 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 35 TIMESHARE SALES AGENT REGULATION 
 863-035-0000 
 Application and Purpose 
 
 (1) This division sets forth the requirements and process for licensing timeshare sales agents, as that term is defined in ORS 696.010.  

 (2) The purpose of this division is to specify the requirements for obtaining a timeshare sales agent license.  

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 696.022 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  HB 2373, 2025 Regular Session 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 REA 5-2025, adopt filed 12/22/2025, effective 01/01/2026
