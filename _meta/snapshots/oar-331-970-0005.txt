 Oregon Health Authority 
 Health Licensing Office - Chapter 331 
 
 
 
 
 Division 970 REQUIREMENTS FOR AUTHORIZATION HOLDERS TO ENSURE PROFESSIONAL AUTHORIZATION, RELEVANT WORK HISTORY AND TO RETAIN AND PROVIDE RECORDS 
 331-970-0005 
 WORK EXPERIENCE FOR PERSONNEL 
 
 In addition to the requirements outlined in Section 4 of House Bill 2665 (2023), every authorization holder must ensure that any personnel placed with a client or hospital entity has the appropriate work experience, training, and background needed to provide the services required by the client or hospital entity. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  Senate Bill 1549 (2022), ORS 676.560-676.625 & House Bill 2665 (2023) 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  Senate Bill 1549 (2022), ORS 676.560-676.625 & House Bill 2665 (2023)
