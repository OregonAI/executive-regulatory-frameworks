 Columbia River Gorge Commission 
 Chapter 350 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 82 LAND USE ORDINANCE 
 350-082-0560 
 Land Divisions 
 
 (1) New land divisions in the SMAs are not allowed, unless the creation of a new parcel will facilitate land acquisition by the federal government to achieve the policies and guidelines in the Management Plan. 
(2) Creation of a parcel, regardless of size, or any division of land shall be subject to the guidelines in this land use ordinance. 
(3) At the time of creation of one or more new parcels, consolidation of access shall be considered in order to reduce adverse effects on scenic, cultural, natural and recreation resources. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 196.150, RCW 43.97.015, 16 USC § 544e(c) & 16 USC § 544f(l) 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 196.150, RCW 43.97.015, 16 USC § 544e(c) & 16 USC § 544f(l)
