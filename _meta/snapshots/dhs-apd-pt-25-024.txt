Discussion/interpretation
Older Americans Act (OAA) Rule changes became effective March 15, 2024. APD has updated
existing policies and wrote new policies as needed to be in compliance with Code of Federal
Regulations (CFR). These policies are effective October 1, 2025.
Implementation/transition instructions
As part of the review process, APD has created a single policy and procedure document to
house all applicable OAA policies and procedures. This document includes references to
existing procedures as well as new policies that were created as a result of the OAA Rule
Changes.

Local/branch action required
Area Agencies on Aging (AAAs) should review this policy document to ensure their local policies
and procedures are in line with OAA and state requirements. AAAs should reference this central
policy document as needed. An Action Request will be concurrently released to provide AAAs
with required actions.

Central office action required
Training will be provided.
