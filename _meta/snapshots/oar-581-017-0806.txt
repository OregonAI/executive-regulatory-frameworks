 Oregon Department of Education 
 Chapter 581 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 17 STRATEGIC INVESTMENTS 
 581-017-0806 
 Early Literacy Community Grants Program: Reporting 
 
 Grant recipients must report project or program outcomes in the manner and form required by the Department of Education. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 327.829 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 327.825 to 327.827 & ORS 327.843 to 327.845 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 ODE 13-2024, adopt filed 03/22/2024, effective 03/22/2024
