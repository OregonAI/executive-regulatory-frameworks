 Public Utility Commission 
 Oregon Board of Maritime Pilots - Chapter 856 
 
 
 
 
 Division 15 Training and Licensing 
 856-015-0036 
 Certificate in Lieu of Training - Columbia & Willamette River Pilotage Ground 
 
 Up to one-half of the turns required by OAR 856-015-0011(2)(d), OAR 856-015-0035(2)(e), and OAR 856-015-0035(6)(i) may be satisfied by a trainee who delivers to the Training Course Monitor and to the Board's administrator one or more certificates evidencing satisfactory completion of a course, or courses, designed by the Training Course Monitor to simulate the specific turns described in those rules on a vessel simulator at a nationally recognized, independent, marine education and training facility designated by the Board. The simulator training must have been scheduled by the Training Course Monitor.  

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 776 & ORS 670 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 776 
 
 History:
