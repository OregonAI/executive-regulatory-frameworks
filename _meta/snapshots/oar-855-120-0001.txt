 Board of Pharmacy 
 Chapter 855 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 120 INTERNS AND PRECEPTORS 
 855-120-0001 
 Applicability 
 
 This Division applies to any individual who is: 

 (1) Enrolled in or has completed a Bachelor or Doctor of Pharmacy at a College of Pharmacy or School of Pharmacy (COP or SOP) or is certified by the Foreign Pharmacy Graduate Examination Committee (FPGEC), and who acts as Intern; or 

 (2) Licensed by the board as a Preceptor to supervise an Intern.  

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 689.205 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 689.225 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 BP 17-2023, adopt filed 08/16/2023, effective 03/01/2024
