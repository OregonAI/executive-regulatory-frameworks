 Oregon State Athletic Commission - Chapter 230 
 
 
 
 
 Division 145 CONDUCT OF BOUT- COMBAT JIU JITSU 
 230-145-0016 
 When a Competitor is Deemed Down 
 
 To be considered a grounded competitor either both hands (palms or fists) need to be down, or any body part(s), with the exception of a single knee, other than the soles of the feet are touching the fighting area floor. Any time both knees, an arm, hip, buttocks, back, chest, head, leg, etc. are in contact with the fighting area floor that competitor is considered grounded. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 463.113 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 463.113
