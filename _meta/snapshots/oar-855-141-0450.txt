 Board of Pharmacy 
 Chapter 855 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 141 PHARMACY PRESCRIPTION KIOSK 
 855-141-0450 
 Drugs and Devices: Disposal 
 
 Drugs and devices that are outdated, damaged, deteriorated, misbranded, or adulterated must be quarantined and physically separated from other drugs until they are destroyed or returned to their supplier. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 475.035, ORS 689.205, ORS 689.305 & ORS 689.315 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 689.155 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 BP 50-2022, adopt filed 12/20/2022, effective 02/15/2023
