 Department of Corrections 
 Chapter 291 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 124 HEALTH SERVICES (AIC) 
 291-124-0200 
 Doula Services 
 
 Health Services shall operate a doula program for pregnant and postpartum adults in custody at Coffee Creek Correctional Facility. Doula services are available to an adult in custody who is pregnant, or who has been pregnant or has given birth in the preceding 12 months. Doula services include those set forth in ORS 421.173(4) including, but not limited to, one-on-one patient visits and assistance, preparation of the birth plan, on-site support at the birth, education, and group work. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 179.040, 423.020, 423.030, 423.075 & 421.173 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 179.040, 423.020, 423.030, 423.075 & 421.173
