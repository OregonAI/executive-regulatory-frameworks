 Chapter 813 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 261 OREGON EVICTION DIVERSION AND PREVENTION (ORE-DAP) PROGRAM 
 813-261-0020 
 Program Guidance 
 
 The ORE-DAP program guidance, dated October 1, 2025 (the “Program Guidance”), is incorporated into and adopted as part of this division of administrative rules, by reference. The ORE-DAP Program Guidance may be accessed online at the OHCS website. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 456.555 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 456.515-456.725 & 458.505 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 OHCS 43-2025, adopt filed 10/07/2025, effective 10/07/2025
