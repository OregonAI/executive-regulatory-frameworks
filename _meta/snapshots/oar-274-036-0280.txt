 Department of Veterans' Affairs 
 Chapter 274 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 36 VETERANS' EDUCATIONAL BRIDGE GRANT PROGRAM 
 274-036-0280 
 Misrepresentation of Emergency Financial Assistance 
 
 The Department may require immediate reimbursement of funds, either in total or in part, if it is determined that applicant intentionally submitted invalid, incomplete, or fraudulent information when applying for funds from this Program. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 406.005(4) & ORS 406.030 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 408.115, ORS 408.125 & HB 4132 (2026) 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 DVA 1-2026, adopt filed 06/30/2026, effective 07/01/2026
