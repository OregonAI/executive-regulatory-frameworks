 Department of Human Services 
 Office of Training, Investigations and Safety - Chapter 419 
 
 
 
 
 Division 300 REFERRAL AGENTS FOR PROGRAMS 
 419-300-0050 
 General Liability Insurance Requirements 
 
 (1) An applicant must demonstrate that they maintain at least one million dollars in general liability insurance. 

 (2) A referral agent must demonstrate that they maintain at least one million dollars in general liability insurance upon renewal of registration. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 409.050, ORS 419B.005 to 419B.050 & ORS 418.351 to 418.357 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 409.050
