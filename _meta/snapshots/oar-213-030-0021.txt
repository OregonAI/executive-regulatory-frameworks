 Oregon Criminal Justice Commission 
 Chapter 213 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 30 THE IMPROVING PEOPLE'S ACCESS TO COMMUNITY-BASED TREATMENT, SUPPORTS, AND SERVICES (IMPACTS) PROGRAM 
 213-030-0021 
 Purpose 
 
 The purpose of these rules is to administer the Improving People's Access to Community-Based Treatment, Supports, and Services Program (IMPACTS), established by Sections 1 to 14, Chapter 563, 2019 Oregon Laws. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  SB 973 (2019) & Or Laws 2019, ch 563, § 1-14 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  SB 973 (2019) & Or Laws 2019, ch 563, § 1-14 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 CJC 1-2021, adopt filed 09/15/2021, effective 09/15/2021
