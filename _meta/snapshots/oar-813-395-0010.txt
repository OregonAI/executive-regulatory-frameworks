 Oregon Housing and Community Services Department 
 Chapter 813 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 395 PUBLICLY SUPPORTED HOUSING SELLER'S TAX CREDIT 
 813-395-0010 
 Definitions 
 
 Terms used throughout OAR chapter 813, division 395 may be defined in Oregon Revised Statute 315.281 or in OAR 813-005-0005 General Definitions. Terms used within this division observe those definitions, except as defined below: 

 (1) “Oregon Centralized Application (ORCA)” has the meaning in OAR 813-002-0010. 

 (2) “Publicly Supported Housing (PuSH)” has the meaning in ORS 456.250 (6). 

 (3) “Publicly Supported Housing Seller’s Tax Credit Manual” means the manual for the PuSH Seller’s Tax Credit described in OAR 813-395-0015. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 315.281 - ORS 315.291 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 315.281 - ORS 315.291
