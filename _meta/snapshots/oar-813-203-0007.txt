 Oregon Housing and Community Services Department 
 Chapter 813 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 203 LOW-INCOME HOUSEHOLD WATER ASSISTANCE PROGRAM (LIHWA) 
 813-203-0007 
 Manuals 
 
 The LIHWA Intake Operations Manual (program manual) with the requirements and standards therein, dated (as revised) May 30, 2024, is incorporated into and adopted as part of this division of administrative rules, by reference. The program manual may be accessed online at the OHCS website.
