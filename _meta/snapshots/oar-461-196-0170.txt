 Department of Human Services 
 Self-Sufficiency Programs - Chapter 461 
 
 
 
 
 Division 196 SUMMER EBT PROGRAM 
 461-196-0170 
 Concurrent and Duplicate Program Benefits; SEBT 
 
 (1) An individual is not eligible to receive benefits from both Oregon and another state or Indian Tribal Organization for the same time period. 

 (2) An individual is not eligible to receive benefits as a member of more than one filing group (see OAR 461-196-0020) for the same time period. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  411.060 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  411.060
