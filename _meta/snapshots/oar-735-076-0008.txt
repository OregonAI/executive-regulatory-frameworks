 Department of Transportation 
 Driver and Motor Vehicle Services Division - Chapter 735 
 
 
 
 
 Division 76 AT-RISK DRIVER PROGRAM — NON-MANDATORY REPORTING 
 735-076-0008 
 Designating At-Risk Medical Determination Standards 
 
 DMV adopts the “At-Risk Medical Determination Standards,” revised May 2025, as the standards used by DMV’s Medical Determination Officers when determining a person’s medical eligibility for driving privileges and whether one or more of the following are required: 

 (1) Additional medical information; 

 (2) Successful completion of DMV tests; or 

 (3) Medical recertification at a later date.
