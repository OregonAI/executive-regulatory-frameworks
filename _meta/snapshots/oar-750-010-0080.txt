 State Board of Towing 
 Chapter 750 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 10 General Administrative and Operational Rules. 
 750-010-0080 
 Board Member Assignments. 
 
 (1) State Board of Towing members may be assigned, individually or as a committee, to a task or project by volunteering or by Board vote or consensus during a public meeting. 
(2) No more than three (3) active Board Members will be assigned to any one committee. 
(3) The authority of the Board Member Assignments is limited to research, evaluation, or information gathering for presentation and recommendation to the Board unless otherwise approved by the Board. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 822.265 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 822.265 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 SBOT 1-2023, adopt filed 10/31/2023, effective 01/01/2024
