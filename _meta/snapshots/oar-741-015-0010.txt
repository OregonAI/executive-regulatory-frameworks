 Department of Transportation 
 Rail Division - Chapter 741 
 
 
 
 
 Division 15 STATE RAIL REHABILITATION PROGRAM 
 741-015-0010 
 Purpose 
 
 ORS 824.016 establishes the State Rail Rehabilitation Fund for the purpose of financing investments in passenger and freight rail in Oregon. The purpose of these Chapter 741, Division 15, administrative rules is to establish the State Rail Rehabilitation Program for the distribution of the funds and the procedures, standards and criteria for the program’s operation. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 184.619 & 824.016 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 824.016
