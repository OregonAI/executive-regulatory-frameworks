 Department of Consumer and Business Services 
 Insurance Regulation - Chapter 836 
 
 
 
 
 Division 80 TRADE PRACTICES 
 836-080-0905 
 Language used for co-insureds on property and casualty personal lines policies 
 
 No property and casualty personal lines policy may contain any provision or other language that would deny coverage to any innocent person insured under such policy for any covered loss arising out of any act or omission of any other person insured under such policy. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 742.005 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 731.244 
 
 History:
