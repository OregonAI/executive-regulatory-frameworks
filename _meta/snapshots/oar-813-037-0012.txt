 Division 37 AFFORDABLE HOUSING LAND ACQUISITION REVOLVING LOAN PROGRAM (LAP) 
 813-037-0012 
 Land Acquisition Revolving Loan Program Manual 
 
 (1) Additional guidance and application instructions are outlined in the LAP Manual dated March 2022, incorporated into, and adopted as part of this division of administrative rules by reference. 

 (2) The manual may be accessed online at OHCS’s website.
