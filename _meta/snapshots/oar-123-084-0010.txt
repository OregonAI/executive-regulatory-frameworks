 Oregon Business Development Department 
 Chapter 123 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 84 OUTDOOR GEAR AND APPAREL MATCHING GRANTS 
 123-084-0010 
 Purpose 
 
 The Oregon Business Development Department (“Department” or “Business Oregon”), pursuant to Oregon Laws 2023, chapter 546, section 5, shall develop and administer an Outdoor Gear and Apparel Matching Grant Program (“Program”) to provide financial assistance to one or more Membership Organizations and Business Accelerators that support emerging outdoor gear and apparel companies with capacity building and technical assistance in the outdoor gear and apparel industry. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 285A.075 & 2023 Or. Laws, chapter 546, section 5(3)(c) 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  Or. Laws 2023, Ch.546, Sections 3-6 
 
 History:
