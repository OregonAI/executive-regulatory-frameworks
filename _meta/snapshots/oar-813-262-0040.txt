 Oregon Housing and Community Services Department 
 Chapter 813 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 262 YOUTH EMERGENCY HOUSING ASSISTANCE (YEHA) PROGRAM 
 813-262-0040 
 Use of Funds 
 
 (1) Use of YEHA funds must be in compliance with YEHA requirements as specified in the Program Guidance. 

 (2) To the extent of available funding, eligible YEHA expenses include, but are not limited to: 

 (a) Street outreach; 

 (b) Outreach and engagement activities; 

 (c) Training and technical assistance; 

 (d) Block (master) leasing; 

 (e) Emergency and transitional shelter; 

 (f) Hotel/motel stays; 

 (g) Homelessness prevention and rapid re-housing; 

 (h) Rental assistance; 

 (i) Moving expenses; 

 (j) Transportation; 

 (k) Case management; 

 (l) Direct support payments; and 

 (m) Data collection. 

 (3) YEHA funds may be used to supplement existing funds or to support existing programs or establish new programs. Organizations or entities receiving YEHA funds shall not use YEHA funds to replace funds currently being received from other sources or available or reasonably expected to be available. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 456.555 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 456.555
