 Oregon Business Development Department 
 Chapter 123 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 41 AQUIFER RECHARGE DUE DILIGENCE GRANT PROGRAM 
 123-041-0100 
 Waivers and Appeals 
 
 (1) The Department’s funding decisions are final and may not be appealed. 

 (2) In its sole discretion, the Director or designee Department may waive non-statutory requirements of the Program if it is demonstrated that such a waiver would serve to further the goals or objectives of the Program. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 285A.075 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  OR Laws 2023, Chapter 606 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 OBDD 21-2024, adopt filed 10/08/2024, effective 10/08/2024
