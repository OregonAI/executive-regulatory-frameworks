 Department of Human Services 
 Aging and People with Disabilities and Developmental Disabilities - Chapter 411 
 
 
 
 
 Division 29 COMMUNITY-BASED CARE DISTRESSED PROVIDER RELIEF FUND 
 411-029-0050 
 Approval and Payment 
 
 (1) The Department will notify all facilities, in writing, whether approved or not approved. 

 (2) Once approved, the Department will process the payment within ten (10) business days of written approval. 

 (3) The Department will only authorize funds to the extent of the appropriation authorized by the Oregon legislature. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 410.070 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 410.070
