 Employment Department 
 Chapter 471 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 70 Paid Leave Oregon 
 471-070-5240 
 Compromise of Amount Due 
 
 It shall be the policy of the director to compromise the amount due from an employer or former employer with a delinquent account pursuant to the provisions of ORS 657B.320(9) where it appears that such action would be in the best interests of all parties involved and the statutory criteria for a settlement have been met. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 657B.340 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 657B.320
