 Department of Fish and Wildlife 
 Chapter 635 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 21 SOUTHEAST ZONE SPORT ANGLING REGULATIONS 
 635-021-0500 
 Klamath District 
 
 (1) The current annual Oregon Sport Fishing Regulations provide requirements for the Southeast Zone. However, additional regulations may be adopted in this rule from time to time and to the extent of any inconsistency, they supersede the current annual Oregon Sport Fishing Regulations. 

 (2) Effective upon filing, the angling regulations for Crystal Creek, Fourmile Creek, Fourmile Canal, Harriman Creek, Klamath Lake, Odessa Creek, Recreation Creek, Short Creek, and Thomason Creek are: 

 (a) catch-and-release for rainbow trout June 15 through October 31 and 

 (b) flies and artificial lures only for all species June 15 through October 31. 

 (3) All other permanent rules for the Southeast Zone, as stated in the current annual Oregon Sport Fishing Regulations, remain in effect. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 496.138 & ORS 496.146 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 496.162
