 Oregon State Athletic Commission - Chapter 230 
 
 
 
 
 Division 110 ENFORCEMENT 
 230-110-0060 
 Advances to an Unarmed Combat Sports Manager
 
 
 Without prior written permission of the superintendent, no promoter shall, directly or indirectly, incur any indebtedness on behalf of an unarmed combat sports competitor, or entertainment wrestler or manager whereby such person is obligated to repay such indebtedness. No promoter at any time shall, directly or indirectly, make any loan or advance to any manager. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 463.113 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 463.113
