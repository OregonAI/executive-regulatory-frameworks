 Oregon Housing and Community Services Department 
 Chapter 813 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 22 FLEX LENDING PROGRAM (FLEX) NEXTSTEP PRODUCT 
 813-022-0020 
 Approved Lenders 
 
 All Program Loan(s) will be originated and closed using Approved Lenders  who apply for and meet the requirements of a solicitation issued by the Agency. Mortgage Loans delivered to the Department by Approved Lenders must also meet the requirements of the Department’s Servicer. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 456.555 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 456.555 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 OHCS 1-2023, adopt filed 02/06/2023, effective 02/08/2023
