 Oregon Health Authority 
 Health Licensing Office - Chapter 331 
 
 
 
 
 Division 250 GENETIC COUNSELING GENERAL ADMINISTRATION 
 331-250-0020 
 Fees 
 
 (1) An applicant or license holder is subject to the provisions of OAR 331-010-0010 and OAR 331-010-0020 regarding the payment of fees, penalties, and charges. 

 (2) Fees established by the Office pursuant to ORS 676.576 are: 

 (a) Application:  

 (A) License: $150.  

 (B) Temporary license: $150 

 (b) Original issuance: 

 (A) License: $50 – valid for one year.  

 (B) Temporary license: $50 – valid for one year. 

 (c) Renewal of license: 

 (A) License: $50 – valid for one year.  

 (B) Temporary license: $50 – valid for one year. 

 (d) Replacement: $25.  

 (e) Late renewal of authorization fee: $40 for each year of inactive status up to three years. 

 (f) Administrative fee: $25.   

 (g) Affidavit of licensure, as defined in OAR 331-030-0040: $50. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  2021 House Bill 2619 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  2021 House Bill 2619
