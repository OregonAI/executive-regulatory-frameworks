 Department of Energy 
 Chapter 330 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 280 OREGON RENTAL HOME HEAT PUMP PROGRAM 
 330-280-0010 
 Definitions 
 
 (1) “Department” means the Oregon Department of Energy. 

 (2) “Director” means the director of the Oregon Department of Energy. 

 (3) “Heat pump” means an air-source or ground-source heat pump with an energy efficiency rating established under OAR 330-280-0040(1) or a higher efficiency rating. 

 (4) “Purchase price” means the cost to purchase and install a heat pump, before considering incentives. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 469.040 & Oregon Laws 2022, Chapter 86 (Senate Bill 1536) 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  Oregon Laws 2022, Chapter 86 (Senate Bill 1536) 
 
 History:
