 Water Resources Department 
 Chapter 690 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 215 MAINTENANCE, REPAIR AND DEEPENING OF WATER SUPPLY WELLS 
 690-215-0201 
 Dedicated Measuring Tube 
 
 A dedicated measuring tube as described in 690-215-0060 shall be installed in any water supply well at the time of pump installation, pump repair or pump replacement in the following areas (See Figures 200-4, 200-5, 200-7 and 200-8):  

 (1) Petes Mountain Area of Clackamas County (See OAR 690-200-0028(2));  

 (2) Eola Hills Ground Water Limited Area of Polk and Yamhill Counties (See OAR 690-200-0028(3));  

 (3) "Mosier Area" Special Area Standards area of Wasco County (See OAR 690-200-0028(4)).
