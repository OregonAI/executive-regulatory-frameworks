 Water Resources Department 
 Chapter 690 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 100 PAYMENT FOR PUBLIC BENEFITS IN WATER PROJECTS 
 690-100-0005 
 Purpose 
 
 (1) ORS 541.830 and 542.075 authorize the Legislative Assembly to partially fund water development projects which afford public benefits, in proportion to those benefits. This legislation was enacted in 1981.  (2) These rules provide the procedure for determining eligibility, applying for payment, determining public benefits and for the Water Resources Commission’s recommendation to the Governor and the Legislative Assembly. 
 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 541 & 542 
 
 
 History:
