 Department of Agriculture 
 Oregon Dairy Products Commission - Chapter 617 
 
 
 
 
 Division 40 PER DIEM COMPENSATION, REIMBURSEMENT OF TRAVEL AND OTHER EXPENSES AND REIMBURSEMENT FOR HIRING A SUBSTITUTE 
 617-040-0131 
 COMMISSIONER PER DIEM AND REIMBURSEMENTS - Procedures for Payment of Per Diem Compensation 
 
 A member must submit a written claim for compensation to the Oregon Dairy Products Commission no later than the 15th day of the calendar month following the month for which the member seeks compensation.  For example, a claim for compensation for May 1 and May 10 must be submitted by June 15.  The member must specify the amount of time the member spent performing official duties, as well as the nature of the official duties performed, for any day or portion thereof for which the member claims compensation. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 576.265 & ORS 576.304 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 576.265 & ORS 292.495
