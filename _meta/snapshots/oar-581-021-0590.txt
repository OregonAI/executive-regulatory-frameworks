 Oregon Department of Education 
 Chapter 581 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 21 SCHOOL GOVERNANCE AND STUDENT CONDUCT 
 581-021-0590 
 Menstrual Dignity for Students: Requirements 
 
 All education providers shall install in every student bathroom at least one dispenser that: 

 (1) Does not require inserting coins or money; 

 (2) Is clearly marked as free in at least two languages; 

 (3) Is ADA compliant; 

 (4) Is located in a place where all students can access products; and 

 (5) Provides a variety of products with consideration of absorbency and size. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 326.545 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 326.545
