 Department of Public Safety Standards and Training 
 Chapter 259 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 59 PRIVATE SECURITY ENTITY LICENSING 
 259-059-0100 
 Designation of an Executive Manager 
 
 (1) Private security entities must have a designated executive manager who is licensed by the Department under ORS 181A.870 and OAR Chapter 259 Division 60. 

 (2) Private security entities may employ or contract with more than one executive manager. 

 (3) Private security entities must notify the Department in writing within 10 business days when the private security entity changes its designated executive manager. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 181A.870 & ORS 181A.900 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 181A.900
