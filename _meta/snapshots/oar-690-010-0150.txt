 Water Resources Department 
 Chapter 690 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 10 APPROPRIATION AND USE OF GROUNDWATER 
 690-010-0150 
 Engagement with Federally Recognized Indian Tribes 
 
 (1) Prior to convening the Rules Advisory Committee under ORS 183.333, the Department shall initiate engagement with any federally recognized Indian tribes with reservation lands within the proposed critical groundwater area boundary and with any federally recognized Indian tribes in Oregon who have expressed an interest in the proposed critical groundwater area. 

 (2) To aid with the engagement the Department will provide a copy of the draft report that will be posted on the Department’s website under OAR 690-010-0130(4)(c)(B) . 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 536.027 & ORS 537.780 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 537.780, ORS 537.730 -537.742, ORS 537.525, ORS 183 & ORS 536-537 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 WRD 12-2023, adopt filed 10/10/2023, effective 10/10/2023
