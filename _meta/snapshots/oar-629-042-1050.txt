 Department of Forestry 
 Chapter 629 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 42 FIRE CONTROL 
 629-042-1050 
 Limitations on the use of Certified Burn Managers 
 
 A Certified Burn Manager shall only supervise: 
(1) Prescribed burning activities as identified within an approved prescribed burn plan; 

 (2) Prescribed burning for which they are certified to conduct; and 

 (3) Prescribed burning conducted within a forest protection district, as identified in OAR 629-041-0500 to 629-041-0575. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 526.360 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 526.016 & 526.041
