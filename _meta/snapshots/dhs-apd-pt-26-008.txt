Discussion/interpretation
This Policy Transmittal (PT) establishes a standard process for local office staff to
respond to Homecare Worker (HCW) payment questions and sets expectations
for prior authorization (PA) of HCW hours and services. It explains when services
must be authorized, what documentation is required, and what staff must review
when a payment issue is reported. This process supports timely resolution of
payment discrepancies and consistent review of an individual’s eligibility, service
authorization, and payment information.

Implementation/transition instructions
  1. Prior authorization of hours

        a. A case manager (CM) must authorize services before an HCW works
           the hours.
        b. An HCW is authorized only when both of the following are in place:
              i. Authorization in the Oregon Provider Time Capture Direct Care
                   Innovations (OR PTC DCI) system or written notice of approved
                   hours and services; and
             ii. A CM sent the HCW a 598 Task List.
        c. Use form SDS 4105 whenever an HCW starts work for an individual or
           when the HCWs hours or services change.
        d. Authorization must be in writing. Verbal authorization is not allowed.
        e. CMs must provide the Task List to an HCW within seven days after the
           individual reports hiring an HCW.
        f. Do not pay an HCW for hours worked before PA is in place. Do not
           backdate pay. CM should inform an HCW that they are not authorized
           and should not work until the PA is completed.
        g. Hours cannot be authorized if the individual was not eligible for
           services. Confirm eligibility before authorizing services and ensure the
           HCW knows they are not authorized to work.
        h. Individuals must report changes in service needs within two business
           days.
     i. For emergency or unexpected situations when an HCW must provide
       critical care to protect the individual’s health or safety, see OAR 411-
       030-0070(7) for exceptions.
     j. When timing is critical, email may serve as written prior authorization.
     k. Send questions about back pay cases to Aging and People with
       Disabilities (APD) Medicaid Policy at
       APD.MedicaidPolicy@odhsoha.oregon.gov.
2. Timely and accurate pay
     a. If an HCW believes payment was late or incorrect, the HCW should
       contact the local office right away.
     b. To request a late or partial payment penalty fee, the HCW must submit
       the Oregon Home Care Commission (OHCC) late/partial payment
       request form within 60 calendar days after the pay processing date for
       the late or partial payment. Penalty form
     c. If the HCW does not submit the request within 60 calendar days, the
       HCW is not eligible for a late or partial payment penalty fee. The local
       office should direct the HCW to complete the form and eligibility for
       late or partial penalty fees are determined by OHCC.
     d. When an HCW notifies the local office of a payment issue, the local
       office must provide the link. Penalty form
3. Pay processing
  Staff must review the items below and document the review in the
  individuals case narration in Oregon ACCESS (OA).
  Eligibility and coding verification
     a. Confirm that ONE shows the correct approved and authorized medical
       and service Types of Assistance (TOAs) for the period at issue. See the
       Service Categories and TOAs EG.
  b. For in-home cases, the benefit line in the service plan may be
     backdated to match eligibility and TOA. The effective date should
     reflect when prior authorization was established and does not need to
     match the benefit line.
  c. Confirm that any exception hours are approved in the current service
     plan and coded by Central Office, if needed.
  d. For financial eligibility:
        i. check alerts in ONE;
        ii. verify that ONE shows active approved and authorized service
           TOAs for the time frame of the SELG record in the Mainframe;
       iii. Oregon Project Independence-Medicaid (OPI-M) does not require
           Oregon Health Plan Plus (OHP Plus) medical eligibility;
       iv. State Plan Personal Care (SPPC) requires an active medical TOA.
Service authorization and plan verification
  e. Confirm that the case in ONE is pending only for service eligibility and
     not for other medical items. See OAR 461-180-0040 for effective dates
     and service start dates.
  f. Review the Case Manager (CM) Alert Log daily.
  g. See the Case Manager Homepage Overview and Case Manager Alert
     Descriptions resources for more information about alerts in ONE.
  h. Confirm that OA shows an approved service benefit and service plan.
  i. Confirm that the service benefit type in OA matches the medical or
     service benefit in ONE.
        i. For the approved service benefit dates in OA, confirm that the
           benefit is compatible with the approved and authorized medical
           and/or service TOAs in ONE. Example: MSERV = KPS; NMAGISERV
           and LTCSERV = APD.
       ii. SPPC does not require a service TOA, but the OA benefit should
          be BPA or BPO.
  j. Confirm that OR PTC DCI shows an authorization and that the
     authorized hours match the service plan.
  k. To create an authorization in OR PTC DCI, staff must first create a
     voucher in the mainframe. Before the authorization appears in OR PTC
     DCI, the individual and HCW must have profiles, funding accounts, and
     service accounts in the system.
  l. The PTC support team creates those profiles, funding accounts, and
     service accounts through its daily process.
  m. If the individual and HCW are actively linked in OR PTC DCI, the
     mainframe sends authorization data to OR PTC DCI in real time after
     the voucher is created.
  n. The voucher specialist starts the authorization process by entering the
     authorization in the mainframe using the APD 546N.
Payment processing verification
  o. Confirm that a voucher was created in the mainframe.
  p. Verify that all punches were submitted and approved in OR PTC DCI.
  q. Confirm that the HCW submitted claims correctly and within required
     timelines.
  r. Review the STIQ screen for any individual or HCW eligibility errors. See
     the Mainframe STIQ Errors – Prevent and Resolve quick reference
     guide.
  s. Additional resources are available on the OR PTC DCI Troubleshooting
     guide, HCW Authorization PowerPoint, and the Consumer-Employed
     Provider Manual.
