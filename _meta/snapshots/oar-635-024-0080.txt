 Department of Fish and Wildlife 
 Chapter 635 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 24 SNAKE RIVER ZONE SPORT ANGLING REGULATIONS 
 635-024-0080 
 Purpose and Scope 
 
 (1) The purpose of Division 024 is to provide for management of sport fisheries in the Snake River Zone over which the State has jurisdiction. 

 (2) Division 024 incorporates by reference the most current version of the annual 2026 or 2027 Oregon Sport Fishing Regulations. Therefore, persons must consult the current annual Oregon Sport Fishing Regulations in addition to Division 011 and Division 024 to determine all applicable sport fishing requirements for the Snake River Zone. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 496.138 & ORS 496.146 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 496.162
