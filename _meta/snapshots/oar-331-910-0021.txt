 Oregon Health Authority 
 Health Licensing Office - Chapter 331 
 
 
 
 
 Division 910 ELECTROLOGY 
 331-910-0021 
 Electrology Temporary Trainee License 
 
 (1) A trainee license allows an individual to participate in the Electrology Training Program pursuant to OAR 331-910-0007. 

 (2) A trainee license is valid for one year and may be renewed two times.   

 (3) An individual holding a trainee license under this rule may provide electrology services under the supervision of a supervisor. 

 (4) An individual holding a trainee license under this rule may not provide electrology services unless the services are being performed under the appropriate direct or indirect supervision. 

 (5) Before providing electrology services, a trainee must inform the client that they are a trainee working under supervision and document this information in the client record. 

 (6) A trainee must work in a licensed facility in accordance with ORS 690.365 and OAR 331, division 925. 

 (7) A trainee must notify the Office prior to changing supervisors within 15 calendar days. 

 (8) A trainee must adhere to all standards within OAR 331-910-0065, 331-910-0070, 331-910-0075, 331-910-0080, 331-910-0085, and all applicable rules listed in OAR 331 divisions 925. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  676.568, 676.615, 676.572, 690.350, 690.405 & 690.390 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  676.572 & 690.350
