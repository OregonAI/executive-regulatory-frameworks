 Board of Pharmacy 
 Chapter 855 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 120 INTERNS AND PRECEPTORS 
 855-120-0155 
 Grounds for Discipline - Intern 
 
 The following are grounds for discipline: 

 (1) Continuing to practice as an Intern when one of the following has occurred: 

 (a) Dismissal from the Doctor of Pharmacy degree program enrolled in to obtain the Intern license; or 

 (b) Failure to maintain an active Intern license; or 

 (2) Any other grounds found in ORS 689.405. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 689.205 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 689.405 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 BP 17-2023, adopt filed 08/16/2023, effective 03/01/2024
