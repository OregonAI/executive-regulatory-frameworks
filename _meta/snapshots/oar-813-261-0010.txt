 Oregon Housing and Community Services Department 
 Chapter 813 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 261 OREGON EVICTION DIVERSION AND PREVENTION (ORE-DAP) PROGRAM 
 813-261-0010 
 Definitions 
 
 Terms used throughout this division 261 may be defined in Oregon Revised Statutes (ORS) or in the OHCS General Definitions (OAR 813-005-0005). Terms used within this division observe those definitions, except as defined below:  

 (1) "Homeless Management Information System" or "HMIS" is defined in 24 CFR 576.2.  

 (2) “Household” means an individual living alone, a family with or without children, or a group of individuals who are living together as one economic unit. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 456.555 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 456.515-456.725 & 458.505 
 
 History:
