 Board of Pharmacy 
 Chapter 855 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 141 PHARMACY PRESCRIPTION KIOSK 
 855-141-0130 
 Drug: Loss 
 
 A PPK and its PPK Affiliated Pharmacy must: 

 (1) Ensure that disasters, accidents and emergencies which may affect the strength, purity, or labeling of drugs or devices are reported to the board immediately. 

 (2) Ensure that confirmed significant drug loss or any loss related to suspected drug theft is reported to the board within one business day. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 689.205, ORS 689.305 & ORS 689.315 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 689.155 & ORS 689.527 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 BP 50-2022, adopt filed 12/20/2022, effective 02/15/2023
