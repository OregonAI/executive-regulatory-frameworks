 Department of Human Services 
 Office of Training, Investigations and Safety - Chapter 419 
 
 
 
 
 Division 300 REFERRAL AGENTS FOR PROGRAMS 
 419-300-0000 
 Purpose 
 
 The purpose of OAR Chapter 419 Division 300 is to ensure that Referral Agents who refer children to programs are registered with the Oregon Department of Human Services. This rule applies to both children who are residents of Oregon being referred to a program in or out of the State of Oregon and children from other states who are being referred to a  program in the State of Oregon. 

   

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 418.351 - 418.357 & ORS 409.050 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 409.050
