 Oregon Department of Education 
 Chapter 581 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 3 EDUCATION ACCOUNTABILITY 
 581-003-0085 
 Intensive Coaching Program & Student Success Teams: Funding 
 
 (1) The grant in aid allocation shall be determined as follows: 

 (a) Available funds shall be distributed based on ADMw. 

 (b) The maximum allocation a school district shall receive may not exceed $5,000,000. 

 (c) The minimum allocation a school district shall receive may not fall below $500,000. 

 (d) Grant in aid will be allocated for each year the district participates in the program. 

 (2)(a) The Department shall provide additional funds to each participating school district to support collaboration and coordination of the program at the district level. 

 (b) The Department shall allocate collaboration and coordination funding to participating school districts only if there are available programmatic funds; 

 (c) Each school district’s funding allocations for collaboration and coordination shall be based on a calculation determined by the Department; 

 (d) Funds to support collaboration and coordination are in addition to the participating school district’s grant in aid allocation described in section OAR 581-003-0085(1); 

 (e) Funds to support collaboration and coordination shall be provided and available for the duration of the school district’s participation in the program; 

 (f) Unspent funds to support collaboration and coordination shall roll-over to the subsequent year; 

 (g) The participating school district shall ensure that funds are spent in accordance with federal and state law as well as Department guidance to support collaboration and coordination; and 

 (h) Funds to support collaboration and coordination are not required to be approved as a formal recommendation by the Student Success Team. 

 (3) With the exception of the collaboration and coordination funds referenced in OAR 581-003-0085(2), all remaining funds shall be spent in accordance with recommendations from the Student Success Teams. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 327.169 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 327.169
