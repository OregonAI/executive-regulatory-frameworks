 Department of Human Services 
 Aging and People with Disabilities and Developmental Disabilities - Chapter 411 
 
 
 
 
 Division 3 HOME AND COMMUNITY-BASED SERVICES CAREGIVER QUALIFICATIONS AND REGISTRY 
 411-003-0040 
 Enforcement 
 
 Employers who fail to comply with these administrative rules will be referred to their licensing authority for review and corrective action. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 410.070 & 443.450 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 441.705 - 441.745, 443.400 - 443.455 & 443.991
