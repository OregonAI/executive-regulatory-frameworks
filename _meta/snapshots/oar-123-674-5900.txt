 Chapter 123 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 674 STANDARD EXEMPTION ON TAXABLE ENTERPRISE ZONE PROPERTY 
 123-674-5900 
 Property at Time of Zone Termination 
 
 OAR 123-650-9500 to 123-650-9700 address treatment of qualified property in an enterprise zone that has terminated. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 285A.075 & 285C.060(1) 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 285C.175 & 285C.245 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 OBDD 14-2024, adopt filed 06/10/2024, effective 06/10/2024
