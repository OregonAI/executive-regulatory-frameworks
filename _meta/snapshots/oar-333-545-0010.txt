 Oregon Health Authority 
 Public Health Division - Chapter 333 
 
 
 
 
 Division 545 PURPOSE, APPLICABILITY AND DEFINITIONS FOR CERTIFICATE OF NEED 
 333-545-0010 
 Purpose and Applicability of Rules 
 
 (1) OAR 333-545 through 670 establish procedures for the certificate of need program.  (2) OAR 333-545 through 670 apply to the division and persons subject to provisions of the certificate of need law.  (3) The authority for OAR 333-545 through 670 is ORS 431.120(6) and 442.315. 
 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 431.120(6) & 442.315 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 431.120(6) & 442.315
