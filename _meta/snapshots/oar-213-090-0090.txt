 Oregon Criminal Justice Commission 
 Chapter 213 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 90 Organized Retail Theft 
 213-090-0090 
 Evaluating Efficacy 
 
 The Commission will evaluate the progress and efficacy of programs and services funded by the Organized Retail Theft Grant Program through fiscal expenditure and program data collection. The Commission may develop other methods for evaluating program efficacy.      

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  Statutory/Other Authority: 2023 OL Ch.582 §1-2  
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  Statutes/Other Implemented: 2023 OL Ch.582 §1-2 
 
 History:
