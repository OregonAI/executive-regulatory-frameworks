 Oregon Health Authority 
 Health Policy and Analytics - Chapter 409 
 
 
 
 
 Division 62 VALUE-BASED BENEFITS SUBCOMMITTEE 
 409-062-0100 
 Purpose 
 
 These rules establish a public notice for advance meeting materials, which must be posted for an advance comment period, before being considered by the Value-based Benefits Subcommittee (VbBS) of the Health Evidence Review Commission (HERC). Public comments help HERC learn about community needs and perspectives to inform its deliberations about coverage recommendations and prioritization of health services. An accessible public comment policy supports HERC’s goals to eliminate health inequities and ensure that covered services are appropriate for the populations affected by its decisions.  

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 413.042 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 414.688 
 
 History:
