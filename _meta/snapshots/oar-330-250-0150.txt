 Department of Energy 
 Chapter 330 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 250 COMMUNITY RENEWABLE ENERGY PROJECT GRANT PROGRAM 
 330-250-0150 
 Compliance 
 
 The department reserves the right to conduct a physical inspection of all projects and to audit all grantee or partner documentation relating to a project for which a grantee and the department have entered into a performance agreement. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 469.040 & Oregon Laws 2021, chapter 508 (House Bill 2021) 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  Oregon Laws 2021, chapter 508 (House Bill 2021)
