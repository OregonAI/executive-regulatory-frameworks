 Board of Pharmacy 
 Chapter 855 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 141 PHARMACY PRESCRIPTION KIOSK 
 855-141-0150 
 Outlet: Sanitation 
 
 A PPK and its PPK Affiliated Pharmacy must ensure the PPK is kept clean. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 689.305 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 689.305 & ORS 689.527 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 BP 50-2022, adopt filed 12/20/2022, effective 02/15/2023
