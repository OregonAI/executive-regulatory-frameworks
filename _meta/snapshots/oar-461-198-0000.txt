 Department of Human Services 
 Self-Sufficiency Programs - Chapter 461 
 
 
 
 
 Division 198 YOUTH EXPERIENCING HOMELESSNESS HOST HOME PROGRAM 
 461-198-0000 
 Purpose 
 
 OAR chapter 461, division 198 rules establish criteria for awarding grants under the Youth Experiencing Homelessness – Host Home Program (YEH-HHP). 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 417.803 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 417.803 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 SSP 9-2025, adopt filed 04/24/2025, effective 05/01/2025
