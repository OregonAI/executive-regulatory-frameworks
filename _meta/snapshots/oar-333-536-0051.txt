 Oregon Health Authority 
 Public Health Division - Chapter 333 
 
 
 
 
 Division 536 IN-HOME CARE AGENCIES 
 333-536-0051 
 Policies and Procedures 
 
 An agency shall develop, implement and enforce written policies and procedures that include, but are not limited to: 

 (1) Administrative operations; 

 (2) Personnel operations, including but not limited to: 

 (a) Requirements for traveling with a client; 

 (b) Reporting requirements if a client's condition changes while traveling outside the geographic service area; 

 (c) Client care practices including personal care services provided; 

 (d) Investigation of any complaint, grievance, adverse event, provision of personal care, medication or treatment error, and allegation of abuse or neglect; 

 (3) Preemployment screening including but not limited to criminal records check requirements and fitness determinations in accordance with OAR 333-536-0093; 

 (4) Client notification requirements including but not limited to: 

 (a) Increase in the cost of service; 

 (b) Deposit and refunds; 

 (c) Cancellation of service; 

 (d) Termination of service; 

 (e) Reimbursement for stolen, broken or misappropriated property or funds; 

 (5) Management of client records including but not limited to access, storage, retention and destruction; 

 (6) Infection control in accordance with OAR 333-536-0082; 

 (7) Medical and non-medical emergency response; 

 (8) Medication reminding, medication assistance and medication administration in accordance with OAR 333-536-0075; 

 (9) Nursing services; and 

 (10) Quality assessment and performance improvement activities. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 443.340 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 443.340
