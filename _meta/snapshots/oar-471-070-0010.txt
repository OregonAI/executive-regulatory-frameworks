 Employment Department 
 Chapter 471 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 70 Paid Leave Oregon 
 471-070-0010 
 Definitions 
 
 (1) “Paid Leave Oregon” means the Paid Family and Medical Leave Insurance program as described under ORS chapter 657B. 

 (2) “Volunteer” as used in ORS 657B.010(14)(b)(E), means an individual who performs services for a public agency or private non-profit organizations for civic, charitable, or humanitarian reasons, without promise, expectations or receipt of compensation for services rendered. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 657B.340 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 657B.340 & 657B.010
