Statement
Agencies are responsible for maintaining personnel records, including individual employee
records, in accordance with the Secretary of State Archives Division.

Applicability
All employees, including temporary, where not in conflict with a collective bargaining
agreement.

Policy
   1. General
           a. The official employee personnel record shall be established, maintained and
              reside in the Department of Administrative Services human resource
              information system (HRIS).
           b. Employee personnel records and their official personnel file documents prior
              to 2019 are not required to be uploaded to the HRIS.
           c. Upon transfer or reemployment, agencies shall be responsible for requesting
              copies of paper personnel records and employee official personnel file
              documents prior to 2019 from an employee's previous agency.
   2. Official Personnel File
           a. The documents contained in the official employee personnel file are outlined
              in OAR 166-300-0040(7), Employee Personnel Records.
           b. No information reflecting critically upon an employee shall be placed in the
              employee's official personnel file unless the employee is notified.
           c. The employee is entitled to prepare a written explanation/response regarding
              critical information they believe to be incorrect or a misrepresentation of
              facts. The written explanation/response shall be included as part of the
              employee's official personnel file and retained until the related critical
              documents are removed.
           d. The Department of Administrative Services HRIS is the system of record.
   3. Medical Records
           a. Records containing an employee’s medical history shall be kept in the
              Department of Administrative Services HRIS.
           b. Medical records are not personnel records and must be kept separate from
              all other employee personnel records, as required by the American with
              Disabilities Act, federal, and state leave laws.

   4. Other Records
           a. All other personnel records shall be maintained in accordance with the State
              Archives Division regulations—OAR 166-300-0035 (Payroll Records), OAR
              166-300-0040 (Personnel Records), and OAR 166-300-0045 (Risk
              Management Records)—and shall be entered into the Department of
              Administrative Services HRIS as the corresponding document types become
              available within the system.
   5. Transferring Records
           a. Records stored in the Department of Administrative Services HRIS will be
              available to an employee’s current agency of employment.
           b. If an employee works for more than one agency, each agency has access to
              and maintains the personnel record for the position.
   6. Inspection of Records
           a. Public inspection of records shall be conducted pursuant to ORS 192.311
              through 192.431.
                   i. Current employees may view their personnel record and official
                      personnel file in the Department of Administrative Services HRIS.
                  ii. Current employees requesting copies of personnel records not
                      included in the Department of Administrative Services HRIS may
                      submit a request to their human resources office to obtain a copy of
                      the contents of their own personnel record.
                 iii. Former employees may submit a request to the human resources
                      office of the agency of separation to obtain a copy of the contents of
                      their own personnel record.
                 iv. Within 45 days of the request, the agency shall provide the employee
                     with a copy of the requested records.
                         1. If a former employee’s personnel record is not readily
                            available, the agency and the former employee may agree to
                            extend the time in which the agency will furnish a copy of the
                            personnel record.

                   i. Agencies that receive lawful notice of inspection from a federal
                      agency compelling the employer to provide access to records, forms,

                      identity and employment eligibility of the employees shall notify
                      employees within three (3) business days of receiving a notice of an

                         1. The notice must be posted in a conspicuous and accessible
                            location in English and in any other language the agency
                            typically uses to communicate with employees, and
                         2. The agency must make a reasonable attempt to individually

                            language.
                  ii. The notice shall include:
                         1. A copy of the federal agency’s notice of inspection received by
                            the agency.
                         2. The date of the inspection.
                         3. To the extent the agency knows, the scope of the federal
                            agency’s inspection.
                         4. The agency’s obligations in providing information within the
                            scope of the federal agency’s notice of inspection.
                         5. The hotline phone number provided by the Bureau of Labor
