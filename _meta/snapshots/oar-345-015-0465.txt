 Department of Energy 
 Energy Facility Siting Council - Chapter 345 
 
 
 
 
 Division 15 PROCEDURES GOVERNING COUNCIL AND DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY PROCEEDINGS, INCLUDING SITE CERTIFICATE HEARINGS 
 345-015-0465 
 Stays 
 
 (1) The hearing officer has the power to stay a proceeding for good cause, including to ensure a full, fair, and impartial proceeding. 

 (2) Neither the filing of a motion nor the certification of a question to the Energy Facility Siting Council stays a contested case proceeding or extends the time for the performance of any act. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 469.470 & ORS 469.370(5) 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 183.415, ORS 469.370, ORS 469.405, ORS 469.440, ORS 469.605, ORS 469.615 & ORS 469.992
