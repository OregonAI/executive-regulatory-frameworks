 Oregon Housing and Community Services Department 
 Chapter 813 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 275 STATEWIDE SHELTER PROGRAM (SSP) 
 813-275-0060 
 Funding Agreement 
 
 Prior to providing services using SSP funding, Regional Coordinators must: 

 (1) Enter into an agreement with OHCS; and 

 (2) Submit a Regional Assessment and Plan to OHCS. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 456.555 & HB 3644 (2025) 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 456.625 & HB 3644 (2025) 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 OHCS 53-2025, adopt filed 12/30/2025, effective 01/01/2026
