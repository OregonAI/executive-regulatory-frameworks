 Department of Justice 
 Chapter 137 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 74 CHILDREN'S ADVOCACY CENTER ONE-TIME GRANT 
 137-074-0050 
 Reallocation of Funds Not Applied For or Used 
 
 (1) One-time grant funds that were not allocated due to an Applicant’s failure to request its allocated funds, or an Applicant’s failure to submit a complete application, or failure to enter into a Grant Agreement, may be distributed to other eligible applicants as a supplemental award. 

 (2) This reallocation of funds will occur no later than June 30, 2025. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  SB 1579 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  SB 1579 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 DOJ 8-2024, adopt filed 05/22/2024, effective 05/23/2024
