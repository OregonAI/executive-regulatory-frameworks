 Oregon Health Authority 
 Public Health Division - Chapter 333 
 
 
 
 
 Division 333 PSILOCYBIN 
 333-333-8220 
 Product Tracking - Unique Identification Numbers 
 
 (1) A service center, manufacturer or laboratory licensee must use unique identification numbers generated by the product tracking system for inventory tracking activities required by these rules. 

 (2) A manufacturer licensee must: 

 (a) Assign and affix a unique identification number to each separated area containing a cultivation batch of mycelium or fruiting bodies as described in OAR 333-333-8230. 

 (b) After harvest, assign and affix a unique identification number to all psilocybin products or receptacles containing psilocybin products. 

 (c) Assign and affix unique identification numbers in a manner that: 

 (A) Establishes an accurate record of cultivation, harvest and drying of fungi, including documentation of harvest lots and batches as described in OAR 333-333-2020 and OAR 333-333-7090. 

 (B) Establishes an accurate record when one psilocybin product is converted to another product type. 

 (C) Uses a new unique identification number each time a psilocybin product is added to a quantity of psilocybin products grouped together under a pre-existing unique identification number. 

 (d) Affix unique identification numbers in a position that can be clearly read by an individual standing next to the item. 

 (3) To allow for a drying period, the requirements of subsection (2)(c) of this rule do not apply to harvested mycelium or fruiting bodies in the first 15 days after harvest. 

 (4) The requirements of section (2) of this rule do not apply to psilocybin spores or mycelium that are in a preproduction process at a manufacturer’s licensed premises. 

 (5) A manufacturer licensee may not combine psilocybin products of different package or serving size, potency, or type under a single unique identification number, except for  mixed lots of homogenized fungi or psilocybin extracts that will undergo further processing. 

 (6) A service center, manufacturer or laboratory licensee may not reuse unique identification numbers. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 475A.235 & ORS 475A.400 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 475A.235 & ORS 475A.400
