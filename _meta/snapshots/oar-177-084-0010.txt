 Oregon State Lottery 
 Chapter 177 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 84 CASH POP 
 177-084-0010 
 Game Description 
 
 Cash Pop is a one of 15 Lottery game with set prize amounts. For each drawing, a player may choose to play from one to 15 numbers. At the time of purchase, a prize amount is randomly assigned to the player’s ticket and appears underneath the player’s number or numbers that are selected. The Lottery then randomly selects one winning number from one to 15 in a drawing. The Lottery awards prizes based on the extent to which the game play appearing on a player’s ticket matches the winning number selected in the drawing for which the player’s ticket was purchased. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  Or Const, Art XV, § 4(4)(a), ORS 461.120, 461.210, 461.220, 461.230, 461.240, 461.250 & 461.260 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  Or Const, Art XV, § 4(4), ORS 461.120, 461.210, 461.220, 461.230, 461.240, 461.250 & 461.260
