 Mental Health Regulatory Agency 
 Oregon Board of Licensed Professional Counselors and Therapists - Chapter 833 
 
 
 
 
 Division 110 COMPLIANCE 
 833-110-0051 
 Registered Health Care Interpreters 
 
 Licensees, registered associates, and individuals granted a temporary practice authorization must comply with the provisions of ORS 413.559 and OAR 950-050-0010 related to working with health care interpreters. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 413.559 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 413.559
