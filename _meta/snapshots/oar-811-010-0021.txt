 Board of Chiropractic Examiners 
 Chapter 811 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 10 GENERAL RULES AND LICENSING 
 811-010-0021 
 Name Changes 
 
 (1) Each licensee and certificate holder shall update any name changes with the OBCE by providing a completed Name Change Affidavit and copies of necessary documentation. 

 (2) Licensees and certificate holders shall notify the OBCE in writing (email) within 30 calendar days of any change. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 684 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 684.054
