 Department of Fish and Wildlife 
 Chapter 635 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 21 SOUTHEAST ZONE SPORT ANGLING REGULATIONS 
 635-021-0510 
 Southeast District 
 
 Effective June 1, 2026, through September 30, 2026, in Thief Valley Reservoir, Wolf Creek Reservoir, and Pilcher Creek Reservoir located in Union County, the following regulations apply: 

 (1) No minimum or maximum size limits, 

 (2) No daily or possession limits for rainbow trout and bass, 

 (3) Harvest will be allowed by hand, dip net, or angling and, 

 (4) A person with a valid Two-Rod Validation may use any number of rods. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 496.138 & ORS 496.146 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 496.162
