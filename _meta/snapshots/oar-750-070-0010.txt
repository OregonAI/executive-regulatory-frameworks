 State Board of Towing 
 Chapter 750 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 70 Investigations and Investigation Reports. 
 750-070-0010 
 State Board of Towing Investigations 
 
 (1) The State Board of Towing may investigate a complaint when: 
(a) The complaint subject appears to be within the Board’s jurisdiction and authority, and 
(b) There is sufficient evidence to justify a Board investigation to determine if the allegations against the Respondent(s) are such that, if proven, could result in disciplinary action being imposed by the Board. 
(2) The Board Administrator oversees the investigation process on behalf of the Board and serves as the primary point of contact. 
(3) To the extent permitted by law, the Board will cooperate with other regulatory boards and other agencies when conducting an investigation. 
(4) Nothing in this section is intended to limit the Board's authority to initiate and conduct investigations on the Board’s own motion. 

   

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 822.265
