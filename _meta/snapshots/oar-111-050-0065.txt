 Oregon Educators Benefit Board - Chapter 111 
 
 
 
 
 Division 50 CONTINUATION OF COVERAGE 
 111-050-0065 
 Continuation of Coverage for Eligible Employees Covered under the Oregon Family Leave Act
 
 
 OEBB will allow Entities to continue medical, dental and vision coverage for Active Eligible Employees when the employee is granted leave under the Oregon Family Leave Act (OFLA) as required under related state rules and regulations. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 243.860 - 243.886 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 243.864(1)(a)
