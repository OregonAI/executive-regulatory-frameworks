 Oregon Watershed Enhancement Board 
 Oregon Watershed Enhancement Board, Environmental Restoration Council - Chapter 699 
 
 
 
 
 Division 10 Tribal Nation Natural Resource Program Fund 
 699-010-0070 
 Sources of Funds 
 
 Moneys in the Tribal Nation Natural Resource Program Fund consist of moneys transferred to the fund from the Oregon Environmental Restoration Fund and moneys deposited or transferred into the fund from any other public or private source.  

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  541.876 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  541.857 - 541.882 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 ERC 2-2026, adopt filed 07/22/2026, effective 07/22/2026
