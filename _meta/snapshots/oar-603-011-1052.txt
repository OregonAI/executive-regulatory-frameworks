 Department of Agriculture 
 Chapter 603 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 11 LIVESTOCK HEALTH AND SANITATION 
 603-011-1052 
 Official Identification 
 
 The following species of livestock must bear official individual identification, regardless of age, if present at an exhibition after January 1, 2027: 
(1) Beef Cattle; 
(2) Cervidae (deer and elk); 
(3) Dairy Cattle; 
(4) Goats; 
(5) Sheep; and 
(6) Swine. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 596.020 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 596.020
