 Department of Human Services 
 Child Welfare Programs - Chapter 413 
 
 
 
 
 Division 205 RESPITE PROVIDER CERTIFICATION 
 413-205-0028 
 Purpose and Applicability of Certification Standards 
 
 (1) The   purpose of OAR 413-205-0028 to 413-205-0085  is to describe the criteria for approval as a  Certified Respite Provider. 

 (2) These rules apply to any individual who: 

 (a) Has applied to become a certified respite provider; 

 (b) Is currently a certified respite provider; or 

 (c) Has applied to renew certified respite certificate of approval. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 418.005, ORS 409.050 & ORS 418.640 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 418.005, ORS 409.010, ORS 418.640, ORS 418.015, ORS 418.315, ORS 418.625, ORS 418.627, ORS 418.630, ORS 418.635, ORS 418.642, ORS 418.643, ORS 418.644, ORS 418.645, ORS 418.647 & ORS 418.648
