 Board of Pharmacy 
 Chapter 855 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 102 BOARD ADMINISTRATION 
 855-102-0050 
 Coaching from Board Members and Staff 
 
 A board member or board staff must not: 

 (1) Discuss the contents of an examination, its preparation or use with any candidate or other person;  

 (2) Coach a candidate or any other person on materials that may be used in the examination; or 

 (3) Accept any fees for any act of assistance that would bear on the examination. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 689.205 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 689.195 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 BP 14-2023, adopt filed 08/15/2023, effective 03/01/2024
