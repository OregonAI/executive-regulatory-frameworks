 Department of Agriculture 
 Chapter 603 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 25 FOOD ESTABLISHMENT STANDARDS AND STANDARDS FOR RETAIL FOOD SERVICE ACTIVITIES 
 603-025-0520 
 Scope and Purpose 
 
 OAR 603-025-0520 to 603-025-0535 set forth the procedures that the Department of Agriculture will follow when it orders the closure of a food establishment that has failed to obtain a required authorization from the department, or whose required authorization has lapsed.
