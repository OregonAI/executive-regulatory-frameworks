Temporary rule language in effect until 12/31/2026.

Department of Fish and Wildlife

Chapter 635

Division 24
SNAKE RIVER ZONE SPORT ANGLING REGULATIONS

635-024-0090
Inclusions and Modifications

(1) The current annual Oregon Sport Fishing Regulations provide requirements for the Snake River Zone. However, additional regulations may be adopted in this rule from time to time and to the extent of any inconsistency, they supersede the current annual Oregon Sport Fishing Regulations.

(2) Effective June 4 ,2026 through August 17, 2026, closed for Chinook salmon from the Dug Bar Boat Ramp upstream to Hells Canyon Dam.

(3) Effective August 18, 2026 through October 31, 2026, for the Snake River from the Oregon Washington state border, upstream to the boundary 400 ft below Hells Canyon dam, the bag limit for adult Chinook salmon is 3 per day, of which only one may be wild.

(4) All other permanent rules for the Snake River Zone, as stated in the current annual Oregon Sport Fishing Regulations, remain in effect.

Statutory/Other Authority: ORS 496.138 & ORS 496.146
Statutes/Other Implemented: ORS 496.162
