 Department of Human Services 
 Chapter 407 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 100 RESILIENCE HUBS AND NETWORKS GRANT 
 407-100-0010 
 Purpose and Scope 
 
 (1) OAR 407-100 establishes the Resilience Hubs and Networks Grant Program within the Oregon Department of Human Services (ODHS) Office of Resilience and Emergency Management (OREM). The Program offers grants to organizations or individuals who wish to develop, expand, or strengthen a Resilience Hub or Network in their community. 

 (2) The purpose of the Resilience Hubs and Networks Grant Program is to empower communities to become more resilient. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 409.760 & ORS 409.050 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 409.760
