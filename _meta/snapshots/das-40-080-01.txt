Statement
Oregon state government provides a non-competitive method of rehiring experienced
former state employees who separated from state service in good standing.

Applicability
Classified, management service, and unclassified service employees, excluding temporary
employees.

Forms
   •   None

Definitions
Rehire: a non-competitive process to hire a qualified former state employee who left state
service in good standing.

Policy
   1. An appointing authority may rehire a former state employee, including limited
      duration employees, within two years from the separation date, if the employee left
      in good standing, voluntarily demoted, demoted in lieu of layoff, or reclassed
      downward in an agency in the same, equal or lower classification.
   2. An appointing authority may rehire an unclassified service employee in a position
      for which qualified if the employee has been employed full time for at least 12
      months consecutively in such service.
   3. Rehire of an unclassified service employee shall occur only after any current
      classified represented employee has exhausted any right under an applicable
      collective bargaining agreement and after a current classified unrepresented or
      management service employee has exhausted layoff under applicable
      administrative rules or state HR policies.
   4. In the event of a subsequent appointment to a classification with a lower salary
      range, rehire eligibility continues for the original classification from which the
      employee separated.
