 Oregon Department of Education 
 Chapter 581 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 3 EDUCATION ACCOUNTABILITY 
 581-003-0060 
 Reconsideration 
 
 After a minimum of two years in either Directed Coaching or the Intensive Coaching Program, the department will take into consideration interim test data as well as the school district’s performance growth target data to determine if the school district will no longer be required to participate in Directed Coaching or the Intensive Coaching Program. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  SB 141 (2025) 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  SB 141 (2025) 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 ODE 24-2026, adopt filed 05/29/2026, effective 05/29/2026
