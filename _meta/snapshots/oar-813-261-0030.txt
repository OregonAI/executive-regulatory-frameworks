 Oregon Housing and Community Services Department 
 Chapter 813 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 261 OREGON EVICTION DIVERSION AND PREVENTION (ORE-DAP) PROGRAM 
 813-261-0030 
 Administration 
 
 (1) OHCS may enter into agreements with entities or organizations to provide eligible ORE-DAP services (see OAR 813-261-0040) in such manner as to provide holistic coverage statewide without duplication or overlap of services. 

 (2) A designated portion of ORE-DAP funds are reserved for recipients’ administrative costs, up to a determined amount as authorized by OHCS and outlined in the funding agreement. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 456.555 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 456.515-456.725 & 458.505 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 OHCS 43-2025, adopt filed 10/07/2025, effective 10/07/2025
