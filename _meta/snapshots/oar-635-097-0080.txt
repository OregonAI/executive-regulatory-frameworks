 Department of Fish and Wildlife 
 Chapter 635 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 97 PRIVATE FOREST ACCORD GRANT PROGRAM 
 635-097-0080 
 Private Forest Accord Grant Program Application 
 
 Project applications for Regular Grants must be submitted on the most current form prescribed by the PFA Advisory Committee. In addition, applicants must provide a written explanation if they cannot provide any of the information required in the application. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 496.012, 496.138, 496.146 & Oregon Laws 2022, Chapter 33, Sections 30-32 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 496.012 & Oregon Laws 2022, Chapter 33, Sections 30-32
