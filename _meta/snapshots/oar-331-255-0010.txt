 Oregon Health Authority 
 Health Licensing Office - Chapter 331 
 
 
 
 
 Division 255 LICENSURE OF GENETIC COUNSELORS 
 331-255-0010 
 Application Requirements for Initial Licensure as a Licensed Genetic Counselor 
 
 An individual applying for initial licensure as a licensed genetic counselor must: 

 (1) Meet the requirements of OAR 331 Division 30. 

 (2) Submit a completed application form prescribed by the Office, which must contain the information listed in OAR 331-030-0000. 

 (3) Be at least 18 years old. 

 (4) Arrange for official documentation to be mailed to the Office from the certifying organization proving that the applicant has an active credential specific to genetic counseling from the ABGC or the ABMGG. 

 (5) Pay all licensing fees. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  2021 House Bill 2619 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  2021 House Bill 2619
