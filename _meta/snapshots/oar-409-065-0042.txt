 Oregon Health Authority 
 Health Policy and Analytics - Chapter 409 
 
 
 
 
 Division 65 SUSTAINABLE HEALTH CARE COST GROWTH TARGET PROGRAM 
 409-065-0042 
 Confidential Information 
 
 (1) An entity that submits information to the Authority may claim portions of the information a trade secret and exempt from disclosure under ORS 192.311 to 192.478.  
(a) The entity must submit two versions of such materials to the Authority:  
(A) One must be marked as “CONFIDENTIAL” and must contain the full unredacted version of the document.  
(B) The second must be marked as “PUBLIC” and must contain a redacted version of the document (from which the confidential portions have been removed or obscured) and must be posted on the Program website.  
(b) An entity claiming confidentiality must include a redaction log that provides a reasonably detailed statement of the grounds on which confidentiality is claimed and the reasoning for such redaction. This redaction log must be posted on the Program website. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 442.386 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 442.386
