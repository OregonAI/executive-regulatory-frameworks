 Water Resources Department 
 Chapter 690 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 380 WATER RIGHT TRANSFERS 
 690-380-7000 
 Types of Permit Amendments 
 
 As provided in ORS 537.211(4), the water right permit holder of record may request to change the point of diversion or appropriation pursuant to OAR 690-380-7010, the point of diversion to allow the appropriation of groundwater pursuant to OAR 690-380-7020, or the place of use pursuant to OAR 690-380-7030. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 536.025 & ORS 536.027 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 537.211 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 WRD 3-2026, adopt filed 03/20/2026, effective 04/01/2026
