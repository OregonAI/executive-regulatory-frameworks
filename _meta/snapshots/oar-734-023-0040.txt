 Department of Transportation 
 Delivery and Operations Division - Chapter 734 
 
 
 
 
 Division 23 ENVIRONMENTAL PRODUCT DECLARATION SUPPORT FUND 
 734-023-0040 
 Who May Apply for Environmental Product Declaration Support Fund Grants 
 
 (1) An eligible applicant for the Environmental Product Declaration Support Fund is a Bidder or Proposer, as defined in ORS 279A.010. 

 (2) An eligible applicant, if applicable, must also be current on all state and local taxes, fees and assessments. 

 (3) At the time of applying to the Environmental Product Declaration Support Fund for a grant, an eligible applicant may not have previously received external funding to develop an environmental product declaration for a covered material. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 184.619 & 184.879 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 184.879
