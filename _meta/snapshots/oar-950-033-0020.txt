 Oregon Health Authority 
 Equity and Inclusion Division - Chapter 950 
 
 
 
 
 Division 33 REALD AND SOGI GRANT PROGRAM 
 950-033-0020 
 Grant Announcement 
 
 The Oregon Health Authority must announce availability of funding from the REALD & SOGI Grant Program and the announcement must include: 

 (1) Available funding; 

 (2) Eligible entities and services; 

 (3) Term of the grant; 

 (4) Template grant agreement; 

 (5) Template budget; 

 (6) Application criteria; and 

 (7) Evaluation and review process. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 413.116 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 413.166 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 EID 3-2024, adopt filed 10/01/2024, effective 10/01/2024
