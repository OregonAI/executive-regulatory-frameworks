 Department of Early Learning and Care 
 Chapter 414 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 330 MICRO-CENTER PILOT PROGRAM 
 414-330-0100 
 Purpose 
 
 The purpose of OAR 414-330-0100 through OAR 414-330-0600 is to establish and administer a pilot program to support the development of a sustainable model for micro-centers to provide affordable, high quality early learning opportunities to communities in this state. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  section 1, chapter 374, Oregon Laws 2023 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  section 1, chapter 374, Oregon Laws 2023
