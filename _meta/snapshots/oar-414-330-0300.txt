 Department of Early Learning and Care 
 Chapter 414 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 330 MICRO-CENTER PILOT PROGRAM 
 414-330-0300 
 Program Eligibility Criteria 
 
 To be considered for eligibility to participate in the Micro-Center Pilot Program, applicants must:  
(1)    Hold an active license administered by the Department;  
(2)    Serve a minimum of three children and have a maximum licensed capacity of 30 children; and 
(3)    Provide child care for more than four hours a day.  
  

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  section 1, chapter 374, Oregon Laws 2023 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  section 1, chapter 374, Oregon Laws 2023
