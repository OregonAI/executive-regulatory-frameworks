 State Library 
 Chapter 543 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 80 PUBLIC LIBRARIES 
 543-080-0005 
 Scope 
 
 OAR chapter 543, division 80, applies to public libraries as defined in ORS 357.400(3) that were established under one of the methods described in ORS 357.216-286 or 357.400-621. 
  

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 357 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 357 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 OSL 3-2025, adopt filed 11/06/2025, effective 12/01/2025
