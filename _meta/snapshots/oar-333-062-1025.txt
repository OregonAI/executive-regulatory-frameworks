 Oregon Health Authority 
 Public Health Division - Chapter 333 
 
 
 
 
 Division 62 PUBLIC AQUATIC FACILITIES – OPERATIONS AND POLICIES 
 333-062-1025 
 Closure Criteria 
 
 (1) If one or more of the conditions outlined in subpart 6.6.3 is present at the AQUATIC VENUE or AQUATIC FACILITY, the AQUATIC VENUE license holder, operator or responsible supervisor must immediately close the AQUATIC VENUE until the situation is resolved. 

 (2) If an AQUATIC VENUE license holder, operator or responsible supervisor has not acted in accordance with section (1) of this rule, the AUTHORITY may issue an emergency suspension order and close an AQUATIC VENUE in accordance with ORS 183.430(2). 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 448.011 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 448.005 - 448.100 & ORS 448.990
