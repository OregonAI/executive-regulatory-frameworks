 Oregon Health Authority 
 Public Health Division - Chapter 333 
 
 
 
 
 Division 53 SPECIAL SUPPLEMENTAL NUTRITION PROGRAM FOR WOMEN, INFANTS AND CHILDREN (WIC) PARTICIPANT ADMINISTRATION 
 333-053-0075 
 eWIC Cards 
 
 (1) At the time of certification, a first cardholder will be identified and issued a eWIC card for the family.  (a) If a family requires more than one authorized shopper, a second eWIC card for use by the family’s second cardholder will be issued.  (b) Each family will have no more than two activated eWIC cards at a time.  (c) No more than one activated eWIC card can be assigned to a cardholder at a time.  (2) The Authority may issue replacement eWIC cards at its discretion. 
 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 413.500 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 413.500 
 
 History:
