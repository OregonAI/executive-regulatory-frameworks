 Oregon Board of Naturopathic Medicine 
 Chapter 850 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 10 GENERAL 
 850-010-0360 
 Unlicensed Practice of Naturopathic Medicine Prohibited 
 
 (1) No person shall practice naturopathic medicine within the meaning of ORS 685.010, without an active license from the Board. 

 (2) A person holding a doctoral degree from a naturopathic medical school or program, may only practice naturopathic medicine within the meaning of ORS 685.010, under the direct supervision of an active Oregon licensee. 

 (3) Only a person holding an active Oregon license may use the terms “naturopathic doctor” “naturopathic physician” or “ND” 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 685 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 685.010 
 
 History:
