 Oregon Watershed Enhancement Board 
 Oregon Watershed Enhancement Board, Environmental Restoration Council - Chapter 699 
 
 
 
 
 Division 10 Tribal Nation Natural Resource Program Fund 
 699-010-0010 
 Purpose 
 
 (1) This Division provides requirements for the Tribal Nation Natural Resource Program Fund.  

 (2) The Tribal Nation Natural Resource Program Fund provides funds to federally recognized Tribes in Oregon for projects and purposes consistent with the terms of the Monsanto Settlement Agreement.   

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  541.879 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  541.857 - 541.882 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 ERC 2-2026, adopt filed 07/22/2026, effective 07/22/2026
