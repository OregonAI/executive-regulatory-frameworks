Statement
This is the state’s policy that describes the circumstances under which modifications will
be made in the Workday Payroll System to process voluntary deductions from the pay of
state employees.

Applicability
This policy applies to all state agencies that are subject to the Oregon Accounting Manual
(OAM), as outlined in OAM 01.05.00, Introduction: Scope and Applicability. If any
provisions of OAM policies or procedures conflict with collective bargaining agreements,
the provisions of collective bargaining agreements would supersede OAM guidance.

Policy
Authorization to Create Deduction Codes
101. Under DAS authority to provide centralized payroll services (refer to ORS 292.014 -
     292.026) the Workday Oregon Program maintains the standardized deduction-code
     framework used to process voluntary payroll deductions from employee pay.

Procedure to Establish a Deduction Code
102. The agency proposing or requesting a new deduction code, makes this request by
     creating a case to OSPS in the Workday Payroll System. The agency must specify if
     the deduction is to be limited to specific agencies. OSPS will evaluate the request,
     reach out to the Workday Oregon Program providing their approval and verifying the
     permissions. Once the Workday Oregon Program has created and tested the new
     deduction code, OSPS will respond to the agency and verify the date by which the
     code is planned to be released and available for use. Entities outside of the
     Workday Oregon Program will need to work with OSPS to make this request.
103.   When notified that the deduction code request has been approved, the funds
       recipient may submit the information required to establish Electronic Funds
       Transfer (EFT) for the deducted funds to OSPS. The following information must be
       provided either in writing or electronically. Without this information a check will be
       created and mailed to the funds recipient. Staff from OSPS will contact a funds
       recipient that is an external entity to obtain the following EFT information:
       a. Complete business name and address of the funds recipient.
       b. Name and address of the funds recipient’s bank.
       c. American Banking Association (ABA) routing/transit number and the account
          number for the destination of the funds.
104.   The Workday Oregon Program will create the deduction code and notify OSPS, who
       will notify the funds recipient and the agency making the request when the code is
       ready for use.

Implementing Deductions from Employee Pay
105. The employee must supply the employer agency with voluntary deduction
     authorization to record the employee’s permission to activate the deduction.
     Authorization must indicate the funds recipient, the monthly amount, the effective
     date of the deduction, length of time for which the deduction is authorized (either
     fixed term or permanent), and a clear statement of authorization from the
     employee. Refer to ORS 652.610(3)(b) & (c).

106.   Employees make a request for voluntary contributions for the Public Employees
       Retirement System (PERS), the Oregon Savings Growth Plan (OSGP), and insurance
       through the Public Employees Benefit Board (PEBB) by contacting these
       organizations directly. The organizations will notify the employees’ agency to make
       the necessary updates in the Workday Payroll System.

107.   Upon receipt of authorization, the agency may activate the deduction code in the
       Workday Payroll System. The agency must retain employee deduction
       authorizations in accordance with the state records retention schedule.
