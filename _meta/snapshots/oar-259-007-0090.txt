 Department of Public Safety Standards and Training 
 Chapter 259 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 7 PUBLIC CONTRACTING AND PROCUREMENT 
 259-007-0090 
 Fragmentation 
 
 A Procurement may not be artificially divided or fragmented for the purpose of restricting competition or circumventing a legal requirement. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 181A.410, ORS 279A.050, ORS 279A.065 & ORS 279A.070 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 279A.065 & ORS 279A.070 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 DPSST 7-2023, adopt filed 09/18/2023, effective 10/01/2023
