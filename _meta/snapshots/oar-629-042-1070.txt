 Department of Forestry 
 Chapter 629 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 42 FIRE CONTROL 
 629-042-1070 
 Fees 
 
 (1) To cover the cost of materials and testing for the Certified Burn Manager program, the application fee for an initial certificate shall be $50.00. 

 (2) Training providers may charge reasonable fees for the training they provide or document. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 526.360 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 526.016 & 526.041 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 DOF 8-2022, adopt filed 11/29/2022, effective 11/30/2022
