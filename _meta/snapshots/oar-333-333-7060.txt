 Oregon Health Authority 
 Public Health Division - Chapter 333 
 
 
 
 
 Division 333 PSILOCYBIN 
 333-333-7060 
 Pesticide Testing 
 
 (1) A licensee must submit one or more batches from a harvest lot or process lot for pesticide testing upon written request by the Oregon Health Authority. 

 (2) A batch fails pesticide testing if the test detects the presence of a pesticide above action levels in any sample, including a field duplicate: 

 (a) During an initial test where no reanalysis is requested; or 

 (b) Upon reanalysis as described in OAR 333-333-7120. 

 (3) If a sample from a harvest lot or process lot fails pesticide testing, the batch may not be remediated and must be destroyed. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 475A.235 & ORS 475A.590 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 475A.235 & ORS 475A.590
