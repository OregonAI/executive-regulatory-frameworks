Agency: Oregon Health Authority

Unit:  Transformation Center

Program Description:
The Transformation Center is responsible for technical assistance to health system partners,
including Coordinated Care Organizations. It shares innovations and best practices.

Program Records
001 Transformation Center Technical Assistance Records
Retain 5 years after superseded or obsolete, destroy
002 Transformation Center Publications
Retain 10 years, destroy

Administrative Records (OAR 166-300-0015)

Correspondence
Legislative Development Advisory Committee Records

Program Description:
The Patient-Centered Primary Care Home (PCPCH) collaborates with stakeholders to set the
standard for transformative, whole-person and evidence-based primary care.

Program Records
003 PCPCH Recognition Records
(a) Retain annual recognition data 20 years, destroy
(b) Retain individual clinic documentation (evidence of recognition) 10 years, destroy
(c) Retain other records 5 years, destroy
