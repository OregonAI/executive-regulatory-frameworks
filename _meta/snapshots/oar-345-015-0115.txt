 Department of Energy 
 Energy Facility Siting Council - Chapter 345 
 
 
 
 
 Division 15 PROCEDURES GOVERNING COUNCIL AND DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY PROCEEDINGS, INCLUDING SITE CERTIFICATE HEARINGS 
 345-015-0115 
 Appointment of Special Advisory Groups 
 
 After receiving the notice of intent, the Department shall designate as a special advisory group the governing body of any local government within whose jurisdiction the facility is proposed to be located. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 469.470 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 469.480 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 EFSC 1-2023, adopt filed 08/29/2023, effective 08/29/2023
