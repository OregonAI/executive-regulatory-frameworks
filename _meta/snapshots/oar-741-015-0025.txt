 Department of Transportation 
 Rail Division - Chapter 741 
 
 
 
 
 Division 15 STATE RAIL REHABILITATION PROGRAM 
 741-015-0025 
 Application Submission Periods 
 
 (1) The Department will announce periods for submitting applications for funding from the State Rail Rehabilitation Fund, as funding is made available. 

 (2) The Department reviews Project applications for compliance with the requirements in OAR 741-015-0040 and as prescribed in OAR 741-015-0050. 

 (3) If a Project is not funded, the Applicant may resubmit the Project during subsequent application submission periods prescribed by the Department. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 184.619 & 824.016 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 824.016
