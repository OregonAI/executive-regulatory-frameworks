 Department of Forestry 
 Chapter 629 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 42 FIRE CONTROL 
 629-042-1030 
 Certified Burn Manager certification requirements. Tests. 
 
 (1) The Forester: 

 (a) Shall provide for the development and administration of all tests required by this rule; 

 (b) Shall establish a passing score for all tests required by this rule; 

 (c) May not administer the same test to an individual more frequently than once every 30 calendar days; and 

 (d) May not prohibit the use of written reference material by individuals taking tests. 

 (2) Individuals taking tests required by this rule: 

 (a) Shall display an approved government issued picture identification to the Forester or authorized Training Provider, prior to taking a test; and 

 (b) Shall comply with all test taking requirements established by the Forester or authorized Training Provider. 

 (3) Individuals shall successfully pass a test prior to requesting a field certification book from the Forester. 

 (4) An individual who was unsuccessful in passing the test may appeal in accordance with ORS 183.484. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 526.016 & 526.041 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 526.360
