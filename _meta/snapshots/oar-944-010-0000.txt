 Oregon Health Authority 
 Drug Treatment and Recovery Services - Chapter 944 
 
 
 
 
 Division 10 Behavioral Health Resource Networks 
 944-010-0000 
 Behavioral Health Resource Networks 
 
 (1) These rules prescribe general criteria and requirements for each Behavioral Health Resource Network (BHRN), individually and jointly, including the requirements to collect and report information necessary for the Secretary of State to conduct financial and performance audits required by ORS 430.392. 

 (2) These rules 944-010-0000 through 944-010-0090 apply to a BHRN and/or funded entities authorized to provide services on and after July 1, 2025. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 430.389(2)(a), 430.390, 413.042 & SB 610 (2025) 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 430.389(2)(a) & 430.390
