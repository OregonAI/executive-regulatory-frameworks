 Department of Human Services 
 Aging and People with Disabilities and Developmental Disabilities - Chapter 411 
 
 
 
 
 Division 14 OREGON PROJECT INDEPENDENCE - MEDICAID (OPI-M) ELIGIBILITY 
 411-014-0000 
 Purpose 
 
 The purpose of these rules is to define the eligibility requirements for the Oregon Project Independence-Medicaid (OPI-M) Program. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 410.070 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 410.070 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 APD 65-2024, adopt filed 11/20/2024, effective 11/22/2024 
 
 
 
 
 


 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 APD 23-2024, temporary adopt filed 05/20/2024, effective 05/31/2024 through 11/26/2024
