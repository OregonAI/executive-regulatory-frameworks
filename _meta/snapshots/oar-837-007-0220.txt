 Department of the State Fire Marshal 
 Chapter 837 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 7 CRIMINAL RECORDS CHECK AND FITNESS DETERMINATION RULES 
 837-007-0220 
 Subject Individual 
 
 The Department may require a Subject Individual to complete a criminal records check due to the following reasons: 
(1) Is applying for employment or is employed with the Department; or 
(2) Provides services or seeks to provide services to the Department as a contractor, subcontractor, vendor or volunteer. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 181A.195 & 2024 Senate Bill 1510 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 181A.195(9) 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 OSFM 5-2025, adopt filed 09/18/2025, effective 09/18/2025
