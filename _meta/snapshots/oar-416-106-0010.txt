 Oregon Youth Authority 
 Chapter 416 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 106 Disclosure of Adjudicated Youth Information to Victims 
 416-106-0010 
 Purpose 
 
 The Oregon Youth Authority (OYA) values treating victims of crime with dignity and respect, and keeping them informed as prescribed by law. These rules delineate when OYA may disclose certain information about adjudicated youth to victims of crime.  

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 420A.025 & ORS 420A.115 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 420A.115 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 OYA 16-2023, adopt filed 05/30/2023, effective 05/31/2023
