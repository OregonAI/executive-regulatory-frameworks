 Public Utility Commission 
 Chapter 860 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 22 RATES 
 860-022-0550 
 Moratorium on Residential Rate Increases 
 
 Any increase in residential rates may not take effect from November 1 to March 31. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 756.040 & ORS 756.060 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 757.025, ORS 757.210 & ORS 757.240 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 PUC 1-2026, adopt filed 03/19/2026, effective 03/19/2026
