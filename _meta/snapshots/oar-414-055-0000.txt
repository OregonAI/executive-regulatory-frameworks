 Department of Early Learning and Care 
 Chapter 414 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 55 RATES FOR COMMISSIONER PER DIEM AND REIMBURSEMENT 
 414-055-0000 
 Definitions 
 
 (1) “Commission or Council” means those official bodies identified in ORS 326.425. 

 (2) “Qualified member” means a member who is not in full-time public service and who had an adjusted gross income in the previous tax year: 

 (a) Of less than $50,000, as reported on an income tax return other than a joint income tax return; or 

 (b) Of less than $100,000, as reported on a joint income tax return. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 292.495 & ORS 326.425 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 292.495 & ORS 326.425
