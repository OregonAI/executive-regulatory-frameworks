 Chapter 951 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 8 OREGON PRODUCTION INVESTMENT FUND CREATIVE OPPORTUNITY PROGRAM 
 951-008-0000 
 Purpose 
 
 The purpose of these rules is to provide guidance for the administration of the portion of the Oregon Production Investment Fund that is to be used for the Creative Opportunity Program as authorized by HB4153 (2022). 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 284.300 to 284.385 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  HB 4153, 2022; Chapter 75 (2022 Laws) 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 FVO 2-2022, adopt filed 07/11/2022, effective 07/11/2022
