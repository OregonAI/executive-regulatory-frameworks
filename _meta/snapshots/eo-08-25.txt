    EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 08 — 25

    AMENDMENT TO EXECUTIVE ORDER 06-14 ESTABLISHING
    TRANSPORTATION AND TOURISM TASK FORCE

    On October 9, 2006, I issued Executive Order 06-14, establishing the Transportation
    and Tourism Task Force in Oregon. The goals of the Task Force remain unchanged;
    however, to implement its mission, the Task Force must remain in existence for a
    longer period of time.

    IT IS HEREBY ORDERED AND DIRECTED:

            1.     Executive Order 06-14 is amended to expire on December 31, 2009.
