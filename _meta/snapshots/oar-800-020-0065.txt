 State Board of Tax Practitioners 
 Chapter 800 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 20 PROCEDURES 
 800-020-0065 
 Displaying of Licenses
 
 
 Oregon Licensed Tax Consultants, Oregon Licensed Tax Preparers, and Oregon Registered Tax Aides must display their current active license or registration in public view in their place(s) of business. The license or registration display platform may be digital or paper. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 670.310(1) & 673.730(10) 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 673.660
