 Department of the State Fire Marshal 
 Chapter 837 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 45 SMOKE ALARMS AND SMOKE DETECTORS 
 837-045-0055 
 Hotels with Approved Sprinkler Fire Suppression Systems 
 
 Hotels that have installed an approved sprinkler fire suppression system are exempt from the requirements of ORS 479.257(1). 
 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 479.257 & 479.295 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 479.250 - 479.300 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 OSFM 10-2000, f. 9-14-00, cert. ef. 10-1-00
