 Oregon State Lottery 
 Chapter 177 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 84 CASH POP 
 177-084-0000 
 Purpose 
 
 The purpose of these rules is to authorize the Oregon State Lottery game, Cash Pop. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  Or Const, Art XV, § 4(4)(a), ORS 461.120, 461.210, 461.220, 461.230, 461.240, 461.250 & 461.260 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  Or Const, Art XV, § 4(4), ORS 461.120, 461.210, 461.220, 461.230, 461.240, 461.250 & 461.260 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 LOTT 2-2024, adopt filed 12/20/2024, effective 01/13/2025
