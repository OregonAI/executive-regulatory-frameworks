 Oregon Housing and Community Services Department 
 Chapter 813 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 262 YOUTH EMERGENCY HOUSING ASSISTANCE (YEHA) PROGRAM 
 813-262-0000 
 Purpose and Objectives 
 
 (1) The purpose of OAR chapter 813, division 262 of the administrative rules is to implement the Oregon Housing and Community Services (OHCS) Youth Emergency Housing Assistance (YEHA) program.  

 (2) The purpose of YEHA is to provide housing-focused services to school-age children K-12 or their families at risk of or experiencing homelessness, to enhance family stability and school success.  

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 456.555 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 456.625 & HB 5019 (2023) 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 OHCS 46-2025, adopt filed 10/20/2025, effective 10/20/2025
