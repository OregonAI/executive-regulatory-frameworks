 Oregon Housing and Community Services Department 
 Chapter 813 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 2 AFFORDABLE RENTAL HOUSING DIVISION RULES 
 813-002-0035 
 Funding Allocations 
 
 (1) Applications will be evaluated based on standards and requirements published in the Funding Process.     

 (2) All HSC Funding approvals are considered conditional.    

 (3) HSC may conditionally approve maximum subsidy amounts, early project parameters, and project restrictions.    

 (a) Applicants may alter project plans for projects conditionally approved by HSC if the changes are immaterial and do not fundamentally alter the project scope or intent.   

 (b) Applicants may not alter project plans for projects conditionally approved by HSC if the changes are material and fundamentally alter the project scope or intent. If an Applicant seeks to make such changes, the project must return to HSC for approval of the changes prior to Applicant implementing the change(s).  

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  456.250 - 456.395, 456.500 - 456.723 & 458.210 - 458.740 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  456.250 - 456.395, 456.500 - 456.723 & 458.210 - 458.740
