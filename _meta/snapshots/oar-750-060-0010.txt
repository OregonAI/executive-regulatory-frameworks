 State Board of Towing 
 Chapter 750 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 60 Consumer Protection and Complaints. 
 750-060-0010 
 Consumer Protection Committee. 
 
 (1) The State Board of Towing may appoint a Consumer Protection Committee comprised of three Board Members, but no more than two tow industry members, to: 
(a) Provide direction and consultation to Board Staff; and 
(b) Make recommendations for action to the Board when necessary. 
(2) Any Board member or Board staff who has a conflict of interest to a complaint will declare the conflict and will not participate in any Committee activity concerning the complaint. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 822.265 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 822.265 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 SBOT 1-2023, adopt filed 10/31/2023, effective 01/01/2024
