 Oregon Housing and Community Services Department 
 Chapter 813 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 22 FLEX LENDING PROGRAM (FLEX) NEXTSTEP PRODUCT 
 813-022-0005 
 Program Definitions 
 
 The following definitions and those in ORS Chapters 456 and 458 apply to OAR Chapter 822, unless the context indicates otherwise, or the term is otherwise defined in a subsequent division: 

 (1) “Approved Lender” is a mortgage lender that is authorized by Oregon law to originate mortgage loans in the state  and has executed an agreement to provide mortgage loans to the Department. 

 ( 2) "Department's Servicer" means the loan servicer that has an agreement with the Department to provide mortgage loan servicing. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 456.555 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 456.555
