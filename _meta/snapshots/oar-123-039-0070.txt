 Oregon Business Development Department 
 Chapter 123 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 39 AQUIFER RECHARGE TESTING FORGIVABLE LOAN PROGRAM 
 123-039-0070 
 Application Prioritization 
 
 The Department may set forth prioritization or tie breaking considerations in the request for applications or other notices of funding availability. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 285A.075 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  OR Laws 2023, Chapter 606 
 
 History:
