 Department of Corrections 
 Chapter 291 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 180 INTERSTATE COMPACT 
 291-180-0107 
 Definitions 
 
 Adult on Supervision: Any person under the supervision of the Department of Corrections or a county community corrections agency who is on probation, parole, post-prison supervision, leave, or any other form of supervision. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 144.600, 179.040, 423.020, 423.030 & 423.075 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 144.600, 179.040, 423.020, 423.030 & 423.075 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 DOC 1-2025, adopt filed 01/14/2025, effective 01/14/2025
