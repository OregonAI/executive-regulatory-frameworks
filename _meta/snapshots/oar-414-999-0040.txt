 Department of Early Learning and Care 
 Chapter 414 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 999 WORKFORCE REGISTRY 
 414-999-0040 
 Foreign Language Translation 
 
 

 The Oregon Registry shall develop and implement a process for receiving and translating into English any applications and documentation related to professional development submitted under these rules. 

 (1) The Oregon Registry shall not charge Early Childhood Workforce members for translation of these documents. 

 (2) The Oregon Registry shall allow an early childhood workforce member to submit documents either directly or through a representative authorized by the Early Childhood Workforce member. 

 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  329A.525 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  329A.525
