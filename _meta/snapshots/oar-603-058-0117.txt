 Department of Agriculture 
 Chapter 603 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 58 FEEDS 
 603-058-0117 
 Feed Terms 
 
 For the purposes of ORS 633.006 to 633.089, 633.992, and OAR 603-058-110 to 603-058-290 the feed terms used in reference to commercial feed ingredients shall be those found in chapter 6 of the AAFCO Official Publication. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 663.067 & 633.055 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 633.006-089, 633.992, 561.605 & 561.620
