 Department of Forestry 
 Chapter 629 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 42 FIRE CONTROL 
 629-042-2000 
 Purpose 
 
 The purpose of OAR 629-042-2000 to 629-042-2060 is to set forth the standards, requirements, and procedures by which the Prescribed Fire Liability Pilot Program will be operated, pursuant to Sections 14 – 17, chapter 611, Oregon Laws 2023. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 526.016 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  Chapter 611 Oregon Laws, Sections 14 through 17 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 DOF 18-2024, adopt filed 09/30/2024, effective 10/01/2024
