 Oregon State Treasury 
 Chapter 170 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 130 PROCEDURE TO RECOVER ESCHEAT PROPERTY 
 170-130-0000 
 Purpose 
 
 These rules prescribe the procedures to recover Escheat Property held by the Treasurer as provided for by ORS 98.319,112.055, 114.555, 116.193, 116.203, 708A.430, 722.262, 723.466, any form of non-probate administration of an Escheat Property, and property received by the Department of State Lands pursuant to ORS 179.540–179.550 prior to October 4, 1997. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 178.050 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 178.050 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 OST 2-2021, adopt filed 06/29/2021, effective 07/01/2021
