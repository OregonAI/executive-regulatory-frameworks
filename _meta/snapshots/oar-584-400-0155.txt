 Teacher Standards and Practices Commission 
 Chapter 584 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 400 STATE APPROVAL PROCESS FOR EDUCATOR PREPARATION PROVIDERS 
 584-400-0155 
 Registered Apprenticeships 
 
 The Oregon Teaching Apprenticeship Standard is approved by the Oregon State Apprenticeship and Training Council and is in alignment with the TSPC Program Review and Standards Handbook. Those seeking to establish a Registered Teaching Apprenticeship program should contact the Administrator of the Joint Apprenticeship Training Committee (JATC) for the K-12 Teacher Occupation listed here: https://www.oregon.gov/boli/apprenticeship/Pages/apprenticeship-opportunities.aspx. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 342.147 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 342.147 
 
 History:
