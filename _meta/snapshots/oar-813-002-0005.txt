 Oregon Housing and Community Services Department 
 Chapter 813 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 2 AFFORDABLE RENTAL HOUSING DIVISION RULES 
 813-002-0005 
 Purpose and Objectives 
 
 OAR Chapter 813, Division 2 establishes the rules and guidelines for the Affordable Rental Housing (ARH) Division within the Oregon Housing and Community Services Department (OHCS). Division 2 is designed to govern the ARH Oregon Centralized Application (ORCA) process. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 458.210 - 458.740, HB 3395 (2023), ORS 456.250 - 456.395, ORS 456.500 - 456.723 & ORS 458.210 - 458.740 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 458.210 - 458.740, HB 3395 (2023), ORS 456.250 - 456.395 & ORS 458.210 - 458.740
