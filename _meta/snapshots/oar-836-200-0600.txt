 Department of Consumer and Business Services 
 Insurance Regulation - Chapter 836 
 
 
 
 
 Division 200 DEPARTMENT REGULATORY PROGRAMS 
 836-200-0600 
 Purpose and Statutory Authority 
 
 The purpose of OAR 836-200-0600 to 836-200-0670 is to administer the licensure of pharmaceutical representatives doing business in the state of Oregon pursuant to Oregon Laws 2021, chapter 593. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 731.244 & Or Laws 2021, ch 593 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  Or Laws 2021, ch 593
