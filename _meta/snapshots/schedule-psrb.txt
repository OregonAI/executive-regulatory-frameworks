Agency:  Psychiatric Security Review Board (PSRB)

Program Description
The Psychiatric Security Review Board (PSRB) is an independent, quasi-judicial state
agency responsible for protecting public safety by exercising statutory authority over
individuals whose involvement with the criminal or civil legal system has been
judicially determined to be sufficiently connected to a qualifying mental disorder.

The Board has continuing jurisdiction and supervisory authority over individuals
found Guilty Except for Insanity (GEI), Responsible Except for Insanity (REI), and
individuals committed as Extremely Dangerous Persons with Mental Illness. For these
populations, the PSRB conducts ongoing administrative and full hearings to
determine continued jurisdiction, conditions of release, including level of care,
revocation, and discharge.

Separately, the PSRB functions as a petition-based adjudicative body for two limited
statutory relief matters, including restoration of firearm rights and sex offender
classification, reclassification, and relief. In these matters, the Board does not exercise
ongoing supervision but rather adjudicates discrete petitions to restore rights or
relieve individuals from statutory restrictions or registration requirements. In addition,
the Board has the statutory duty to report classification levels of sex offender
registrants and firearm prohibitions to the Oregon State Police.

PSRB records consist of the documentation necessary to support these
determinations and to ensure due process, statutory compliance, and defensible
decision-making.
