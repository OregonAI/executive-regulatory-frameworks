 State Board of Towing 
 Chapter 750 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 10 General Administrative and Operational Rules. 
 750-010-0040 
 Contracts and Procurement. 
 
 The State Board of Towing may contract for services when: 
(1) The specialized skills, knowledge, and resources are not available within the Board or its staff; 
(2) The work cannot be done in a reasonable time with the Board’s own workforce; 
(3) An independent and impartial evaluation of a situation is required by a consultant with recognized professional expertise and stature in a field; or 
(4) Cost and expenses incurred by the Board will be reduced under a contract for the work. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 822.265 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 822.265 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 SBOT 1-2023, adopt filed 10/31/2023, effective 01/01/2024
