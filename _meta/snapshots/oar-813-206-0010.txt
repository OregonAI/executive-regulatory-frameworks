 Chapter 813 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 206 OREGON MULTIFAMILY ENERGY PROGRAM (ORMEP) 
 813-206-0010 
 Manuals and Guidance 
 
 Policies and instructions for this program are outlined in the Multifamily Energy Program Manual, dated January 27, 2026, incorporated herein by this reference (“Manual”). Other applicable policies, standards, and requirements are contained in the department's administrative rules of Chapter 813.
