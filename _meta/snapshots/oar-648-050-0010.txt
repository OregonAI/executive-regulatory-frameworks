 Department of Agriculture 
 Oregon Hemp Commission - Chapter 648 
 
 
 
 
 Division 50 INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY PROCEDURES 
 648-050-0010 
 Purpose and Objectives 
 
 OAR chapter 648, division 50, implements ORS 571.444, which authorizes Commission to develop intellectual property that relates to hemp or assists in the implementation, maintenance or development of Commission programs. ORS 571.444 requires the Commission to adopt rules to govern the ownership, management, disposal and use of intellectual property and other activities of the Commission relating to intellectual property. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 571.423 (19) & ORS 571.444 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 571.444 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 HEMP 3-2022, adopt filed 08/15/2022, effective 08/15/2022
