 Oregon Health Authority 
 Health Licensing Office, Board of Cosmetology - Chapter 817 
 
 
 
 
 Division 10 SAFETY AND INFECTION CONTROL 
 817-010-0008 
 Servicing Clients 
 
 When providing services in a field of practice an authorization holder must: 

 (1) Perform thorough hand washing with soap and water or other alternative hand-washing products, such as gel, aerosol spray, foam, or packaged hand wipes, immediately before and after serving each client to prevent cross contamination and exposure to blood or other potentially infectious materials. 

 (2) Wear single-use disposable protective gloves when performing a service or procedure that routinely involves exposure to blood or other potentially infectious materials. 

 (3) Wear eye goggles, shields or a mask if spattering is likely to occur while services are being performed. 

 (4) Dispose of refuse and waste materials that come in contact with blood or other potentially infectious materials according to provisions of OAR 817-010-0060. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 690.165, ORS 690.225 & ORS 690.205 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 690.165, ORS 690.225 & ORS 690.205
