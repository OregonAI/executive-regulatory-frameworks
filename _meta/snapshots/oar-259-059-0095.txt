 Department of Public Safety Standards and Training 
 Chapter 259 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 59 PRIVATE SECURITY ENTITY LICENSING 
 259-059-0095 
 Prohibited Entity Names 
 
 (1) Private security entities are prohibited from using a name that implies that the private security entity is, or is affiliated with, an existing law enforcement unit or public safety agency as defined in ORS 181A.355, the organized militia as described in ORS 396.105, the Armed Forces of the United States, a federal law enforcement agency or a federal intelligence agency. 

 (2) Private security entities operating under a name prior to July 1, 2016, are exempt from this restriction for as long as the private security entity is owned by the person that owned it on July 1, 2016 . 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 181A.870 & ORS 181A.893 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 181A.870 & ORS 181A.893
