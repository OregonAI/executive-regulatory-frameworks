 Oregon Housing and Community Services Department 
 Chapter 813 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 261 OREGON EVICTION DIVERSION AND PREVENTION (ORE-DAP) PROGRAM 
 813-261-0000 
 Purpose and Objectives 
 
 (1) The purpose of OAR chapter 813, division 261 of the administrative rules, is to implement the Oregon Housing and Community Services (OHCS) Oregon Eviction Diversion & Prevention (ORE-DAP) program. OHCS is designated as the state agency responsible for administering state and federal antipoverty programs in Oregon. 
 
(2) The purpose of ORE-DAP is to provide eviction prevention and housing stabilization services to households at risk of or currently experiencing homelessness. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 456.555 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 456.515-456.725 & 458.505 
 
 History:
