 Oregon Racing Commission 
 Chapter 462 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 10 Administration 
 462-010-0010 
 Elections of Chair, Vic- Chair and Secretary 
 
 The Chair, Vice-Chair and Secretary (should the commission opt to appoint a secretary) shall be elected biennially. Commissioners may be elected to the positions of Chair, Vice-Chair and Secretary for consecutive terms. Elections shall be held on the last scheduled commission meeting of each even calendar year. Terms of office shall run for two years beginning January 1 of the odd calendar year after elections. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 462.270 & ORS 462.270 (3) 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 462.270 & ORS 462.270 (3)
