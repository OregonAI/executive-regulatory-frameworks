 Oregon Liquor and Cannabis Commission 
 Chapter 845 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 26 CANNABIS CONCENTRATION LIMITS & HEMP PRODUCT REGISTRY 
 845-026-5700 
 Industrial Hemp-derived Vapor Item Testing Requirements 
 
 (1) A hemp vapor item manufacturer must comply with the Authority’s testing rules in OAR 333-007-0300 to 333-007-0490 and OAR 333, division 64 prior to the sale or transfer of an industrial hemp-derived vapor item, as specified in those rules. 

 (2) The Commission may impose a civil penalty of up to $500 per day per violation for failure to comply with this rule unless the person is a hemp handler licensed under ORS 571.281. 

 (3) This rule is effective on and after July 1, 2022. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 475C.898 & ORS 475C.544 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 475C.898 & ORS 475C.544
