 Water Resources Department 
 Chapter 690 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 380 WATER RIGHT TRANSFERS 
 690-380-7020 
 Change from Surface Water Point of Diversion to a Groundwater Appropriation 
 
 The surface water point of diversion of a water right permit, or portion thereof, may be changed to allow the appropriation of groundwater instead of surface water, provided the proposed change meets the standards set forth in: 

 (1) ORS 540.531(2) or (3); 

 (2) OAR 690-380-2130(2) to (11), except that the water right permit need not be a water use subject to transfer; and 

 (3) OAR 690-380-7300. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 536.025 & ORS 536.027 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 537.211, ORS 540.531(2) & ORS 540.532(3) 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 WRD 3-2026, adopt filed 03/20/2026, effective 04/01/2026
