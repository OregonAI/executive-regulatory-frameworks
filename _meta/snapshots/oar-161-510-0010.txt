 Appraiser Certification and Licensure Board 
 Chapter 161 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 510 FEES 
 161-510-0010 
 Fees
 
 
 The Board shall charge and collect the following non-refundable fees: 

 (1) Application fee — $1,000; 

 (2) Registration fee (two years) — $3,000; 

 (3) Fee for Registration Renewed (two years) — $4,250; 

 (4) Fee for Late Renewal — $1,000; 

 (5) Fee for Duplicate Registration — $40; 

 (6) Annual Appraisal Subcommittee (ASC) Fee — Actual Fee; 

 (7) Fingerprint and Background Checks — Actual Fee; 

 (8) Fee for Change or Addition of Subject Individual — $500; 

 (9) Fee for Change of Business Name — $1,000; 

 (10) Fee for Registration History — $50; 

 (11) Fee for Late Annual Report — $1,500. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 183.355, ORS 674.305(7), ORS 674.310 & 2011 OL Ch. 447 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 674.310 & 2011 OL Ch. 447
