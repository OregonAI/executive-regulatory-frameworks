 Department of Environmental Quality 
 Chapter 340 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 257 OREGON LOW EMISSION VEHICLES 
 340-257-0160 
 Severability 
 
 Each section of this division is severable, and if any section of this regulation is held invalid, the remainder will continue in full force and effect. 
 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 468.020, 468A.025 & 468A.360 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 468.020
