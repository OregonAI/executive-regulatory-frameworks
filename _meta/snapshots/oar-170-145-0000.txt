 Oregon State Treasury 
 Chapter 170 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 145 ADMINISTRATION OF UNCLAIMED PROPERTY 
 170-145-0000 
 Purpose 
 
 The purpose of these rules is to provide consistent procedures for the administration of the Uniform Disposition of Unclaimed Property Act, Oregon Revised Statutes (ORS) 98.302 to 98.436, 98.991 and 98.992, and to ensure that all unclaimed money and Property held in safekeeping are reported and paid over to the Treasurer in an accurate and timely manner. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 178.050 & ORS 98.422 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 178.050 & ORS 98.422 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 OST 2-2021, adopt filed 06/29/2021, effective 07/01/2021
