 Water Resources Department 
 Chapter 690 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 100 PAYMENT FOR PUBLIC BENEFITS IN WATER PROJECTS 
 690-100-0015 
 Eligibility 
 
 (1) To be eligible to apply for partial repayment of a project financed under the Water Development Loan Program, an applcant shall meet the definition of a Water Developer, as stated in ORS 541.700.  (2) Any sponsor of a Proposed Project as defined in these rules is eligible for partial repayment of a water development project affording a public benefit. 
 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 541 & 542
