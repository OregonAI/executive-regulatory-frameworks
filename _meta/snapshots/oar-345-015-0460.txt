 Department of Energy 
 Energy Facility Siting Council - Chapter 345 
 
 
 
 
 Division 15 PROCEDURES GOVERNING COUNCIL AND DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY PROCEEDINGS, INCLUDING SITE CERTIFICATE HEARINGS 
 345-015-0460 
 Interlocutory Appeals to Council 
 
 (1) Except as otherwise specifically provided for in the rules of this division, a party or limited party may not take an interlocutory appeal to the Energy Facility Siting Council from a ruling of the hearing officer unless such ruling would terminate that party's right to participate in the contested case proceeding. 

 (2) A party or limited party shall submit an appeal involving that party's right to participate in a contested case proceeding, with supporting arguments and documents, to the Council within seven calendar days after the date of the ruling of the hearing officer. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 469.470, ORS 469.370(5) & ORS 183.417(2) 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 183.415, ORS 469.370, ORS 469.405, ORS 469.440, ORS 469.605, ORS 469.615 & ORS 469.992 
 
 History:
