 Oregon Department of Education 
 Chapter 581 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 14 STUDENT INVESTMENT ACCOUNT 
 581-014-0028 
 Optional Targets Criteria for Mental and Behavioral Health for the Student Investment Account 
 
 (1) Eligible applicants can develop optional targets to monitor student, staff, and system changes that meet the following criteria: 

 (a) Are measurable in a consistent way each time new data is available; 

 (b) Are evidence-based; 

 (c) There is disaggregated data by focal student groups and/or staff, to the extent possible; 

 (d) Are culturally responsive; 

 (e) Align with the purpose of optional targets; and 

 (f) Connect to an investment. 

 (2) Optional targets not meeting these criteria must be approved by ODE prior to an SIA grant agreement being executed. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 327.190 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 327.190
