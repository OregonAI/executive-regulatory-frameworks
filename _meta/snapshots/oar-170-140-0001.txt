 Oregon State Treasury 
 Chapter 170 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 140 UNCLAIMED PROPERTY CLAIMS 
 170-140-0001 
 Applicability 
 
 (1) These rules to recover Unclaimed Property under OAR 170-140-0100 through 170-140-0140 (the “Rules for Recovery of Unclaimed Property”) apply to Claims made under the Treasurer’s statutory authority or by agreement with the Treasurer and another Entity. 

 (2) The Treasurer may waive procedural requirements when justice so requires. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 98.422 & ORS 178.050 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 98.422 & ORS 178.050 
 
 History:
