 Oregon Health Authority 
 Public Health Division - Chapter 333 
 
 
 
 
 Division 4 OREGON REPRODUCTIVE HEALTH PROGRAM 
 333-004-4000 
 Description of Abortion Access Plan 
 
 The purpose of OAR 333-004-4000 through 333-004-4130 is to establish requirements for the Abortion Access Plan under which authorized providers and agencies may receive reimbursement from the Oregon Health Authority for abortion services provided to individuals enrolled in health benefit plans described in ORS 743A.067(7)(e) and (9). 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 413.042 & ORS 435.230 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 413.032 & ORS 435.230
