 Department of Human Services 
 Self-Sufficiency Programs - Chapter 461 
 
 
 
 
 Division 196 SUMMER EBT PROGRAM 
 461-196-0000 
 General Purpose and Scope; SEBT 
 
 Summer Electronic Benefit Transfer (SEBT) program is established for the purpose of providing nutrition assistance during the summer months for each eligible child, to ensure continued access to food when school is not in session for the summer. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 411.060 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 411.060
