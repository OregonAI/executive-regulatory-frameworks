 Employment Department 
 Chapter 471 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 70 Paid Leave Oregon 
 471-070-0001 
 Purpose 
 
 The purpose of the division 70 rules is to provide procedures, standards, responsibilities, and criteria for administration of the Paid Leave Oregon program, defined in OAR 471-070-0010. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 657B.340 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 657B.340
