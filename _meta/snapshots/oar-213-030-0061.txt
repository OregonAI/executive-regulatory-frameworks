 Oregon Criminal Justice Commission 
 Chapter 213 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 30 THE IMPROVING PEOPLE'S ACCESS TO COMMUNITY-BASED TREATMENT, SUPPORTS, AND SERVICES (IMPACTS) PROGRAM 
 213-030-0061 
 Grant Application Processing 
 
 (1) The grant review committee will evaluate each application based on the criteria provided in the "Grant Application Review Criteria" section of these rules. 

 (2) The grant review committee will review each application and the recommendations of grant review committee staff. The grant review committee may reject portions of the application that fail to meet the criteria set forth in OAR 213-030-0050. 

 (3) The grant review committee will review and evaluate each application and provide a final approval or denial, in whole or in part. The grant review committee will notify applicants of an award decision within 75 days from the expiration of the grant application deadline. If there are extenuating circumstances, the grant review committee may, in its sole discretion, extend the deadline for award notification. The grant review committee staff will prepare a grant award agreement for each grant awarded, which will set forth the terms, conditions, and requirements of the grant award. 

 (4) The grant review committee may amend a grant award. After the grant is awarded, the grant review committee may amend the amount originally awarded based on non-compliance with the terms of the award or based on awardee's changed circumstances. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  SB 973 (2019) & Or Laws 2019, ch 563, § 1-14 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  SB 973 (2019) & Or Laws 2019, ch 563, § 1-14
