 Department of Corrections 
 Chapter 291 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 160 DOC PROCUREMENTS FOR OPERATIONAL SUPPORT AND ADULT IN CUSTODY PROGRAMS 
 291-160-0250 
 Notice of Intent to Award 
 
 (1) Notice of Intent to Award. DOC may provide Written notice of its intent to award to all Offerors at least seven calendar days before the Award of a Contract, unless DOC determines that circumstances justify prompt execution of the Contract, in which case DOC may provide a shorter notice period. DOC shall document the specific reasons for the shorter notice period in the procurement file. 

 (2) Finality. DOC’s Award shall be final. 

 (3) This rule does not apply to Contracts awarded as an Opportunity Buy, sole-source procurement, or specialty procurements. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 179.040, 421.438, 423.020, 423.030 & 423.075 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 179.040, 421.438, 423.020, 423.030 & 423.075
