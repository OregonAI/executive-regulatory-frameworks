 Oregon Housing and Community Services Department 
 Chapter 813 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 125 CO-LOCATION OF AFFORDABLE RENTAL HOUSING AND EARLY CARE AND EDUCATION (CARE) 
 813-125-0046 
 Third Party Administrator Oversight 
 
 In cooperation and collaboration with the DOE Early Learning Division, the Department will provide oversight to a Third Party Administrator if one is determined to be in the best interest of the program. Oversight may be established through a cooperative review process or may be done through the establishment of a CARE Review Committee that includes stakeholders as outlined in 813-125-0040. Joint reviews shall be done at least annually and may be more often if determined appropriate. The Department shall establish report criteria for the Third Party Administrator that includes, at a minimum, the information outlined in 813-125-0040.      

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 456.502 & ORS 456.555 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 456.502 & ORS 456.555 
 
 History:
