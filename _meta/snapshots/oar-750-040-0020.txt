 State Board of Towing 
 Chapter 750 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 40 Violations. 
 750-040-0020 
 Separate Violations. 
 
 Each violation of a law assigned to the State Board of Towing for administration, an administrative rule adopted by the Board, or an Order issued by the Board is a separate violation subject to separate Board disciplinary actions and sanctions, including, but not limited to, civil penalties assessed by the Board. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 822.265 & ORS 822.995 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 822.265 & ORS 822.995 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 SBOT 1-2023, adopt filed 10/31/2023, effective 01/01/2024
