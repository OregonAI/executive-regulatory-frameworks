Agency:  Oregon Board of Optometry

Program Description
The Oregon Board of Optometry is responsible for the licensure of doctors of
optometry (optometrists/optometric physicians) and enforcement of the statutes and
administrative rules governing the practice of optometry in Oregon.

The Board of Optometry is composed of five volunteer members who are appointed
by the Governor and confirmed by the Oregon Senate. Four of the members are
actively licensed Oregon optometric physicians and one is a public member.

The agency investigates and prosecutes violations of laws as they apply to
optometrists and acts on complaints filed with the agency and upon information
obtained while monitoring the profession. The agency has broad investigatory
powers, including authority to subpoena documents and take testimony. It may take
administrative action against persons or entities that violate ORS Chapter 683. It also
can suspend or revoke a license to work as an optometrist in Oregon.

The Board meets approximately every 12 weeks to review cases and to make rulings
on a case- by-case basis. The Board can either dismiss the case or issue a preliminary
finding of violation. If a preliminary finding of violation is issued, a settlement may be
negotiated. All settlements are approved by the Board.

If a hearing is requested, it is adjudicated through the Office of Administrative
Hearings in the Employment Department. Collections of fines are performed by
agency staff and the Department of Revenue and private collection agencies in
accordance with Legislative Fiscal Office procedures and continue until paid.

The Executive Director of the Board of Optometry is appointed by the Board with the
approval of the Governor to oversee the administration of the agency and the
handling of complaints. The Executive Director makes the initial assessment of
complaints received by the agency and establishes if the complaint will be
investigated based upon the jurisdictional scope of the agency. Investigations are
conducted in accordance with Oregon Revised Statutes Chapters 676 and 683.

Board staff receive and process license and renewal applications and maintain
licensed optometrist records. In addition, staff correspond with licensees, the public
and other agencies about the program and provide historical information on
licensees. License fees received are processed in the agency’s licensing database and
monies are recorded in the agency’s financial management system. Board staff also
provide direct customer assistance and information about applicable laws and
related topics.
