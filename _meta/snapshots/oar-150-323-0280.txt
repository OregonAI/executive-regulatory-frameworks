 Department of Revenue 
 Chapter 150 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 323 CIGARETTE TAXES 
 150-323-0280 
 Collection of Unsecured, Unpaid Tax After Deficiency or Jeopardy Determination; Collection Charge; Warrants 
 
 The provisions of OAR 150-314-0277 shall be followed to secure payment of deficiency determinations given under ORS 323.380(6) and of taxes determined under ORS 323.385. The amount of the deficiency or tax shall be the “amount assessed” referred to in OAR 150-314-0277. 
 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 305.100 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 323.390
