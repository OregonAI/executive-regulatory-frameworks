 Department of Revenue 
 Chapter 150 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 323 CIGARETTE TAXES 
 150-323-0130 
 Payment Type for Cigarette Stamps 
 
 Every licensed distributor purchasing tax stamps from the department must pay for the stamps with cash, cashier’s check, money order, or an electronic form of payment such as credit card, debit card, or electronic funds transfer (ACH Debit). 
 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 305.100 & 323.440 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 323.170
