 Oregon Health Authority 
 Oregon Educators Benefit Board - Chapter 111 
 
 
 
 
 Division 30 PLAN DESIGN DEVELOPMENT AND SELECTION 
 111-030-0045 
 Employee Assistance Program (EAP) Plan Selection Criteria 
 
 (1) Entities may select or allow an EAP option to be available to all Entity employees including, but not limited to, OEBB benefit-eligible employees and their dependents.  (2) Enrollment will happen automatically if selected by an Entity. 
 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 243.860 - 243.886 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 243.864(1)(a), 243.868(1) & 243.872(2)
