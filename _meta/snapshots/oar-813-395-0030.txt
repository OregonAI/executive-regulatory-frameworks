 Oregon Housing and Community Services Department 
 Chapter 813 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 395 PUBLICLY SUPPORTED HOUSING SELLER'S TAX CREDIT 
 813-395-0030 
 Monthly Reporting on Transaction Status 
 
 (1) Awarded applicants must submit a report relating to the status of their transaction on a monthly basis using a form provided by OHCS until the transaction closes or is cancelled. 

 (2) If a transaction that has been awarded the PuSH Seller’s Tax Credit is cancelled, applicants must make OHCS aware within 30 days of the cancellation. 
  

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 315.281 - ORS 315.291 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 315.281 - ORS 315.291
