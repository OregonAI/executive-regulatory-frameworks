 Department of Environmental Quality 
 Chapter 340 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 261 EMISSION STANDARDS FOR NEW HEAVY-DUTY TRUCKS 
 340-261-0090 
 Severability 
 
 Each requirement of this division is severable, and if any requirement of this division is held invalid, the remainder or the requirements in this division will continue in full force and effect. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 468.020, ORS 468A.025 & 468A.360 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 468A.025, 468A.360, ORS 468A.010, 468A.015, 468A.050 & 468A.279 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 DEQ 17-2021, adopt filed 11/17/2021, effective 11/17/2021
