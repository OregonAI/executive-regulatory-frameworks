 Division 70 Paid Leave Oregon 
 471-070-0455 
 Wages: Cafeteria Plans 
 
 Employee benefits paid through a cafeteria plan, as defined in the Internal Revenue Code Section 125, are not wages if listed as excluded in ORS 657.115, even if paid through a payroll deduction. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 657B.340 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 657B.010 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 ED 4-2022, adopt filed 01/31/2022, effective 01/31/2022
