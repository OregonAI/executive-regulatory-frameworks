 State Board of Towing 
 Chapter 750 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 10 General Administrative and Operational Rules. 
 750-010-0050 
 Board Meetings. 
 
 (1) The State Board of Towing will hold regular public Board Meetings at least once every fiscal quarter. 
(2) Regularly scheduled Public Board Meetings will be noticed to the public a minimum of ten (10) calendar days before the scheduled date of the meeting by: 
(a) Posting on the Board website; and 
(b) Posting on the Public Calendar of the Oregon Transparency website; and 
(c) Distributing a copy of the Notice by electronic mail to persons on the Board's mailing list 
(3) The Board will hold emergency or special meetings at the request of any Board member or the Board Administrator to address emergency or time-sensitive board matters or business, noticed to the public a minimum of three (3) calendar days before the scheduled date of the meeting by the same means described in OAR 750-010-0040 (2). 
(4) In case of an emergency, a meeting may be held pursuant to ORS 192.640. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 192.620, ORS 822.255 (5) & ORS 822.265 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 192.620, ORS 822.255 (5) & ORS 822.265 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 SBOT 1-2023, adopt filed 10/31/2023, effective 01/01/2024
