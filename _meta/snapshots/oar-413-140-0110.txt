 Department of Human Services 
 Child Welfare Programs - Chapter 413 
 
 
 
 
 Division 140 INDEPENDENT ADOPTION SERVICES 
 413-140-0110 
 Confidentiality 
 
 Adoption records contain confidential information. The Department may only disclose confidential information relating to an adoption as follows:  (1) As provided in Child Welfare Policies I-A.3.2, "Confidentiality of Client Information", OAR 413-010-0000 to 413-010-0075; I-G.3.3, "Adoption Registry", OAR 413-130-0300 to 413-130-0360; and I-G.3.4, "Assisted Search Program", OAR 413-130-0400 to 413-130-0520; or  (2) As otherwise authorized by law. 
 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 409.050 & 418.005 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 409.010 & 418.005
