 Oregon Government Ethics Commission 
 Chapter 199 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 40 EXECUTIVE SESSION 
 199-040-0060 
 Prohibition on Making Decisions in Executive Session 
 
 Unless otherwise authorized by statute, a governing body shall not make a decision, including a vote, in executive session. A governing body may reach an informal consensus in executive session, but any decision may only be made in open session.   

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 244.290 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 192.610, ORS 192.685 & ORS 192.660 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 GEC 1-2024, adopt filed 09/24/2024, effective 10/01/2024
