 Oregon Criminal Justice Commission 
 Chapter 213 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 90 Organized Retail Theft 
 213-090-0030 
 Definitions 
 
 As used in OAR 213- 090 -0010 to 213-090- 0090 , unless the context requires otherwise:   

 (1) “Applicant” means cities and counties, that submit an application to the Commission for a grant under the Organized Retail Theft Grant Program, and the Oregon Department of Justice.    

 (2) “Commission” means the Oregon Criminal Justice Commission.      

 (3 ) "Grantee acceptance period” means the period of time commencing when an applicant receives notice, in writing, from the Commission, that the Comm ission has decided to award the applicant grant funds, and concluding when an applicant has returned an executed grant agreement to the Commission.   

 (4) “Organized retail theft” has the meaning set forth in ORS 164.098.   

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  2023 OL Ch.582 §1-2  & 2025 SB 236 Section 24 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  2023 OL Ch.582 §1-2
