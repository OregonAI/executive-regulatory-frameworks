 Department of Transportation 
 Rail Division - Chapter 741 
 
 
 
 
 Division 110 PROTECTIVE DEVICES AT CROSSINGS 
 741-110-0050 
 Authority Required for Installation, Removal or Change of Protective Devices/Exceptions 
 
 No protective device shall be installed, removed or substituted for any other device, without prior authorization by Order of the Department, except:  (1) Additional flashing light units may be installed on existing installations of Flashing-light signals and Cantilevered Flashing-light signals by the railroad.  (2) Worn out or destroyed protective devices may be replaced by a similar device; however, the replacement shall comply with the MUTCD as adopted by OAR 734-020-0005. 
 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 184.616, 184.619, 823.011, 824.202 & 824.220 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 824.202
