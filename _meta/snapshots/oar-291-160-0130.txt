 Department of Corrections 
 Chapter 291 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 160 DOC PROCUREMENTS FOR OPERATIONAL SUPPORT AND ADULT IN CUSTODY PROGRAMS 
 291-160-0130 
 Late Offers, Late Withdrawals and Late Modifications 
 
 Any Offer received after Closing is late. An Offeror's request for withdrawal or modification of an Offer received after Closing is late. DOC shall not consider late Offers, withdrawals or modifications except as permitted in OAR 291-160-0140. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 179.040, 421.438, 423.020, 423.030 & 423.075 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 179.040, 421.438, 423.020, 423.030 & 423.075 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 DOC 11-2022, adopt filed 11/07/2022, effective 01/01/2023
