 Higher Education Coordinating Commission 
 Chapter 715 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 25 Common Course Numbering and Credit Transfer 
 715-025-0045 
 PROGRAM TERMINATION 
 
 An institution may terminate a program subject to a CAP. It shall notify the Council at the time the termination decision is made, and shall ensure students enrolled in the affected program pursuant to the CAP at the time of termination are provided a completion pathway that satisfies the conditions of the CAP. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 350.395 & ORS 350.429 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 350.395, ORS 350.429, ORS 350.404 & ORS 350.412 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 HECC 6-2023, adopt filed 08/11/2023, effective 08/11/2023
