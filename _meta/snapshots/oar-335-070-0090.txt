 Board of Examiners for Speech-Language Pathology and Audiology 
 Chapter 335 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 70 PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT 
 335-070-0090 
 Extenuating Circumstances 
 
 The Board may consider a waiver of professional development requirements or acceptance of partial fulfillment based on Board review of written documentation. 
 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 681.420(5) & 681.460 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 681.320(1)(a) 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 SPA 2-1996, f. & cert. ef. 7-22-96
