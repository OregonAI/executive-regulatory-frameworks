 Department of State Police 
 Oregon State Athletic Commission - Chapter 230 
 
 
 
 
 Division 110 ENFORCEMENT 
 230-110-0030 
 Records
 
 
 Every promoter and manager shall maintain a full, true and accurate set of books and records in connection with all licensed events and unarmed combat sports competitors . These records and any other records required by statute or rule shall be kept for at least five years and shall be open to inspection and audit by authorized representatives of the superintendent upon reasonable notice. 

   

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 463.113 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 463.113 & 463.320
