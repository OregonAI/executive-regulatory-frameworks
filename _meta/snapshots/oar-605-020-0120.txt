 Department of Agriculture 
 Oregon Beef Council - Chapter 605 
 
 
 
 
 Division 20 RATES FOR COMMISSIONER PER DIEM AND REIMBURSEMENT 
 605-020-0120 
 Non-Qualified Members 
 
 (1) In accordance with the procedures set forth in OAR 605-020-01440, the Oregon Beef Council shall provide to a non-qualified member of the Oregon Beef Council reimbursement of actual and necessary travel or other expenses actually incurred in the performance of a non-qualified member’s official duties within the limits provided in Oregon Accounting Manual Policy 40.10.00. 

 (2) A non-qualified member of the Oregon Beef Council may decline to accept reimbursement of expenses related to the non-qualified member’s service on the Oregon Beef Council. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 577.260 & 577.290 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 577.260 & 292.495
