 Board of Nursing 
 Chapter 851 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 41 STANDARDS FOR THE NURSE INTERN: LICENSURE AND FUNCTIONS 
 851-041-0040 
 Nurse Intern Practice Team Member Role 
 
 (1) A nurse intern must function under the direct supervision of an RN who agrees to provide direct supervision as described in ORS 678.398 and OAR 851-045-0060(3). 

 (2) The nurse intern’s role on the practice team is limited to the performance of authorized functions pursuant to ORS 678.396 as assigned by the RN providing direct supervision. 

 (3) The nurse intern’s performance of authorized functions must occur: 

 (a) In adherence to these rules; and 

 (b) Within the parameters of the supervising RN’s established plan of care for a client. 

 (4) The nurse intern must disclose their license type and practice team member role to the client and with other practice team members. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 678.111, ORS 678.150, ORS 678.396 & ORS 678.398 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 678.111 & ORS 678.150
