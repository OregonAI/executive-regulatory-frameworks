Oregon Housing and Community Services Department

Chapter 813

Division 375
MODULAR HOUSING FUND

813-375-0016
EVALUATION CRITERIA

Funds will be awarded by the Department using application evaluation criteria, including but not limited to:

(1) Provide a plan to comply with legislative demand prioritization categories as by the Department that must be met in the order prescribed:

(a) First, state and local governments following a wildfire or other disaster;

(b) Second, low-income housing construction in this state; and

(c) Third, middle income housing construction in this state.

(2) Attest that Fund award will be utilized for the portion of their business operation that conforms with Oregon State Building Codes definition of Modular Housing, or “prefabricated structure.”

(3) Verify that all Grant funding will be utilized in entity’s Oregon business operations.

(4) Provide a sound business plan and demonstrate financial stability.

(5) Complete an Equity Centered Management Plan.

(6) Provide a reporting and outcomes tracking plan.

(7) Any other provision as established by the Department.

Statutory/Other Authority: HB 2761 (2023)
Statutes/Other Implemented: HB 2761 (2023)
History:
OHCS 30-2024, adopt filed 08/28/2024, effective 09/02/2024
OHCS 11-2024, temporary adopt filed 05/24/2024, effective 05/29/2024 through 11/24/2024
