 Parks and Recreation Department 
 Chapter 736 
 
 
 
 
 
 Division 56 OREGON MAIN STREET 
 736-056-0012 
 Oregon Main Street 
 
 Role as statewide Main Street Coordinating Program 

 (1) The Department holds an agreement with Main Street America to be the official statewide coordinating program. 

 (2) The Department meets the requirements of Main Street America as the official statewide coordinating program. 

 
 
 
 
 Statutory/Other Authority:  ORS 390.124(1) & 390.262(9) 
 
 
 Statutes/Other Implemented:  ORS 390.262 & 390.264 
 
 History: 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 PRD 6-2024, adopt filed 09/23/2024, effective 09/23/2024
