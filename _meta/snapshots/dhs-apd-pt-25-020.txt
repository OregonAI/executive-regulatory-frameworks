Discussion/interpretation
Currently adult foster home (AFH) licensors use their initials or note their last name when
marking plans of correction as accepted or not accepted in the Corrective Action and Licensing
Management System (CALMS). This information is posted publicly on the long-term care
licensing website and potentially places licensor(s) at risk. Since caregiver initials and names
are deidentified in reports, Licensors shall also no longer use their initials or name when
confirming corrections in their licensing reports.
Implementation/transition instructions
When the Licensor is noting the plan of correction in CALMS as accepted or not accepted the
Licensor shall indicate the following:

“Accepted by the LLA” with the date recorded or “Not accepted by the LLA” with the date
recorded. CALMS history will show which Licensor made the notation if that information is
needed.

Local/branch action required
Implementation of policy as written.

Training and support as needed.
