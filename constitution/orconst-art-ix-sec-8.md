# Stationery for use of state (Or. Const. Art. IX, sec. 8)

## At a glance

Or. Const. Art. IX, sec. 8 — Stationery for use of state. Article IX (FINANCE), Oregon Constitution, in effect following the approval of amendments and revisions on November 5, 2024.

## Full text

Section 8. Stationery for use of state. All stationary [sic] required for the use of the State shall be furnished by the lowest responsible bidder, under such regulations as may be prescribed by law. But no State Officer, or member of the Legislative Assembly shall be interested in any bid, or contract for furnishing such stationery.—
