# Compensation of members (Or. Const. Art. IV, sec. 29)

## At a glance

Or. Const. Art. IV, sec. 29 — Compensation of members. Article IV (LEGISLATIVE BRANCH), Oregon Constitution, in effect following the approval of amendments and revisions on November 5, 2024.

## Full text

Section 29. Compensation of members. The members of the Legislative Assembly shall receive for their services a salary to be established and paid in the same manner as the salaries of other elected state officers and employes. [Constitution of 1859; Amendment proposed by S.J.R. 3, 1941, and adopted by the people Nov. 3, 1942; Amendment proposed by H.J.R. 5, 1949, and adopted by the people Nov. 7, 1950; Amendment proposed by H.J.R. 8, 1961, and adopted by the people May 18, 1962]
