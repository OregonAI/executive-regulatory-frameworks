# Deficiency of funds; tax levy to pay (Or. Const. Art. IX, sec. 6)

## At a glance

Or. Const. Art. IX, sec. 6 — Deficiency of funds; tax levy to pay. Article IX (FINANCE), Oregon Constitution, in effect following the approval of amendments and revisions on November 5, 2024.

## Full text

Section 6. Deficiency of funds; tax levy to pay. Whenever the expenses, of any fiscal year, shall exceed the income, the Legislative Assembly shall provide for levying a tax, for the ensuing fiscal year, sufficient, with other sources of income, to pay the deficiency, as well as the estimated expense of the ensuing fiscal year.—
