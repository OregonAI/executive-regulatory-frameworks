# Distribution of school fund income (Or. Const. Art. VIII, sec. 4)

## At a glance

Or. Const. Art. VIII, sec. 4 — Distribution of school fund income. Article VIII (EDUCATION AND SCHOOL LANDS), Oregon Constitution, in effect following the approval of amendments and revisions on November 5, 2024.

## Full text

Section 4. Distribution of school fund income. Provision shall be made by law for the distribution of the income of the common school fund among the several Counties of this state in proportion to the number of children resident therein between the ages, four and twenty years.—
