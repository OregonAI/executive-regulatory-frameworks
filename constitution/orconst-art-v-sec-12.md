# Governor may convene legislature (Or. Const. Art. V, sec. 12)

## At a glance

Or. Const. Art. V, sec. 12 — Governor may convene legislature. Article V (EXECUTIVE BRANCH), Oregon Constitution, in effect following the approval of amendments and revisions on November 5, 2024.

## Full text

Section 12. Governor may convene legislature. He may on extraordinary occasions convene the Legislative Assembly by proclamation, and shall state to both houses when assembled, the purpose for which they shall have been convened.—
