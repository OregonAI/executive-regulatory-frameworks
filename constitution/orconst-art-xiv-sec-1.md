# Seat of government (Or. Const. Art. XIV, sec. 1)

## At a glance

Or. Const. Art. XIV, sec. 1 — Seat of government. Article XIV (SEAT OF GOVERNMENT), Oregon Constitution, in effect following the approval of amendments and revisions on November 5, 2024.

## Full text

Section 1. Seat of government. The permanent seat of government for the state shall be Marion County. [Created through S.J.R. 41, 1957, and adopted by the people Nov. 4, 1958 (this section and former 1958 section 3 of this Article adopted in lieu of former original sections 1 and 3 of this Article)]
