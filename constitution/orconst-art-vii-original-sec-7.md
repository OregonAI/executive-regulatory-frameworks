# Term of Supreme Court; statements of decisions of court (Or. Const. Art. VII (Original), sec. 7)

## At a glance

Or. Const. Art. VII (Original), sec. 7 — Term of Supreme Court; statements of decisions of court. Article VII (Original) (THE JUDICIAL BRANCH), Oregon Constitution, in effect following the approval of amendments and revisions on November 5, 2024.

## Full text

Section 7. Term of Supreme Court; statements of decisions of court. The terms of the Supreme Court shall be appointed by Law; but there shall be one term at the seat of Government annually:— And at the close of each term the Judges shall file with the Secretary of State, Concise written Statements of the decisions made at that term.— Note: Section 7 is in substance the same as section 4 of amended Article VII.
