# Amending Executive Order 17-09 (Executive Order 17-10)

## At a glance

Executive Order 17-10: Amending Executive Order 17-09. (Title as printed in the Governor's office listing of record; see Provenance.)

## Full text

Office of the Governor
State of Oregon


    EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 17-10

                    AMENDING EXECUTIVE ORDER NO, 17-09



    NOW THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY DIRECTED AND ORDERED:

    Executive Order 17-09 was issued on May 5, 2017. In Section III of Executive
    Order 17-09, the last sentence is amended to provide as follows:

           The Governor will set a debt collection benchmark to measure performance
           of state agencies by January 1, 2018.


                         in
           Done this \Z day of May, 2017, at Salem, Oregon.




                                              eas
                                         Kate Brown
                                         GOVERNOR

                                         ATTEST:




                                         Dennis Richardson     *
                                          SECRETARY OF STATE
                                                                                      le
