# Rescinding Executive Order 25-30 (Executive Order 25-31)

## At a glance

Executive Order 25-31: Rescinding Executive Order 25-30. (Title as printed in the Governor's office listing of record; see Provenance.)

## Full text

OfficeoftheGovernor
State of Oregon
EXECUTIVE ORDER 25-31
RESCINDING EXECUTIVE ORDER 25-30
On November 24, 2025, I signed Executive Order 25-30 declaring an emergency
due to potential disruptions in fuel delivery because of the second major Olympic
Pipeline shutdown in the past three months.
As the Olympic Pipeline system has returned to full service, pursuant to my
authority under ORS 401.204(1), I hereby proclaim and order that Executive Order
25-30 is rescinded, and that the state of emergency declared therein is terminated.
Done at Salem, Oregon, this 9th day of December, 2025.
I in Katet
Tina Kotek
GOVERNOR
ATTEST
Ponias G Read
Tobias Read
SECRETARY OF STATE

Materials
