Agency: Board of Chiropractic Examiners

Program Description
The board is responsible for administering the Chiropractic Practice Act, as well as
establishing the rules and regulations governing chiropractic medicine in Oregon.
The seven-member board is comprised of five licensed chiropractors and two public
members appointed by the governor for three-year terms.

The board’s mission is to serve the public; regulate the practice of chiropractic
medicine; and promote quality, competent and ethical health care. The board’s
programs include application, examination, continuing education, public information
and investigation of complaints. If violations are found, the board issues disciplinary
actions and/or rehabilitation plans to meet competency standards.

Program Records
001  Complaint and Disciplina ry Records, Individual
(a) Retain audio records 4 years or until transcribed, destroy
(b) Retain records of investigations leading to discipline 50 years or until the
death of licensee, whichever is later, destroy
(c) Retain records of investigations not leading to discipline 25 years after case
close, destroy
