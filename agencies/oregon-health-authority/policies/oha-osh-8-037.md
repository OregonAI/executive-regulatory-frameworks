I.    PURPOSE AND APPLICABILITY
      A. This policy explains who is responsible for taking care of patient property at
         Oregon State Hospital (OSH).
      B. All OSH Staff shall follow this policy.

II.   POLICY
      A. When a patient is admitted, OSH shall tell the patient or their representative
         about property rights, rules, and procedures. This will be done both verbally and
         in writing.
         1. Incoming patient property is handled as described in Procedures A.
             a. Staff may not throw away patient property unless allowed by OSH rules.
      B. Patients have the right to keep a reasonable number of personal items and have
         storage in their room.
         1. These rights can be limited for safety or treatment reasons.
         2. Staff can only restrict property if there are reasonable grounds to believe an
             item is unsafe or conflicts with the patient’s care plan. Restrictions shall follow
             OSH policies, including:
             a. 4.010, “Patient Funds.”

  C. Patients may not have items that:
     1. Are not allowed in their treatment care plan (TCP),
     2. Are more than reasonable amounts allowed by this policy or by a patient’s IDT,
     3. Are contraband or prohibited, or
     4. Inappropriate clothing.
     5. Staff may not give patients items that are contraband or prohibited,
     6. Staff will help patients check in and out items with limited access as described
         in Procedures D.
  D. Unless restricted by law, the patient’s treatment care plan, the Product Risk
     Assessment (PRA) procedure in OSH Policy 4.018, or other OSH policy, items
     not on the prohibited list are allowed in reasonable amounts.
  E. OSH shall review and investigate claims if a patient’s property is lost, damaged,
     or stolen while in OSH custody per Procedures C.
  F. Staff shall not borrow, sell, buy, or trade property with patients.
  G. Patients, visitors, and staff shall follow OSH policy 7.001, “Mail and Packages for
     Patients.”
  H. OSH follows all applicable regulations, including federal and state statutes and
     rules; Oregon Department of Administrative Services, Shared Services, and
