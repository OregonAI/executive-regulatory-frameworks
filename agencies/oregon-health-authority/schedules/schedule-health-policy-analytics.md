# Health Authority (formerly DHS) - Health Policy and Analytics

## At a glance

Special records retention schedule for Oregon Health Authority, published by the Oregon Secretary of State, Archives Division, schedule number 2019-0010, effective date as printed: February 2020. It runs to 2 pages and lists the agency's own record series with a retention and disposition clause for each. Record series that every state agency holds are NOT here: they are covered by the OAR chapter 166 general schedules, which this corpus references rather than copies — 1 distinct general-schedule rule(s) are cited in the text below.

## Full text

OREGON STATE ARCHIVES
Records Retention Schedule
Edition: February 2020

Organizational Placement   Schedule number: 2019-0010
Agency: Oregon Health Authority
Division: Health Policy & Analytics
Unit:  Transformation Center

Program Description:
The Transformation Center is responsible for technical assistance to health system partners,
including Coordinated Care Organizations. It shares innovations and best practices.

Program Records
001 Transformation Center Technical Assistance Records
Retain 5 years after superseded or obsolete, destroy
002 Transformation Center Publications
Retain 10 years, destroy

State Agency General Records Retention Schedule Records
Records include but are not limited to:
Administrative Records (OAR 166-300-0015)
Conference, Seminar, and Training Program Records
Correspondence
Legislative Development Advisory Committee Records
Policy and Procedure Guidelines and Manuals
Policy Development and Planning Records
Publication Preparation Records
Staff Meeting Records

Databases
None

OREGON STATE ARCHIVES
Records Retention Schedule
Edition: February 2020

Organizational Placement   Schedule number: 2019-0010
Agency: Oregon Health Authority
Division: Health Policy & Analytics
Unit:  Transformation Center
Program: Patient-Centered Primary Care Home (PCPCH)

Program Description:
The Patient-Centered Primary Care Home (PCPCH) collaborates with stakeholders to set the
standard for transformative, whole-person and evidence-based primary care.

Program Records
003 PCPCH Recognition Records
(a) Retain annual recognition data 20 years, destroy
(b) Retain individual clinic documentation (evidence of recognition) 10 years, destroy
(c) Retain other records 5 years, destroy
004 PCPCH Publications
Retain 10 years, destroy

State Agency General Records Retention Schedule Records
Records include but are not limited to:
Administrative Records (OAR 166-300-0015)
Conference, Seminar, and Training Program Records
Correspondence
Legislative Development Advisory Committee Records
Legislative Tracking Records

Publication Preparation Records
