 Discussion/interpretation:
 The Emergency Response Management System is the official system of record for all
 emergency situations involving the Office of Aging and People with Disabilities (APD)
 in-home consumers and its licensed Long-Term Care (LTC) facilities and Adult Foster
 Homes (AFHs).

 This policy establishes expectations for using the Emergency Response Management
 System (ERM). Effective immediately, all APD, Area Agency on Aging (AAA) Type B,


                                                                           DHS 0079 (01.19)
and Safety Oversight and Quality staff must use ERM when supporting LTSS in-home
consumers, LTC facilities and AFHs during disasters and emergencies.

ERM users doing outreach to In-Home Consumers, Adult Foster Homes and Long-
Term Care facilities shall follow guidance in training materials, Quick Reference
Guides (QRGs) and knowledge articles (uploaded in ERM) and as directed by the APD
Safety, Emergency Response Team.

At a minimum, ERM users will need to be able to complete the:
      • Rapid assessment to assessment immediate needs (when appropriate),
      • Log a call to document the contact,
      • Create a task to document assistance being provided or requested and
      • Create an emergency facility, which could include a private residence, to
         identify where consumers and residents evacuate to.

This will enable staff to conduct appropriate follow-up, ensuring the safety of
consumers and residents, as well as the continuation of services.

Implementation/transition instructions:
Local office leadership will need to designate staff to use ERM by immediately if not
already done and notify CALMS support using the CALMS access request form. New
users will need to attend a new user training before using the ERM. Training will be
provided by APD Central Office virtually, either live or recorded.

The APD Emergency Response Team consist of designated ODHS staff with
additional ERM training to analyze events, map, export data and liaison with local
offices and agency partners such as ODHS Office of Resilience and Emergency
Management (OREM), Oregon Health Authority and Local Emergency Services.

The APD Emergency Response Team will send communication regarding emergency
events from a centralized e-mail, DHS.APD.ERM@odhsoha.oregon.gov. Please send
all communication through this e-mail box to allow for the entire APD Safety,
Emergency Response Team to have visibility.

mitigation during emergency events. Please use the link below to account for your time
This will help us more clearly identify the amount of time staff are spending supporting
consumers and residents during emergency events. This will also allow us to request
reimbursement from FEMA when state or federal disasters are declared and to support
the need for resources to assist with emergency event response.
