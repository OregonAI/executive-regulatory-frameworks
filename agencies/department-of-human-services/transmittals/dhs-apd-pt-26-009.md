       •   Assisted Living Facilities
       •   Residential Care Facilities
       •   Memory Care Facilities
       •   In-Home Care Agencies
       •   Adult Day Services

Effective July 1, 2026, the nursing facility rate will increase per OAR 411-070-0442.


Effective July 1, 2026, PacificSource PACE will be serving most of Lane County.


APD Central Office will make all changes necessary to implement these rate changes.

See attached rate schedule. The above links for the rate schedule will not be available until July
1, 2026.
