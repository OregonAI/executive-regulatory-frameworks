Discussion/interpretation
Updates in bold [brackets].
The Oregon Department of Human Services (ODHS) Aging and People with
Disabilities (APD) must make changes to meet several Home and Community-
Based Services (HCBS) waiver performance measures required by the Centers for
Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS). A recent audit shows Oregon is out of
compliance in these areas:
  •   Performance Measure 18 requires that a waiver participant’s service plan
      addresses risk and safety concerns.

  •   Performance Measure 19 requires that a waiver participant’s service plan
      addresses personal goals and preferences.

  •   Performance Measure 23 requires that services are delivered in the type,
      scope, amount, duration, and frequency authorized in the service plan.

  •   Performance Measure 24 requires that waiver participants are offered a
      choice between institutional care and home and community-based services.

  •   Performance Measure 25 requires that waiver participants are offered a
      choice among providers.

This Policy Transmittal (PT) outlines the actions APD is taking to improve
compliance, and what the case managers (CM) must do to ensure compliance.
APD has created new trainings, tools and resources for case managers to use
during service planning to meet CMS requirements.
CMs must immediately implement these actions after completing the required
training (available starting May 18, 2026), and no later than June 12, 2026.

Implementation/transition instructions
Case managers and applicable staff must complete the 1915(c) HCBS Waiver
Compliance Training Series (APD-AR-26-013) and implement the actions below no
later than June 12, 2026.
Performance Measure 18: Service plans address risk and safety concerns
To meet the requirements of Performance Measure 18:
  •   For all service plans implemented on or after June 12, 2026, or earlier if
      training is completed, CMs must complete the required risk assessment
      before service planning for individuals and for settings where a risk
      assessment is required. For Oregon Project Independence – Medicaid (OPI-
      M) cases, please refer to APD-PT-24-017 for additional guidance.

  •   CMs must identify risk levels, document risk and safety concerns, record
      mitigation strategies, and document the back-up plan in the risk assessment
      comments.

  •   CMs must use the Risk Assessment Comment with the risk back-up plan, and
      the appropriate Mitigation and Monitoring templates available on CM Tools
      when documenting the risk assessment in Oregon ACCESS (OA). There is a
      template for none/low risk assessments and a different template for
      medium/high risk assessments.

  •   Until system changes are released, CMs must copy and paste the
      appropriate template into the narration box in the risk assessment screen in
      OA. (We expect OA to be updated fall 2026.)

  •   CMs must complete risk-focused monitoring at the required frequency
      based on the risk level identified in the risk assessment. Please review APD-
      PT-19-022 for additional information.

Performance Measure 19: Service plans address personal goals and preferences
To bring Oregon into compliance with Performance Measure 19:
  • At each new intake and each reassessment, CMs must review and update
      the Client Details section in Oregon Access (OA). At a minimum, CMs must
      review and update:
        o   Goals

        o   Preferences

        o   Strengths
  •   [Staff must add or update comments for each section and document the
      date each section was reviewed with the individual. OA system changes are
      planned to support this requirement in the future. Additional directions will
      be provided in a Policy Transmittal (PT) when the system update is ready for
      release. APD is updating Module 3: Goals and Preferences in the ODHS -
      APD - AAA - 1915(c) HCBS Waiver Compliance Training Series to reflect this
      clarification].

  •   This review must be completed before service planning. The service plan
      and authorized services should help the individual meet their goals.

Note for Performance Measures 18 and 19:
OA will be updated to build the risk template into the risk assessment module. OA
will also require CMs to complete goals, preferences and strengths for new
intakes and renewals before service planning can be completed. Details about
these system changes will be issued in a future transmittal when the changes are
ready for release.
Performance Measure 23: Services are delivered in the type, scope, amount,
duration, and frequency authorized in the service plan
This measure compares the hours authorized in the service plan to the hours the
individual uses.
To ensure compliance with Performance Measure 23:
  •   ODHS is developing a new report to identify individuals who use less than 90
      percent of their authorized service hours.

  •   A future transmittal will provide instructions for running the report, required
      follow-up steps, and will cover:

         o   When CMs must review use of authorized hours monthly.

         o   When an individual uses less than 90 percent of authorized hours, the
             CM must follow up with the individual to determine the reason for the
             underuse.
              ▪    CMs must utilize the Service Underutilization Response (SUR)
                   template to document the follow-up and any actions taken in OA.
                   These contacts may count as Direct and Indirect Waivered Case
                   Management contacts.

Performance Measures 24 and 25: Choice of services and choice of providers
These measures are currently documented through a signed SPAN/SPA. CMS
requires that the consumer sign the SPAN and SPA. However, return rates are
low, which affects compliance.
After completion of the required training and no later than June 12, 2026, CMs
must use the new Service Options Counseling (SOC) form to document
compliance.
To achieve compliance with Performance Measures 24 and 25:
  •   CMs must obtain a signed SOC form at the initial assessment and at each
      reassessment while with the consumer.

  •   The SOC form documents the individual’s choice of program benefits, care
      settings, and providers.

  •   CMs or support staff must upload the signed SOC form to Laserfiche within
      10 business days after the individual signs it.

  •   For efficiency, CMs are encouraged to use Docusign to obtain the required
      signature.

  •   CMs must be prepared to provide choice counseling so individuals can make
      an informed decision about available program options, care settings, and
      providers.

CMs will continue to send the SPAN/SPA and upload signed copies to Laserfiche.
This is required as part of the service planning process. Quality Assurance (QA)
will no longer use the SPAN/SPA to verify compliance with this measure and will
review the SOC form moving forward.

Local offices must implement this policy no later than June 12, 2026, and ensure
all staff are informed of the updated guidance.
Supervisors must ensure staff have completed the required training and direct
staff to CM Tools for resources.
