Discussion/interpretation
The attached rate schedule has the following changes effective January 2026:
   •   Per the Collective Bargaining Agreement (CBA), Adult Foster Homes (AFH) will receive a
       rate methodology change from base to tiers and an increase. Specific Need Contracts
       will receive a 3% increase. ECOS will receive the 3% increase or the tier rate, whichever is
       higher.
   •   Per the CBA, effective January 1, 2026, Home Care Workers will receive a $1.25 increase
       and mileage is increasing to $.70 per mile.
   •   For Residential Care Facilities (RCF), effective January 1, 2026, a new payment is added
       under Tier 5.
   •   The PACE Rate will change effective January 1, 2026, for PACE providers.
   •   CBC Room and Board rates, as well as CBC and NF Personal Allowance (PIF) will increase.

APD Central Office will make all changes necessary to implement these rate changes.

See attached rate schedule. The above links for the rate schedule will not be available until
January 1, 2026.
