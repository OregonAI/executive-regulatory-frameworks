Discussion/interpretation
Updated August 14, 2026, to remove language regarding notifying the reporter
from section 4.

Pilot project to explore centralized screening of Adult Protective Services (APS)
incoming calls. Reporters calling to report abuse through SAFELINE are prompted to
provide their zip code so the call can be sent to the appropriate local office.

When a zip code is not provided, the call will be sent to the Central Office APS
(COAPS) screeners.

  1. COAPS screeners will receive SAFELINE abuse report calls.

        a. Based on information received, the COAPS screener will conduct the
           intake, make the screening decisions, and as appropriate, assign the
           intake to the responding Local Office (LO).

        b. COAPS screener must screen reports as described in OAR 411-020-0060
           including but not limited to evaluating information for APS jurisdiction,
           eligibility requirements and determining if the reported concern meets
           the definition of abuse or self-neglect or qualifies for APS Risk
           Management.


  2. COAPS screeners must enter the intake information in Central Abuse
     Management (CAM) following APS rules, policies and procedures.


  3. COAPS screeners must provide the reporter with an intake number for
     future reference and explain how to contact APS.


  4. After making the screening decision, COAPS screeners will notify the Local
     Office (LO) when the intake has been assigned to them. In addition, inform
     the LO of any actions taken by the COAPS screener.


        a. For abuse investigations, once sent to the LO, the LO must assign the
           APS Specialist and finish intake notifications and referrals.


        b. For APS Risk Management cases that are assigned at intake, the LO will
           assign to an APS Specialist and finish intake notifications and referrals.


        c. When the intake report is screened out as non-abuse, and no further
           action is required, the COAPS screener closes the Intake. The COAPS
           screener must finish intake notifications and referrals.


        d. If screened out, but further action is needed, COAPS may refer the
           intake to the LO to complete. This finishing of an intake may include
           required intake notifications and referrals.


  5. Because this is a pilot, some Central Office SAFELINE calls may be directed to a
     LO to conduct the Intake and make screening decisions



Implementation/transition instructions
This policy transmittal will provide a framework for a pilot project that will test the
effectiveness of centralized screening for reports of abuse or self-neglect.

Training/communication plan
The policy transmittal will be posted at the APS Staff Tools website, APD’s
Transmittals website, and the CAM Knowledge tab with other APS policy
transmittals. Communication and discussion regarding this policy transmittal will
occur during APS supervisor meetings, APS Training, and other venues as needed.
Requests for technical assistance on applying this policy transmittal should be
sent to the Central APS Unit at aps.techassistance@odhsoha.oregon.gov.
