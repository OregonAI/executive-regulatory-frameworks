 Discussion/interpretation: ODHS and OHA have created a joint policy based on
 the 2025-2027 Oregon Home Care Commission (OHCC) Collective Bargaining
 Agreement (CBA). Article 15, Section 2 states:
      The State may authorize a provider to receive mileage reimbursement to
      travel from their home to the individual’s home on a case-by-case basis
      due to no available providers living within a reasonable distance to the
      Individual.

In this transmittal, provider refers to Homecare Worker (HCW), Personal Care
Attendant (PCA) and Personal Support Worker (PSW). This policy will be
implemented in selected rural areas that meet state criteria for frontier areas or
Health Professional Shortage Areas (HPSA). The intent is to assist individuals who
live in remote rural areas of the state in accessing provider services.

This policy applies when the one-way travel distance to an individual’s home
exceeds 50 miles and the individual lives in a qualifying ZIP code. Mileage
reimbursement will only be provided for miles beyond the initial 50 miles, up to
50 additional miles. Requests for reimbursements exceeding 50 miles will require
approval per program-specific guidance. Below you will find examples, the
general implementation instructions, program specific guidance, and qualifying
ZIP codes. Attached is a ZIP code list by county, which will also be available
through your program.

Examples:
  • Provider drives 99 and under (to and from) they will receive no
    reimbursement.
  • Provider drives 100 miles (to and from individuals’ residence) will get
    nothing.
  • Provider drives 110 (to and from) they will get reimbursed for 10 miles.
  • Provider drives 120 miles (to and from) they will get reimbursed for 20
    miles.

Implementation instructions:
  1. Identify whether the individual lives in a designated ZIP code area approved
     for rural milage reimbursement.
  2. Confirm and document that the individual has been unable to find providers
     within a 50-mile radius of their home.
  3. Determine whether the provider must travel more than 50 miles from the
     provider’s home to the individual’s home using Google Maps.
  4. Calculate the number of miles traveled more than 50 miles one way (i.e.,
     reimbursable miles = one way distance minus 50 miles). This mileage will
     count towards reimbursement both to and from the provider’s home.
  5. If a provider requests mileage reimbursement for travel exceeding 100
     miles in one direction, approval may be granted on a case-by-case basis. See
     program-specific guidance below for the appropriate approval process.
  6. Only approve if the provider has a valid driver’s license and vehicle
     insurance.
  7. Rural mileage reimbursement will only be approved until the individual’s
     next assessment, until the provider changes their home address, or until the
     provider stops working for the individual. At the reassessment, or if the
     provider changes address, mileage reimbursement must be reviewed to
     verify if the criteria continue to be met for the provider.
  8. See specific program instructions for additional documentation and
     approval requirements.

Documentation:
For ODDS Case Management Entities:
Case Managers will need to authorize mileage reimbursement for a PSW who
must travel more than 50 miles one way to provide services using the newly
created PSW Mileage Authorization Form. This approved mileage must be billed
and reimbursed through eXPRS. The form must be uploaded to eXPRS Plan of
Care and will only be valid for the current individual support plan (ISP) period.

Mileage reimbursement between the service recipient’s and PSW’s home does
not need to be authorized in the service recipient’s ISP or in a service agreement.

For APD/AAA Staff:
Form APD 397950 has been created for this mileage reimbursement, which can
be found on the forms server. Copies of this form should be provided to the HCW.

It is the HCW’s responsibility to fill out and submit the form for reimbursement.

The HCW may email or mail the form to:
     Provider Relations Unit (PRU)
              Mail: Oregon Department of Human Services
                     Attn: Provider Relations Unit
                     500 Summer St NE, E-02
                    Salem, OR 97301
              Email: apd.ceppayments@odhsoha.oregon.gov

If a HCW submits to the local office for processing, the local office must email the
form to PRU at the above email.

Mileage reimbursement over 100 miles one way must be approved by Central
Office. Please email Central Office with reasoning for the request to:
APD.MedicaidPolicy@odhsoha.oregon.gov

Staff must narrate in the individual’s case when mileage is approved for a specific
HCW. Narration must include:
             ▪ HCW name and provider #
             ▪ Why mileage was approved (ex: no available providers and what
                was checked to confirm)
