Discussion/interpretation:

Updates are being implemented related to documentation of screening decisions and
notification to reporters following a recent internal audit by the Oregon Department of
Human Services internal audits unit.

This policy transmittal is effective immediately and changes the process for how to
document when a reporter is notified in CAM.

APS SCREENING DECISIONS: DOCUMENTATION AND NOTIFICATION TO
REPORTERS

A. SCREENING DECISION DOCUMENTATION:

  1. Local APS staff must document a clear and comprehensive explanation for all
     screening decisions in CAM. Pursuant to policy, APD-APS-040-001 Timeframes
     and Deviations, the following applies:

     a. Screening, as defined in OAR 411-020-0060, begins upon receipt of a report
        of suspected abuse or self-neglect by the local office. A report of abuse is
        considered to have been “received,” and screening begun, on the date and at
        the time of its receipt, if received during Business Hours. After Business
        Hours, the report is considered to have been received at the beginning of the
        next Business Day (8:00 AM to 5:00 PM Monday through Friday, excluding
        holidays as determined by the state).

     b. A screening decision (i.e., whether the report of abuse or self-neglect meets
        criteria for investigation) is required to be made no later than the end of the
        next Business Day following the day of receipt of the report.

  2. For all intakes with allegations, the rationale for the screening decision must be
     documented in the “Reason for Screening Decision Detail” field in CAM.

  3. For all non-abuse intakes, the rationale for the screening decision must be
     documented under the “Additional Notes” field in CAM.

     Examples and instructions are available in QRG - APS Screening Decisions:
     Documentation and Notification to Reporters.

  4. Enough detail must be provided so that a reviewer can understand why the
     screening decision was made and what available information and screening-
     related rules and policies were used to reach the screening decision.

    a. The following information must be documented in the CAM intake for the
       screening decision, as applicable, and as described in the QRG above:

         i. Information regarding the alleged victim’s (AV’s) eligibility, including age,
            reported disability, location of the reported incident, etc.

         ii. The nature of the reported incident or conduct, and the reasoning for why
             the allegation didn’t meet the APS definition of abuse.

        iii. APS rule and policy citations relevant to the screening decision.

        iv. References to any supplemental information available to the screener
            from other sources (collateral contacts, databases such as CAM and
            Oregon Access, etc.) that supports the screening decision.


B. REPORTER NOTIFICATION & DOCUMENTATION:

  1. Local APS staff must consistently notify reporters of screening decisions and
     document in CAM that they have done so. If more than one person reports the
     same concern, all persons making the report are reporters and receive
     notification.

  2. Local APS staff must notify the reporter of the screening decision no later than
     the end of the next Business Day following the screening decision. If the first
     attempt is unsuccessful, a second attempt must be made by the end of the
     second Business Day following receipt of the report.

    a. Notification may occur in the same way the report was made, e.g., by
       telephone if the initial report was made by phone, by email if the initial report
       was made by email, etc. If staff leave a voicemail or send an email as part of
       the first notification attempt, advising the reporter of the screening decision
       and providing the CAM intake number and a contact number for calling back,
       a second attempt at notification is not necessary.

    b. Contacting the reporter with the screening decision must be documented in
       the CAM intake detail as identified in QRG - APS Screening Decisions:
       Documentation and Notification to Reporters.

    c. Intakes should never be left open or not assigned while attempts to reach the
       reporter to provide notification are underway. Once an attempt to notify the
       reporter is made and documented, the intake can be closed in CAM, even if
       the attempt wasn’t successful. Intakes assigned for investigation cannot be

        closed in CAM until an attempt to notify the reporter has occurred and been
        documented.

  3. Notification of the screening decision is not required as described below, If the
     reporter is:

     a. A routine cross-report from a public safety entity, e.g., law enforcement, fire,
        or emergency medical services. However, local offices are expected to
        confirm notification expectations with the local public safety entities to avoid
        misunderstandings;

     b. Reports transmitted in writing from an entity with no identified contact person;

     c. Reports from any reporter who requests to not receive notification;

     d. A routine facility self-report of an incident.


C. QUALITY ASSURANCE REVIEWS:

  1. Central APS will conduct periodic quality assurance reviews of CAM intakes and
     share the results with local APS offices, beginning in early 2025.

  2. The screening quality assurance review will look at timeliness of screening
     decisions, timeliness of screening notifications, and thoroughness of
     documentation of screening decisions.

  3. Performance targets will be set, and corrective actions by local APS offices will
     be required when they perform below the performance targets.

Implementation/transition instructions:

On March 20, 2025, a CAM Refinement was implemented that changed fields under
the intake detail page in CAM.
