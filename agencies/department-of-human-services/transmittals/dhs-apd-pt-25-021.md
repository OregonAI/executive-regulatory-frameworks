Discussion/interpretation
Due to mandatory Federal regulations, Oregon Department of Human Services – Aging and
People with Disabilities (ODHS-APD) is required to reinstate the liability payment obligation,
effective January 01, 2026, to individuals enrolled in PACE (Program of All-inclusive Care for the
Elderly) and who reside in a community-based care (CBC) facility (adult foster home, assisted
living, memory care facility, residential care facility) or a nursing facility (NF). Individuals
receiving PACE services in their home are not required to pay a monthly liability payment.
This transmittal changes the previous policy APD-PT-21-042 that states PACE participants are
not required to pay a monthly liability payment.

Implementation/transition instructions
PACE organizations will be responsible for managing the liability payments. Local ODHS offices
should not process liability payments received from PACE participants. The Office of Financial
Services (OFS) will not process liability payments from PACE participants. Any liability payments
received from PACE participants should be returned to the participant with instructions to
contact the PACE organization for information regarding where they should send the monthly
liability payment.

Important:
   •   Room and board payments should continue to be paid to the CBC provider.

   •   There is no liability requirement for PACE participants in 2025.

When a PACE participant fails to make liability payments, they may be disenrolled from PACE
and set up with APD long-term services and supports (LTSS) to receive their care. All eligibility
rules regarding non-payment of liability will apply while receiving APD-LTSS.

When a participant receives PACE services in their home and moves into a CBC or NF, the
liability may be waived for the initial month of placement (OAR 461-160-0610).

Participants will receive a letter about this this change. A copy of the letter is provided at the
end of this transmittal.

Local/branch action required
Staff who manage PACE cases should:

   •   Ensure the CA/PS is up to date with the correct PACE benefit type (PACE – In Home; PACE
       – Residential; PACE – Nursing Facility).

          o REMINDER: DO NOT create a 512 or a POC in MMIS for PACE participants.

   •   Complete assessments timely and update CA/PS as soon as PACE enrollment begins.
       Submit extension requests, if necessary, providing enough time for processing to avoid
       loss of benefits to the participant.

   •   Review ONE or MMIS to ensure the liability amount is correct. ONE will send notification
       to the participant once the liability is calculated. Instructions on how to view the liability
       in ONE can be found on the Case Management Tools page on the ONE System page,
       under the Medicaid Financial Eligibility and LTC/ONE Training session 5.

          o The liability notices will begin issuing in mid-December for January liability. Please
             note that ONE will initially issue a liability notice based upon 2025 income.
             However, an additional liability notice (or notices) will be issued, with the latest
             reflecting the liability amount owed, based upon 2026 income. Participants will
             need to utilize the latest notice that is received.

   •   As new individuals enroll in PACE, staff will need to inform them of the monthly liability
       requirement. When an individual already residing in a CBC or NF setting enrolls in PACE,
       if requested, staff may send a copy of the current 512 document or liability notice to the
       PACE organization.

Central office action required
   •   Central Office will send a letter to current PACE participants, notifying them of the
       change in policy and who will be affected by this change.

   •   Changes will be made to the ONE system to allow the liability calculation for PACE
       participants residing in a CBC or nursing facility. PACE participants receiving care in their
       home will not be required to pay a liability and therefore should not receive a liability
       notice.

          o The changes in ONE are expected to be completed by 12/10/2025. ONE will show
             the calculation using 2025 income until the 2026 COLA updates are computed in
             ONE.

   •   Central Office will send a monthly report to the PACE organization showing who has a
       liability and the liability amount.

   •   Updates to transmittal APD-PT-21-042 will be made and published.

   •   Update OAR 461-160-0610

   •   Central Office staff will provide technical assistance when needed.

**Letter being sent to PACE participants**



Dear PACE participant,

We want to let you know about an important change coming soon.

If you live in a nursing facility (NF) or a community-based care (CBC) facility (assisted living,
residential care, adult foster home, or memory care facility), you may be responsible for paying
a monthly amount for your care.

People who get in-home services are not affected by this change.

What is changing?

   •   In the past, Oregon did not collect this monthly amount (called a “liability amount”) for
       people in NF or CBC settings while enrolled in PACE (Program of All-inclusive Care for the
       Elderly).

   •   Due to mandatory Federal regulations, the State is required to reinstate the liability
       payment obligation to participants living in a CBC facility and nursing facility – effective
       January 01, 2026.

   •   You will make your liability payment directly to the CBC or NF provider. Do not pay/send
       the monthly payment to the State or to any local ODHS office and AAA office.

NOTE: You will still pay the room and board amount to the facility provider if you live in assisted
living, memory care facility, residential care facility, or an adult foster home.

What will I owe?

Your monthly amount is based on your gross income and allowable deductions. The State’s
eligibility system figures it out using rules that apply to long-term services and supports. If it
finds that you need to pay something, you will get a separate notice that shows the amount you
owe. You may get two or more notice about what you owe. The latest notice that you receive
will reflect your income for 2026, so this should be used to know how much liability is owed.
Example of the liability calculation for someone living in a CBC facility:

   $1,200 (Gross monthly income)
   -$967 (personal needs and room and board costs)
   =$233 (Estimated monthly liability amount)
Example of the liability calculation for someone living in a nursing facility:

   $1,200 (Gross monthly income)
   -$79.07 (personal needs)
   =$1,120.93 (Estimated monthly liability amount)

Note: These are examples. Your payment amount will be different based on your gross income
and allowable deductions.

Who will bill me? How do I pay?

   •   You will make your liability payment directly to the CBC or NF provider.

What if I get services in my own home?

   •   If you receive in-home services, you are not affected. You will not be asked to pay a
       monthly liability amount.

What if paying this monthly amount will be hard for me?

   •   Please call the PACE organization or your case manager. They can explain your options
       and provide resources.

Questions?

   •   Call your PACE center or the PACE organization main number: Providence PACE 503-215-
       6556 or North Coast 503-717-7150

   •   Call your case manager

We’re here to help.

Sincerely,
Oregon Department of Human Services – Aging & People with Disabilities
