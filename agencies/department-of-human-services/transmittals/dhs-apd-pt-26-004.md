Discussion/interpretation
This transmittal introduces a new policy allowing AAA Directors, APD District Managers, AAA
Program Managers, and APD and AAA local office Managers/Supervisors supporting OPI-M
case managers to approve Oregon Project Independence – Medicaid (OPI-M) assistive
technology and special medical equipment and supplies up to $500 for individuals receiving
OPI-M services.

Please note that the limit for lift chair approvals is $1,500 as specified in this transmittal:
https://www.oregon.gov/odhs/transmittals/APDTransmittals/pt26001.pdf

The policy of local office approval does not apply to:

   •   Chore services,
   •   Home modifications, or
   •   Items costing $500 or more.

Requests for these items must continue to be submitted to Central Office (CO) for review.

Implementation/transition instructions
   1. Services Case Manager (SCM) Responsibilities:
      1) Follow the OPI-M Ancillary Services Basic Process Flow.
      2) Complete and narrate the 3406 Ancillary Request Form, including:
         • The need for the item/service.
         • Alternatives explored.
         • Confirmation that the item is not covered by insurance or other payor source.
      3) Submit requests for assistive technology or special medical equipment and supplies
         costing $500 or less to the LO Supervisor for review and approval.
      4) Forward request to KPlan.Requests@odhsoha.oregon.gov with “OPI-M request” in
         the subject line when:
         • The request exceeds 200 units for incontinence supplies. Please see APD-PT-24-027
            for additional information on how to calculate units.
         • The total cost exceeds $500 per request.

   2. Supervisors Responsibilities:
      Prior to approval, Supervisors must verify:

       Eligibility & Documentation
       1) The individual has an active OPI-M benefit in Oregon ACCESS (OA).
       2) A completed and signed OPI-M PLAN in on file that:
           • Clearly identifies the need; and
           • Links the request to assessed needs, identified risks, or service goals.
       3) The 3406 Ancillary Request Form is accurately completed
       4) OA narration clearly documents:
           • The need for the item or service;
           • Alternatives explored; and
           • Confirmation that no other payor source is available.
    For assistive technology requests, Supervisors must verify that:
       • Three bids were obtained.
       • If fewer than three bids are available, justification is documented in OA narration.

For Special Medical Equipment and Medical Supplies requests, Supervisors must verify that:
     1) The supplies must not be covered by the individual’s insurance (fully or partially) or
        unpaid natural supports.
     2) Ongoing supplies may be approved only through the end of the current OPI-M benefit
        period.
     3) Gloves:
        • May be authorized only for use by the individual or unpaid natural supports.
        • May not be authorized for Homecare Worker (HCW) or In-Home Care Agency
            (IHCA).
        • HCWs may request a monthly order of gloves through the Oregon Home Care
            Commission (OHCC) using the PPE Order Form:
            https://survey.alchemer.com/s3/7265662/PPE-Order-Form.
        • IHCAs are responsible for providing gloves for their caregivers.
     4) Incontinence supplies:
        • Total authorized units must not exceed 200 per month.
        • Refer to the OPI-M Ancillary Services transmittal for additional guidance on how to
            calculate units.
     5) Total medical supply cost:
        • Must not exceed $500 per month, including delivery charges.
     6) Requests exceeding 200 units per month or exceeding $500 per month must be
        forward to KPlan.Requests@odhsoha.oregon.gov with “OPI-M request” in the subject
        line.
 3. Additional Requirements:
 The following requirements apply to all OPI-M ancillary service requests:
    1) Delivery fees must be included in the total requested amount.
    2) Bids must not include warranties or other optional add-ons.
    3) All documents must be saved in Laserfiche.
    4) Requests must be submitted to CO for review and approval when the request
       involves:
       •   Chore services;
       •   Home or environmental modifications;
       •   Any request exceeding $500;
       •   Incontinence supplies exceeding 200 units per month.
    5) Items must not be fully or partially covered by insurance or any other payor source.
    6) Any change to an approved amount requires a new request and review.
  Examples Include, but are not limited to:
     •   A change in product size that increases or decreases cost.
     •   Removal of authorized gloves once a paid HCW begins providing care.

  4. Local Office Approval/Denial Process
     1) Supervisor documents all approvals and denials in OA narration.
     2) Supervisors must submit approvals using the Local Supervisor Approval Form.
     3) For denials:
            a. The Supervisor must notify the SCM.

            b. The SCM must issue SDS 0540 – Notification of Planned Action.
            c. Supervisors may consult CO for assistance with denial language, as needed.

     4) Upload all related documents to Laserfiche.


  5. Payment Process:
     1) OPI-M ancillary services cannot be paid locally.
     2) Payment will occur only after delivery and acceptance of approved item or service.
     3) Payment will be issued by invoice only.
     4) Invoices must include the delivery date and be sent to:
         KPlan.Requests@odhsoha.oregon.gov Include “OPI-M Invoice” in the subject line.


  6. Quality Assurance Monitoring

     CO will conduct quarterly audits of Local Office approvals to ensure compliance with
     policy and administrative rules.

     Audit findings will be shared with Managers and District Managers to identify training
     and technical assistant needs.

Local/branch action required
  Local offices must implement this policy immediately and ensure all staff are informed of the
  updated guidance. Managers must direct staff to CM Tools for complete instructions and
  resources.

Central office action required
  CO will monitor the implementation, provide technical support as needed, and conduct LO
  audits to ensure compliance.
