Discussion/interpretation
The Work Number (TWN) external website has been used to obtain employment
and income data for Oregon Project Independence – Medicaid (OPI-M) eligibility
determinations. As of February 14, 2026, access to the external TWN site will no
longer be available. Employment verification for OPI-M must instead be
completed through the ONE system or direct client documentation. The
instructions below are only required if an applicant and/or their spouse reports
they are working or there are indicators they are working.

Implementation/transition instructions
Effective February 14, 2026, OPI-M staff must discontinue use of the external TWN
website for employment verification.

1. OPI-M cases in the ONE system

  •   For new OPI-M applications and renewals where a case is maintained in the
      ONE system, employment verification will be obtained using TWN
      integration within ONE when available.

  •   Staff should complete all necessary employment and income verification
      using the integrated TWN functionality in ONE as part of eligibility
      processing.

  •   The steps for this process can be found in the TWN section of the IEVS
      EG, and the TWN training remains current and available on Workday, ODHS -
      OEP-LET – The Work Number (TWN) Interface.

2. OPI-M cases outside of ONE

  •   For all other OPI-M cases not in the ONE system, the external TWN site can
      no longer be used.

  •   New OPI-M applicants and renewal cases outside of ONE must provide
      verification of employment directly.

When employment information is not available through ONE and must be
obtained from the applicant:

  1. Issue a ‘Notice of Request for Information or Verification’ (DHS 4234) to
     request verification of employment.

  2. Request the following documentation:
          o   Employer name, address, and phone number

          o   Dates of employment (start and end, if applicable)

          o   Current rate of pay and payroll frequency

          o   Recent pay stubs (e.g., last 4–6 weeks)

Staff must allow at least 10-day notice for applicants to provide this information
per due process requirements.

Local/branch action required
Effective February 14, 2026, local offices must discontinue use of the external TWN website.
Staff must verify employment using TWN integration in ONE when available or issue a 4234
pending notice to request verification for OPI-M cases outside of ONE.

Central office action required
Central Office will disable access to the external TWN website, update policy and training
materials, and provide guidance and support to ensure a smooth transition for staff.
