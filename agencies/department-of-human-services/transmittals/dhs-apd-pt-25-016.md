Discussion/interpretation
Aging and People with Disabilities (APD) has contracted with Docusign to help send, sign and
management documents electronically for individuals applying for and receiving APD Medicaid
long-term care services.
What is DocuSign?
DocuSign is a cloud-based electronic signature platform. It lets staff send documents for digital
signatures and securely manage them without printing or mailing paper copies. It can help
save time, reduce costs and improve compliance. Docusign can be used on phones, tablets,
laptops or desktop computers.

Implementation/transition instructions
APD has given Docusign access to all districts for APD and Type B staff that handle case
management related documents. Docusign should be the first option offered to individuals for
signing of forms available through the platform.

There are two roles available for local office staff in Docusign:

   •   DS Sender
          o Sends documents (called envelopes) electronically to individuals for signing.
   •   District Administrator – Each district has a minimum of one dedicated District
       Administrators who:
          o Add or remove users
          o Reassign envelopes to other users
          o Manage shared access and shared envelopes.

If you haven’t received an activation email from DocuSign and need access, contact your
District Administrator. They can add you or resend the invitation if previously sent. If you don’t
know who your District Administrator is, please ask your supervisor.

Central office action required

apd.docusignsupport@odhsoha.oregon.gov. In the coming months, CO will reach out to local

platform.
