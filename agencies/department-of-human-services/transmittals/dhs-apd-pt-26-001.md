Discussion/interpretation
Note: [Updated information in brackets]
This transmittal introduces a new process that allows Local Office (LO) Managers and District
Managers to approve lift chair purchases up to $1,500 for individuals receiving Long-Term
Services and Supports (LTSS) or Oregon Project Independence – Medicaid (OPI-M). Purchases
above this amount must continue to be submitted to Central Office (CO) for review and
approval.

Previously, all lift chair requests required CO approval. This created delays in service delivery
and added administrative steps for LOs. This change is being implemented to streamline
decision-making, improve timeliness, and support individuals in accessing needed equipment.

Implementation/transition instructions
Managers are responsible for verifying the following before an approval is made:
   •   The individual is receiving LTSS or OPI-M. Individuals receiving State Plan Personal Care
       (SPPC) are not eligible.
   •   The individual is assessed at least minimum assist in transfer assistance as identified in
       the most recent CA/PS or OPI-M PLAN.
   •   Case Manager (CM) has recorded the request in Oregon ACCESS (OA) case narration,
       including alternatives discussed.
   •   Three bids are obtained; if fewer, the reason must be documented in OA narration.

Important:
  • Delivery fees and disposal fees are allowable and are not included in the $1500 approval
     limit. Delivery fees and disposal fees may not exceed $300.00.
  • Bids cannot include warranties or other add-ons.
  • Recliners without a lift function cannot be approved since they are not an assistive
     device.
  • When possible, the individual needs to test out the chair and ensure it will meet their
     needs prior to authorization and delivery.
  • Selected lift chair must fit the individual’s size and needs. As an example, the seat width
     and depth will fit the individual’s body. When possible, choose a chair with a weight
     capacity at least 50 pounds above the individual’s current weight.
  • Special fabrics and other options that are above and beyond the minimum necessary to
     meet the needs of the individual are not permitted.
  • For replacement lift chairs, the reason must be clearly documented in OA.
Approvals and Denials

All decisions must be documented in the individual’s OA case. For approvals, a Manager Lift
Chair Approval Checklist has been created and can be found in the K-plan Forms section of CM
Tools. This checklist must be signed by the approving manager and a copy saved to the
individual’s file. Digital signatures/approvals are acceptable and must be attached to the
checklist when saved to the individuals file if on a different document (for example, email).
Managers may deny lift chair requests and the CM will notify the individual of the denial using
form APD 540, Notification of Planned Action. Managers can reach out to CO for assistance with
denial language and the following denial language examples may be used and adjusted as
needed:

   •   Recliner chair: The request for a recliner is denied. A recliner is considered furniture, not
       assistive technology. OAR 411-035-0010(5)(11), 411-035-0025, 411-035-0030(4)
   •   Lift Chair: You do not have an assessed need for a lift chair. A lift chair will not increase
       your independence, decrease the need for assistance from another person, or improve
       your health and safety. OAR 411-035-0010(5)(11), 411-035-0030(1)(a)-(c)

Payment

Lift chair purchases cannot be made using a SPOTS card and all payments must be made after
the chair is delivered and accepted by the individual. Once this is complete, LOs must complete
the following steps:

For LTSS cases:

       Staff will enter the payment into the MRP ONE system after the invoice has been received.
          Guidance for entering MRPs in ONE can be found in the Program Payments and
          Support QRG.

       The ONE system currently limits payments to a maximum of a $1000. If the purchase is
         over $1000 it will require two payment entries.

             o    For example: One payment for $1000 and second payment for $500.

       A manager or district manager will approve the MRP payment(s) in ONE after entry. LO
         sends the LO Lift Chair Checklist to KPlan.Requests@odhsoha.oregon.gov. This is
         required for expenditure tracking and audit purposes.

For OPI-M cases:

Lift chairs can only be paid through invoice. LO staff will need to send the checklist, invoice, and
a W-9 from the provider to the CO OPI-M team at [KPlan.Requests@odhsoha.oregon.gov and
include “OPI-M Invoice” in the subject line.]

Quality Assurance Monitoring

CO will audit local office lift chair approvals quarterly to ensure compliance with policy and
rules. Findings will be shared with Managers and District Managers to help identify training and
support needs.
Local/branch action required
Local offices should begin using the Lift Chair Checklist immediately and ensure all staff are
aware of the new guidance. Managers should direct staff to CM Tools for full instructions and
resources.

Central office action required
CO will monitor the implementation of this change, provide support as needed, and conduct LO
audits. To support LO leadership, CO has scheduled the following webinar for leadership to
discuss the best practice for reviewing and approving lift chair requests. Please contact CO at
least two weeks before the webinar if accommodations are needed.
